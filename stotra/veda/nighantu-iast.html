Nighaṇṭu

The Vedic word-lists commented on by Yāska · five adhyāyas, 78 khaṇḍas

The Nighaṇṭu, the Vedic word-lists on which Yāska wrote the Nirukta: lists of synonyms (adhyāyas 1–3), obscure words (4) and deities (5). The text is Lakshman Sarup's critical edition (1927). Words in square brackets are found in only part of the manuscripts. Sarup prints svaras, which are not carried here. The glosses are a plain English key to each list.
