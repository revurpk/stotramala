Rukmiṇī Kalyāṇam

Pōtana · the wedding of Rukmiṇī and Kṛṣṇa

Bammera Pōtana's Telugu Bhāgavatam, 10th skandha (first part), verses 10.1-1680 to 1792, as printed by Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams. Rukmiṇī's love for Kṛṣṇa, her letter carried by a brāhmaṇa, her abduction from the temple of Gaurī, Rukmī's defeat and the wedding at Dvārakā. The Telugu is the source; the IAST is a reading aid; prose passages (vacanam) keep the book's line breaks.

[Gadyam] Thus, in the Śrī Mahābhāgavatam, the great Purāṇa composed by Pōtanāmātya — son of the minister Kesana, whose poetry is wonderful through the grace of the Supreme Lord, whose learning was inborn — ends the first part of the tenth skandha, which holds the tales of Devakī's marriage, the voice from the sky, Kaṃsa's frenzy, and the rest of Kṛṣṇa's childhood and youth, down to the birth of Rukmiṇī, her message, Vāsudeva's coming, the taking of Rukmiṇī, the flight of the kings, the humbling of Rukmī and Rukmiṇī's wedding.
