word by word · translation

aiśvaryam lordship yat-prasādena by whose grace saubhāgya-ārogya-sampadaḥ good fortune, health, prosperities śatru-hāniḥ destruction of enemies paraḥ mokṣaḥ highest liberation stūyate is praised sā she na kim why not janaiḥ by people

iti devyāḥ kīlakastotraṃ saṃpūrṇam
