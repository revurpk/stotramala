Śrī Durgā Sapta-ślokī

The Seven Verses of Durgā

śiva uvāca

dēvī tvaṃ bhaktasulabhē sarvakāryavidhāyini |
kalau hi kāryasiddhyarthamupāyaṃ brūhi yatnataḥ ‖

translation

Śiva said: O Goddess, so easily won by those who love You, fulfiller of every task — in this age of Kali, tell me carefully the means by which every endeavor may be brought to fruition.

dēvyuvāca

śṛṇu dēva pravakṣyāmi kalau sarvēṣṭasādhanam |
mayā tavaiva snēhēnāpyambāstutiḥ prakāśyatē ‖

translation

The Goddess said: Listen, O Lord, and I will reveal the means to fulfill every desire in the age of Kali. Out of love for you alone, I now disclose this hymn in praise of the Divine Mother.

asya śrī durgā saptaślōkī stōtramantrasya nārāyaṇa ṛṣiḥ, anuṣṭup chandaḥ, śrī mahākāḻī mahālakṣmī mahāsarasvatyō dēvatāḥ, śrī durgā prītyarthaṃ saptaślōkī durgāpāṭhē viniyōgaḥ |

translation

For this sacred Durgā Sapta-ślokī hymn, the seer is Nārāyaṇa, the meter is Anuṣṭubh, and the presiding deities are Śrī Mahākālī, Mahālakṣmī, and Mahāsarasvatī. It is recited to win the loving grace of Śrī Durgā.

❧

jñānināmapi chētāṃsi dēvī bhagavatī hi sā |
balādākṛṣya mōhāya mahāmāyā prayacchati ॥ 1 ॥

translation

That divine and blessed Goddess, the Great Illusion (Mahāmāyā), forcibly draws away even the minds of the wise and casts them into delusion.

durgē smṛtā harasi bhītimaśēṣajantōḥ
svasthaiḥ smṛtā matimatīva śubhāṃ dadāsi |
dāridryaduḥkhabhayahāriṇi kā tvadanyā
sarvōpakārakaraṇāya sadārdrachittā ॥ 2 ॥

translation

O Durgā, when remembered in distress, You dispel the fear of every living being; when remembered in peace, You grant a mind serene and radiant with grace. Remover of poverty, sorrow, and fear — who other than You exists, whose heart is always tender and devoted to the good of all?

sarvamaṅgaḻamāṅgaḻyē śivē sarvārthasādhikē |
śaraṇyē tryambakē gaurī nārāyaṇi namō'stu tē ॥ 3 ॥

translation

O auspiciousness of all that is auspicious, O gracious one who fulfills every purpose, O refuge of all, O three-eyed Gaurī, O Nārāyaṇī — salutations to You.

śaraṇāgatadīnārtaparitrāṇaparāyaṇē |
sarvasyārtiharē dēvi nārāyaṇi namō'stu tē ॥ 4 ॥

translation

O You who are devoted to rescuing the lowly and the afflicted who come to You for shelter, O Devī who removes the suffering of all, O Nārāyaṇī — salutations to You.

sarvasvarūpē sarvēśē sarvaśaktisamanvitē |
bhayēbhyastrāhi nō dēvi durgē dēvi namō'stu tē ॥ 5 ॥

translation

O You who are the form of all, sovereign of all, endowed with every power — protect us from all fears, O Devī Durgā; salutations to You.

rōgānaśēṣānapahaṃsi tuṣṭā-
ruṣṭā tu kāmān sakalānabhīṣṭān |
tvāmāśritānāṃ na vipannarāṇāṃ
tvāmāśritā hyāśrayatāṃ prayānti ॥ 6 ॥

translation

When pleased, You take away every illness without exception; when angered, You undo all that is longed for and desired. Yet those who take refuge in You never fall into ruin — indeed, those who are sheltered by You become a shelter for others.

sarvabādhāpraśamanaṃ trailōkyasyākhilēśvari |
ēvamēva tvayā kāryamasmadvairivināśanam ॥ 7 ॥

translation

O Sovereign of all three worlds, You who quiet every affliction — so too may You bring about the destruction of all that stands against us.

❧

iti śrī durgā saptaślōkī

Thus concludes the sacred Seven Verses of Durgā.
