Mahānyāsa prayogaḥ · the great placing ↑

Bodhāyana's Mahānyāsa: before worshipping Rudra the worshipper becomes Rudra, placing (nyāsa) Rudra's mantras on his own body — only one who is Rudra should worship Rudra. The page follows one printed tradition in full; Vedic mantras carry their svaras (shown in Devanāgarī). Where a nyāsa mantra ends with a limb ('śikhāyai namaḥ', 'to the crest'), touch that limb.

Oṃ. Salutation to Śiva, the teacher.

śrīmṛtyuṃjaya pārvatīpriya sadā māṃ pāhi sarveśvara ||
saudheratnamaye navotpaladalākīrṇe catuṣkāṃtare
kauśeyena manohareṇa dhavale nācchādite sarvaśaḥ |
karpūrāṃcita dīpadīptimilite ramyopadhānadvaye
pārvatyāḥ karapadmalālita padaṃ mṛtyuṃjayaṃ bhāvaye ||
catuścatvāriṃśat vilasadupacārairabhimatai
rmanaḥpadme bhaktyā bahirapica pūjāṃ śubhakarām |
karoti pratyūṣe niśidivasamadhye ca sapumān
prayāti śrīmṛtyuṃjaya pada mane kādbhutapadam ||
prātarliṃga mumāpate raharaha ssaṃdarśanātsvargadaṃ
madhyāhne hayamedha tulyaphaladaṃ sāyaṃtane mokṣadam |
bhāno rastamaye pradoṣa samaye paṃcākṣarārādhanaṃ
tatkāla traya tulyamiṣṭa phaladaṃ sadyo'navadye dhṛtam ||
śrī bhavānīśaṃkara devatābhyo namaḥ kṣamāyācana pūrvaka prārthanaṃ samarpayāmi ||

translation

In this world of birth with its fevers and sickness, crying out in the three pains, bound fast by the snares of children, wife and wealth, blind with pride, laden with sins — Lord Mṛtyuñjaya, dear to Pārvatī, Lord of all, protect me always with your compassionate glance. I contemplate Mṛtyuñjaya, his feet caressed by Pārvatī's lotus hands, in the jewelled palace strewn with fresh lotus petals, spread with lovely white silk, lit by camphor lamps, on a pair of fine pillows. Whoever worships with devotion, at dawn, noon and night, within and without, with these forty-four offerings, reaches the wondrous state of Mṛtyuñjaya. Seeing the liṅga of Umā's lord at dawn gives heaven; at noon, the fruit of a horse-sacrifice; at dusk, liberation; the five-syllable worship at sunset in the pradoṣa hour gives at once, faultless, the fruit of all three. — I offer prayer, asking forgiveness.

yatpūjitaṃ mayādeva paripūrṇaṃ tadastu te ||
anayā śrīmadādyaśaṃkara bhagavatpāda viracita

bhaktyupacāra pūjayā ca bhagavān sarvātmakaḥ śrī bhavānī śaṃkara devatā
suprīta ssuprasanno varado bhavatu ||
|| sarvaṃ śrīparameśvarārpaṇamastu ||

translation

Whatever I have worshipped, Sadāśiva, lacking in mantra, in rite, in devotion — may it be complete, O god. By this worship with the forty-four offerings composed by Śrī Ādi Śaṅkara Bhagavatpāda, with all royal offerings, and with offerings as my means and devotion allow, may the Lord, Self of all, Śrī Bhavānī-Śaṅkara, be pleased, gracious and bountiful. All is offered to the Supreme Lord.

Thus the worship of Śrī Śiva.

Śrī Śivārghya mantrāḥ · arghya on Mahāśivarātri ↑

On Mahāśivarātri, after the night's worship, three special arghyas are offered with flowers, akṣata and a coin, and the coins with a gift are given to a brahmin.

gaurīvallabha deveśa trinetra śaśibhūṣaṇa |
sarvapāpaviśuddhyarthaṃ gṛhāṇā'rghyaṃ namo'stu te ||
iti prathamārghyaṃ samarpayāmi ||
|| 1 ||

translation

Beloved of Gaurī, lord of the gods, three-eyed, moon-adorned — for the cleansing of all sins accept this arghya; salutation to you. — I offer the first arghya.

vyomakeśa namaste'stu vyomātman vyomarūpiṇe |
nakṣatrarūpī bhagavan gṛhāṇā'rghyaṃ namo'stu te ||
iti dvitīyārghyaṃ samarpayāmi ||
|| 2 ||

translation

Salutation to you, sky-haired, whose self and form is the sky, Lord in the form of the stars; accept this arghya, salutation to you. — I offer the second arghya.

māghamāse site pakṣe caturdaśyāṃ mahāniśi |
liṃgodbhavasya velāyāṃ gṛhāṇā'rghyaṃ namo namaḥ ||
iti tṛtīyārghyaṃ samarpayāmi ||
|| 3 ||

translation

In the month of Māgha, on the fourteenth of the fortnight, in the great night, at the hour when the liṅga arose, accept this arghya; salutation, salutation. — I offer the third arghya.

ekā caṃḍyā raveḥ sapta tisraḥ kāryā vināyake |
