# English key to Nirukta adhyāya 2, one khaṇḍa a line: number | heading | gloss. Original.
1 | The rules of derivation | How to derive a word: where accent and form fit a known root, derive it by that; where they do not, look for a common sense, and failing that, a common letter or sound; never leave a word underived, and do not be bound by grammatical form. The changes words undergo: loss of the root's initial (sta, santi from as), of its final, of the penultimate, change of the penultimate, loss of a letter or two, transposition of initials and of initial and final.
2 | Words that change their last sound; dialects; compounds | Changes at the end of a word (ogha, megha, madhu); insertion of a letter. Where a semivowel follows a vowel the word may come from two roots. Verbs of the common speech give Vedic nouns and the reverse; some roots live in one region, their derivatives in another (śavati "to go" only among the Kambojas, dāti "to cut" in the east, dātra in the north). Compounds are taken apart member by member: daṇḍya, kakṣyā.
3 | Rājapuruṣa and other compounds; who may be taught | Rājan, puruṣa ("dwelling in the fort", or "filling all within"), with the verse on the Puruṣa who fills the world; viśvakadrākarṣa and kalyāṇavarṇarūpa. Single words should not be explained to one who is no grammarian, not a pupil, or does not know the subject; only to a pupil able to understand, intelligent or devoted to austerity.
4 | The verses on guarding knowledge | Verses: Knowledge came to the Brāhmaṇa and said, guard me, I am your treasure; do not tell me to the envious, the crooked or the unrestrained. One who fills the ears with truth is to be honoured as father and mother; pupils who do not honour their teacher get no good of what they learnt.
5 | Go: the earth, the cow, milk, hide, sinew | The word-list begins. Go is a name of the earth, "because it goes far". By derivation it also means the cow, and in the hymns its products: milk (gobhiḥ śrīṇīta matsaram), the hide of the pressing, sinew and glue in the chariot and the arrow, and the bowstring.
6 | Go: the bowstring, the sun, the rays | Ṛgveda 6.75.11: the bowstring roars on the bow and the birds fly off; vṛkṣa, mīmayati, vi. The sun too is go, and one of its rays, the one that lights the moon; all the rays are called go.
7 | The highest step of Viṣṇu; doubtful words | Ṛgveda 1.154.6: the dwellings where the many-horned cows go, where Viṣṇu's highest step shines. Śṛṅga, pada. Where one word has several meanings, derive it by each; so the twenty-one names of the earth. Nirṛti, "destruction", is confused with the earth.
8 | Nirṛti; Śākapūṇi and the deity | Ṛgveda 1.164.32 explained two ways: the ascetics read it of one with many children coming to misery, the etymologists of the rain entering the earth. Śākapūṇi resolved to know all the gods; a deity with the marks of both appeared and gave him a verse.
9 | Lightning, the voice of the mid-air | Ṛgveda 1.164.29 on the cow that lows and the lightning that throws back its covering: speech of the middle region, abiding in the cloud, which covers the earth with rain and draws it up again.
10 | Gold, the mid-air, the ocean; Devāpi and Śantanu | The fifteen names of gold and sixteen of the mid-air, derived. Samudra (ocean) is shared by sky and earth. The story of the brothers Devāpi and Śantanu: Śantanu, the younger, was made king; for twelve years it did not rain; Devāpi became his priest and made the hymn for rain.
11 | Devāpi's hymn | Ṛgveda 10.98.5: Devāpi, son of Ṛṣṭiṣeṇa, sitting as hotṛ, released the heavenly waters. Putra, ṛṣi ("from seeing"), and the Brāhmaṇa on how the seers came to be.
12 | Śantanu and Bṛhaspati | Ṛgveda 10.98.7: when Devāpi, chosen as Śantanu's priest, meditated, Bṛhaspati gave him the speech that wins rain. Śantanu, purohita, rarāṇa.
13 | Words shared by sky and sun; Āditya | Six names shared by the sky and the sun. Āditya ("he takes the juices", or "son of Aditi"), and how the other Ādityas, Mitra, Varuṇa and the rest, are praised under that name; vrata, "work", and also "food".
14 | Svar, pṛśni, nāka, go, viṣṭap, nabhas | The six shared names derived, each for the sun and for the sky; nāka as "where there is no unhappiness".
15 | Rays and quarters; kāṣṭhā | Fifteen names of rays and eight of the quarters. Kāṣṭhā means several things that "stand having stepped": the quarters, the sun, the course's end, the waters.
16 | Vṛtra | Ṛgveda 1.32.10: the waters flow over Vṛtra's hidden body. Who is Vṛtra? The cloud, say the etymologists; an asura son of Tvaṣṭṛ, say the story-tellers. Rain comes from the mingling of water and light; the fight is a figure.
17 | Vṛtra's name | Ṛgveda 1.32.11: the waters stood shut in like the Paṇi's cows, and Indra opened their outlet. Dāsa, ahi, paṇi, bila, and the Brāhmaṇa's three derivations of Vṛtra.
18 | Night and dawn | The twenty-three names of night and sixteen of dawn, derived.
19 | Dawn | Ṛgveda 1.113.1: the best of lights has come; as night was born for Savitṛ's impulse, so she leaves her place to dawn.
20 | Dawn and night; day | Ṛgveda 1.113.2: the bright one with her bright calf has come; dawn and night, immortal, of one kin, move together changing colour. The twelve names of day.
21 | Day and night; cloud | Ṛgveda 6.9.1: the dark day and the bright turn by their known ways; Agni Vaiśvānara, born like a king, drives off the darkness. The thirty names of cloud.
22 | The cloud | Ṛgveda 10.27.23: the first gods stood at the measuring; three warm the earth, two carry the water. Pṛthivī, anūpa, bṛbūka, purīṣa.
23 | Speech; Sarasvatī as a river | The fifty-seven names of speech. Sarasvatī is praised both as river and as goddess; here as river.
24 | Sarasvatī; water; rivers; Viśvāmitra's crossing | Ṛgveda 6.61.2: Sarasvatī breaks the ridges of the hills with her waves. The hundred and one names of water and thirty-seven of rivers. The story of Viśvāmitra, priest of Sudās, who came to the meeting of the Vipāś and Śutudrī and praised the rivers to let him cross.
25 | Viśvāmitra's prayer to the rivers | Ṛgveda 3.33.5: stay a moment at my word, you rivers; muhūrta, kṣaṇa, kāla, kuśika.
26 | The rivers answer | Ṛgveda 3.33.6: Indra dug our channels, and Savitṛ led us; pāṇi, urvī. At last they heard him.
27 | The rivers consent; the horse | Ṛgveda 3.33.10: we will hear your words, singer, and bow to you as a mother to her child. The twenty-six names of the horse; dadhikrā.
28 | Dadhikrā; the last lists | Ṛgveda 4.40.4: the steed bound at neck and flank hastens along the bends of the road. Ten words used only by specification; eleven verbs of blazing and as many names of flame.
