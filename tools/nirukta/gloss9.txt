# English key to Nirukta adhyāya 9, one khaṇḍa a line: number | heading | gloss. Original.
1 | The deified things of the earth; the horse | Now the beings of the earthly region that receive praise. First the horse.
2 | The horse's verse; mā naḥ | Ṛgveda 9.112.4: the horse wants an easy chariot, jesters want laughter, the frog wants water; flow for Indra, soma. Sukha, "good". Mā naḥ (Ṛgveda 1.162.1) follows.
3 | The horse-sacrifice hymn; the bird | Ṛgveda 1.162.1: may Mitra, Varuṇa, Aryaman, Āyu, Indra and the Maruts not pass us over when we proclaim the deeds of the god-born steed. Śakuni, the bird, "able to rise".
4 | The bird of good omen | Ṛgveda 2.42.1: crying out, announcing its birth, it sends forth its voice like a helmsman a ship; be auspicious, bird. Maṅgala. The kapiñjala that cried to Gṛtsamada.
5 | The kapiñjala; frogs | Ṛgveda 2.43.3: speak good to the south, the north, the east and the west, kapiñjala. Gṛtsamada; maṇḍūka, the frog.
6 | The frogs as Brāhmaṇas | Ṛgveda 7.103.1: having lain a year like Brāhmaṇas keeping a vow, the frogs have raised their voice quickened by Parjanya. Vasiṣṭha praised Parjanya for rain; the frogs joined in, and he praised them.
7 | The frog verse; the dice | Ṛgveda 7.103 (khila): speak out, little frog, call the rain; swim in the middle of the pool. Akṣa, the dice.
8 | The dice | Ṛgveda 10.34.1: the dangling nuts of the great tree, born in a windy place, rolling on the board, delight me like soma from Mount Mūjavat. Iriṇa, Mūjavat, vibhīdaka. The gambler's hymn praises them first, then blames them. The pressing-stones.
9 | The pressing-stones; Narāśaṃsa verses | Ṛgveda 10.94.1: let them speak, let us speak to the speaking stones. A mantra praising men is nārāśaṃsa.
10 | The king on the Sindhu | Ṛgveda 1.126.1: I bring unfailing praises for Bhāvya who dwells on the Sindhu, the king who arranged a thousand sacrifices for me. Bāla.
11 | The chariot | The king, the war-gear; first the chariot, ratha.
12 | The chariot's verse; the drum | Ṛgveda 6.47.26: lord of the forest, be firm of limb, our friend, carrying us across; you are bound with straps. The drum, dundubhi.
13 | The drum; the quiver | Ṛgveda 6.47.29: fill earth and heaven with your roar, drum; drive the enemies far away. The quiver.
14 | The quiver; the hand-guard | Ṛgveda 6.75.5: father of many, with many sons, it rattles as it goes to battle. The hastaghna, the hand-guard.
15 | The hand-guard; the reins | Ṛgveda 6.75.14: it coils round the arm like a snake, guarding against the bowstring. Pums. The reins.
16 | The reins; the bow | Ṛgveda 6.75.6: standing in the chariot the good charioteer drives the horses wherever he wishes; praise the greatness of the reins. The bow.
17 | The bow; the bowstring | Ṛgveda 6.75.2: by the bow may we win cows, the contest, the fierce battles. Samad. The bowstring, jyā.
18 | The bowstring; the arrow | Ṛgveda 6.75.3: as if about to speak, it comes to the ear, embracing the arrow like a dear friend. The arrow.
19 | The arrow; the whip | Ṛgveda 6.75.11: it wears a feather, its tooth is of deer-horn; bound with sinew it flies. The horse-whip, kaśā.
20 | The whip; the mortar | Ṛgveda 6.75.13: it strikes their flanks and thighs; urge the horses on in battle, whip. The mortar, ulūkhala, "making wide".
21 | The mortar | Ṛgveda 1.28.5: though you are yoked in every house, mortar, sound out here brightest, like the drum of the victors.
22 | The bull | Vṛṣabha, the bull, "raining" seed.
23 | Mudgala's bull and club | Ṛgveda 10.102.5: they made the bull roar in the midst of the battle; with it Mudgala won a thousand cows. Pradhana. The club (drughaṇa); the story of Mudgala Bhārmyaśva, who won a race with a bull and a club yoked together.
24 | The club; pitu, food | Ṛgveda 10.102.9: see the club lying by the bull, with which Mudgala won the cows in battle. Pṛtanājya; Mudgala, Bhārmyaśva. Pitu, food.
25 | Pitu | Ṛgveda 1.187.1: now I will praise food, the great upholder of strength, by whose might Trita tore Vṛtra apart. Taviṣī. The rivers.
26 | The rivers of the Punjab | Ṛgveda 10.75.5: Gaṅgā, Yamunā, Sarasvatī, Śutudrī, hear my praise with Paruṣṇī; Asiknī, Marudvṛdhā, Vitastā, Ārjīkīyā with Suṣomā. Each name derived: Paruṣṇī is the Irāvatī, Ārjīkīyā the Vipāś, freed of Vasiṣṭha's fetters.
27 | The waters; the plants | Ṛgveda 10.9.1: you waters are refreshing; give us strength for great joy and sight. The plants, oṣadhi.
28 | The plants | Ṛgveda 10.97.1: of the plants born three ages before the gods, I think of the hundred and seven forms of the brown ones. Dhāman: abodes, names, births. Night.
29 | Night | Ṛgveda 10.127.2: you have filled the earthly region, night, and the seats of the sky. The forest spirit, Araṇyānī.
30 | Araṇyānī | Ṛgveda 10.146.1: Araṇyānī, you who seem to vanish, why do you not ask the way to the village? Does fear not find you? Faith, śraddhā.
31 | Faith | Ṛgveda 10.151.1: by faith the fire is kindled, by faith the oblation offered. The earth.
32 | The earth | Ṛgveda 1.22.15: be pleasant, earth, thornless, a resting place; give us wide shelter. Ṛkṣara, kaṇṭaka. Apvā.
33 | Apvā | Ṛgveda 10.103.12: bewilder their minds, Apvā, seize their limbs, burn their hearts with grief. Agnāyī, Agni's wife.
34 | Agnāyī | Ṛgveda 1.22.12: I call here Indrāṇī, Varuṇānī and Agnāyī to drink the soma.
35 | The eight pairs; mortar and pestle | Now the eight pairs, first the mortar and pestle; musala, the pestle.
36 | Mortar and pestle; the two havirdhāna carts | Ṛgveda 1.28.7: the two worshipful ones, best winners of food, are raised high like two bays eating their fodder. The havirdhāna carts.
37 | The carts; heaven and earth | Ṛgveda 2.41.21: let the worshipful gods sit in your lap to drink soma. Heaven and earth.
38 | Heaven and earth; Vipāś and Śutudrī | Ṛgveda 2.41.20: may heaven and earth carry this sacrifice to the gods. The two rivers.
39 | Vipāś and Śutudrī; the bow-tips | Ṛgveda 3.33.1: eager, from the mountains' lap, like two mares let loose, like two bright mother cows, Vipāś and Śutudrī rush with their waters. Ārtnī, the bow-tips.
40 | The bow-tips; Śunāsīra | Ṛgveda 6.75.4: moving like two women of one mind, holding the arrow as a mother her child, may the bow-tips pierce the enemy. Śuna (Vāyu) and Sīra (the sun).
41 | Śunāsīra; the two goddesses Joṣṭrī | Ṛgveda 4.57.5: Śunāsīra, enjoy this praise; sprinkle this earth with the milk you made in heaven. The goddesses Joṣṭrī: heaven and earth, day and night, or crop and year.
42 | Joṣṭrī; Ūrjāhutī | The praiṣa for the two Joṣṭrīs, one driving away evils, the other bringing wealth. The goddesses Ūrjāhutī.
43 | Ūrjāhutī | The praiṣa for the two Ūrjāhutīs, one bringing food and drink, the other shared eating and drinking.
