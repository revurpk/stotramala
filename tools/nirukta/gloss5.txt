# English key to Nirukta adhyāya 5, one khaṇḍa a line: number | heading | gloss. Original.
1 | Sasni, dūta, vāvaśāna, vārya, andhas | The Naigama-kāṇḍa continues. Sasni, the well-bathed cloud; dūta, messenger; vāvaśāna; vārya, "choice"; andhas means food (Ṛgveda 10.116 "pour the gladdening drink with goblets") and also darkness and the blind.
2 | Asaścantī, vanuṣyati, bhandanā, Nada | Asaścantī (heaven and earth, "not clinging"); vanuṣyati and taruṣyati, "to strike"; pāpa; bhandanā, "praise"; āhanas; the seer Nada and the lament of a seer's daughter for a restrained student.
3 | Akṣāḥ, śvātra, ūti, sasa, dvitā | Akṣāḥ from kṣi "to dwell" or kṣar "to flow"; śvātra, "swift"; ūti; vamraka; sasa, the sleep-like light of the mid-air; dvitā, in two places; vrā, the troops.
4 | Varāha, svasara, śaryā, arka | Varāha, the cloud, the boar, the Aṅgirases and the Maruts; svasara, days; śaryā, fingers or arrows; arka, the god, the hymn, food and the arka plant.
5 | Vaṃśa, pavi, dhanva, sina, sacā, cit, ā, dyumna | Ṛgveda 1.10.1: the singers raise you like a bamboo pole. Pavi, the tyre; dhanva, mid-air; sina, food; sacā, "with"; cit, "thought"; ā as "upon"; dyumna, glory or food.
6 | Pavitra | Pavitra, "purifier": the mantra, the rays, the waters, and Agni, Vāyu, Soma, Sūrya and Indra; toda.
7 | Toda, svañcas; Śipiviṣṭa | Ṛgveda 1.150.1: I have called on you much, Agni, as one in the shelter of the great. Svañcas. Śipiviṣṭa and Viṣṇu, two names of Viṣṇu; Aupamanyava takes the first as a term of blame.
8 | Why Viṣṇu is called Śipiviṣṭa | Ṛgveda 7.100.6: what is there to hide, Viṣṇu, in calling yourself Śipiviṣṭa? Read as blame, or as praise: covered with rays (śipi). Varpas, "form".
9 | Śipiviṣṭa praised; āghṛṇi; pṛthujrayas | Ṛgveda 7.100.5: I praise your name, Śipiviṣṭa; I, the lord, praise you the mighty. Tavas, parāka; āghṛṇi, "glowing"; pṛthujrayas, "of wide course".
10 | The fingers kindle Agni | Ṛgveda 7.1.1: men brought forth Agni from the fire-sticks with their fingers, the far-seen lord of the house. Dīdhiti, araṇi.
11 | Kāṇukā; the thirty lakes | Ṛgveda 8.77.4: Indra drank thirty lakes of soma at one draught. Kāṇukā; the ritualists' thirty cups and the etymologists' thirty days and nights of the waning moon, whose waters the rays drink.
12 | Adhrigu, āṅgūṣa; āpāntamanyu | Adhrigu: the mantra, Agni, Indra. Āṅgūṣa, the hymn. Ṛgveda 10.89.5 on Soma or Indra, impetuous, swift-striking, whom no measure can reach; ṛjīṣa, the dregs of soma; dhānā, bhrāṣṭra; śmaśā, the channel.
13 | Urvaśī, apsaras | Urvaśī the apsaras, "wide-reaching"; apsaras and apsas, "form". At the sight of her the seed of Mitra and Varuṇa fell.
14 | Vasiṣṭha born of Urvaśī; puṣkara | Ṛgveda 7.33.11: you are the son of Mitra and Varuṇa, Vasiṣṭha, born of the mind of Urvaśī; the gods held you in a lotus. Drapsa, puṣkara (mid-air, water, lotus); vayuna.
15 | Vayuna, vājapastya, gadhya, kaurayāṇa | Ṛgveda 6.21.3: he made the darkness that was without knowledge knowable by the sun. Vājapastya, vājagandhya, gadhya, gadhyati; kaurayāṇa, taurayāṇa, ahrayāṇa, harayāṇa; ārita; vrandin.
16 | Vrandin, vīḷayati, niṣṣapin, tūrṇāśa, kṣumpa | Ṛgveda 1.54.5: you struck the head of the snorting Śuṣṇa. Vīḷayati and vrīḷayati; niṣṣapin; tūrṇāśa, water; kṣumpa, the mushroom.
17 | Kṣumpa, aṅga; nicumpuṇa | Ṛgveda 1.84.8: when will he kick the ungenerous mortal aside like a mushroom? Aṅga, "quickly". Nicumpuṇa, Soma.
18 | Nicumpuṇa; padi | Ṛgveda 8.93.22: these pressed somas go eagerly to drink. Nicumpuṇa as the sea and the closing bath (avabhṛtha); padi, a snare.
19 | Mukṣījā, pādu, busa | Ṛgveda 1.125.2: the host who welcomes a guest prospers, like a boy freed from a snare. Mukṣījā; pādu; Ṛgveda 10.27.24 on the sun hiding the water (busa).
20 | Vṛka, the moon | Vṛka is the moon, "of open light".
21 | Vṛka: moon, sun, dog, wolf | Ṛgveda 1.105.18: the ruddy wolf, maker of months, saw me on the path. The sun as wolf (the quail freed by the Aśvins); the dog; the she-wolf; urāmathi; joṣavāka.
22 | Joṣavāka, kṛtti, śvaghnin, sama | Ṛgveda 6.59.4 on the idle talker the gods do not eat with. Kṛtti, skin or garment (kṛttivāsāḥ pinākahastaḥ); śvaghnin, the gambler; sama, "any".
23 | Sama; kuṭa, śamba, kepi | Ṛgveda 8.75.9: let not the distress of any ill-wisher strike us as a wave a boat. Sama in other cases; pipṛti, kuṭa, śamba (thunderbolt), kepi.
24 | The sacrificial boat | Ṛgveda 10.44.6: the first invokers of the gods went apart and won fame; those who could not board the sacrificial boat sank down here, the base ones.
25 | Īrma, aṃsatra | Īrma, "here", "in this world", or "the arm". Ṛgveda 10.50.6; aṃsatra, the armour or the bow.
26 | The well of battle; kākuda | Ṛgveda 10.101.7: water the horses, make the chariot that wins, pour into the drinking-trough with its stone wheel. Droṇa, aśmacakra, kośa; kākuda, the palate, with jihvā and tālu.
27 | The seven rivers into Varuṇa's palate | Ṛgveda 8.69.12: you are a good god, Varuṇa, into whose palate the seven rivers flow as into a hollow tube. Sarup also prints, in brackets, another version of the comment, with bīriṭa.
28 | Bīriṭa, sṛṇi | Ṛgveda 7.39.2: the sacred grass is spread; the two lords come as kings in the crowd (bīriṭa); Vāyu and Pūṣan with their teams. Accha; sṛṇi, the goad; ankuśa.
