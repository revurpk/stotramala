# English key to Nirukta adhyāya 10, one khaṇḍa a line: number | heading | gloss. Original.
1 | The deities of the mid-air; Vāyu | Now the deities whose place is the mid-air. First Vāyu, the wind, from vā "to blow" or vī "to go"; Sthaulāṣṭhīvi derives it from i, the v being meaningless.
2 | Vāyu's verse | Ṛgveda 1.2.1: come, Vāyu, beautiful one; these somas are made ready; drink them, hear our call. Who else but the god of the mid-air would be meant?
3 | Vāyu and Indra; Varuṇa | Ṛgveda 4.48.4 (or a like verse): the swift horses bring Indra; may the food of Vāyu never fail. Some take the verse as Indra's, the work as Vāyu's in passing; others as both. Varuṇa, "the coverer".
4 | Varuṇa pours out the cloud | Ṛgveda 5.85.3: Varuṇa poured out the cask with its opening downward into heaven, earth and mid-air; with it the king of the world wets the ground as rain the barley. Kabandha, the cloud.
5 | Varuṇa praised with Nābhāka's hymns; Rudra | Ṛgveda 8.41.2: I praise him with the hymns of the fathers and of Nābhāka, him of the seven sisters at the rivers' source; let all our other foes perish. Rudra, "the roarer", "who runs roaring", or "who makes weep"; the Kāṭhaka and Hāridravika on his name.
6 | Rudra the archer | Ṛgveda 7.46.1: bring these songs to Rudra of the steady bow and swift arrows, the unconquerable, with sharp weapons; may he hear us.
7 | Rudra's lightning | Ṛgveda 7.46.3: may your lightning, loosed from the sky, that moves on the earth, pass us by; you have a thousand remedies; harm not our children and grandchildren. Agni too is called Rudra.
8 | Agni as Rudra; Indra | Ṛgveda 1.27.10: awaken the praise for each clan, a fair hymn for Rudra. Indra derived many ways: who breaks or gives or holds the drink (irā), who runs to or delights in soma, who kindles the beings; the Brāhmaṇa's "they kindled him with the breaths"; Āgrāyaṇa and Aupamanyava.
9 | Indra opens the springs | Ṛgveda 5.32.1: you split the spring, opened its outlets, set free the pent-up floods; you opened the great mountain, let the streams loose, and slew the Dānava. Utsa, arṇava, rambh.
10 | "He, O people, is Indra" | Ṛgveda 2.12.1: the god who, as soon as born, first and wise, surpassed the gods in power, before whose strength heaven and earth trembled, he, O people, is Indra. The seer's joy at what he saw, joined to a story.
11 | Parjanya | Parjanya, from tṛp with the sounds reversed, "the satisfier", or "the far winner", "the far begetter", "the bringer of the juices".
12 | Parjanya's thunder; Bṛhaspati | Ṛgveda 5.83.2: he strikes the trees and the demons; all the world fears the great weapon; even the guiltless flees when Parjanya thundering strikes the evil-doers. Bṛhaspati, "lord of the great".
13 | Bṛhaspati frees the honey; Brahmaṇaspati | Ṛgveda 10.68.8: he saw the honey shut in the rock like a fish in shallow water, and drew it out like a bowl from a tree. Camasa. Brahmaṇaspati, "lord of the prayer".
14 | Brahmaṇaspati's spring; Kṣetrapati | Ṛgveda 2.24.4: Brahmaṇaspati broke open the stone-mouthed well with its stream of honey, and all the sun-seeing ones drank from it. Kṣetrapati, lord of the field.
15 | The lord of the field | Ṛgveda 4.57.1: with the lord of the field as friend we win; may he bring cattle and horses and be kind to such as us. Mṛḷati, "to give".
16 | The field's honeyed wave; repetition in verse | Ṛgveda 4.57.2: lord of the field, milk for us the honeyed wave as a cow her milk. When a word is repeated in one verse, or in one pāda, it is a fault, say some; any difference saves it ("like frogs from the water"). Vāstoṣpati, lord of the dwelling.
17 | Vāstoṣpati; Vācaspati | Ṛgveda 7.55.1: Vāstoṣpati, dispeller of sickness, entering all forms, be our kind friend. Śeva, "happy". A god becomes whatever form he wishes. Vācaspati, lord of speech.
18 | Vācaspati; Apāṃ Napāt | Atharvaveda 1.1.2: come again, lord of speech, with the divine mind; lord of wealth, keep it in me. Apāṃ Napāt, explained with Tanūnapāt.
19 | Apāṃ Napāt; Yama | Ṛgveda 10.30.4: he who shines without fuel within the waters, whom the priests praise; give the honeyed waters, Apāṃ Napāt, by which Indra grew strong. Yama, "the restrainer".
20 | Yama, king of the dead; Agni as Yama | Ṛgveda 10.14.1: worship with oblation King Yama, son of Vivasvat, gatherer of men, who went along the great heights and found the path for many. Agni too is called Yama.
21 | Agni as Yama; Mitra | Two-pāda verses (Ṛgveda 1.66): like a host let loose he strikes terror; Yama is what is born and what will be born; the lover of maidens, the husband of wives. "Your third husband is Agni". Mitra, "who protects from destruction".
22 | Mitra; Ka | Ṛgveda 3.59.1: Mitra, speaking, sets men to work; Mitra upholds earth and heaven; Mitra watches men unblinking. Kṛṣṭi, "men". Ka, "the lovely", "the striding", or "the happy".
23 | Hiraṇyagarbha | Ṛgveda 10.121.1: the golden germ arose in the beginning; born, he was the one lord of what is; he upheld earth and this heaven; to what god shall we offer? Garbha derived. Sarasvat.
24 | Sarasvat | Ṛgveda 7.96.5 (or a like verse): with your honeyed waves dripping ghee, Sarasvat, be our helper.
25 | Viśvakarman | Viśvakarman, maker of all.
26 | Viśvakarman, the seven seers and the one beyond | Ṛgveda 10.82.2: Viśvakarman, wide of mind, creator, disposer, highest seer, where they rejoice beyond the seven seers in the One. Read of the sky (the seven stars and the sun) and of the self (the seven senses and the self). The story of Viśvakarman Bhauvana offering all beings and at last himself in the all-sacrifice.
27 | Viśvakarman's sacrifice; Tārkṣya | Ṛgveda 10.81.6: Viśvakarman, growing by the oblation, sacrifice heaven and earth yourself; let others around be bewildered. Tārkṣya, explained with Tvaṣṭṛ.
28 | Tārkṣya | Ṛgveda 10.178.1: let us call here for welfare Tārkṣya, the strong steed sped by the gods, the victor whose wheel is unharmed.
29 | Tārkṣya's speed; Manyu | Ṛgveda 10.178.3: he who at once spreads the waters over the five peoples like the sun with light; none can check his course, swift as a feathered arrow. Manyu, wrath.
30 | Manyu | Ṛgveda 10.84.1: with you, Manyu, on one chariot, rejoicing and undaunted, let the heroes go forth sharpening their arrows, fire-like. Dadhikrā.
31 | Dadhikrā; Savitṛ | Ṛgveda 4.38.10: Dadhikrā spreads the waters over the five peoples like the sun; may the steed mingle these words with honey. Savitṛ, impeller of all.
32 | Savitṛ holds the earth | Ṛgveda 10.149.1: Savitṛ made the earth fast with bonds, fixed the sky in the unsupported space, milked the roaring cloud like a horse. The sun too is called Savitṛ; Hiraṇyastūpa's hymn.
33 | Hiraṇyastūpa; Tvaṣṭṛ | Ṛgveda 10.149.5: as Hiraṇyastūpa the Āṅgirasa called you in this contest, Savitṛ, so I praise you, watchful as over the soma shoot. Stūpa. Tvaṣṭṛ.
34 | Tvaṣṭṛ the all-shaped; Vāta | Ṛgveda 3.55.19: god Tvaṣṭṛ, Savitṛ, of all forms, nourished and begot creatures in many ways; great and unique is the lordship of the gods. Asuratva. Vāta, the wind.
35 | Vāta; Agni | Ṛgveda 10.186.1: may the wind blow healing to us, wholesome for the heart, and lengthen our lives. Agni.
36 | Agni with the Maruts | Ṛgveda 1.19.1: you are called to this fair sacrifice to drink; come, Agni, with the Maruts. Who else but the god of the mid-air?
37 | The first drink | Ṛgveda 1.19.9: I pour out the honeyed soma for your first drink; come, Agni, with the Maruts.
38 | Vena | Vena, "the longing one".
39 | Vena; Asunīti | Ṛgveda 10.123.1: Vena, wrapped in light, urges the dappled ones; the singers lick him like a child at the meeting of the waters and the sun. Jarāyu, śiśu. Asunīti, "leader of the breath".
40 | Asunīti; Ṛta | Ṛgveda 10.59.5: Asunīti, keep our mind in us for long life; let us see the sun. Radh, "to be subject". Ṛta, order.
41 | Ṛta; Indu | Ṛgveda 4.23.8: the waters of order are many; the thought of order slays wrongs; the call of order pierces even deaf ears. Indu, the drop.
42 | Indu; Paruccheda; Prajāpati | Ṛgveda 1.129.6: I proclaim it to Indu the worthy; he drives off the evil-speaker like a trickle. Repetition intensifies, as with Paruccheda the seer. Twenty-seven names are done, four of them without oblation (Vena, Asunīti, Ṛta, Indu). Prajāpati.
43 | Prajāpati; Ahi | Ṛgveda 10.121.10: Prajāpati, none but you encompasses all these born things; may we be lords of riches. Ahi, the serpent of the deep.
44 | Ahi; Ahi Budhnya | Ṛgveda 7.34.16: I praise with hymns the water-born serpent sitting in the depth of the rivers. Budhna, the mid-air. Ahi Budhnya.
45 | Ahi Budhnya; Suparṇa | Ṛgveda 7.34.17: may Ahi Budhnya not harm us, nor our sacrifice fail. Suparṇa, the bird.
46 | The one bird | Ṛgveda 10.114.4: one bird has entered the ocean and looks on all this world; I saw him near with a simple mind; his mother licks him and he licks his mother. Purūravas.
47 | Purūravas | Ṛgveda 10.95.7: the women sat round him as he was born, and the rivers made him grow, when the gods raised you, Purūravas, for the great battle and the slaying of the Dasyus.
