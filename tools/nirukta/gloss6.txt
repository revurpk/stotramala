# English key to Nirukta adhyāya 6, one khaṇḍa a line: number | heading | gloss. Original.
1 | Āśuśukṣaṇi, āśā, kāśi | Ṛgveda 2.1.1: you, Agni, are born from the days, the waters and the stone, pure lord of men. Āśuśukṣaṇi, "swiftly blazing", and śuci. Āśā, the quarters; kāśi, the fist (Ṛgveda 3.30.5); rodasī; piṇak kuṇāru.
2 | Alātṛṇa, vala, vraja | Ṛgveda 3.30.10: the cloud Vala, the cows' pen, burst open in fear before it was struck; Indra made easy paths. Dhamati, "to go".
3 | Sallūka; katpaya, visruh, vīrudh, nakṣaddābha, askṛdhoyu | Ṛgveda 3.30.17: tear out the demon root and all, Indra. Sallūka; katpaya; visruh, the waters; vīrudh, plants; nakṣaddābha; askṛdhoyu; niśṛmbha.
4 | Bṛbaduktha … jūrṇi, ghraṃsa | Ṛgveda 6.55.6: may the goats draw Pūṣan in his chariot. Bṛbaduktha, ṛdūdara, pulukāma, asinvatī, kapana (worms), bhāṛjīka, rujānā (rivers), jūrṇi, ghraṃsa (heat, day).
5 | Upalaprakṣiṇī; the nine livelihoods | Upalaprakṣiṇī, "who throws grain on the millstones". In brackets, Indra's question to the seers: how does one live in famine? A cart, a vegetable plot, cows, a net, a stream, a forest, the sea, a mountain, a king: nine ways.
6 | I a bard, my father a physician | Ṛgveda 9.112.3: "I am a bard, my father a physician, my mother grinds the grain; with various thoughts, seeking wealth, we follow like cattle." Upasi; prakalavid, the merchant; abhyardhayajvan; īkṣe; kṣoṇa.
7 | The forms of asme; pāthas; savīman, saprathas, vidatha | Asme used for every case of "we", nominative to locative, with examples. Pāthas: mid-air, water, food. Savīman, saprathas, vidatha.
8 | Śrāyanta; āśir; jigarti; mūra, śaśamāna, kṛpā | Ṛgveda 8.99.3: clinging like the rays to the sun, share Indra's wealth. Ojas; āśir, the milk mixed with soma, and the blessing; jigarti; mūra, "foolish"; śaśamāna; kṛpā.
9 | The son-in-law and the brother-in-law | Ṛgveda 1.109.2: I have heard you two are more generous than a poor son-in-law or a brother-in-law. Vijāmātṛ, the bought husband of the south; jāmātṛ; syāla; lāja, śūrpa; omāsa is left for later.
10 | Somāna, Kakṣīvat | Ṛgveda 1.18.1: make the soma-presser famous, Brahmaṇaspati, like Kakṣīvat son of Uśij.
11 | The demon-haters; kimīdin | Ṛgveda 7.104.2: Indra and Soma, burn the evil-speaker; give lasting hate to the eater of raw flesh and the kimīdin ("what now?"), the informer. Agha, caru, kravya.
12 | Pājas, prasiti; durṇāman, apvā, amati, śruṣṭi | Ṛgveda 4.4.1: spread your might like a wide net, go like a king with his retinue. Prasiti, tṛṣvī; durṇāman, the worm; krimi; apvā, disease or fear; amati; śruṣṭi, "quickly".
13 | Nāsatya; purandhi; ruśat | Ṛgveda 1.44.3 on the Nāsatyas, "true" (Aurṇavābha), "leaders of truth" (Āgrāyaṇa), or "born of the nose". Purandhi: Bhaga, Indra or Varuṇa. Ruśat, "bright".
14 | Sajātya, āpya; sudatra, suvidatra, ānuṣak, turvaṇi, girvaṇas | Ṛgveda 8.27.10: you gods have kinship and friendship with us. Sudatra, suvidatra, ānuṣak, turvaṇi, girvaṇas.
15 | Asūrta, sūrta; amyak, yādṛśmin, jāraya | Ṛgveda 10.82.4 on the gods who made the beings in the unmade and the made region. Amyak, yādṛśmin, and "he was born like a cowherd from the sacrifices".
16 | Agriya, pacata; śurudh, amina, jajjhatī, apratiṣkuta, śāśadāna | Ṛgveda 3.35.10: eat, Indra, the oblations set out, the cooked food and the soma. Medas; śurudh, waters; amina; jajjhatī; apratiṣkuta; śāśadāna.
17 | Sṛpra … tuñja | Sṛpra (ghee or oil), karasnau (arms), suśipra, śipra (jaws or nostrils), dhenā, raṃsu, dvibarhas, akra, urāṇa, stiyā (waters), stipā, javāru, jarūtha, kuliśa (thunderbolt); tuñja, "gift".
18 | Indra's praise unending; barhaṇā | Ṛgveda 1.7.7: at every gift, the further praises of Indra; I find no end of his praise. Barhaṇā.
19 | Ghraṃsa, ūdhan; the strongholds of Ilībiśa | Ṛgveda 5.34.3: who presses soma by day or night grows bright; Śakra drives off the niggard and the dandy. Ghraṃsa, day; ūdhan, night; kavāsakha. Ṛgveda 1.33.12: Indra pierced the strongholds of Ilībiśa and split horned Śuṣṇa.
20 | Kiyedhās, bhṛmi, viṣpita | Ṛgveda 1.61.12: hurl the thunderbolt at Vṛtra, cutting him joint by joint like a cow. Kiyedhās; bhṛmi; viṣpita.
21 | Turīpa, asmayu, rāspina, ṛñjati, ṛju, pratadvasu | Ṛgveda 1.142.10: may Tvaṣṭṛ release for our increase his swift, wonderful seed. Asmayu, rāspina, ṛñjati, ṛju, pratadvasu.
22 | Śruṣṭīvarī, coṣkūyamāṇa, sumat, diviṣṭi, Kuruṅga | Ṛgveda 10.30.11: send forth our sacrifice; waters, be favourable. Śakaṭa; coṣkūyamāṇa; sumat; diviṣṭi; sthūra; King Kuruṅga; jinvati.
23 | Amatra, ṛcīṣama, anarśarāti, anarvan, asāmi | Amatra, ṛcīṣama, anarśarāti ("whose gifts are not evil"), anarvan (Bṛhaspati), asāmi.
24 | Galda, dhamani | Ṛgveda 8.1.20: may I not anger you by always begging with the trickle of soma; galda, the veins.
25 | Na pāpāsaḥ; bakura | Ṛgveda 8.61.11: we are not evil, poor or lacking fire, said the seer. Bakura, "the shining".
26 | Vṛka, the plough; bekanāṭa | Ṛgveda 1.117.21: sowing barley with the plough, Aśvins, and milking food for man. Vṛka, lāṅgala; bekanāṭa, the usurer.
27 | The fish in the net; the seven boundaries; bata | Ṛgveda 8.67.5, said of fish caught in a net. Aṃhura. Ṛgveda 10.5.6: the sages made seven boundaries: theft, defiling the bed, killing a Brāhmaṇa or an embryo, drinking spirits, persisting in evil, lying about sin. Bata, of grief or pity.
28 | Bata; vātāpya, vāya, rathar | Ṛgveda 10.10.13: weak you are, Yama; another will embrace you as a creeper a tree. Libujā; vātāpya, water; vāya and Śākalya's word-division; rathar.
29 | Asakrā, ādhava, anavabrava | Asakrā, "not to be passed"; ādhava, "the stirrer"; anavabrava, "whose word is not set aside".
30 | Arāyi … karūlatī | Ṛgveda 10.155.1: begone, one-eyed, crooked, miserly hag, to the mountain. Kāṇa, vikaṭa, śirimbiṭha; Parāśara, son of the aged Vasiṣṭha, and Indra as Parāśara; krivirdatī; karūlatī.
31 | Karūlatī; mṛdhravāc, śarāru, idaṃyu | Ṛgveda 4.30.24: may Aryaman, Pūṣan, Bhaga and the toothless god give you good things; "Pūṣan is toothless". Mṛdhravāc, śarāru, idaṃyu.
32 | The Kīkaṭas and Pramaganda | Ṛgveda 3.53.14: what do the cows do for you among the Kīkaṭas? Bring us the wealth of Pramaganda. Kīkaṭa, a non-Āryan land; maganda, the usurer; naicāśākha; bunda, the arrow.
33 | Tuvikṣa, ṛdūpa | Ṛgveda 8.77.11: your bow is well made, your arrow golden; your arms are fit for battle, hitting the mark.
34 | Bunda, vṛnda | Ṛgveda 8.77.6: Indra pierced the cloud from the mountains, the well-stretched arrow. Vṛnda, vṛndāraka.
35 | Kiru; ulba, ṛbīsa | Ṛgveda 10.52.3: who is this priest, the maker for Yama, born every day? Ulba, the caul; ṛbīsa, "whose light is gone".
36 | Atri in the ṛbīsa | Ṛgveda 1.116.8: Aśvins, you checked the fire with cold and brought Atri up from the pit unharmed with all his band. The Naigama-kāṇḍa and the first half of the work end.
