### 1. [ganapati-atharvashirsha](../../../stotra/ganesha/ganapati-atharvashirsha-iast.html) · unit 13 · text
<!-- id: ganapati-atharvashirsha:13:text:dc446f -->

> anena gaṇapatimabhiṣiñcati sa vāgmī bhavati  
> caturthyāmanaśnan japati sa vidyāvān bhavati  
> sa yaśovān bhavati  
> ityatharvaṇavākyam  
> brahmādyācaraṇaṃ vidyāt na bibheti kadācaneti  ‖ 12 ‖  
>
> *Whoever consecrates Gaṇapati with this becomes eloquent. Whoever recites it fasting on the fourth lunar day becomes learned and renowned. So runs the Atharvaṇa saying. One who knows the way of conduct that begins with Brahman fears nothing, ever.*
>
> <sub>anena = with this · gaṇa-patim = Gaṇapati · abhiṣiñcati = one who bathes · saḥ = he · vāgmī = eloquent · bhavati = becomes · caturthyām = on the fourth day · anaśnan = fasting · japati = one who repeats it · saḥ = he · vidyāvān = learned · bhavati = becomes · saḥ = he · yaśovān = famous · bhavati = becomes · iti = thus · atharvaṇa-vākyam = the word of Atharvan · brahma-ādi-ācaraṇam = the practice beginning with Brahman · vidyāt = one should know · na = not · bibheti = he fears · kadācana = ever · iti = thus</sub>

**Issue.** The page reads brahmādyācaraṇaṃ vidyāt (“one should know the practice beginning with Brahmā”). Many recensions read brahmādyāvaraṇaṃ (“the covering, i.e. protection, of Brahmā and the rest”). Both are transmitted; the sense differs.

- A. Keep ācaraṇaṃ as the source has it.
- B. Change to āvaraṇaṃ and retranslate.
- C. Keep, and mention the other reading in the translation.

**Recommended.** A or C; this is a real variant, not a slip.
