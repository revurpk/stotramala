### 1. [kanakadhara-stotram](../../../stotra/devi/kanakadhara-stotram-iast.html) · unit 9 · text
<!-- id: kanakadhara-stotram:9:text:07cc14 -->

> dadyāddayānupavano draviṇāmbudhārām-  
> asminnakiñcanavihaṅgaśiśau viṣaṇṇe  
> duṣkarmadharmamapanīya cirāya dūraṃ  
> nārāyaṇapraṇayinīnayanāmbuvāhaḥ  ‖ 9 ‖  
>
> *Driven by the breeze of compassion, may the rain-cloud of the eyes of Nārāyaṇa's beloved pour its stream of wealth upon this dejected, penniless fledgling, driving the burden of ill deeds far off for long.*
>
> <sub>dadyāt = may it give · dayā-anupavanaḥ = driven by the wind of compassion · draviṇa-ambu-dhārām = a shower of wealth · asmin = on this · akiñcana-vihaṅga-śiśau = fledgling bird who has nothing · viṣaṇṇe = dejected · duṣkarma-dharmam = the burden of evil deeds · apanīya = removing · cirāya = for ever · dūram = far away · nārāyaṇa-praṇayinī-nayana-ambu-vāhaḥ = the rain-cloud that is the eye of Nārāyaṇa's beloved</sub>

**Issue.** The page reads duṣkarmadharmam apanīya. The usual text is duṣkarmagharmam, “the heat of ill deeds”, which is what a rain-cloud removes; dharmam looks like a misprint of gha as dha. The translation (“burden of ill deeds”) covers neither exactly.

- A. Emend to duṣkarmagharmam, translate “the scorching heat of ill deeds”, and log it.
- B. Keep dharmam and the present translation.

**Recommended.** A: the image requires it and it is the common reading.

### 2. [kanakadhara-stotram](../../../stotra/devi/kanakadhara-stotram-iast.html) · unit 8 · translation
<!-- id: kanakadhara-stotram:8:translation:42852f -->

> iṣṭāviśiṣṭamatayo'pi yayā dayārdra-  
> dṛṣṭyā triviṣṭapapadaṃ sulabhaṃ labhante  
> dṛṣṭiḥ prahṛṣṭakamalodaradīptiriṣṭāṃ  
> puṣṭiṃ kṛṣīṣṭa mama puṣkaraviṣṭarāyāḥ  ‖ 8 ‖  
>
> *By whose compassion-melting gaze even the undistinguished easily win heaven — may that gaze, bright as the joyful heart of a lotus, work in me the prosperity I long for: the gaze of her enthroned upon the lotus.*
>
> <sub>iṣṭa-aviśiṣṭa-matayaḥ = those of no special understanding (the compound is read in more than one way) · api = even · yayā = by which · dayā-ārdra-dṛṣṭyā = glance moist with compassion · tri-viṣṭapa-padam = the state of heaven · su-labham = easily · labhante = attain · dṛṣṭiḥ = the glance · prahṛṣṭa-kamala-udara-dīptiḥ = bright as the heart of a full-blown lotus · iṣṭām = the desired · puṣṭim = abundance · kṛṣīṣṭa = may it bring about · mama = for me · puṣkara-viṣṭarāyāḥ = of her whose seat is the lotus</sub>

**Issue.** iṣṭāviśiṣṭamatayo'pi: the translation takes it as “even the undistinguished”. Others divide iṣṭā viśiṣṭamatayaḥ (“the dear, the discerning”) or take iṣṭa as sacrifices (“those intent only on rites”).

- A. Keep “even the undistinguished”.
- B. “even those whose minds go no further than their rites”.
- C. Your wording.

**Recommended.** A: with api (“even”) it gives the best sense.
