### 1. [subrahmanya-bhujangam](../../../stotra/subrahmanya/subrahmanya-bhujangam-iast.html) · unit 31 · text
<!-- id: subrahmanya-bhujangam:31:text:c724d4 -->

> namaḥ kekine śaktaye cāpi tubhyaṃ  
> namaśca tubhyaṃ namaḥ kukkuṭāya  
> namaḥ sindhave sindhudeśāya tubhyaṃ  
> punaḥ skandamūrte namaste namo'stu  ‖ 31 ‖  
>
> *Salutation to the peacock, and to the spear; salutation to you, salutation to the rooster (of your banner); salutation to the ocean and to the sea-land; again, O form of Skanda, salutation, salutation to you.*
>
> <sub>namaḥ = salutation · kekine = to the peacock · śaktaye = to the spear · ca = and · api = also · tubhyam = to you · namaḥ = salutation · ca = and · tubhyam = to you · namaḥ = salutation · kukkuṭāya = to the cock · namaḥ = salutation · sindhave = to the ocean · sindhu-deśāya = to the seashore shrine · tubhyam = to you · punaḥ = again · skanda-mūrte = O form of Skanda · namaḥ = salutation · te = to you · namaḥ = salutation · astu = be</sub>

**Issue.** Line 2, namaśca tubhyaṃ namaḥ kukkuṭāya, is one syllable short of the metre (11 for 12). The usual text is namaśchāga tubhyaṃ, “salutation to you, O ram” (the ram is Skanda’s other mount), which fits the verse’s list: peacock, spear, ram, cock.

- A. Emend to namaśchāga tubhyaṃ and translate “salutation to you, O ram”.
- B. Keep as it is.

**Recommended.** A: the metre proves a syllable is lost and the list wants the ram.

### 3. [subrahmanya-bhujangam](../../../stotra/subrahmanya/subrahmanya-bhujangam-iast.html) · unit 32 · text
<!-- id: subrahmanya-bhujangam:32:text:30181b -->

> jayānandabhūmanjayāpāradhāman  
> jayāmoghakīrte jayānandamūrte  
> jayānandasindho jayāśeṣabandho  
> jaya tvaṃ pitā muktidāneśasūno  ‖ 32 ‖  
>
> *Victory, O ground of bliss! Victory, O boundless abode! Victory, O of unfailing fame! Victory, O embodiment of joy! Victory, O ocean of bliss! Victory, O kinsman of all! Victory to you, our father, O son of the Lord who grants liberation!*
>
> <sub>jaya = victory · ānanda-bhūman = O fullness of bliss · jaya = victory · apāra-dhāman = O boundless light · jaya = victory · amogha-kīrte = O you of unfailing fame · jaya = victory · ānanda-mūrte = O form of bliss · jaya = victory · ānanda-sindho = O ocean of bliss · jaya = victory · aśeṣa-bandho = O friend of all · jaya = victory · tvam = to you · pitā = our father · mukti-dāna-īśa-sūno = O son of the Lord, giver of liberation</sub>

**Issue.** Last line: jaya tvaṃ pitā muktidāneśasūno. The usual text has sadā for pitā (“be ever victorious, O son of the Lord who gives liberation”); pitā, a nominative among vocatives, is awkward, and the translation has to supply “our father”.

- A. Change to sadā and translate “Be ever victorious”.
- B. Keep pitā.

**Recommended.** A if you know the verse with sadā; otherwise B until the source is checked.
