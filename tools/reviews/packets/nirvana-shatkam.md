### 1. [nirvana-shatkam](../../../stotra/advaita/nirvana-shatkam-iast.html) · unit 6 · text
<!-- id: nirvana-shatkam:6:text:cec9cf -->

> ahaṃ nirvikalpo nirākārarūpaḥ  
> vibhutvācca sarvatra sarvendriyāṇām  
> na cāsaṅgataṃ naiva muktirna meyaḥ  
> cidānandarūpaḥ śivo'haṃ śivo'ham  ‖ 6 ‖  
>
> *I am without division and without form, pervading all, present in every sense; for me there is no detachment, no liberation, nothing to be known. I am consciousness and bliss — I am Śiva, I am Śiva.*
>
> <sub>aham = I am · nirvikalpaḥ = without distinctions · nirākāra-rūpaḥ = formless in nature · vibhutvāt = being all-pervading · ca = and · sarvatra = everywhere · sarva-indriyāṇām = of all the senses · na = not · ca = and · asaṅgatam = unattachment · na = nor · eva = at all · muktiḥ = liberation · na = nor · meyaḥ = anything to be measured · cit-ānanda-rūpaḥ = of the nature of consciousness and bliss · śivaḥ = Śiva · aham = am I · śivaḥ = Śiva · aham = am I</sub>

**Issue.** Line 3 reads na cāsaṅgataṃ naiva muktir na meyaḥ. A widely recited form is sadā me samatvaṃ na muktir na bandhaḥ (“I am ever the same; for me neither liberation nor bondage”).

- A. Keep the source’s reading.
- B. Change to the recited form and retranslate.

**Recommended.** A unless your recitation text has the other.
