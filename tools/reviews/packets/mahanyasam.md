### 2. [mahanyasam](../../../stotra/shiva/mahanyasam-iast.html) · unit 317 · text
<!-- id: mahanyasam:317:text:ab19a9 -->

> māghamāse site pakṣe caturdaśyāṃ mahāniśi  
> liṃgodbhavasya velāyāṃ gṛhāṇā'rghyaṃ namo namaḥ  
> iti tṛtīyārghyaṃ samarpayāmi  ‖ 3 ‖  
>
> *In the month of Māgha, on the fourteenth of the fortnight, in the great night, at the hour when the liṅga arose, accept this arghya; salutation, salutation. — I offer the third arghya.*
>
> <sub>māgha-māse = in the month of Māgha · site = in the bright · pakṣe = fortnight · caturdaśyām = on the fourteenth day · mahā-niśi = at dead of night · liṅga-udbhavasya = of the arising of the liṅga · velāyām = at the hour · gṛhāṇa = accept · arghyam = the water of welcome · namaḥ namaḥ = salutation again and again · iti = with this · tṛtīya-arghyam = the third water of welcome · samarpayāmi = I offer</sub>

**Issue.** Third Śivarātri arghya: māghamāse site pakṣe caturdaśyām. Śivarātri is the fourteenth of the dark fortnight, so this must be 'site (asite, “dark”) with the a- elided; as printed it reads “bright fortnight”. The translation avoids the point (“the fourteenth of the fortnight”).

- A. Print 'site and translate “the fourteenth of the dark fortnight”.
- B. Keep both as they are.

**Recommended.** A.

### 5. [mahanyasam](../../../stotra/shiva/mahanyasam-iast.html) · unit 111 · translation

> oṃ bhūrbhuvassuvaḥ

> namaśśaṃbhave ca … śivatarāya ca

> <sub>om = om · bhūḥ bhuvaḥ suvaḥ = earth, mid-region, heaven · om = om · am = seed syllable · namaḥ = salutation · śambhave = to the source of happiness · ca = and · śiva-tarāya = the more auspicious · ca = and · vibhūḥ = all-pervading · asi = you are · pravāhaṇaḥ = the carrier · raudreṇa = with Rudra's · anīkena = host · pāhi = protect · mā = me · agne = O Agni · pipṛhi = carry across · mā = me · mā = do not · mā = me · hiṃsīḥ = harm · namaḥ = salutation · śambhave = to the source of happiness · ca = and · śiva-tarāya = the more auspicious · ca = and · om = om · namaḥ = salutation · bhagavate = to the blessed · rudrāya = Rudra · am = seed syllable · om = om · bhūḥ bhuvaḥ suvaḥ om = earth, mid-region, heaven, om · śikhā-sthāne = at the place of the top-knot · rudrāya = to Rudra · namaḥ = salutation</sub>

- A. Keep “host of Rudras”.

**Recommended.** A.
