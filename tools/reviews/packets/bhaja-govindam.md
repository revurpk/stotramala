### 1. [bhaja-govindam](../../../stotra/vishnu/bhaja-govindam-iast.html) · unit 33 · text
<!-- id: bhaja-govindam:33:text:4a59b3 -->

> mūḍhaḥ kaścana vaiyākaraṇo  
> ḍukṛñkaraṇādhyayanadhuriṇaḥ  
> śrīmacchaṅkarabhagavacchiṣyai  
> bodhita āsīcchodhitakaraṇaḥ  ‖ 32 ‖  
>
> *A certain foolish grammarian, engrossed in the study of the rule ḍukṛñ-karaṇa, was awakened and had his faculties made pure, by the disciples of the venerable Lord Śaṅkara.*
>
> <sub>mūḍhaḥ = foolish · kaścana = a certain · vaiyākaraṇaḥ = grammarian · ḍukṛñ-karaṇa-adhyayana-dhuriṇaḥ = burdened with studying the rule 'ḍukṛñ karaṇe' · śrīmat-śaṅkara-bhagavat-śiṣyaiḥ = by the disciples of the revered Śaṅkara · bodhitaḥ = enlightened · āsīt = was · śodhita-karaṇaḥ = his faculties purified</sub>

**Issue.** Two small slips in the colophon verse: śrīmacchaṅkarabhagavacchiṣyai / bodhita lacks the r of the instrumental plural (śiṣyair bodhita), and dhuriṇaḥ should be dhurīṇaḥ.

- A. Correct both (śiṣyair, dhurīṇaḥ).
- B. Keep as in the source.

**Recommended.** A: both are plain misspellings; the word gloss already reads śiṣyaiḥ.
