### 1. [vishnu-sahasranamam](../../../stotra/vishnu/vishnu-sahasranamam-iast.html) · whole page · translation

**Issue.** For some forty names the sentence translation and the word gloss give different explanations, both traditional (most are alternatives in Śaṅkara’s commentary). Examples: rudraḥ “remover of sorrow” / “who makes all weep at the end”; janārdanaḥ “the refuge sought by people” / “who afflicts the wicked”; pavanaḥ “the wind” / “the purifier”; dāśārhaḥ “worthy of offerings” / “born in the Daśārha line”; vīrahā (v. 99) “who ends the round of births”. A reader comparing the two layers will see them disagree.

- A. Leave as it is: the two layers together show both explanations.
- B. Make the word gloss repeat the translation’s sense everywhere.
- C. Keep both, but add to the page note one sentence saying that where the two differ, both are traditional explanations.

**Recommended.** C: it keeps the information and removes the appearance of error.
