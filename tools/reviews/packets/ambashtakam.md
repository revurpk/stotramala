### 6. [ambashtakam](../../../stotra/devi/ambashtakam-iast.html) · unit 2 · translation
<!-- id: ambashtakam:2:translation:ffe011 -->

> kūlātigāmibhayatūlāvalijvalanakīlā nijastutividhākolāhalakṣapitakālāmarī kuśalakīlālapoṣaṇanabhāḥ  
> sthūlā kuce jaladanīlā kace kalitalīlā kadambavipine  
> śūlāyudhapraṇatiśīlā vibhātu hṛdi śailādhirājatanayā  ‖ 2 ‖  
>
> *May Śailarāja’s daughter, who is a flame of fire to the cotton-heaps of fears that overflow every bank, who drives away Time (death) with the tumult of her own praise, a cloud nourishing the water of well-being, broad in the breast, dark as a rain-cloud in her hair, playing in the Kadamba forest and ever bowing to the one with the trident, shine in my heart.*
>
> <sub>kūlāti-gāmi-bhaya-tūlāvali-jvalana-kīlā = a flame of fire to the cotton-heaps of fears that overflow every bank · nija-stuti-vidhā-kolāhala-kṣapita-kālā = who drives Time (Death) away with the clamour of her own praise · amarī = the goddess · kuśala-kīlāla-poṣaṇa-nabhāḥ = a cloud that nourishes the water of well-being · sthūlā kuce = broad in the breast · jaladanīlā kace = dark as a rain-cloud in the hair · kalita-līlā = playful · kadamba-vipine = in the kadamba forest · śūlāyudha-praṇati-śīlā = ever bowing to the bearer of the trident · vibhātu = may she shine · hṛdi = in the heart · śailādhirāja-tanayā = the Mountain King's daughter</sub>

**Issue.** śūlāyudha-praṇati-śīlā can be “who is wont to bow to the Trident-bearer” (as now) or “to whom the Trident-bearer is wont to bow”. The compound allows both; they differ in who honours whom.

- A. Keep “ever bowing to the one with the trident”.
- B. Change to “to whom the Trident-bearer bows”.

**Recommended.** A: it is the plainer reading of praṇati-śīlā.
