### 1. [satyanarayana-vratakalpam](../../../stotra/vishnu/satyanarayana-vratakalpam-iast.html) · unit 56 · text
<!-- id: satyanarayana-vratakalpam:56:text:c88f71 -->

> yāmye gadāśaktidharaśca śūlī varaprado yāmyamukho'tiriktaḥ  
> kujastvavantīviṣayastrikoṇastasminbharadvājakule prasūtaḥ  
> raktāmbaro raktavapuḥ kirīṭī caturbhujo meṣagamo gadābhṛt  
> dharāsutaśśaktidharaśca śūlī sadā mama syādvaradaḥ praśāntaḥ  
>
> *Dhyāna: in the south, bearing mace, spear and trident, giver of boons, facing south, fierce; Mars, of the Avantī country, on a triangular seat, born in Bharadvāja's line. Red-robed, red of body, crowned, four-armed, riding a ram, mace-bearing — may the son of the Earth, holding spear and trident, be ever gracious and calm toward me.*
>
> <sub>yāmye = in the south · gadā-śakti-dharaḥ = bearing mace and spear · ca = and · śūlī = with a trident · vara-pradaḥ = granting boons · yāmya-mukhaḥ = facing south · atiriktaḥ = surpassing · kujaḥ = Mars · tu = indeed · avantī-viṣayaḥ = of the Avantī country · tri-koṇaḥ = with a triangular seat · tasmin = there · bharadvāja-kule = in the family of Bharadvāja · prasūtaḥ = born · rakta-ambaraḥ = red-robed · rakta-vapuḥ = red-bodied · kirīṭī = crowned · catuḥ-bhujaḥ = four-armed · meṣa-gamaḥ = riding a ram · gadā-bhṛt = mace-bearer · dharā-sutaḥ = son of the Earth · śakti-dharaḥ = bearing a spear · ca = and · śūlī = with a trident · sadā = always · mama = to me · syāt = may he be · vara-daḥ = a giver of boons · praśāntaḥ = serene</sub>

**Issue.** Mars dhyāna: yāmyamukho'tiriktaḥ. atiriktaḥ (“surpassing, in excess”) fits poorly; the translation has “fierce”, the gloss “surpassing”. The word is probably atiraktaḥ, “deep red”, the colour the next line gives him.

- A. Emend to atiraktaḥ and translate “deep red”.
- B. Keep the booklet's reading and make the translation “surpassing”.
- C. Check your booklet.

**Recommended.** C, then A if the booklet allows it.

### 2. [satyanarayana-vratakalpam](../../../stotra/vishnu/satyanarayana-vratakalpam-iast.html) · unit 192 · text
<!-- id: satyanarayana-vratakalpam:192:text:38aacb -->

> vighneśaḥ padmajā viṣṇurmahādevaśca pārvatī  
> ādityādigrahāssarve śakrādyaṣṭadigīśvarāḥ  
> atrāṅgadevatāḥ proktāstasmādagre prapūjayet  
> agrataḥ kalaśe devaṃ varuṇaṃ pṛthagarcayet  
>
> *“Gaṇeśa, Lakṣmī, Viṣṇu, Mahādeva and Pārvatī, all the planets beginning with the Sun, and the eight guardians of the quarters beginning with Indra — these are declared the attendant deities here, so they should be worshipped first. Before them, one should worship Varuṇa separately in the vessel.”*
>
> <sub>vighna-īśaḥ = Gaṇeśa · padma-jā = the lotus-born goddess · viṣṇuḥ = Viṣṇu · mahā-devaḥ = Mahādeva · ca = and · pārvatī = Pārvatī · āditya-ādi-grahāḥ = the planets, the Sun and the rest · sarve = all · śakra-ādi-aṣṭa-dik-īśvarāḥ = the eight lords of the quarters, Indra and the rest · atra = here · aṅga-devatāḥ = as attendant deities · proktāḥ = are declared · tasmāt = therefore · agre = first · prapūjayet = one should worship them · agrataḥ = in front · kalaśe = in the vessel · devam = the god · varuṇam = Varuṇa · pṛthak = separately · arcayet = one should worship</sub>

**Issue.** Kathā 1: vighneśaḥ padmajā viṣṇur mahādevaś ca pārvatī lists the five attendant deities. The pūjā itself worships Gaṇapati, Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Rudra and Gaurī, so the second should be padmajaḥ (Brahmā, “the lotus-born”; before viṣṇuḥ the form would be padmajo). The page has the feminine padmajā, and the translation says “Lakṣmī”.

- A. Emend to padmajo and translate “Brahmā”.
- B. Keep padmajā and “Lakṣmī”.
- C. Check your booklet.

**Recommended.** A: the list must match the five deities the rite has just worshipped.

### 3. [satyanarayana-vratakalpam](../../../stotra/vishnu/satyanarayana-vratakalpam-iast.html) · unit 152 · text
<!-- id: satyanarayana-vratakalpam:152:text:94d38f -->

> oṃ vasudhāriṇyai namaḥ  
> oṃ kamalāyai namaḥ  
> oṃ kāntāyai namaḥ  
> oṃ kāmākṣyai namaḥ  
> oṃ krodhasambhavāyai namaḥ  
> oṃ anugrahapradāyai namaḥ  
> oṃ buddhaye namaḥ  
> oṃ anaghāyai namaḥ  
> oṃ harivallabhāyai namaḥ  
> oṃ aśokāyai namaḥ  
> oṃ amṛtāyai namaḥ  
> oṃ dīptāyai namaḥ  
>
> *25–36: upholder of the earth; Kamalā; the beloved; lovely-eyed; born of wrath; bestower of grace; wisdom; the sinless; dear to Hari; free of sorrow; immortal; the shining.*
>
> <sub>om vasu-dhāriṇyai namaḥ = om, salutation to the bearer of wealth · om kamalāyai namaḥ = om, salutation to Kamalā · om kāntāyai namaḥ = om, salutation to the beloved · om kāma-akṣyai namaḥ = om, salutation to her of loving eyes · om krodha-sambhavāyai namaḥ = om, salutation to her who arose from wrath · om anugraha-pradāyai namaḥ = om, salutation to the giver of grace · om buddhaye namaḥ = om, salutation to Wisdom · om anaghāyai namaḥ = om, salutation to the sinless one · om hari-vallabhāyai namaḥ = om, salutation to the beloved of Hari · om aśokāyai namaḥ = om, salutation to the sorrowless one · om amṛtāyai namaḥ = om, salutation to the immortal one · om dīptāyai namaḥ = om, salutation to the blazing one</sub>

**Issue.** Lakṣmī's name 29 is given as krodhasambhavā (“born of wrath”). Many texts of the Lakṣmī Aṣṭottara read kṣīrodasambhavā, “born of the ocean of milk”, at this place.

- A. Keep krodhasambhavā as the booklet has it.
- B. Change to kṣīrodasambhavā.

**Recommended.** A unless your booklet reads otherwise; both are in circulation.
