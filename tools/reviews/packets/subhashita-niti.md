### 1. [subhashita-niti](../../../stotra/subhashita/subhashita-niti-iast.html) · unit 84 · text
<!-- id: subhashita-niti:84:text:c3c78d -->

> aiśvaryasya vibhūṣaṇaṃ sujanatā śauryasya vāksaṃyamo jñānasyopaśamaḥ śrutasya vinayo vittasya pātre vyayaḥ  
> akroghastapasaḥ kṣamā prabhaviturdharmasya nirvyājatā sarveṣāmapi sarvakāraṇamidaṃ śīlaṃ paraṃ bhūṣaṇam  ‖ 83 ‖  
>
> *The ornament of lordship is goodness, of valour restraint of speech, of knowledge calm, of learning modesty, of wealth giving to the worthy, of austerity freedom from anger, of power forbearance, of dharma sincerity; and the greatest ornament, the cause of all of them, is good character.*
>
> <sub>aiśvaryasya vibhūṣaṇam = the ornament of power · sujanatā = goodness · śauryasya = of valour · vāk-saṃyamaḥ = restraint of speech · jñānasya upaśamaḥ = of knowledge, calm · śrutasya vinayaḥ = of learning, humility · vittasya pātre vyayaḥ = of wealth, spending on the worthy · akrodhaḥ tapasaḥ = of austerity, freedom from anger · kṣamā prabhavituḥ = of the powerful, forbearance · dharmasya nirvyājatā = of righteousness, sincerity · sarveṣām api sarva-kāraṇam idam = for all, the root of all these · śīlam param bhūṣaṇam = is character the highest ornament</sub>

### 2. [subhashita-niti](../../../stotra/subhashita/subhashita-niti-iast.html) · unit 22 · text
<!-- id: subhashita-niti:22:text:152d09 -->

> kṣāntiścedvacanena kiṃ kimaribhiḥ krodho'sti ceddehināṃ  
> jñātiścedanalena kiṃ yadi suhṛddivyauṣadhaiḥ kiṃ phalam  
> kiṃ sarpairyadi durjanāḥ kimu dhanairvidyā'navadyā yadi  
> vrīḍā cetkimu bhūṣaṇaiḥ sukavitā yadyasti rājyena kim  ‖ 21 ‖  
>
> *If one has patience, what use are words; if one has anger, what need of enemies; with kinsfolk, what need of fire; with friends, what use are divine herbs; with rogues about, why snakes; with spotless learning, why wealth; with modesty, why ornaments; with good poetry, why a kingdom?*
>
> <sub>kṣāntiḥ cet = if there is patience · vacanena kim = what use are words · kim aribhiḥ = what need of enemies · krodhaḥ asti cet = if anger is there · dehinām = in beings · jñātiḥ cet = if there are kinsmen · analena kim = what need of fire · yadi suhṛt = if a friend · divya-auṣadhaiḥ kim phalam = what gain from divine herbs · kim sarpaiḥ = what need of snakes · yadi durjanāḥ = if there are wicked men · kimu dhanaiḥ = what need of riches · vidyā anavadyā yadi = if one has faultless learning · vrīḍā cet = if there is modesty · kimu bhūṣaṇaiḥ = what need of ornaments · su-kavitā yadi asti = if there is fine poetry · rājyena kim = what need of a kingdom</sub>

**Issue.** Verse 21 begins kṣāntiś ced vacanena kim (“if there is patience, what use are words”), as Wikisource has it. The usual text is kavacena (“what need of armour”), which suits the verse better; the metre allows both.

- A. Keep the source's vacanena and add the other reading in brackets to the translation.
- B. Adopt kavacena in the text and translate “what need of armour”.
- C. Leave everything as it is.

**Recommended.** A.
