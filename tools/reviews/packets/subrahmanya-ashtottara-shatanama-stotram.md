>
> *Kāraṇotpattideha (whose body is the origin of causes); Kāraṇātītavigraha (whose form is beyond all cause); Anīśvara (without a lord above him); Amṛta (immortal); Prāṇa (the life-breath); Prāṇāyāmaparāyaṇa (devoted to the control of breath).*
>
> <sub>kāraṇa-utpatti-dehaḥ = whose body is the origin of causes · ca = and · kāraṇa-atīta-vigrahaḥ = whose form is beyond cause · anīśvaraḥ = without a lord above · amṛtaḥ = immortal · prāṇaḥ = the life-breath · prāṇāyāma-parāyaṇaḥ = devoted to control of breath</sub>

**Issue.** Verse 14 reads kāraṇotpattidehaḥ (the Wikisource text). The TTD nāmāvali, now checked against its page image, reads kāraṇopāttadeha (“who took on a body for a purpose”), which is also the usual reading. Two other TTD readings were already adopted in this verse.

- A. Adopt kāraṇopāttadehaś ca, with the meaning “who took on a body for a purpose”.
- B. Keep the Wikisource reading.

**Recommended.** A.
