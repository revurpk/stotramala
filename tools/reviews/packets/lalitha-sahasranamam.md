> *Enchanting, with a cluster of kadamba blossoms set at her ear; the orbs of sun and moon become the pair of her earrings.*
>
> <sub>kadamba-mañjarī-kḷpta-karṇa-pūra-manaḥ-harā = charming with kadamba blossoms over her ears · tāṭaṅka-yugalī-bhūta-tapana-uḍupa-maṇḍalā = whose pair of earrings are the orbs of sun and moon</sub>

- A. Correct both to kḷpta.
- B. Keep.

**Recommended.** A.
