### 1. [achyutashtakam](../../../stotra/vishnu/achyutashtakam-iast.html) · unit 3 · text

> viṣṇave jiṣṇave śaṅkhine cakriṇe  
> rukmaṇīrāgiṇe jānakījānaye  
> vallavīvallabhāyārcitāyātmane  
> kaṃsavidhvaṃsine vaṃśine te namaḥ  ‖ 3 ‖  
>
> *To Viṣṇu, the ever-victorious, bearer of conch and discus; lover of Rukmiṇī, lord of Jānakī; beloved of the cowherd women, the worshipped Self; destroyer of Kaṃsa, player of the flute — to you, salutation.*
>
> <sub>viṣṇave = to Viṣṇu · jiṣṇave = the victorious · śaṅkhine = bearer of the conch · cakriṇe = bearer of the discus · rukmiṇī-rāgiṇe = the lover of Rukmiṇī · jānakī-jānaye = the husband of Jānakī · vallavī-vallabhāya = the beloved of the cowherd women · arcitāya = the worshipped · ātmane = the Self · kaṃsa-vidhvaṃsine = the destroyer of Kaṃsa · vaṃśine = the flute-player · te = to you · namaḥ = salutation</sub>

**Issue.** The page prints rukmaṇīrāgiṇe; the name is Rukmiṇī, and the word gloss already reads rukmiṇī-rāgiṇe. The spelling rukmaṇī does occur in some prints of this hymn, so it may be the source’s own.

- A. Change the text to rukmiṇīrāgiṇe and log it in SOURCES.md as a correction.
- B. Keep rukmaṇī as the source’s spelling and make the word gloss match it.

**Recommended.** A, if the Wikisource page reads rukmiṇī or you are content to normalise; the meaning is not affected either way.

### 2. [achyutashtakam](../../../stotra/vishnu/achyutashtakam-iast.html) · unit 5 · text
<!-- id: achyutashtakam:5:text:12c819 -->

> rākṣasakṣobhitaḥ sītayā śobhito  
> daṇḍakāraṇyabhūpuṇyatākāraṇam  
> lakṣmaṇenānvito vānaraiḥ sevito  
> 'gastyasaṃpūjito rāghavaḥ pātu mām  ‖ 5 ‖  
>
> *Harried by demons yet graced by Sītā at his side, the cause of the Daṇḍaka forest's sanctity; accompanied by Lakṣmaṇa, served by the monkey-host, worshipped by Agastya — may Rāghava protect me.*
>
> <sub>rākṣasa-kṣobhitaḥ = troubled by the rākṣasas · sītayā = by Sītā · śobhitaḥ = adorned · daṇḍaka-araṇya-bhū-puṇyatā-kāraṇam = the cause of the holiness of the Daṇḍaka forest · lakṣmaṇena = by Lakṣmaṇa · anvitaḥ = accompanied · vānaraiḥ = by the monkeys · sevitaḥ = served · agastya-saṃpūjitaḥ = honoured by Agastya · rāghavaḥ = Rāghava · pātu = may he protect · mām = me</sub>

**Issue.** daṇḍakāraṇyabhūpuṇyatākāraṇam ends in -kāraṇam (neuter) among masculine nominatives describing Rāghava; many prints read -kāraṇaḥ. Grammatical as it stands (kāraṇa is a neuter noun in apposition).

- A. Keep the text as it is.
- B. Change to -kāraṇaḥ.

**Recommended.** A: the page reading is defensible and is the source’s.
