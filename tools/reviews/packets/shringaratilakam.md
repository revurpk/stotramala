### 1. [shringaratilakam](../../../stotra/kavya/shringaratilakam-iast.html) · unit 8 · text
<!-- id: shringaratilakam:8:text:daff6a -->

> nāhaṃ no mama vallabhaśca kupitaḥ supto na vā sundaro  
> vṛddho no na ca bālakaḥ kṛśatanurna vyādhito no śaṭhaḥ  
> māṃ navayauvanāṃ śaśimukhīṃ kaṃdarpabāṇāhato  
> mukto daityaguruḥ priyeṇa purataḥ paścādgato vihvalaḥ  ‖ 8 ‖  
>
> *“I am not angry, nor is my lover; he was not asleep, nor is he ugly, or old, or a boy, or thin, or sick, or a cheat. At the sight of me, young and moon-faced, he was struck by Love’s arrows: my dear one let ‘the demons’ teacher’ go at the very start, and then went off in confusion.” (The demons’ teacher is Śukra, a pun on śukra, seed. The third quarter is two syllables short in the print; the usual text has māṃ dṛṣṭvā, “seeing me”.)*
>
> <sub>na aham = not I (am angry) · no = nor · mama vallabhaḥ = my lover · ca = and · kupitaḥ = angry · suptaḥ na = he was not asleep · vā = or · sundaraḥ = (un)handsome · vṛddhaḥ no = not old · na ca bālakaḥ = nor a boy · kṛśa-tanuḥ na = not thin · vyādhitaḥ no = not sick · śaṭhaḥ = (nor) a cheat · mām = me · nava-yauvanām = in fresh youth · śaśi-mukhīm = moon-faced · kaṃdarpa-bāṇa-āhataḥ = struck by Love’s arrows · muktaḥ = was let go · daitya-guruḥ = the demons’ teacher, Śukra (a pun on śukra, seed) · priyeṇa = by my dear one · purataḥ = at the very start · paścāt = afterwards · gataḥ = he went off · vihvalaḥ = confounded</sub>

**Issue.** Quarter 3 reads māṃ navayauvanāṃ śaśimukhīṃ…, two syllables short of the metre (17 for 19). The usual text is māṃ dṛṣṭvā navayauvanāṃ…, “seeing me”.

- A. Restore dṛṣṭvā and log it.
- B. Keep the print’s reading with the note in the translation.

**Recommended.** A: the metre proves the loss and the sense needs the word.

### 2. [shringaratilakam](../../../stotra/kavya/shringaratilakam-iast.html) · unit 20 · text
<!-- id: shringaratilakam:20:text:465521 -->

> dṛṣṭvā yāsāṃ nayanasuṣamāmaṅga vārāṅganānāṃ  
> deśatyāgaḥ paramakṛtibhiḥ kṛṣṇasārairakāri  
> tāsāmeva stanayugajitā dastinaḥ santi mattāḥ  
> prāyo mūrkhāḥ paribhavavidhau nābhimānaṃ tyajanti  ‖ 20 ‖  
>
> *Seeing the beauty of the courtesans’ eyes, my friend, the black antelopes — wisest of creatures — left the country; the elephants, though outdone by those same women’s breasts, stay on, proud in their rut. Fools, as a rule, do not give up their pride even when they are put to shame.*
>
> <sub>dṛṣṭvā = seeing · yāsām = whose · nayana-suṣamām = beauty of eyes · aṅga = my friend · vāra-aṅganānām = of the courtesans · deśa-tyāgaḥ = the leaving of the country · parama-kṛtibhiḥ = most wise · kṛṣṇasāraiḥ = by the black antelopes · akāri = was done · tāsām eva = of those same women · stana-yuga-jitāḥ = outdone by the pair of breasts · dastinaḥ = the elephants (a misprint for hastinaḥ) · santi = remain · mattāḥ = proud in rut · prāyaḥ = as a rule · mūrkhāḥ = fools · paribhava-vidhau = when put to shame · na = not · abhimānam = their pride · tyajanti = give up</sub>

**Issue.** dastinaḥ in quarter 3 is not a word; hastinaḥ (“elephants”) is certainly meant.

- A. Correct to hastinaḥ.
- B. Keep.

**Recommended.** A.

### 3. [shringaratilakam](../../../stotra/kavya/shringaratilakam-iast.html) · unit 2 · text
<!-- id: shringaratilakam:2:text:a711fb -->

> āyātā madhuyāminī yadi punarnāyāta eva prabhuḥ  
> prāṇā yāntu vibhāvasau yadi punarjanmagrahaṃ prārthaye  
> vyādhaḥ kokilabandhane vidhuparidhvaṃse ca rāhugrahaḥ  
> kaṃdarpa haranetradīdhitiriyaṃ prāṇeśvare manmathaḥ  ‖ 2 ‖  
>
> *The spring night has come, and still my lord has not: let my life go into the fire. And if I am to take birth again, I pray to be the hunter who snares the cuckoo, Rāhu’s grip that destroys the moon, the blaze of Śiva’s eye upon Love — and Love himself upon the lord of my life.*
>
> <sub>āyātā = has come · madhu-yāminī = the spring night · yadi = if · punaḥ = yet · na āyātaḥ eva = has still not come · prabhuḥ = my lord · prāṇāḥ = my life · yāntu = let it go · vibhāvasau = into the fire · yadi punaḥ = and if again · janma-graham = the taking of birth · prārthaye = I pray (to be) · vyādhaḥ = the hunter · kokila-bandhane = for the snaring of the cuckoo · vidhu-paridhvaṃse = for the destruction of the moon · ca = and · rāhu-grahaḥ = the grip of Rāhu · kaṃdarpa = on Love (the usual text has the locative kandarpe) · hara-netra-dīdhitiḥ = the blaze of Śiva’s eye · iyam = (so the print; the usual text has aham, “I”) · prāṇa-īśvare = upon the lord of my life · manmathaḥ = Love himself</sub>

**Issue.** Quarter 4 reads kaṃdarpa haranetradīdhitiriyaṃ; the usual text is kandarpe haranetradīdhitir ahaṃ (“may I be the fire of Śiva’s eye upon Love”). The translation follows the usual sense.

- A. Emend to kandarpe … ahaṃ.
- B. Keep the print.

**Recommended.** A: the parallel locatives (bandhane, paridhvaṃse, prāṇeśvare) require kandarpe.

### 4. [shringaratilakam](../../../stotra/kavya/shringaratilakam-iast.html) · unit 25 · text
<!-- id: shringaratilakam:25:text:7a1dcd -->

> enaṃ payodharayugaṃ patitaṃ nirīkṣya  
> svedaṃ vṛthā vahasi kiṃ hariṇāyatākṣi  
> stabdho vivekarahito janatāpakārī  
> yo'tyunnataḥ prapatatīti kimatra citram  ‖ 25 ‖  
>
> *“Seeing this pair of breasts fallen, why, long-eyed one, do you shed sweat in vain? Whatever is stiff, thoughtless, a trouble to people, and too high, falls — what wonder is there in this?”*
>
> <sub>enam = this · payodhara-yugam = pair of breasts · patitam = fallen · nirīkṣya = seeing · svedam = sweat · vṛthā = in vain · vahasi = you shed · kim = why · hariṇa-āyata-akṣi = O long-eyed one · stabdhaḥ = stiff · viveka-rahitaḥ = thoughtless · janatā-apakārī = a trouble to people · yaḥ ati-unnataḥ = whatever is too high · prapatati = falls · iti = so · kim atra citram = what wonder in this</sub>

**Issue.** svedaṃ vṛthā vahasi (“you bear sweat in vain”) is probably khedaṃ (“grief”), the usual reading; the translation has “shed sweat”.

- A. Emend to khedaṃ and translate “why do you grieve in vain”.
- B. Keep svedaṃ.

**Recommended.** A.
