### 1. [ghatakarpara](../../../stotra/kavya/ghatakarpara-iast.html) · unit 3 · text
<!-- id: ghatakarpara:3:text:13a9b9 -->

> meghāvṛtaṃ niśi na bhāti nabho vitāraṃ  
> nidrābhyupaiti ca hariṃ sukhasevitāram  
> sendrāyudhaśca jalado dharasannibhānāṃ  
> saṃrambhamāvahati bhūdharasannibhānām  ‖ 3 ‖  
>
> *By night the cloud-covered sky does not shine, starless; sleep comes to Hari, who takes his ease; and the cloud with Indra’s bow, roaring, rouses the fury of elephants that are like mountains. (The third quarter is translated from the usual text, jalado’dya rasann ibhānām; the print’s reading gives no sense.)*
>
> <sub>megha-āvṛtam = covered with clouds · niśi = at night · na bhāti = does not shine · nabhaḥ = the sky · vitāram = starless · nidrā = sleep · abhyupaiti = comes · ca = and · harim = to Hari · sukha-sevitāram = who takes his ease · sa-indra-āyudhaḥ = with Indra’s bow · ca = and · jaladaḥ = the cloud · dharasannibhānām = (so the print; the usual text is adya rasan ibhānām, “roaring today, of the elephants”) · saṃrambham = fury · āvahati = rouses · bhūdhara-sannibhānām = that are like mountains</sub>

**Issue.** Quarter 3 reads sendrāyudhaśca jalado dharasannibhānāṃ, which gives no sense. The usual text is jalado’dya rasann ibhānāṃ (“the cloud, roaring today, [rouses the fury] of elephants”), which also makes the yamaka with bhūdharasannibhānām. Same syllable count. The translation now follows the usual text and says so.

- A. Emend to jalado’dya rasannibhānāṃ and log it.
- B. Keep the print’s reading with the note.
- C. Re-read the scan first (Kāvyasaṅgraha vol. 1).

**Recommended.** A: sense and yamaka both require it; dha for dya is an easy misreading of the scan.
