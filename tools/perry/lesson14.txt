## Lesson XIV
¶ **193. Verbs, ⟨a⟩-conjugation. Present Imperative Active.** The inflection of this mode is as follows:
T  | Sing. | Dual. | Plural.
R 1. | ⟨vadāni⟩ | ⟨vadāva⟩ | ⟨vadāma⟩
R 2. | ⟨vada⟩ | ⟨vadatam⟩ | ⟨vadata⟩
R 3. | ⟨vadatu⟩ | ⟨vadatām⟩ | ⟨vadantu⟩
¶ **194.** The three first persons are properly subjunctive forms, and accordingly often express a wish or future action.
¶ **195.** The second and third persons of the imperative express oftenest a command; sometimes a wish or future action. The negative used with the imv. is ⟨mā⟩.
¶ **196.** A rare imv. form, either 2nd or 3rd pers. sing. (or plur.), is made with the ending ⟨tāt⟩; thus, ⟨bhavatāt⟩. Its value is that of a posterior or future imv. (like the Latin forms in *to* and *tote*).

## Exercise XIV
¶ The exercise opens with a verse, in two halves (the translations are the editor’s):
S a | dharmaṃ carata mādharmaṃ satyaṃ vadata mānṛtam | Practise righteousness, not unrighteousness; speak the truth, not untruth. | ధర్మాన్ని ఆచరించండి, అధర్మాన్ని కాదు; సత్యం పలకండి, అసత్యాన్ని కాదు.
S b | dīrghaṃ paśyata mā hrasvaṃ paraṃ paśyata māparam | Look far, not near; look to the highest, not to the lower. | దూరాన్ని చూడండి, దగ్గరిదాన్ని కాదు; ఉన్నతాన్ని చూడండి, తక్కువదాన్ని కాదు.
