## Lesson XXXI
¶ **328. Numerals. Cardinals:** ⟨eka⟩ 1, ⟨dva⟩ 2, ⟨tri⟩ 3, ⟨catur⟩ 4, ⟨pañca⟩ 5, ⟨ṣaṣ⟩ 6, ⟨sapta⟩ 7, ⟨aṣṭa⟩ 8, ⟨nava⟩ 9, ⟨daśa⟩ 10. — ⟨ekādaśa⟩ 11, ⟨dvādaśa⟩ 12, ⟨trayodaśa⟩ 13, ⟨caturdaśa⟩ 14, ⟨pañcadaśa⟩ 15, ⟨ṣoḍaśa⟩ 16, ⟨saptadaśa⟩ 17, ⟨aṣṭādaśa⟩ 18, ⟨navadaśa⟩ 19, ⟨viṃśati⟩ 20. — ⟨ekaviṃśati⟩ 21, ⟨dvāviṃśati⟩ 22, etc. — ⟨triṃśat⟩ 30, ⟨catvāriṃśat⟩ 40, ⟨pañcāśat⟩ 50, ⟨ṣaṣṭi⟩ 60, ⟨saptati⟩ 70, ⟨aśīti⟩ 80, ⟨navati⟩ 90, ⟨śata⟩ 100. — ⟨dviśata⟩ or ⟨dve śate⟩ 200. — ⟨sahasra⟩ 1000, ⟨dvisahasra⟩ or ⟨dve sahasre⟩ 2000, ⟨śatasahasra⟩ or ⟨lakṣa⟩ 100 000.
¶ **329.** The numbers between the even tens are made by prefixing the unit-number to the ten; thus, ⟨pañcaviṃśati⟩ 25. But note: ⟨ekādaśa⟩, not ⟨ekadaśa⟩, 11. 42, 52, 62, 72 and 92, either ⟨dvicatvāriṃśat⟩ or ⟨dvāca-⟩, etc.; 43–73, and 93, either ⟨tri-⟩ or ⟨trayaś catvā-⟩ etc.; 48–78, and 98, either ⟨aṣṭa-⟩ or ⟨aṣṭā catvā-⟩ etc. 96 is ⟨ṣaṇṇavati⟩.
¶ **330.** There are other ways of expressing the numbers between the tens. Thus: 1. By the use of the adj. ⟨ūna⟩ ‘deficient’, in composition; e. g. ⟨ekonaviṃśati⟩ ‘20 less 1’, i. e. 19. This usage is not common except for the nines. Sometimes ⟨eka⟩ is left off, and ⟨ūnaviṃśati⟩, etc., have the same value. 2. By the adj. ⟨adhika⟩ or ⟨uttara⟩ ‘more’, also in composition; e. g., ⟨aṣṭādhikanavati⟩ (also ⟨aṣṭādhikā navati⟩) 98.
@146
¶ **331.** The same methods are used to form the odd numbers above 100. Thus, ⟨ekaśatam⟩ 101, ⟨aṣṭaśatam⟩ 108, ⟨pañcādhikaṃ śatam⟩ 105, ⟨saptottaraṃ śatam⟩ 107.
¶ **332. Inflection of cardinals.** 1. ⟨eka⟩ is declined like ⟨sarva⟩, at § 231 (pl.: ‘some’, ‘certain ones’). The dual does not occur. ⟨eka⟩ sometimes means ‘a certain’; or even ‘an, a’, as an indefinite article.
¶ 2. ⟨dva⟩ (dual only) is quite regular; thus, nom.-acc.-voc. m., ⟨dvau⟩, f. n. ⟨dve⟩; ⟨dvābhyām⟩, ⟨dvayos⟩.
¶ 3. ⟨tri⟩ is in masc. and neut. nearly regular; the fem. has the stem ⟨tisṛ⟩. Thus, nom. m. ⟨trayas⟩, acc. m. ⟨trīn⟩, nom.-acc. n. ⟨trīṇi⟩; instr. ⟨tribhis⟩, dat.-abl. ⟨tribhyas⟩, gen. ⟨trayāṇām⟩, loc. ⟨triṣu⟩. Fem.: nom.-acc. ⟨tisras⟩, instr. ⟨tisṛbhis⟩, dat.-abl. ⟨tisṛbhyas⟩, gen. ⟨tisṛṇām⟩, loc. ⟨tisṛṣu⟩.
¶ 4. ⟨catur⟩ has ⟨catvār⟩ in strong cases; the fem. stem is ⟨catasṛ⟩. Thus, nom. m. ⟨catvāras⟩, acc. m. ⟨caturas⟩; nom.-acc. n. ⟨catvāri⟩; instr. ⟨caturbhis⟩ etc. Fem.: nom.-acc. ⟨catasras⟩, instr., etc., ⟨catasṛbhis⟩, ⟨catasṛbhyas⟩, ⟨catasṛṇām⟩, ⟨catasṛṣu⟩.
¶ (5–19.) These numbers have no distinction of gender. They are inflected with some irregularity as plurals. Thus:
¶ 5, 7, 9, 10. ⟨pañca⟩, ⟨pañcabhis⟩, ⟨-bhyas⟩, ⟨pañcānām⟩, ⟨pañcasu⟩. ⟨sapta⟩, ⟨nava⟩, ⟨daśa⟩, and compounds of ⟨daśa⟩, are similarly declined.
¶ 6. ⟨ṣaṣ⟩ as follows: ⟨ṣaṭ⟩, ⟨ṣaḍbhis⟩, ⟨ṣaḍbhyas⟩, ⟨ṣaṇṇām⟩, ⟨ṣaṭsu⟩.
¶ 8. ⟨aṣṭa⟩ may follow ⟨pañca⟩, or be declined thus: ⟨aṣṭau⟩, ⟨aṣṭābhis⟩, ⟨-bhyas⟩, ⟨aṣṭānām⟩, ⟨aṣṭāsu⟩.
¶ 20, 30, etc. ⟨viṃśati⟩, ⟨triṃśat⟩, etc., are declined regularly as fem. stems, in all numbers.
¶ 100, 1000. ⟨śata⟩ and ⟨sahasra⟩ are declined regularly as neut. stems, in all numbers.
¶ **333. Construction of numerals.** 1. The words from 1 to 19 are used as adjectives, agreeing in case (and in gender, if possible)
@147
¶ with the nouns. 2. The numerals above 19 are usually treated as nouns, either taking the numbered noun as a dependent genitive, or standing in the sing. in apposition with it; thus, ⟨śataṃ dāsīnām⟩ or ⟨śataṃ dāsyaḥ⟩ “a hundred female slaves”; ⟨ṣaṣṭyāṃ śaratsu⟩ “in sixty autumns”.
¶ **334. Ordinals.** ⟨prathama⟩∗ ‘first’, ⟨dvitīya⟩, ⟨tṛtīya⟩, ⟨caturtha⟩, ⟨pañcama⟩, ⟨ṣaṣṭha⟩, ⟨saptama⟩, ⟨aṣṭama⟩, ⟨navama⟩, ⟨daśama⟩, ⟨ekādaśa⟩ (to 19th, the same as the cardinals, but declined like ⟨deva⟩, etc.); ⟨viṃśa⟩ or ⟨viṃśatitama⟩ 20th; ⟨triṃśa⟩ or ⟨triṃśattama⟩ 30th, etc. Note also ⟨ekonaviṃśa⟩ or ⟨ūnaviṃśa⟩, ⟨ekonaviṃśatitama⟩ or ⟨ūnaviṃśatitama⟩, 19th. The shorter forms (⟨viṃśa⟩ etc.) are by far the commoner.
¶ **335.** ⟨prathama⟩, ⟨dvitīya⟩ and ⟨tṛtīya⟩ make their fem. in ⟨ā⟩; the rest, in ⟨ī⟩. Occasional forms of the pronominal declension are met with from the first three; but the usual declension of nouns is the normal one for ordinals also.
¶ **336. Numeral adverbs.** 1. ⟨sakṛt⟩ ‘once’; ⟨dvis⟩ ‘twice’; ⟨tris⟩ ‘thrice’; ⟨catus⟩ ‘four times’; ⟨pañcakṛtvas⟩ or ⟨pañcavāram⟩ ‘five times’; and so on, with ⟨-kṛtvas⟩ or ⟨-vāram⟩. — 2. ⟨ekadhā⟩ ‘in one way’; ⟨dvidhā⟩ or ⟨dvedhā⟩ ‘in two ways’; ⟨tridhā⟩ or ⟨tredhā⟩, ⟨caturdhā⟩, ⟨pañcadhā⟩, ⟨ṣoḍhā⟩ or ⟨ṣaḍdhā⟩, etc. — 3. ⟨ekaśas⟩ ‘one by one’; ⟨śataśas⟩ ‘by hundreds’, etc.
¶ ∗ ⟨eka⟩ forms no ordinal.

E 21 | Sometimes 33 gods are reckoned in the Veda, sometimes 3333. | vede kvacit trayastriṃśad devā gaṇyante kvacit trīṇi sahasrāṇi trīṇi śatāni trayastriṃśac ca | వేదంలో కొన్నిసార్లు ముప్పై మూడు దేవతలు లెక్కించబడతారు, కొన్నిసార్లు మూడువేల మూడువందల ముప్పై మూడు.
