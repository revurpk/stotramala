! Perry’s āi and āu are written ai and au; the accent marks that he prints under the vocabulary forms (aśnāti, krīṇāti …) are not carried. The paradigm of krī is given cell by cell with the cerebral ṇ his Devanāgarī shows after r (krīṇā-, krīṇī-); in the imperfect middle he prints a circle (°) for the part that repeats the form above (°ṇi, °ṇīvahi …); it is written out in full here. In § 400 he gives the weak stem as krīṇī and the form before a vowel as krīṇ.
! § 401: the examples of the 2nd singular imperative in āna are badhāna, aśāna, stabhāna, gṛhāṇa; the first and the third differ from the usual badhnīhi, stabhnīhi, as Perry says. § 402: pū makes punāti, punīte, and grah is weakened to gṛh (gṛhṇāti). § 405 footnote: the ending us in the 3rd pl. impf. is also allowed in a few roots in consonants: 1vid “know”, cakṣ, dviṣ, duh, mṛj. The paradigm of yā puts the indicative and imperfect side by side, and the imperative and optative side by side; they are given as four tables here.
! Footnotes to the vocabulary (añjali: the two hands hollowed and opened, and raised to the forehead; adyatana: with the suffix tana, sometimes tya, are made adjectives from adverbs, especially of time; thus pratna “ancient”, prātastana “early”, śvastana “of the morrow”) are given whole. In Vocabulary XXXVII Perry marks the numbering of homophones before the root: 2aś (eat), 2pā (protect), 2luṭh, 2vṛ (choose), 1vara (suitor) and 2vara (choice).
! ## Lesson XXXVII
¶ **399. Verbs. ⟨nā⟩-class.** The class-sign is in the strong forms the syllable ⟨nā⟩ [⟨ṇā⟩], accented, which is added to the root; in the weak forms it is ⟨nī⟩ [⟨ṇī⟩]; but before an initial vowel of an ending the ⟨ī⟩ of ⟨nī⟩ [⟨ṇī⟩] disappears altogether.
¶ **400.** Thus, ⟨krī⟩ ‘buy’: strong stem ⟨krīṇā⟩, weak ⟨krīṇī⟩ (before a vowel, ⟨krīṇ⟩).

¶ **401.** The ending of the 2nd sing. imv. act. is ⟨hi⟩, never ⟨dhi⟩; and there are no examples of its omission. But roots of this class
@176
¶ ending in a consonant substitute for both class-sign and ending in this person the peculiar ending ⟨āna⟩; thus, ⟨badhāna⟩, ⟨aśāna⟩, ⟨stabhāna⟩, ⟨gṛhāṇa⟩ (see §§ 402, 403).
¶ **402.** The roots ending in ⟨ū⟩ shorten that vowel before the class-sign; thus, ⟨pū⟩, ⟨punāti⟩, ⟨punīte⟩. The root ⟨grah⟩ is weakened to ⟨gṛh⟩; thus, ⟨gṛhṇāti⟩.
¶ **403.** A few roots which have a nasal in some forms outside the present-system, lose it in the present; thus, ⟨grath⟩ or ⟨granth⟩, ⟨grathnāti⟩; ⟨bandh⟩, ⟨badhnāti⟩; ⟨stabh⟩ or ⟨stambh⟩, ⟨stabhnāti⟩. Similarly, ⟨jñā⟩ makes ⟨jānāti⟩.
¶ **404. Root-class.** In this class there is no class-sign; the root itself is also present-stem, and to it are added directly the personal-endings; in the opt. (and subj.: § 60, end) of course combined with the mode-sign. The root-vowel takes *guṇa*, if capable of it, in the strong forms.
¶ **405. Roots ending in vowels.** Roots in ⟨ā⟩ of this class are inflected only in the active. In the 3rd pl. impf. act. they may optionally take as ending ⟨us⟩ instead of ⟨an⟩, the ⟨ā⟩ being lost before it.∗

E 13 | He who receives (*part.*) gifts from every one is polluted (⟨duṣ⟩). | yaḥ sarvasmāt pratigṛhṇan dānāni sa duṣyati | ప్రతి ఒక్కరి నుండి దానాలు స్వీకరించేవాడు కలుషితమవుతాడు.
E 14 | The Creator formed the world by his will (⟨svecchayā⟩) alone. | dhātā svecchayaiva jagad niramāt | సృష్టికర్త తన సంకల్పం చేతనే లోకాన్ని నిర్మించాడు.
E 15 | Betake thyself (⟨yā⟩) for salvation to the gods’ protection. | mokṣāya devānāṃ śaraṇaṃ yāhi | ముక్తి కోసం దేవతల శరణు పొందు.
E 16 | Let kings restrain the wicked by punishments. | nṛpā daṇḍaiḥ pāpān nigṛhṇantu | రాజులు శిక్షలతో దుష్టులను అదుపు చేయుదురు గాక.
E 17 | We saw Rāma’s daughter coming out (*pres. part.*) of the house. | vayaṃ rāmasya duhitaraṃ gṛhān niryāntīm apaśyāma | మేము రాముని కూతురు ఇంటి నుండి బయటికి వస్తుండగా చూశాము.
E 18 | Let the bridegroom grasp the maiden’s hand before the fire. | varo 'gneḥ samakṣaṃ kanyāyāḥ pāṇiṃ gṛhṇātu | వరుడు అగ్ని సాక్షిగా కన్య చేతిని పట్టుకొనుగాక.
E 19 | An Aryan must not eat another’s leavings (⟨ud-śiṣ⟩, *pass. part.*, *neut. sing.*). | dvijo 'nyasyocchiṣṭaṃ nāśnīyāt | ద్విజుడు ఇతరుల ఎంగిలిని తినకూడదు.
E 20 | One must bathe daily in unconfined (*part. from* ⟨ni-rudh⟩) water. | pratidinam aniruddhe jale snāyāt | ప్రతిరోజూ అడ్డులేని నీటిలో స్నానం చేయాలి.
E 21 | May the three-eyed *god*, the great-lord (⟨īśvara⟩) whose-forehead-is-adorned-with-the-crescent, protect you. | trinetro devo maheśvaraḥ kalālaṅkṛtalalāṭas tvāṃ pātu | మూడు కన్నుల దేవుడు, చంద్రకళతో అలంకరించిన నుదురు గల మహేశ్వరుడు నిన్ను కాపాడుగాక.
