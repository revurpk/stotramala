## Lesson XXXV
¶ **383. First Conjugation of Verbs. Present System.∗∗∗** In this conjugation the optative act., the 2nd sing. imv. act., and the 3rd pl. mid., are formed otherwise than in the ⟨a⟩-conjugation.
¶ **384. Strong forms.** The forms in which the stem assumes its strong form are these: the three persons sing. of the pres. and impf. indic. act., all first persons of the imv., act. and mid., and the 3rd sing. imv. act. All other forms of the present system are weak.
¶ **385. Endings.** For the middle endings ⟨ante⟩, ⟨anta⟩, and ⟨antām⟩ are substituted ⟨ate⟩, ⟨ata⟩, and ⟨atām⟩; and after reduplicated stems (and a few others) ⟨ati⟩, ⟨atu⟩, and ⟨us⟩ are substituted for the active endings ⟨anti⟩, ⟨antu⟩, and ⟨an⟩ (impf.).
@167
¶ The 2nd sing. imv. often takes the ending ⟨hi⟩ or ⟨dhi⟩. Otherwise the endings are the same as in the ⟨a⟩-conjugation.
¶ **386. Optative mode-sign.** The sign of the opt. act. is ⟨yā⟩, with secondary endings; but ⟨us⟩ is the ending in the 3rd pl., and ⟨ā⟩ is dropped before it; thus, ⟨-yus⟩.
¶ **387. Present participle middle.** In the first conjugation this participle is made with the suffix ⟨āna⟩ [⟨āṇa⟩], before which the stem takes the same form as before the 3rd pl. pres. ind. The fem. is always in ⟨ā⟩.
¶ **388. Class IV. A: ⟨nu⟩-class.** The present-stem is made by adding to the root the syllable ⟨nu⟩ [⟨ṇu⟩], in strong forms ⟨no⟩ [⟨ṇo⟩]. The ⟨u⟩ of the class-sign may be dropped before ⟨v⟩ and ⟨m⟩ of the 1st du. and 1st pl. endings, except when the root ends in a consonant; and the ⟨u⟩ before a vowel-ending becomes ⟨v⟩ or ⟨uv⟩, according as it is preceded by one or by two consonants. The ending ⟨hi⟩ of the 2nd sing. imv. is dropped if the root end in a vowel.
¶ **389. I. Roots in vowels.** ⟨su⟩ ‘press’.
