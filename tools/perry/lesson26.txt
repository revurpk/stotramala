## Lesson XXVI
¶ **273. Some Irregular Substantives.** ⟨ambā⟩ f., ‘mother’: voc. sing. ⟨amba⟩.
¶ **274.** 1. ⟨sakhi⟩ m., ‘friend’: sing. nom. ⟨sakhā⟩, acc. ⟨sakhāyam⟩, inst.
@125
¶ ⟨sakhyā⟩, dat. ⟨sakhye⟩, abl.-gen. ⟨sakhyus⟩, loc. ⟨sakhyau⟩, voc. ⟨sakhe⟩; du. ⟨sakhāyau⟩, ⟨sakhibhyām⟩, ⟨sakhyos⟩; pl. nom. ⟨sakhāyas⟩, acc. ⟨sakhīn⟩, instr. ⟨sakhibhis⟩; the rest like ⟨agni⟩. — 2. ⟨pati⟩ m., is declined regularly (like ⟨agni⟩) in composition, and when meaning ‘lord, master’; when meaning ‘husband’, it follows ⟨sakhi⟩ in the following forms: inst. sing. ⟨patyā⟩, dat. ⟨patye⟩, abl.-gen. ⟨patyus⟩, loc. ⟨patyau⟩.
¶ **275.** The neuter stems ⟨akṣan⟩ ‘eye’, ⟨asthan⟩ ‘bone’, ⟨dadhan⟩ ‘curds’, ⟨sakthan⟩ ‘thigh’, form only the weakest cases; thus, ⟨akṣṇā⟩, ⟨dadhnas⟩, ⟨sakthni⟩ or ⟨sakthani⟩, etc.; the rest of the inflection is made from corresponding stems in ⟨i⟩; thus, nom. sing. ⟨akṣi⟩ etc.
¶ **276.** 1. ⟨lakṣmī⟩ f., ‘goddess of fortune’, makes nom. sing. ⟨lakṣmīs⟩. — 2. ⟨strī⟩ f., ‘woman’, follows a mixed declension; thus, nom. sing. ⟨strī⟩, acc. ⟨striyam⟩ or ⟨strīm⟩, instr. ⟨striyā⟩, dat. ⟨striyai⟩, abl.-gen. ⟨striyās⟩, loc. ⟨striyām⟩; du. ⟨striyau⟩, ⟨strībhyām⟩, ⟨striyos⟩; pl. nom. ⟨striyas⟩, acc. ⟨striyas⟩ or ⟨strīs⟩, instr. ⟨strībhis⟩, etc.; gen. ⟨strīṇām⟩.
¶ **277.** 1. ⟨ap⟩ f., ‘water’, only pl.; its final is changed to ⟨d⟩ before ⟨bh⟩; thus, nom. ⟨āpas⟩, acc. ⟨apas⟩, instr. ⟨adbhis⟩, dat.-abl. ⟨adbhyas⟩, gen. ⟨apām⟩, loc. ⟨apsu⟩. — 2. ⟨div⟩ f.,∗ ‘sky’, makes nom. sing. ⟨dyaus⟩, du. ⟨dyāvau⟩, pl. (sometimes) ⟨dyāvas⟩; the endings are the normal ones, but the root becomes ⟨dyu⟩ before consonant-endings: thus, acc. sing. ⟨divam⟩, nom.-acc. pl. ⟨divas⟩, instr. ⟨dyubhis⟩. Not all the cases are found in use. — 3. Stem ⟨rai⟩ m. (rarely f.), ‘wealth’: sing. ⟨rās⟩, ⟨rāyam⟩, ⟨rāyā⟩ etc.; du. ⟨rāyau⟩, ⟨rābhyām⟩, ⟨rāyos⟩; pl. ⟨rāyas⟩ (nom. and acc.), ⟨rābhis⟩ etc.
¶ **278.** 1. ⟨anaḍuh⟩ or ⟨anaḍvah⟩ m., (from ⟨anas⟩ + ⟨vah⟩ ‘cart-drawing’, i. e.) ‘ox’: strong stem ⟨anaḍvāh⟩, mid. ⟨anaḍud⟩, weakest ⟨anaḍuh⟩; nom. sing. ⟨anaḍvān⟩, voc. ⟨anaḍvan⟩. — 2. The stem ⟨panthan⟩ m., ‘road’, makes all the strong cases, with irregular nom. sing. ⟨panthās⟩; the corresponding middle cases are made from ⟨pathi⟩, and the weakest from ⟨path⟩; thus, acc. sing. ⟨panthānam⟩, dat. ⟨pathe⟩, acc. pl. ⟨pathas⟩,
¶ ∗ In the older language oftener masculine.
@126
¶ dat. ⟨pathibhyas⟩. [The stems ⟨manthan⟩ m., ‘stirring-stick’, and ⟨ṛbhukṣan⟩ m., an epithet of Indra, are said to follow ⟨panthan⟩.]
¶ **279.** The stem ⟨puṃs⟩ m., ‘man’, is very irregular. The strong stem is ⟨pumāṃs⟩, mid. ⟨pum⟩, weakest ⟨puṃs⟩. Thus, sing. ⟨pumān⟩, ⟨pumāṃsam⟩, ⟨puṃsā⟩, etc., voc. ⟨pumān⟩; du. ⟨pumāṃsau⟩, ⟨pumbhyām⟩, ⟨puṃsos⟩; pl. ⟨pumāṃsas⟩, ⟨puṃsas⟩, ⟨pumbhis⟩ etc., ⟨puṃsu⟩.
¶ **280.** For the stem ⟨jarā⟩ f., ‘age’, may be substituted in the cases with vowel-endings forms from ⟨jaras⟩ f.; thus, ⟨jarayā⟩ or ⟨jarasā⟩.
¶ **281.** ⟨hṛd⟩ n., ‘heart’, does not make nom.-voc.-acc. of any number (except in composition), these being supplied from ⟨hṛdaya⟩ n.
¶ **282.** The stem ⟨pad⟩ m., ‘foot’, becomes ⟨pād⟩ in strong cases; and, in compounds, in the middle cases also; thus, nom. sing. ⟨pāt⟩, acc. ⟨pādam⟩, instr. ⟨padā⟩, etc. From ⟨dvipad⟩ ‘biped’, acc. sing. ⟨dvipādam⟩, pl. ⟨dvipadas⟩, instr. pl. ⟨dvipādbhis⟩. [The stem ⟨pāda⟩ m., ‘foot’, has the complete declension of ⟨a⟩-stems.]
¶ **283.** The root ⟨han⟩ ‘slay’, as final member of a compound, becomes ⟨hā⟩ in nom. sing., and loses its ⟨n⟩ in the middle cases and its ⟨a⟩ in the weakest cases (but only optionally in loc. sing.). Further, when ⟨a⟩ is lost, ⟨h⟩ in contact with ⟨n⟩ reverts to its original ⟨gh⟩; thus, ⟨brahmahan⟩ m., ‘killing a Brāhman’, makes nom. sing. ⟨brahmahā⟩, acc. ⟨-haṇam⟩∗, instr. ⟨brahmaghnā⟩, etc., loc. ⟨brahmaghni⟩ or ⟨-haṇi⟩, voc. ⟨-han⟩; du. ⟨brahmahaṇau⟩, ⟨-habhyām⟩ etc.; pl. nom. ⟨-haṇas⟩, acc. ⟨-ghnas⟩.
¶ **284.** The stems ⟨pūṣan⟩ m., *n. pr.*, and ⟨aryaman⟩ m., *n. pr.* (both personifications of the sun), make the nom. sing. in ⟨ā⟩, but otherwise do not lengthen the ⟨a⟩; thus, nom. ⟨pūṣā⟩, acc. ⟨pūṣaṇam⟩, instr. ⟨pūṣṇā⟩.
¶ ∗ In compound words, an altering cause in one member sometimes lingualizes a ⟨n⟩ of the next following member. But a guttural or labial in direct combination with ⟨n⟩ sometimes prevents the combination, as in the instr. ⟨brahmaghnā⟩.

S a | patyau bhaktir vrataṃ strīṇām adroho mantriṇāṃ vratam | For wives devotion to the husband is the vow; for ministers faithfulness is the vow; | భార్యలకు భర్తపట్ల భక్తి వ్రతం; మంత్రులకు విశ్వాసమే వ్రతం;
