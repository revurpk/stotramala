¶ **246. Declension of Consonant-stems, cont’d. Stems in palatals, etc.** 1. Final ⟨c⟩ of a stem reverts to the original guttural when it comes to stand as word-final, and before the *pada*-endings, becoming ⟨k⟩ when final, and before ⟨su⟩, and ⟨g⟩ before ⟨bh⟩. 2. The ⟨s⟩ of ⟨su⟩ becomes ⟨ṣ⟩ after ⟨k⟩;
