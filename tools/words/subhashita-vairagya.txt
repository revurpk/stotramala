# Bhartṛhari, Vairāgyaśatakam — word by word.
1 cūḍa-uttaṃsita-candra-cāru-kalikā-cañcat-śikhā-bhāsvaraḥ = shining with the quivering crest, a lovely crescent-bud set on his head | līlā-dagdha-vilola-kāma-śalabhaḥ = who in play burned Love, the fluttering moth | śreyaḥ-daśā-agre sphuran = shining at the head of the states of bliss | antaḥ-sphūrjat-apāra-moha-timira-prāgbhāram uccāṭayan = uprooting the heavy darkness of boundless delusion thundering within | śvetaḥ-sadmani yoginām = in the white house (heart) of yogis | vijayate = triumphs | jñāna-pradīpaḥ = the lamp of knowledge | haraḥ = Śiva
2 bhrāntam = I have wandered | deśam aneka-durga-viṣamam = in lands rough with many fortresses | prāptam na kiñcit phalam = and gained no fruit | tyaktvā = giving up | jāti-kula-abhimānam = pride of birth and family | ucitam = fittingly | sevā kṛtā niṣphalā = service was done in vain | bhuktam = I have eaten | māna-vivarjitam = without honour | para-gṛheṣu = in others’ houses | āśaṅkayā kāka-vat = fearfully like a crow | tṛṣṇe = O Craving | jṛmbhasi = you spread | pāpa-karma-piśune = you tempter to sinful deeds | na adya api santuṣyasi = even now you are not content
3 utkhātam nidhi-śaṅkayā kṣiti-talam = I dug up the earth in hope of treasure | dhmātāḥ girēḥ dhātavaḥ = I smelted the ores of mountains | nistīrṇaḥ saritām patiḥ = I crossed the ocean | nṛpatayaḥ yatnena santoṣitāḥ = I pleased kings with effort | mantra-ārādhana-tatpareṇa manasā = with a mind set on propitiating mantras | nītāḥ śmaśāne niśāḥ = I spent nights in the cremation ground | prāptaḥ kāṇa-varāṭakaḥ api na mayā = I did not get even a cowrie with a hole | tṛṣṇe = O Craving | sakāmā bhava = be satisfied
4 khala-ālāpāḥ sauḍhāḥ = the talk of the wicked was endured | katham api = somehow | tat-ārādhana-paraiḥ = by those intent on pleasing them | nigṛhya antar-bāṣpam = holding back tears within | hasitam api śūnyena manasā = even laughing with an empty mind | kṛtaḥ vitta-stambha-pratihata-dhiyām añjaliḥ = I folded my hands to those whose minds were dulled by pride of wealth | tvam āśe moghāśe = you, Hope, vain hope | kim aparam ataḥ = what more | nartayasi mām = do you make me dance
5 amīṣām prāṇānām = for these breaths | tulita-visinī-patra-payasām = that are like water on a lotus leaf | kṛte = for the sake of | kim na asmābhiḥ vyavasitam = what have we not done | vigalita-vivekaiḥ = having lost discernment | yat āḍhyānām agre = that before the rich | draviṇa-mada-niḥsaṃjña-manasām = whose minds are senseless with pride of wealth | kṛtam mā avavrīḍaiḥ = we shamelessly did | nija-guṇa-kathā-pātakam api = even the sin of speaking of our own virtues
6 kṣāntam na kṣamayā = we have endured, but not from forbearance | gṛha-ucita-sukham tyaktam = we gave up the comfort of home | na santoṣataḥ = not from contentment | soḍhaḥ = was borne | duḥsaha-śīta-tāpa-pavana-kleśaḥ = the hard suffering of cold, heat and wind | na taptam tapaḥ = but no austerity was practised | dhyātam vittam = wealth was meditated on | aharniśam = day and night | nitya-mita-prāṇaiḥ = with our fixed measure of breaths | na śambhoḥ padam = not the feet of Śiva | tat tat karma kṛtam = we did all the deeds | yat eva munibhiḥ = which the sages did | taiḥ taiḥ phalaiḥ vañcitāḥ = but we were cheated of their fruits
7 bhogāḥ na bhuktāḥ = pleasures were not enjoyed | vayam eva bhuktāḥ = we were consumed | tapaḥ na taptam = austerity was not practised | vayam eva taptāḥ = we were burned | kālaḥ na yātaḥ = time did not go | vayam eva yātāḥ = we went | tṛṣṇā na jīrṇā = craving did not age | vayam eva jīrṇāḥ = we aged
8 balibhiḥ = with wrinkles | mukham ākrāntam = the face is overrun | palitena aṅkitam śiraḥ = the head marked with grey | gātrāṇi śithilāyante = the limbs go slack | tṛṣṇā ekā taruṇāyate = craving alone grows young
9 viveka-vyākośe = when discernment blossoms | vidadhati same = and brings equanimity | śāmyati tṛṣā = thirst quiets | pariṣvaṅge tuṅge = when the embrace is high | prasarati-tarām sā pariṇatā = it spreads yet more, ripened | jarā-jīrṇa-aiśvarya-grasana-gahana-ākṣepa-kṛpaṇaḥ = wretched in the deep complaint at its wealth devoured by age | tṛṣā-pātram = the vessel of thirst | yasyām bhavati = in whom there is | marutām api adhipatiḥ = even a lord of the gods (is the pitiable one)
10 nivṛttā bhoga-icchā = the desire for enjoyment has gone | puruṣa-bahu-mānaḥ api galitaḥ = even the regard of men has slipped | samānāḥ svaryātāḥ = my equals have gone to heaven | sapadi = quickly | suhṛdaḥ jīvita-samāḥ = friends dear as life | śanaiḥ yaṣṭi-utthānam = I rise slowly with a staff | ghana-timira-ruddhe ca nayane = and my eyes are blocked by thick darkness | aho = ah | mūḍhaḥ kāyaḥ = foolish body | tat api = even so | maraṇa-apāya-cakitaḥ = it is frightened of death’s harm
11 āśā nāma nadī = Hope is a river | manoratha-jalā = its water desires | tṛṣṇā-taraṅga-ākulā = troubled by waves of craving | rāga-grāhavatī = full of the crocodiles of passion | vitarka-vihagā = its birds, doubts | dhairya-druma-dhvaṃsinī = destroying the trees of steadiness | moha-āvarta-su-dustara-atigahanā = deep and hard to cross for the whirlpools of delusion | prottuṅga-cintā-taṭī = with high banks of worry | tasyāḥ = of it | param-gatāḥ = who have gone to the far shore | viśuddham alasāḥ nandanti = the pure and unhurried rejoice | yogi-īśvarāḥ = the lords of yogis
12 na saṃsāra-utpannam = nothing born in the world | caritam = conduct | anupaśyāmi kuśalam = do I see as good | vipākaḥ puṇyānām = the ripening of merits | janayati bhayam me = brings fear to me | vimṛśataḥ = as I reflect | mahadbhiḥ puṇya-oghaiḥ = by great floods of merit | cira-parigṛhītāḥ ca viṣayāḥ = and sense-objects long taken up | mahāntaḥ jāyante = grow great | vyasanam iva dātum = as if to give a calamity | viṣayiṇām = to the sensualists
13 avaśyam yātāraḥ = bound to go | cirataram uṣitvā api = though they have stayed very long | viṣayāḥ = the objects of sense | viyoge ko bhedaḥ = what difference at parting | tyajati na janaḥ = does a man not give up | yat svayam amūn = these himself | vrajantaḥ svātantryāt = going of their own accord | atula-paritāpāya manasaḥ = to the mind’s peerless pain | svayam tyaktāḥ hi ete = but when given up by oneself | śama-sukham anantam vidadhati = they bring endless peace and happiness
14 brahma-jñāna-viveka-nirmala-dhiyaḥ = those whose minds are cleansed by knowledge of Brahman and discernment | kurvanti = do | aho = ah | duṣkaram = the hard thing | yat muñcanti = that they give up | upabhoga-bhāñji api = though enjoyable | dhanāni = riches | ekāntataḥ niḥspṛhāḥ = wholly free of longing | samprāptāt na purā na samprati = neither earlier nor now | na ca prāptau dṛḍha-pratyayān = nor with firm confidence in getting | vāñchā-mātra-parigrahān api = even things held only by wish | param tyaktum na śaktāḥ vayam = we cannot give up
15 dhanyānām = of the fortunate | giri-kandareṣu vasatām = who dwell in mountain caves | jyotiḥ param dhyāyatām = meditating on the supreme light | ānanda-aśru-jalam = the water of tears of bliss | pibanti = drink | śakunāḥ = the birds | niḥśaṅkam = without fear | aṅke-śayāḥ = lying in their laps | asmākam tu = but for us | manoratha-uparacita-prāsāda-vāpī-taṭa-krīḍā-kānana-keli-kautuka-juṣām = delighting in palaces, pond-banks and forest-play built by wishes | āyuḥ param kṣīyate = life only wears away
16 bhikṣā-śatam = a hundred scraps of alms | tat api nīrasam = and even that tasteless | eka-bāram = once a day | śayyā ca bhūḥ = and the ground for a bed | pari-janaḥ nija-deha-mātram = our only attendant our own body | vastram = clothing | viśīrṇa-śata-khaṇḍa-mayī ca kanthā = a patched cloak of a hundred torn bits | hā hā = alas | tathā api = yet | viṣayāḥ na parityajanti = the objects of sense do not leave us
17 stanau māṃsa-granthī = the breasts are lumps of flesh | kanaka-kalaśau iti upamitau = compared to golden pots | mukham śleṣma-āgāram = the face is a house of phlegm | tat api ca śaśāṅkena tulitam = yet it is compared to the moon | sravat-mūtra-klinnam = wet with flowing urine | kari-vara-śiraḥ-spardhi jaghanam = the hips vie with an elephant’s head | muhuḥ nindyam rūpam = a form always to be scorned | kavi-jana-viśeṣaiḥ gurukṛtam = made great by poets
18 ekaḥ rāgiṣu rājate = one shines among the passionate | priyatamā-deha-ardha-hārī haraḥ = Śiva, who took half the body of his beloved | nīrāgeṣu = among the dispassionate | janaḥ = a man | vimukta-lalanā-saṅgaḥ = free of women’s company | na yasmāt paraḥ = none greater than he | durvāra-smara-bāṇa-pannaga-viṣa-vyābiddha-mugdhaḥ = the simple man pierced by the poison of the snake-arrows of irresistible Love | janaḥ śeṣaḥ = the rest of the world | kāma-viḍambitāḥ = mocked by desire | na viṣayān bhoktum na moktum kṣamaḥ = neither able to enjoy the objects of sense nor to leave them
19 ajānan = not knowing | dāhātmyam = the power to burn | patatu śalabhaḥ tīvra-dahane = the moth falls into the fierce fire | saḥ mīnaḥ api = and the fish | ajñānāt = from ignorance | baḍiśa-yutam = with the hook | aśnātu piśitam = eats the bait | vijānantaḥ api ete vayam = though we know | iha = here | viyat-jāla-jaṭilān = (objects) tangled in nets of the sky (of desire) | na muñcāmaḥ kān = do not leave them | ahaha = ah | gahanaḥ moha-mahimā = deep is the power of delusion
20 tṛṣā śuṣyati āsye = thirst dries the mouth | pibati salilam śīta-madhuram = he drinks cool sweet water | kṣudhā-ārtaḥ = tormented by hunger | śālyannam kavalayati = he swallows rice | māṃsa-ādi-kalitam = mixed with meat and the like | pradīpte kāma-agnau = when the fire of desire blazes | su-dṛḍha-taram āliṅgati vadhūm = he embraces his wife very tightly | pratīkāram vyādhaḥ = a cure for the disease | sukham iti viparyasyati janaḥ = men mistake it for happiness
21 tuṅgam veśma = a lofty house | sutāḥ satām abhimatāḥ = sons esteemed by the good | saṅkhyā-atigāḥ sampadaḥ = riches beyond count | kalyāṇī dayitā = a lovely wife | vayaḥ ca navam = and fresh youth | iti ajñāna-mūḍhaḥ janaḥ = so the man deluded by ignorance | matvā viśvam anaśvaram = thinking the world is everlasting | niviśate saṃsāra-kārā-gṛhe = settles in the prison-house of the world | saṃdṛśya = seeing | kṣaṇa-bhaṅguram tat akhilam = all that to be fleeting | dhanyaḥ tu sannyasyati = the fortunate one renounces
22 dīnā = wretched | dīna-mukhaiḥ sadā eva śiśukaiḥ = with her pitiful-faced children always | ākṛṣṭa-jīrṇa-ambarā = tugged at her worn cloth | krośadbhiḥ kṣudhitaiḥ = crying with hunger | nirannavidhurā = distraught for lack of food | dṛśyā na cet gehinī = if the housewife could not bear to be seen | yācñā-bhaṅga-bhayena = for fear of a petition being refused | gadgada-gala-truṭyat-vilīna-akṣaram = with letters breaking and fading in a choking throat | kaḥ dehi iti vadet = who would say “give” | sva-dagdha-jaṭharasya arthe = for the sake of his own cursed belly | manasvī pumān = a man of spirit
23 abhimata-mahā-māna-granthi-prabheda-paṭīyasī = most skilful at cutting the knot of cherished great pride | gurutara-guṇa-grāma-ābhoja-sphuṭa-ujjvala-candrikā = a moonlight that makes the lotus-bed of great virtues clearly bright (to wither) | vipula-vilat-lajjā-vallī-vitāna-kuṭhārikā = an axe to the spreading canopy of the creeper of modesty | jaṭhara-piṭharī = the cooking-pot of the belly | duspurā iyam = hard to fill | karoti viḍambanam = brings mockery
24 puṇye grāme vane vā = in a holy village or forest | mahati = great | sita-paṭa-channa-pālī = with the rim covered by a white cloth | kapālim = a begging-bowl | hi ādāya = taking | nyāya-garbha-dvija-huta-hutabhuk-dhūma-dhūmra-upakaṇṭhe = near the smoke of the sacrificial fires of righteous brahmins | dvāram dvāram praviṣṭaḥ = going from door to door | varam = it is better | udara-darī-pūraṇāya = to fill the cave of the belly | kṣudhā-ārtaḥ = tormented by hunger | mānī prāṇaiḥ sa-nāthaḥ = a proud man, keeping his life | na punaḥ = not again | anudinam = every day | tulya-kulyeṣu dīnaḥ = abject among equals
25 gaṅgā-taraṅga-kaṇa-śīkara-śītalāni = cool with spray of the Gaṅgā’s waves | vidyādhara-adhyuṣita-cāru-śilā-talāni = with lovely rock-slabs dwelt on by Vidyādharas | sthānāni kim himavataḥ pralayam gatāni = have the places of the Himālaya gone to ruin | yat = that | sa-avamāna-para-piṇḍa-ratāḥ manuṣyāḥ = men are fond of others’ morsels with insult
26 kim kandāḥ = what, the roots | kandarebhyaḥ pralayam upagatāḥ = have they vanished from the caves | nirjharāḥ vā giribhyaḥ = or the springs from the mountains | pradhvastāḥ vā tarubhyaḥ = or are the trees destroyed | sarasa-gala-bhṛtaḥ valkalinyaḥ ca śākhāḥ = the juicy-sapped, bark-clad branches | vīkṣyante yat-mukhāni = that one has to look at the faces | prasabham apagata-praśrayāṇām khalānām = of rude wicked men who have lost all courtesy | duḥkha-āpta-svalpa-vitta-smaya-pavana-vaśa-anartita-bhrū-latāni = their brow-creepers made to dance by the wind of pride in the little wealth gained with pain
27 puṇyaiḥ mūla-phalaiḥ = with holy roots and fruits | tathā = and | praṇayinīm vṛttim kuruṣva adhunā = make now a loving livelihood | bhū-śayyām = a bed of earth | nava-pallavaiḥ akṛpaṇaiḥ = of generous fresh shoots | uttiṣṭha yāvaḥ vanam = rise, let us go to the forest | kṣudrāṇām = of petty | aviveka-mūḍha-manasām = those of unthinking foolish mind | yatra īśvarāṇām sadā = where among the lords | vitta-vyādhi-vikāra-vihvala-girām = whose words stumble in the sickness of wealth | nāma api na śrūyate = not even the name is heard
28 phalam sva-icchā-labhyam = fruit got at will | prati-vanam akhedam = in every forest, without toil | kṣiti-ruhām = of trees | payaḥ sthāne sthāne = water in every place | śiśira-madhuram = cool and sweet | puṇya-saritām = of holy rivers | mṛdu-sparśā śayyā = a soft-touching bed | sulalita-latā-pallava-mayī = of lovely creeper-shoots | sahante santāpam = they endure the torment | tat api = yet | dhaninām dvāri kṛpaṇāḥ = the wretched at the rich men’s doors
29 ye vartante = those who stay | dhanapati-puraḥ = before the lord of wealth | prārthanā-duḥkha-bhājaḥ = bearing the pain of begging | ye ca alpatvam dadhati = who show smallness | viṣaya-ākṣepa-paryāpta-buddheḥ = for one whose mind is wholly drawn to objects | teṣām = of them | antaḥ-sphurita-hasitam = the inward laughter | vāsarāṇi smareyam = I would remember the days | dhyāna-cchede = when meditation breaks | śikhari-kuhara-grāva-śayyā-niṣaṇṇaḥ = seated on a bed of stone in a mountain cave
30 ye santoṣa-nirantara-pramuditāḥ = those ever glad in contentment | teṣām na bhinnāḥ mudaḥ = their joys are not different | ye tu anye dhana-lubdha-saṅkala-dhiyaḥ = but those others, minds fixed on greed for wealth | teṣām na tṛṣṇā-hatāḥ = are not struck by craving (the verse is partly obscure) | ittham = so | kasya kṛte kutaḥ saḥ vidhinā kīdṛk padam sampadām = for whom, whence, by fate, of what kind the place of riches | sva-ātmani eva samāpta-hema-mahimā = whose glory of gold is complete in the self | meruḥ na me rocate = even Meru does not please me
31 bhikṣā-āhāram = food got by begging | adainyam = without wretchedness | apratisukham = needing no return | bhīti-chidam sarvataḥ = cutting off fear everywhere | durmātsarya-mada-abhimāna-mathanam = crushing envy, pride and conceit | duḥkha-ogha-vidhvaṃsanam = destroying floods of sorrow | sarvatra anvaham = everywhere and every day | aprayatna-sulabham = easy to get without effort | sādhu-priyam pāvanam = dear to the good, purifying | śambhoḥ satram = the alms-house of Śiva | avāyam akṣaya-nidhim = an imperishable store | śaṃsanti yogi-īśvarāḥ = the lords of yogis declare
32 bhoge roga-mayam = in enjoyment, fear of disease | kule cyuti-bhayam = in family, fear of fall | vitte nṛpālāt bhayam = in wealth, fear of the king | māne dainya-bhayam = in honour, fear of humiliation | bale ripu-bhayam = in strength, fear of enemies | rūpe jarāyāḥ bhayam = in beauty, fear of age | śāstre vādi-bhayam = in learning, fear of debaters | guṇe khala-bhayam = in virtue, fear of the wicked | kāye kṛta-antāt bhayam = in the body, fear of death | sarvam vastu = everything | bhaya-anvitam bhuvi nṛṇām = is full of fear for men on earth | vairāgyam eva abhayam = dispassion alone is free from fear
33 ākrāntam maraṇena janma = birth is overrun by death | jarayā ca ati-ujjvalam yauvanam = and bright youth by age | santoṣaḥ dhana-lipsayā = contentment by greed for wealth | śama-mukham prauḍha-aṅganā-vibhramaiḥ = the face of calm by the wiles of mature women | lokaiḥ matsaribhiḥ guṇāḥ = virtues by envious people | vana-bhuvaḥ vyālaiḥ = forest-lands by beasts of prey | nṛpāḥ durjanaiḥ = kings by the wicked | asthairyeṇa vibhūtayaḥ api apahatāḥ = even splendours are destroyed by instability | grastam na kim kena vā = what is not seized by something
34 ādhi-vyādhi-śataiḥ = by hundreds of mental and bodily ills | janasya vividhaiḥ = of many kinds | ārogyam unmūlyate = health is uprooted | lakṣmīḥ yatra patanti = where wealth falls | tatra vivṛta-dvārāḥ iva vyāpadaḥ = there misfortunes come as if doors were opened | jātam jātam avaśyam = everything born | āśu vivaśam = swiftly, helpless | mṛtyuḥ karoti ātmasāt = death makes his own | tat kim tena niraṅkuśena vidhinā = what then is this uncontrolled Creator for | yat nirmitam su-sthiram = what has he made that is well-founded
35 bhogāḥ tuṅga-taraṅga-bhaṅga-taralāḥ = pleasures are as fickle as the break of high waves | prāṇāḥ kṣaṇa-dhvaṃsinaḥ = lives perish in a moment | stokāni eva dināni = days are few | yauvana-sukham = the joy of youth | sphūrtiḥ priyāsu sthitā = the glow lasting in beloved women | tat saṃsāram asāram eva nikhilam = that whole world is empty | buddhvā = knowing | budhāḥ = O wise | bodhakāḥ = teachers | loka-anugraha-peśalena manasā = with a mind kindly toward the world | yatnaḥ samādhīyatām = let effort be made
36 bhogāḥ = pleasures | megha-vitāna-madhya-vilasat-saudāminī-cañcalāḥ = fickle as lightning flashing amid a canopy of cloud | āyuḥ = life | vāyu-vighaṭṭita-abja-paṭalī-līna-ambu-vat bhaṅguram = as fragile as water clinging to lotus leaves shaken by wind | līlā yauvana-lālasāḥ tanu-bhṛtām = the dalliances and youthful longings of embodied beings | iti ākalayya = so considering | drutam = quickly | yoge = in yoga | dhairya-samādhi-siddhi-sulabhe = easily won by steadiness, absorption and accomplishment | buddhim vidadhvam = set your minds | budhāḥ = O wise
37 āyuḥ kallola-lolam = life is fickle as a wave | katipaya-divasa-sthāyinī yauvana-śrīḥ = the glory of youth stays only a few days | arthāḥ saṅkalpa-kalpāḥ = wealth is like a fancy | ghana-samaya-taḍit-vibhramāḥ bhoga-pūgāḥ = masses of pleasures like the flicker of monsoon lightning | kaṇṭha-āśleṣa-upagūḍham = the embrace around the neck | tat api ca na ciram = that too does not last | yat priyābhiḥ praṇītam = given by beloved women | brahmaṇi āsakta-cittāḥ bhavata = fix your minds on Brahman | bhava-maya-ambhodhi-pāram taritum = to cross to the far shore of the ocean of becoming
