# Ambāṣṭakam — word by word. Long ornate compounds are broken only where the members are clear.
1 ceṭībhavan-nikhila-kheṭī-kadamba-taru-vāṭīṣu = in the kadamba-tree gardens where all the sky-faring women are her attendants | nāki-paṭalī-koṭīra-cāru-tara-koṭī-maṇī-kiraṇa-koṭī-karambita-padā = whose feet are touched by the sparkling rays of the gems on the diadems of throngs of gods | pāṭīra-gandhi-kuca-śāṭī = her bodice scented with sandalwood | kavitva-paripāṭīm = the flow of poetry | agādhipa-sutā = the daughter of the Mountain King | ghoṭī-kulāt = than a herd of horses | adhika-dhāṭīm = surpassing in speed | udāra-mukha-vīṭī-rasena = by the juice of the betel from her gracious mouth | tanutām = may she spread
2 kūlāti-gāmi-bhaya-tūlāvali-jvalana-kīlā = a spark of fire to the cotton-heap of fears that overflow every bank | nija-stuti-vidhā-kolāhala-kṣapita-kālā = who drives Time (Death) away with the clamour of her own praise | amarī = the goddess | kuśala-kīlāla-poṣaṇa-nabhāḥ = a cloud that nourishes the water of well-being | sthūlā kuce = broad in the breast | jaladanīlā kace = dark as a rain-cloud in the hair | kalita-līlā = playful | kadamba-vipine = in the kadamba forest | śūlāyudha-praṇati-śīlā = ever bowing to the bearer of the trident | vibhātu = may she shine | hṛdi = in the heart | śailādhirāja-tanayā = the Mountain King's daughter
3 yatra āśayo lagati = wherever my mind attaches itself | tatra = there | agajā = the Mountain's daughter | vasatu = may she dwell | kutrāpi = anywhere | nistula-śukā = with a matchless parrot | sutrāma-kāla-mukha-satrāśana-prakara-sutrāṇa-kāri-caraṇā = whose feet protect the host of sacrificers, Indra, Yama and the rest | chatra-anila-atiraya-patra-abhirāma-guṇa-mitrā-amarī-sama-vadhūḥ = swifter than the wind in her vehicle, her lovely qualities as friends, a goddess among the Mothers | kutrā-sahat-maṇi-vicitra-ākṛtiḥ = of wondrous form, shining like a jewel | sphurita-putra-ādi-dāna-nipuṇā = skilled in granting sons and the like
4 dvaipāyana-prabhṛti-śāpa-āyudha-tridiva-sopāna-dhūli-caraṇā = whose foot-dust is the stair to heaven made by the sages from Dvaipāyana on | pāpa-apaha-sva-nu-jāpa-anulīna-janatā-āpa-panoda-nipuṇā = skilled at removing the distress of the people absorbed in repeating her mantra | nīpa-ālayā = dwelling in the kadamba grove | surabhi-dhūpa-alakā = her hair sweet with incense | duritakūpāt = from the pit of sin | udañcayatu = may she lift | mām = me | rūpa-adhikā = supremely beautiful | śikhari-bhūpāla-vaṃśa-maṇi-dīpāyitā = a jewel-lamp in the line of the Mountain King | bhagavatī = the glorious goddess
5 yālībhiḥ = with her friends | ātma-tanu-tālī = clapping her hands | sakṛt-priya-kapālīṣu khelati = she plays among the skulls of her dear Śiva | bhaya-vyāla-ina-kulya-sita-cūlī-bharā = terrible to fear like a mongoose to a snake; black-tressed | caraṇa-dhūlī-lasat-muni-varā = on whom the great sages glitter in the dust of her feet | bālī-bhṛti śravasi = in the ear that bears an ornament | tālī-dalaṃ vahati = she wears a palm-leaf | yālī-kaśobhi-tilakā = her mark gleaming on her forehead | sālīkarotu = may she make (the mind) a bee | mama kālī manaḥ = my mind, Kālī | svapada-nālīka-sevana-vidhau = at the service of her lotus feet
6 nyaṅka-ākare vapuṣi = in this body, a mine of worms | kaṅka-ādi-rakta-puṣi = nourished by the blood of kites and the like | kaṅka-ādi-pakṣi-viṣaye = a feast for birds like kites | tvam = you | kāmanā-mayasi kiṃ kāraṇam = why do you crave | hṛdaya = O heart | paṅka-ari-mehi girijām = go for refuge to the destroyer of sin, the Mountain's daughter | śaṅkā-śilā-niśita-ṭaṅkāyamāna-pada-saṃkāśa = whose feet are like chisels sharpening against the stones of doubt | māna-sumanaḥ-jhaṃkāri-bhṛṅga-gatimān-upeta-śaśi-saṃkāśi-vaktra-kamalām = whose lotus face shines like the spotless moon while bees hum around her flowers
7 kumbāvatī-sama-viḍambā galena = her throat in mimicry of a fresh lute-gourd | nava-tumbā-bha-vīṇa-savidhā = near a lute like a new gourd | śam = happiness | bāhuleya-śaśi-bimba-abhirāma-mukha-saṃbādhita-stana-bharā = whose heavy breasts are pressed by the moon-like lovely face of Kārttikeya | ambā = the Mother | kuraṅga-mada-jambāla-rociḥ = shining like musk-paste | iha = here | lambālakā = long-haired | diśatu me = may she give me | bimba-adharā = with lips like bimba fruit | vinata-śambāyudha-ādi-nikurambā = to whom hosts of gods with Indra at their head bow | kadamba-vipine = in the kadamba forest
8 indhāna-kīra-maṇi-bandhā = bound with the shining green gems of her necklace | bhave = in the world | hṛdaya-bandhā-vatī-varasikā = delighted in Śiva, the friend of her heart | saṃdhāvatī = who is pledged | bhuvana-saṃdhāraṇe = to upholding the world | apy amṛta-sindhau = in the ocean of nectar | udāra-nilayā = her lofty dwelling | gandha-anubhāva-muhura-andha-ali-vīta-kaca-bandhā = her braid covered with bees drunk on the scent | samarpayatu me = may she grant me | śaṃ-dhāma = the abode of happiness | bhānum api rundhānam āśu = which swiftly outshines even the sun | pada-saṃdhānam = the joy of her feet | apy aga-sutā = the Mountain's daughter
