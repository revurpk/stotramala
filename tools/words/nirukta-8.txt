# Nirukta, adhyāya 8 — word by word. Etymologies are given as Yāska states them; examples quoted for form are glossed as examples.
1 draviṇodāḥ kasmāt = why “Draviṇodas” | dhanam draviṇam ucyate yat enam abhidravanti = wealth is called draviṇa because people run to it | balam vā draviṇam yat enena abhidravanti = or strength is draviṇa, because by it they run against | tasya dātā draviṇodāḥ = its giver is Draviṇodas | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
2 draviṇodāḥ draviṇasaḥ grāva-hastāsaḥ adhvare = the Draviṇodas, the draviṇa-drinkers, with the pressing-stones in hand at the rite | yajñeṣu devam īḷate = at sacrifices they praise the god | draviṇodāḥ yaḥ tvam = Draviṇodas, you who | draviṇasaḥ iti draviṇa-sādinaḥ iti vā draviṇa-sāninaḥ iti vā = draviṇasaḥ: eaters of wealth, or winners of wealth | draviṇasaḥ tasmāt pibatu iti vā = or “may he drink of that wealth” | yajñeṣu devam īḷate yācanti stuvanti vardhayanti pūjayanti iti vā = at sacrifices they praise the god: they ask, praise, increase, honour | tat kaḥ draviṇodāḥ = who then is Draviṇodas | indraḥ iti krauṣṭukiḥ = Indra, says Krauṣṭuki | saḥ bala-dhanayoḥ dātṛtamaḥ = he is the greatest giver of strength and wealth | tasya ca sarvā bala-kṛtiḥ = and all deeds of strength are his | ojasaḥ jātam uta manye enam iti ca āha = and he says “I think of him as born of strength” | atha api agnim drāviṇodasam āha = also he calls Agni Drāviṇodasa | eṣaḥ punaḥ etasmāt jāyate = he is born again from this | yaḥ aśmanoḥ antaḥ agnim jajāna = who produced fire between the two stones | iti api nigamaḥ bhavati = so too a text | atha api ṛtu-yājeṣu drāviṇodasāḥ pravādāḥ bhavanti = also in the seasonal sacrifices there are Drāviṇodasa recitations | teṣām punaḥ pātrasya indra-pānam iti bhavati = and of their cup there is “Indra’s drinking” | atha api enam soma-pānena stauti = also he praises him with soma-drinking | atha api āha = also he says | draviṇodāḥ pibatu drāviṇodasaḥ iti = may Draviṇodas drink, the Drāviṇodasa | ayam eva agniḥ draviṇodāḥ iti śākapūṇiḥ = this very Agni is Draviṇodas, says Śākapūṇi | āgneyeṣu eva hi sūkteṣu drāviṇodasāḥ pravādāḥ bhavanti = for Drāviṇodasa recitations occur only in hymns of Agni | devāḥ agnim dhārayan draviṇodām = the gods held Agni, the wealth-giver | iti api nigamaḥ bhavati = so too a text | yathā u etat saḥ bala-dhanayoḥ dātṛtamaḥ iti sarvāsu devatāsu aiśvaryam vidyate = as for “he is the greatest giver of strength and wealth”: lordship is found in all the deities | yathā u etat ojasaḥ jātam uta manye enam iti ca āha iti = as for “born of strength, I think him”: | ayam api agniḥ ojasā balena mathyamānaḥ jāyate = this Agni too is born when churned with strength | tasmāt enam āha sahasaḥ putram sahasaḥ sūnum sahasaḥ yahum = therefore he calls him son of strength | yathā u etat agnim drāviṇodasam āha iti = as for “he calls Agni Drāviṇodasa” | ṛtvijaḥ atra draviṇodasaḥ ucyante = here the priests are called draviṇodas | haviṣaḥ dātāraḥ te ca enam janayanti = they are givers of the offering and they generate him | ṛṣīṇām putraḥ adhirājaḥ eṣaḥ = he is the son of seers, the overlord | iti api nigamaḥ bhavati = so too a text | yathā u etat teṣām punaḥ pātrasya indra-pānam iti bhavati iti bhakti-mātram tat bhavati = as for “their cup is Indra’s drinking”: that is only a share | yathā vāyavyāni iti sarveṣām soma-pātrāṇām = as “Vāyavya” for all the soma cups | yathā u etat soma-pānena enam stauti iti asmin api etat upapadyate = as for “he praises him with soma-drinking”: this too fits here | somam piba mandasānaḥ gaṇa-śribhiḥ = drink the soma, rejoicing with the hosts | iti api nigamaḥ bhavati = so too a text | yathā u etat draviṇodāḥ pibatu drāviṇodasaḥ iti asya eva tat bhavati = as for “may Draviṇodas drink”: that is his
3 medyantu te vahnayaḥ yebhiḥ īyase = let your carriers thrive, by which you go | ariṣaṇyan vīḷayasva vanaspate = unharmed, make firm, O lord of the wood | āyūya dhṛṣṇo abhigūrya tvam neṣṭrāt somam draviṇodaḥ piba ṛtubhiḥ = O bold one, approving, from the Neṣṭṛ’s cup, Draviṇodas, drink soma in the seasons | medyantu te = may they thrive for you | vahnayaḥ voḍhāraḥ = vahni: bearers | yaiḥ yāsi ariṣyan dṛḍhībhava = by which you go, unharmed: become firm | āyūya dhṛṣṇo abhigūrya tvam neṣṭrīyāt dhiṣṇyāt = O bold one, approving, from the Neṣṭṛ’s, from the dhiṣṇya | dhiṣaṇaḥ dhiṣṇyaḥ dhiṣaṇā-bhavaḥ = dhiṣaṇa, dhiṣṇya, born of dhiṣaṇā | dhiṣaṇā vāk dhiṣeḥ dadhāti-arthe = dhiṣaṇā is speech, from dhiṣ in the sense of “placing” | dhīsādinī iti vā dhīsāninī iti vā = “eating thought” or “winning thought” | vanaspate iti enam āha = he calls him “lord of the wood” | eṣaḥ hi vanānām pātā vā pālayitā vā = for he is the protector or guardian of woods | vanam vanoteḥ = vana from “to win” | piba ṛtubhiḥ kālaiḥ = drink in the seasons: at the times
4 atha ataḥ āpriyaḥ = now therefore the Āprī hymns | āpriyaḥ kasmāt = why “āprī” | āpnoteḥ prīṇāteḥ vā = from “to obtain” or “to please” | āprībhiḥ āprīṇāti iti ca brāhmaṇam = “he pleases with the āprīs”, says a Brāhmaṇa | tāsām idhmaḥ prathama-āgāmī bhavati = of them Idhma comes first | idhmaḥ samindhanāt = idhma from “kindling” | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
5 samiddhaḥ adya manuṣaḥ duroṇe = kindled today in the house of man | devaḥ devān yajasi jātavedaḥ = you, god, sacrifice to the gods, Jātavedas | ā ca vaha mitra-mahaḥ cikitvān = and bring them, O of Mitra’s might, knowing | tvam dūtaḥ kaviḥ asi pracetāḥ = you are the messenger, the seer, of ready mind | samiddhaḥ adya manuṣyasya manuṣyasya gṛhe = kindled today in each man’s house | devaḥ devān yajasi jātavedaḥ = god, you sacrifice to the gods, Jātavedas | ā ca vaha mitra-mahaḥ = and bring the gods, O of Mitra’s might | cikitvān cetanavān = cikitvān: conscious | tvam dūtaḥ kaviḥ asi pracetāḥ pravṛddha-cetāḥ = you are the messenger, the seer, of grown mind | yajña-idhmaḥ iti kātthakyaḥ = the sacrificial fuel, says Kātthakya | agniḥ iti śākapūṇiḥ = Agni, says Śākapūṇi | tanūnapāt ājyam iti kātthakyaḥ = Tanūnapāt is clarified butter, says Kātthakya | napāt iti ananantarāyāḥ prajāyāḥ nāmadheyam = napāt is a name of a not-remote descendant | nirṇatatamā bhavati = it is the most bent down | gauḥ atra tanūḥ ucyate = the cow is here called tanū | tatāḥ asyām bhogāḥ = enjoyments are spread in her | tasyāḥ payaḥ jāyate = her milk is born | payasaḥ ājyam jāyate = from milk ghee is born | agniḥ iti śākapūṇiḥ = Agni, says Śākapūṇi | āpaḥ atra tanvaḥ ucyante = the waters are here called tanū | tatāḥ antarikṣe = they are spread in the mid-air | tābhyaḥ oṣadhi-vanaspatayaḥ jāyante = from them plants and trees are born | oṣadhi-vanaspatibhyaḥ eṣaḥ jāyate = from plants and trees he is born | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
6 tanūnapāt pathaḥ ṛtasya yānān = O Tanūnapāt, the paths of order, the courses | madhvā samañjan svadayā sujihva = anointing with sweetness, make them sweet, O fair-tongued | manmāni dhībhiḥ uta yajñam ṛndhan = prospering our thoughts by hymns and the sacrifice | devatrā ca kṛṇuhi adhvaram naḥ = and make our rite go to the gods | tanūnapāt pathaḥ ṛtasya yānān yajñasya yānān = Tanūnapāt, the paths of order: the courses of sacrifice | madhunā samañjan svadaya kalyāṇa-jihva = anointing with honey, sweeten, O fair-tongued | mananāni ca naḥ dhībhiḥ yajñam ca samardhaya = and prosper our thoughts by hymns and the sacrifice | devān naḥ yajñam gamaya = bring our sacrifice to the gods | narāśaṃsaḥ yajñaḥ iti kātthakyaḥ = Narāśaṃsa is the sacrifice, says Kātthakya | narāḥ asmin āsīnāḥ śaṃsanti = men seated in it recite | agniḥ iti śākapūṇiḥ = Agni, says Śākapūṇi | naraiḥ praśasyaḥ bhavati = he is praised by men | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
7 narāśaṃsasya mahimānam eṣām = the greatness of Narāśaṃsa among these | upa stoṣāma yajatasya yajñaiḥ = we shall praise by the sacrifices of the worshipful | ye su-kratavaḥ śucayaḥ dhiyaṃ-dhāḥ = who are of good will, pure, upholding thought | svadanti devāḥ ubhayāni havyā = the gods enjoy both kinds of offerings | narāśaṃsasya mahimānam eṣām upastumaḥ yajñiyasya yajñaiḥ = we praise the greatness of Narāśaṃsa, worthy of sacrifice, by sacrifices | ye su-karmāṇaḥ śucayaḥ dhiyam dhārayitāraḥ svadayantu devāḥ ubhayāni havīṃṣi = who are of good deeds, pure, upholders of thought: may the gods sweeten both kinds of offerings | somam ca itarāṇi ca iti vā = soma and the others | tāntrāṇi ca āvāpikāni ca iti vā = or the main ones and the added ones | īḷaḥ īṭṭeḥ stuti-karmaṇaḥ = īḷa from īḍ, a verb of praising | indhateḥ vā = or from “to kindle” | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
8 ā juhvānaḥ īḷyaḥ vandyaḥ ca ā yāhi agne vasubhiḥ sajoṣāḥ = being called, to be praised and honoured, come, Agni, together with the Vasus | tvam devānām asi yahva hotā = you, O great one, are the Hotṛ of the gods | saḥ enān yakṣi iṣitaḥ yajīyān = you, sent forth, the best sacrificer, sacrifice to them | āhūyamānaḥ īḷitavyaḥ vanditavyaḥ ca = being called, to be praised and honoured | āyāhi agne vasubhiḥ saha-joṣaṇaḥ = come, Agni, in company with the Vasus | tvam devānām asi yahva hotā = you, O great one, are the Hotṛ of the gods | yahvaḥ iti mahataḥ nāmadheyam = yahva is a name of “great” | yātaḥ ca hūtaḥ ca bhavati = he is gone and called | saḥ enān yakṣi iṣitaḥ yajīyān = you, sent forth, the best sacrificer, sacrifice to them | iṣitaḥ preṣitaḥ iti vā adhīṣṭaḥ iti vā = iṣita: sent, or requested | yajīyān yaṣṭṛtaraḥ = yajīyas is the better sacrificer | barhiḥ paribarhaṇāt = barhis from “enlarging” | tasya eṣā bhavati = of it there is this verse
9 prācīnam barhiḥ pradiśā pṛthivyāḥ = the grass is spread eastward, along the earth’s direction | vastoḥ asyāḥ vṛjyate agre ahnām = by day it is laid first in the days | vi u prathate vitaram varīyaḥ = it spreads wider and broader | devebhyaḥ aditaye syonam = pleasant for the gods and Aditi | prācīnam barhiḥ pradiśā pṛthivyāḥ vasanāya = the grass eastward along the earth for dwelling | asyāḥ pravṛjyate agre ahnām barhiḥ pūrvāhṇe = of her the grass is laid at the head of days, in the forenoon | tat viprathate vitaram vikīrṇataram iti vā vistīrṇataram iti vā = it spreads wider: more scattered or more extended | varīyaḥ varataram urutaram vā = varīyas: choicer or wider | devebhyaḥ ca aditaye ca syonam = pleasant for the gods and for Aditi | syonam iti sukha-nāma syateḥ = syona is a name of happiness, from “to bind” | avasyanti etat sevitavyam bhavati iti vā = or they end with it, it is to be served | dvāraḥ javateḥ vā dravateḥ vā vārayateḥ vā = dvār from “to speed”, “to run”, or “to restrain” | tāsām eṣā bhavati = of them there is this verse
10 vi acasvatīḥ urviyā vi śrayantām = may the broad ones open wide | patibhyaḥ na janayaḥ śumbhamānāḥ = like wives adorned for their husbands | devīḥ dvāraḥ bṛhatīḥ viśvam-invāḥ = the goddess doors, great, all-pervading | devebhyaḥ bhavata su-prāyaṇāḥ = be well-going for the gods | vyañcanavatyaḥ urutvena viśrayantām = may the opening ones open by their breadth | patibhyaḥ iva jāyāḥ = like wives for husbands | ūrū maithune dharme śuśobhiṣamāṇāḥ = desiring to shine, thighs in the act of union | varatamam aṅgam ūrū = the thigh is the choicest limb | devyaḥ dvāraḥ bṛhatyaḥ mahatyaḥ = goddess doors, great, large | viśvaminvā viśvam ābhiḥ eti yajñe = all-pervading: through these one goes to all in the sacrifice | gṛha-dvāraḥ iti kātthakyaḥ = house-doors, says Kātthakya | agniḥ iti śākapūṇiḥ = Agni, says Śākapūṇi | uṣāsānaktā = Dawn and Night | uṣāḥ ca naktā ca = dawn and night | uṣāḥ vyākhyātā = uṣas has been explained | naktā iti rātri-nāma = naktā is a name of night | anakti bhūtāni avaśyāyena = she anoints beings with dew | api vā naktā avyakta-varṇā = or nakta: of indistinct colour | tayoḥ eṣā bhavati = of the two there is this verse
11 ā su-svayantī yajate upāke = the two sleeping well, worshipful, near | uṣāsānaktā sadatām ni yonau = may Dawn and Night sit in the seat | divye yoṣaṇe bṛhatī su-rukme = the divine women, great, well-golden | adhi śriyam śukra-piśam dadhāne = placing on themselves a bright-formed splendour | seṣmīyamāṇe iti vā suṣvāpayantyau iti vā sīdatām iti vā nyāsīdatām iti vā = or “going forth”, or “putting to sleep”, or “let them sit”, “let them sit down” | yajñiye upakrānte divye yoṣaṇe = the two worshipful ones have approached, the divine women | bṛhatyau mahatyau surukme surocane = great, large, well-golden, well-shining | adhidadhāne śukra-peśasam śriyam = placing on themselves a bright-formed splendour | śukram śocateḥ jvalati-karmaṇaḥ = śukra from śuc, a verb of blazing | peśaḥ iti rūpa-nāma piṃśateḥ = peśas is a name of form, from “to cut out” | vipiśitam bhavati = it is shaped out | daivyā hotārā daivyau hotārau = the two divine Hotṛs | ayam ca agniḥ asau ca madhyamaḥ = this Agni and that middle one | tayoḥ eṣā bhavati = of the two there is this verse
12 daivyā hotārā prathamā suvācā = the two divine Hotṛs, first, of good speech | mimānā yajñam manuṣaḥ yajadhyai = measuring out the sacrifice of man for worship | pracodayantā vidatheṣu kārū = urging on, the makers at the rites | prācīnam jyotiḥ pradiśā diśantā = pointing the eastern light along the direction | daivyau hotārau prathamau su-vācau nirmimānau yajñam manuṣyasya yajanāya = the two divine Hotṛs, first, of good speech, measuring out the sacrifice for man’s worship | pracodayamānau yajñeṣu kartārau = urging, makers in sacrifices | pūrvasyām diśi yaṣṭavyam iti pradiśantau = pointing out in the east “here is to be sacrificed” | tisraḥ devīḥ tisraḥ devyaḥ = the three goddesses | tāsām eṣā bhavati = of them there is this verse
13 ā naḥ yajñam bhāratī tūyam etu = may Bhāratī come quickly to our sacrifice | iḷā manuṣvat iha cetayantī = and Iḷā, here, making aware as for Manu | tisraḥ devīḥ barhiḥ idam syonam = the three goddesses to this pleasant grass | sarasvatī su-apasaḥ sadantu = may Sarasvatī and the skilful ones sit | etu naḥ yajñam bhāratī kṣipram = may Bhāratī come quickly to our sacrifice | bharataḥ ādityaḥ tasya bhāḥ = Bharata is the Sun, Bhāratī his light | iḷā ca manuṣya-vat iha cetayamānā = and Iḷā, here, making aware as for man | tisraḥ devyaḥ barhiḥ idam sukham sarasvatī ca su-karmāṇaḥ āsīdantu = may the three goddesses and Sarasvatī, of good works, sit on this pleasant grass | tvaṣṭā tūrṇam aśnute iti nairuktāḥ = Tvaṣṭṛ reaches quickly, say the etymologists | tviṣeḥ vā syāt dīpti-karmaṇaḥ = or from tviṣ, a verb of shining | tvakṣateḥ vā syāt karoti-karmaṇaḥ = or from tvakṣ, a verb of making | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
14 yaḥ ime dyāvāpṛthivī janitrī = who made these two, heaven and earth, the begetters | rūpaiḥ apiṃśat bhuvanāni viśvā = and shaped all the worlds with forms | tam adya hotaḥ iṣitaḥ yajīyān = him today, O Hotṛ, sent forth, the best sacrificer | devam tvaṣṭāram iha yakṣi vidvān = sacrifice here to the god Tvaṣṭṛ, knowing | yaḥ ime dyāvāpṛthivyau janayitryau rūpaiḥ akarot bhūtāni ca sarvāṇi = who made these two, heaven and earth, the begetters, and all beings with forms | tam adya hotaḥ iṣitaḥ yajīyān devam tvaṣṭāram iha yaja vidvān = him today, O Hotṛ, sent forth, best sacrificer, sacrifice here to the god Tvaṣṭṛ, knowing | mādhyamikaḥ tvaṣṭā iti āhuḥ = they say Tvaṣṭṛ is of the mid-air | madhyame ca sthāne samāmnātaḥ = and he is listed in the middle place | agniḥ iti śākapūṇiḥ = Agni, says Śākapūṇi | tasya eṣā aparā bhavati = of him there is this other verse
15 āviṣṭyaḥ vardhate cāruḥ āsu = the manifest one grows, lovely among these | jihmānām ūrdhvaḥ svayaśāḥ upasthe = upright over the crooked, self-famed in the lap | ubhe tvaṣṭuḥ bibhyatuḥ jāyamānāt = both feared Tvaṣṭṛ as he was born | pratīcī siṃham prati joṣayete = they turn and serve the lion | āviḥ āvedanāt = āvis from “making known” | tatyaḥ vardhate cāruḥ āsu = the spread one grows, lovely among these | cāru carateḥ = cāru from “to move” | jihmam jihīteḥ = jihma from “to go crooked” | ūrdhvam uddhṛtam bhavati = ūrdhva is raised up | svayaśāḥ ātma-yaśāḥ = of self-fame | upasthe upasthāne = in the lap: in the seat | ubhe tvaṣṭuḥ bibhyatuḥ jāyamānāt = both feared Tvaṣṭṛ as he was born | pratīcī siṃham prati joṣayete = they turn toward the lion and serve | dyāvāpṛthivyau iti vā ahorātre iti vā araṇī iti vā = heaven and earth, or day and night, or the two fire-sticks | pratyakte siṃham sahanam pratyāsevete = the two anointed ones serve the enduring one in return
16 vanaspatiḥ vyākhyātaḥ = vanaspati has been explained | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
17 upa ava sṛja tmanyā samañjan = send down, anointing by yourself | devānām pāthaḥ ṛtuthā havīṃṣi = the gods’ food, the offerings in due season | vanaspatiḥ śamitā devaḥ agniḥ = Vanaspati, the Slaughterer, and the god Agni | svadantu havyam madhunā ghṛtena = may they sweeten the offering with honey and ghee | upāvasṛja ātmanā ātmānam samañjan = send down, anointing yourself by yourself | devānām annam ṛtau-ṛtau havīṃṣi kāle kāle = the gods’ food, the offerings at each season, each time | vanaspatiḥ śamitā devaḥ agniḥ iti ete trayaḥ svadayantu havyam madhunā ca ghṛtena ca = these three, Vanaspati, Śamitṛ, and Agni, may sweeten the offering with honey and ghee | tat kaḥ vanaspatiḥ = who then is Vanaspati | yūpaḥ iti kātthakyaḥ = the sacrificial post, says Kātthakya | agniḥ iti śākapūṇiḥ = Agni, says Śākapūṇi | tasya eṣā aparā bhavati = of him there is this other verse
18 añjanti tvām adhvare devayantaḥ = the god-desiring anoint you at the rite | vanaspate madhunā daivyena = O Vanaspati, with divine honey | yat ūrdhvaḥ tiṣṭhāḥ draviṇā iha dhattāt = when you stand upright, set wealth here | yat vā kṣayaḥ mātuḥ asyāḥ upasthe = or when your dwelling is in the lap of this mother | añjanti tvām adhvare devān kāmayamānāḥ vanaspate madhunā daivyena ca ghṛtena ca = desiring the gods, they anoint you at the rite, Vanaspati, with divine honey and ghee | yat ūrdhvam sthāsyasi draviṇāni ca naḥ dāsyasi = when you stand upright and give us wealth | yat vā te kṛtaḥ kṣayaḥ mātuḥ asyāḥ upasthe upasthāne = or when your dwelling is made in the lap of this mother, the seat | agniḥ iti śākapūṇiḥ = Agni, says Śākapūṇi | tasya eṣā aparā bhavati = of him there is this other verse
19 devebhyaḥ vanaspate havīṃṣi hiraṇya-parṇa = Vanaspati, of golden leaf, offerings for the gods | pradivaḥ te artham = from of old, your purpose | pradakṣiṇit raśanayā niyūya = going to the right, binding with the cord | ṛtasya vakṣi pathibhiḥ rajiṣṭhaiḥ = you carry by the straightest paths of order | devebhyaḥ vanaspate havīṃṣi hiraṇya-parṇa ṛta-parṇa = Vanaspati, offerings for the gods, of golden leaf, of order-leaf | api vā upamā-arthe syāt = or it may be in the sense of comparison | hiraṇya-varṇa-parṇa iti = of leaf of golden colour | pradivaḥ te artham purāṇaḥ te saḥ arthaḥ yam te prabrūmaḥ = from of old your purpose: that is the old purpose which we declare to you | yajñasya vaha pathibhiḥ rajiṣṭhaiḥ ṛjutamaiḥ = carry the sacrifice by the straightest paths | rajasvalatamaiḥ tapiṣṭha-tamaiḥ iti vā = or most dusty, or most heated | tasya eṣā aparā bhavati = of him there is this other verse
20 vanaspate raśanayā niyūya = Vanaspati, binding with the cord | piṣṭatamayā vayunāni vidvān = knowing ways, with the most shapely one | vaha devatrā didhiṣaḥ havīṃṣi = carry to the gods the offerings you have set | pra ca dātāram amṛteṣu vocaḥ = and speak of the giver among the immortals | vanaspate raśanayā niyūya surūpa-tamayā = binding with the cord, most beautiful | vayunāni vidvān prajñānāni prajānan = knowing ways, knowing understandings | vaha devān yajñe dātuḥ havīṃṣi = carry the gods to the sacrifice, the giver’s offerings | prabrūhi ca dātāram amṛteṣu deveṣu = and speak of the giver among the immortal gods | svāhā-kṛtayaḥ = the svāhā-offerings | svāhā iti etat su āha iti vā = svāhā: “well said” | svā vāk āha iti vā = or “his own speech says” | svam prāha iti vā = or “he declares his own” | svāhutam haviḥ juhoti iti vā = or “he offers a well-offered oblation” | tāsām eṣā bhavati = of them there is this verse
21 sadyaḥ jātaḥ vi amimīta yajñam = just born, he measured out the sacrifice | agniḥ devānām abhavat purogāḥ = Agni became the leader of the gods | asya hotuḥ pradiśi ṛtasya vāci = at this Hotṛ’s direction, in the word of order | svāhā-kṛtam haviḥ adantu devāḥ = may the gods eat the svāhā-made offering | sadyaḥ jāyamānaḥ niramimīta yajñam = he, just being born, measured out the sacrifice | agniḥ devānām abhavat purogāmī = Agni became the forerunner of the gods | asya hotuḥ pradiśi ṛtasya vāci āsye svāhā-kṛtam haviḥ adantu devāḥ iti yajanti = at this Hotṛ’s direction, in the mouth of order’s word, may the gods eat the svāhā-made offering: so they sacrifice | iti imāḥ āprī-devatāḥ anukrāntāḥ = thus these deities of the Āprī hymns have been gone through | atha kim-devatāḥ prayāja-anuyājāḥ = now, which deities do the Prayājas and Anuyājas have | āgneyāḥ iti eke = Agni’s, say some

waters and the person of the plants | agneḥ ca dīrgham āyuḥ astu devāḥ = and may Agni have long life, O gods | tava prayājāḥ anuyājāḥ ca kevalaḥ ūrjasvantaḥ haviṣaḥ santu bhāgāḥ = may your Prayājas and Anuyājas entire be strong shares of the offering | tava agne yajñaḥ ayam astu sarvaḥ = may this whole sacrifice be yours, Agni | tubhyam namantām pradiśaḥ catasraḥ = may the four directions bow to you | āgneyāḥ vai prayājāḥ āgneyāḥ anuyājāḥ iti ca brāhmaṇam = “the Prayājas are Agni’s, the Anuyājas are Agni’s”, says a Brāhmaṇa | chando-devatāḥ iti aparam = another: they have metres as deities | chandāṃsi vai prayājāḥ chandāṃsi anuyājāḥ iti ca brāhmaṇam = “the Prayājas are metres, the Anuyājas are metres”, says a Brāhmaṇa | ṛtu-devatāḥ iti aparam = another: seasons are the deities | ṛtavaḥ vai prayājāḥ ṛtavaḥ anuyājāḥ iti ca brāhmaṇam = “the Prayājas are seasons, the Anuyājas are seasons” | paśu-devatāḥ iti aparam = another: beasts are the deities | paśavaḥ vai prayājāḥ paśavaḥ anuyājāḥ iti ca brāhmaṇam = “the Prayājas are beasts, the Anuyājas are beasts” | prāṇa-devatāḥ iti aparam = another: breaths are the deities | prāṇāḥ vai prayājāḥ prāṇāḥ vai anuyājāḥ iti ca brāhmaṇam = “the Prayājas are breaths, the Anuyājas are breaths” | ātma-devatāḥ iti aparam = another: the self is the deity | ātmā vai prayājāḥ ātmā vai anuyājāḥ iti ca brāhmaṇam = “the Prayājas are the self, the | yasyai devatāyai haviḥ gṛhītam syāt tām manasā dhyāyet vaṣaṭ-kariṣyan iti ha vijñāyate = “one about to say vaṣaṭ should meditate in mind on the deity for whom the offering is taken”, so it is known | iti imāni ekādaśa āprī-sūktāni = thus these eleven Āprī hymns | teṣām vāsiṣṭham ātreyam vādhryaśvam gārtsamadam iti nārāśaṃsa-vanti = of them the Vāsiṣṭha, Ātreya, Vādhryaśva, Gārtsamada have
