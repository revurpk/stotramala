# Nirukta, adhyāya 7 — word by word. Etymologies are given as Yāska states them; examples quoted for form are glossed as examples.
1 oṃ = om | atha ataḥ daivatam = now therefore the deity-section | tat yāni nāmāni prādhānya-stutīnām devatānām tat daivatam iti ācakṣate = the names of deities praised chiefly they call “daivata” | sā eṣā devatā-upaparīkṣā = this is the examination of deities | yat-kāmaḥ ṛṣiḥ yasyām devatāyām ārtha-patyam icchan stutim prayuṅkte tat daivataḥ saḥ mantraḥ bhavati = whatever the seer wishes, wanting lordship of that matter in whichever deity, and applies praise, that mantra has that deity | tāḥ tri-vidhāḥ ṛcaḥ = those verses are of three kinds | parokṣa-kṛtāḥ pratyakṣa-kṛtāḥ ādhyātmikyaḥ ca = indirect, direct, and of the self | tatra parokṣa-kṛtāḥ sarvābhiḥ nāma-vibhaktibhiḥ yujyante = of them the indirect ones use all case-endings of nouns | prathama-puruṣaiḥ ca ākhyātasya = and the third-person endings of the verb
2 indraḥ divaḥ indraḥ īśe pṛthivyāḥ = Indra rules heaven, Indra earth | indram it gāthinaḥ bṛhat = the singers praise Indra with the Bṛhat | indreṇa ete tṛtsavaḥ veviṣāṇāḥ = by Indra these Tṛtsus prevail | indrāya sāma gāyata = sing a sāman for Indra | na indrāt ṛte pavate dhāma kiṃ cana = nothing purifies its place without Indra | indrasya nu vīryāṇi pra vocam = I will now proclaim Indra’s heroic deeds | indre kāmāḥ ayaṃsata = desires have rested in Indra | iti = thus | atha pratyakṣa-kṛtāḥ madhyama-puruṣa-yogāḥ = now the direct ones have second-person endings | tvam iti ca etena sarvanāmnā = and with the pronoun “you” | tvam indra balāt adhi = you, Indra, above in strength | vi naḥ indra mṛdhaḥ jahi = O Indra, destroy our foes | iti = thus | atha api pratyakṣa-kṛtāḥ stotāraḥ bhavanti = also in the direct ones the praisers are addressed | parokṣa-kṛtāni stotavyāni = while the objects of praise are spoken of indirectly | mā cit anyat vi śaṃsata = do not recite anything else | kaṇvāḥ abhi pra gāyata = O Kaṇvas, sing forth | upa preta kuśikāḥ cetayadhvam = come, Kuśikas, become aware | iti = thus | atha ādhyātmikyaḥ uttama-puruṣa-yogāḥ = now the self-verses have first-person endings | aham iti ca etena sarvanāmnā = and with the pronoun “I” | yathā etat indraḥ vaikuṇṭhaḥ lava-sūktam vāk āmbhṛṇīyam iti = as in Indra Vaikuṇṭha, the Lava hymn, the Vāc Āmbhṛṇī hymn
3 parokṣa-kṛtāḥ pratyakṣa-kṛtāḥ ca mantrāḥ bhūyiṣṭhāḥ = indirect and direct mantras are the most numerous | alpaśaḥ ādhyātmikāḥ = self-verses are few | atha api stutiḥ eva bhavati na āśīr-vādaḥ = also there is only praise, not blessing | indrasya nu vīryāṇi pra vocam iti yathā etasmin sūkte = “I proclaim Indra’s deeds”, as in this hymn | atha api āśīḥ eva na stutiḥ = also there is only blessing, not praise | su-cakṣāḥ aham akṣībhyām bhūyāsam = may I be of good sight with my eyes | su-varcāḥ mukhena = of good splendour with my face | su-śrut karṇābhyām bhūyāsam = of good hearing with my ears | iti = thus | tat etat bahulam ādhvaryave yājñeṣu ca mantreṣu = this is common in the Adhvaryu texts and sacrificial mantras | atha api śapatha-abhiśāpau = also oath and curse | adya murīya yadi yātudhānaḥ asmi = may I die today if I am a sorcerer | adha saḥ vīraiḥ daśabhiḥ vi yūyāḥ = then may he be parted from ten heroes | iti = thus | atha api kasyacit bhāvasya ācikhyāsā = also the wish to tell of some state of being | na mṛtyuḥ āsīt amṛtam na tarhi = there was neither death nor immortality then | tamaḥ āsīt tamasā gūḍham agre = there was darkness, hidden by darkness at first | atha api paridevanā kasmāt cit bhāvāt = also lament over some state | sudevaḥ adya prapatet anāvṛt = may the well-gods fall today unreturning | na vi jānāmi yadi vā idam asmi = I do not know whether I am this | iti = thus | atha api nindā-praśaṃse = also blame and praise | kevala-aghaḥ bhavati kevala-ādī = he becomes sin alone, an eater alone | bhojasya idam puṣkariṇī iva veśma = this house of the generous is like a lotus-pond | evam akṣa-sūkte dyūta-nindā ca kṛṣi-praśaṃsā ca = so in the dice hymn there is blame of gambling and praise of farming | evam uccāvacaiḥ abhiprāyaiḥ ṛṣīṇām mantra-dṛṣṭayaḥ bhavanti = so with various intentions seers have visions of mantras
4 tat ye anādiṣṭa-devatāḥ mantrāḥ teṣu devatā-upaparīkṣā = now in mantras whose deity is not named there is an examination of the deity | yat-devataḥ saḥ yajñaḥ vā yajña-aṅgam vā tat-devatāḥ bhavanti = they have the deity of the sacrifice or part of it | atha anyatra yajñāt prājāpatyāḥ iti yājñikāḥ = outside the sacrifice they are Prājāpatya, say the ritualists | na ārāśaṃsāḥ iti nairuktāḥ = “not Nārāśaṃsa”, say the etymologists | api vā sā kāma-devatā syāt = or it may be a deity of wish | prāyaḥ-devatā vā = or a deity for the most part | asti hi ācāraḥ bahulam loke = for there is a custom common in the world | deva-devatyam atithi-devatyam pitṛ-devatyam = for the gods, for the guest, for the ancestors | yājña-daivataḥ mantraḥ iti = a mantra has a sacrificial deity | api hi adevatāḥ devatā-vat stūyante = even non-deities are praised as deities | yathā aśva-prabhṛtīni oṣadhi-paryantāni = as from the horse down to the plant | atha api aṣṭau dvandvāni = also the eight pairs | saḥ na manyeta āgantūn iva arthān devatānām = let him not think the matters of the gods are extraneous | pratyakṣa-dṛśyam etat bhavati = this is seen directly | māhābhāgyāt devatāyāḥ ekaḥ ātmā bahudhā stūyate = by the greatness of the deity, one self is praised in many ways | ekasya ātmanaḥ anye devāḥ pratyaṅgāni bhavanti = the other gods are limbs of the one self | api ca sattvānām prakṛti-bhūmabhiḥ ṛṣayaḥ stuvanti iti āhuḥ = and they say seers praise by the many natures of beings | prakṛti-sārvanāmnyāt ca = and by the pronominal nature | itaretara-janmānaḥ bhavanti = they are born from each other | itaretara-prakṛtayaḥ = each the nature of the other | karma-janmānaḥ = born of action | ātma-janmānaḥ = born of the self | ātmā eva eṣām rathaḥ bhavati = the self is their chariot | ātmā aśvaḥ ātmā āyudham ātmā iṣavaḥ = the self is the horse, weapon, arrows | ātmā sarvam devasya = the self is all of the god
5 tisraḥ eva devatāḥ iti nairuktāḥ = there are only three deities, say the etymologists | agniḥ pṛthivī-sthānaḥ = Agni has the earth as place | vāyuḥ vā indraḥ vā antarikṣa-sthānaḥ = Vāyu or Indra has the mid-air | sūryaḥ dyu-sthānaḥ = Sūrya has heaven | tāsām māhābhāgyāt ekaikasyāḥ api bahūni nāmadheyāni bhavanti = through their greatness each has many names | api vā karma-pṛthaktvāt = or from difference of action | yathā hotā adhvaryuḥ brahmā udgātā iti api ekasya sataḥ = as Hotṛ, Adhvaryu, Brahman, Udgātṛ, though of one person | api vā pṛthak eva syuḥ = or they may be separate | pṛthak hi stutayaḥ bhavanti tathā abhidhānāni = for the praises are separate, so too the names | yathā u etat karma-pṛthaktvāt iti bahavaḥ api vibhajya karmāṇi kuryuḥ = as for “difference of action”, even many persons would divide and do actions | tatra saṃsthāna-ekatvam saṃbhoga-ekatvam ca upekṣitavyam = there sameness of place and of enjoyment are to be considered | yathā pṛthivyām manuṣyāḥ paśavaḥ devāḥ iti saṃsthāna-ekatvam = as men, beasts and gods on earth — sameness of place | saṃbhoga-ekatvam ca dṛśyate = and sameness of enjoyment is seen | yathā pṛthivyāḥ parjanyena ca vāyu-ādityābhyām ca saṃbhogaḥ = as the earth’s enjoyment with Parjanya, Vāyu and Āditya | agninā ca itarasya lokasya = and with Agni of the other world | tatra etat nara-rāṣṭram iva = in this it is like a kingdom of men
6 atha ākāra-cintanam devatānām = now reflection on the form of the deities | puruṣa-vidhāḥ syuḥ iti ekam = one view: they have the form of a person | cetanā-vat-vat hi stutayaḥ bhavanti tathā abhidhānāni = for praises and names are as of conscious beings | atha api pauruṣa-vidhikaiḥ aṅgaiḥ saṃstūyante = also they are praised with limbs of human kind | ṛṣvā te indra sthavirasya bāhū = your two arms, O Indra, the sturdy, the old | yat saṃ gṛbhṇā maghavan kāśiḥ te = when you seize, O bounteous, your fist | atha api pauruṣa-vidhikaiḥ dravya-saṃyogaiḥ = also with connections to things of human kind | ā dvābhyām haribhyām indra yāhi = come, Indra, with your two bays | kalyāṇī jāyā suraṇam gṛhe te = a good wife, a pleasant one, is in your house | atha api pauruṣa-vidhikaiḥ karmabhiḥ = also with actions of human kind | addhi indra piba ca prasthitasya = eat, Indra, and drink of what is set out | āśrutkarṇa śrudhī havam = O quick-eared one, hear our call
7 apuruṣa-vidhāḥ syuḥ iti aparam = another view: they are not of human form | api tu yat dṛśyate apuruṣa-vidham tat = but what is seen is not of human form | yathā agniḥ vāyuḥ ādityaḥ pṛthivī candramāḥ iti = as Agni, Vāyu, Āditya, earth, moon | yathā u etat cetanā-vat-vat hi stutayaḥ bhavanti iti acetanāni api evam stūyante = as for “praises are as of conscious beings”: even unconscious things are so praised | yathā akṣa-prabhṛtīni oṣadhi-paryantāni = as from the dice down to the plant | yathā u etat pauruṣa-vidhikaiḥ aṅgaiḥ saṃstūyante iti acetaneṣu api etat bhavati = as for “praised with human-like limbs”: this happens even with unconscious things | abhi krandanti haritebhiḥ āsabhiḥ = they roar with their green mouths | iti grāva-stutiḥ = this is a praise of the pressing stones | yathā u etat pauruṣa-vidhikaiḥ dravya-saṃyogaiḥ iti etat api tādṛśam eva = as for “with human-like connections to things” this too is of the same kind | sukham ratham yuyuje sindhuḥ aśvinam = the river has yoked a pleasant chariot with horses | iti nadī-stutiḥ = a praise of a river | yathā u etat pauruṣa-vidhikaiḥ karmabhiḥ iti etat api tādṛśam eva = as for “with human-like actions” this too is of the same kind | hotuḥ cit pūrve haviḥ adyam āśata = even before the Hotṛ they ate the offering | iti grāva-stutiḥ eva = it too is a praise of the pressing stones | api vā ubhaya-vidhāḥ syuḥ = or they may be of both kinds | api vā puruṣa-vidhānām eva satām karma-ātmānaḥ ete syuḥ = or while having human form they may be the selves of action | yathā yajñaḥ yajamānasya = as the sacrifice of the sacrificer | eṣaḥ ca ākhyāna-samayaḥ = and this is the doctrine of narration
8 tisraḥ eva devatāḥ iti uktam purastāt = “only three deities” was said before | tāsām bhakti-sāhacaryam vyākhyāsyāmaḥ = we shall explain the shares and associations of them | atha etāni agni-bhaktīni = now these are Agni’s shares | ayam lokaḥ = this world | prātaḥ-savanam = the morning pressing | vasantaḥ = spring | gāyatrī = Gāyatrī | trivṛt-stomaḥ = the Trivṛt stoma | rathaṃtaram sāma = the Rathantara sāman | ye ca deva-gaṇāḥ samāmnātāḥ prathame sthāne = and the hosts of gods listed in the first place | agnāyī pṛthivī iḷā iti striyaḥ = Agnāyī, Pṛthivī, Iḷā: the feminine ones | atha asya karma = now his action | vahanam ca haviṣām āvāhanam ca devatānām = carrying the offerings and calling the deities | yat ca kiṃcit dārṣṭi-viṣayikam agni-karma eva tat = and whatever concerns the seen is Agni’s action | atha asya saṃstavikāḥ devāḥ = now the gods praised with him | indraḥ somaḥ varuṇaḥ parjanyaḥ ṛtavaḥ = Indra, Soma, Varuṇa, Parjanya, the seasons | āgnāvaiṣṇavam haviḥ na tu ṛk-saṃstavikī daśatayīṣu vidyate = there is an Agni-Viṣṇu offering, but no verse-praise together is found in the ten books | atha api āgnāpauṣṇam haviḥ na tu saṃstavaḥ = also an Agni-Pūṣan offering, but no joint praise | tatra etām vibhakta-stutim ṛcam udāharanti = there they cite this verse of separated praise
9 pūṣā tvā itaḥ cyāvayatu pra vidvān = may Pūṣan, the knowing, move you from here | anaṣṭa-paśuḥ bhuvanasya gopāḥ = of unlost cattle, the guardian of the world | saḥ tvā etebhyaḥ pari dadat pitṛbhyaḥ = he gives you over to these fathers | agniḥ devebhyaḥ suvidatriyebhyaḥ = Agni to the gods of good knowledge | pūṣā tvā itaḥ pracyāvayatu = may Pūṣan move you forth from here | vidvān = knowing | anaṣṭa-paśuḥ = of unlost cattle | bhuvanasya gopāḥ iti eṣaḥ hi sarveṣām bhūtānām gopāyitā ādityaḥ = guardian of the world: for this is the Sun, protector of all beings | saḥ tvā etebhyaḥ paridadat pitṛbhyaḥ iti sāṃśayikaḥ tṛtīyaḥ pādaḥ = “he gives you to these fathers”: the third pāda is doubtful | pūṣā purastāt tasya anvādeśaḥ iti ekam = one view: Pūṣan, mentioned before, is referred to | agniḥ upariṣṭāt tasya prakīrtanā iti aparam = another: Agni, who is named after | agniḥ devebhyaḥ suvidatriyebhyaḥ = Agni to the gods of good knowledge | suvidatram dhanam bhavati = suvidatra is wealth | vindateḥ vā eka-upasargāt = from vid with one prefix | dadāteḥ vā syāt dvi-upasargāt = or from dā with two prefixes
10 atha etāni indra-bhaktīni = now these are Indra’s shares | antarikṣa-lokaḥ = the mid-air world | mādhyaṃdinam savanam = the midday pressing | grīṣmaḥ = summer | triṣṭup = Triṣṭubh | pañcadaśa-stomaḥ = the fifteenfold stoma | bṛhat-sāma = the Bṛhat sāman | ye ca deva-gaṇāḥ samāmnātāḥ madhyame sthāne yāḥ ca striyaḥ = and the hosts of gods listed in the middle place and the feminine ones | atha asya karma rasa-anupradānam vṛtra-vadhaḥ = now his action: giving out juices, slaying Vṛtra | yā ca kā ca bala-kṛtiḥ indra-karma eva tat = whatever deed of strength is Indra’s action | atha asya saṃstavikāḥ devāḥ = now the gods praised with him | agniḥ somaḥ varuṇaḥ pūṣā bṛhaspatiḥ brahmaṇaspatiḥ parvataḥ kutsaḥ viṣṇuḥ vāyuḥ = Agni, Soma, Varuṇa, Pūṣan, Bṛhaspati, Brahmaṇaspati, Parvata, Kutsa, Viṣṇu, Vāyu | atha api mitraḥ varuṇena saṃstūyate = also Mitra is praised with Varuṇa | pūṣṇā rudreṇa ca somaḥ = Soma with Pūṣan and Rudra | vāyunā ca pūṣā = and Pūṣan with Vāyu | vātena ca parjanyaḥ = and Parjanya with the wind
11 atha etāni āditya-bhaktīni = now these are the Sun’s shares | asau lokaḥ = that world | tṛtīya-savanam = the third pressing | varṣāḥ = the rains | jagatī = Jagatī | saptadaśa-stomaḥ = the seventeenfold stoma | vairūpam sāma = the Vairūpa sāman | ye ca deva-gaṇāḥ samāmnātāḥ uttame sthāne yāḥ ca striyaḥ = and the hosts listed in the highest place and the feminine ones | atha asya karma rasa-ādānam raśmibhiḥ ca rasa-dhāraṇam = now his action: taking up juices and holding them with rays | yat ca kiṃcit pravahlitam āditya-karma eva tat = and whatever is shining is the Sun’s action | candramasā vāyunā saṃvatsareṇa iti saṃstavaḥ = his joint praise is with the moon, wind, and year | eteṣu eva sthāna-vyūheṣu ṛtu-chandaḥ-stoma-pṛṣṭhasya bhakti-śeṣam anukalpayīta = within these arrangements of place one may supply the remaining shares of season, metre, stoma, and pṛṣṭha | śarat anuṣṭup ekaviṃśa-stomaḥ vairājam sāma iti pṛthivī-āyatanāni = autumn, Anuṣṭubh, the twenty-one stoma, the Vairāja sāman: the abodes of earth | hemantaḥ paṅktiḥ triṇava-stomaḥ śākvaram sāma iti antarikṣa-āyatanāni = winter, Paṅkti, the twenty-seven stoma, the Śākvara sāman: the abodes of mid-air | śiśiraḥ aticchandāḥ trayastriṃśa-stomaḥ raivatam sāma iti dyu-bhaktīni = cool season, Aticchandas, the thirty-three stoma, the Raivata sāman: the shares of heaven
12 mantrāḥ mananāt = mantras from “thinking” | chandāṃsi chādanāt = metres from “covering” | stomaḥ stavanāt = stoma from “praising” | yajuḥ yajateḥ = yajus from “to sacrifice” | sāma sammitam ṛcā = sāman: measured with the ṛc | asyateḥ vā = or from “to throw” | ṛcā samam mena iti naidānāḥ = “equal to the ṛc in measure”, say the Naidānas | gāyatrī gāyateḥ stuti-karmaṇaḥ = gāyatrī from “to sing”, a verb of praising | tri-gamanā vā viparītā = or “three-going”, reversed | gāyataḥ mukhāt udapatat iti ca brāhmaṇam = “it sprang from the singer’s mouth”, says a Brāhmaṇa | uṣṇik utsnātā bhavati = uṣṇih is “well bathed” | snihyateḥ vā syāt kānti-karmaṇaḥ = or from snih, a verb of loving | uṣṇīṣiṇī vā iti aupamikam = or “turbaned”, by comparison | uṣṇīṣam snāyateḥ = uṣṇīṣa from “to wrap” | kakup kakubhinī bhavati = kakubh has a hump | kakup ca kubjaḥ ca kujateḥ ubjateḥ vā = kakubh and kubja from “to be crooked” or “to press” | anuṣṭup anuṣṭobhanāt = anuṣṭubh from “following up” | gāyatrīm eva tripadām satīm caturthena pādena anuṣṭobhati iti ca brāhmaṇam = “being three-footed, it follows up the Gāyatrī with a fourth foot” | bṛhatī paribarhaṇāt = bṛhatī from “enlarging” | paṅktiḥ pañcapadā = paṅkti is five-footed | triṣṭup stobhati uttarapadā = triṣṭubh: it praises with a further foot | kā tu tritā syāt = but what would be “tritā” | tīrṇatamam chandaḥ = the most crossed metre | trivṛt vajraḥ = the threefold is the thunderbolt | tasya stobhanī iti vā = or it is its praiser | yat trir astobhat tat triṣṭubhaḥ triṣṭuptvam iti vijñāyate = “because it praised three times, that is triṣṭubh-ness”, so it is known
13 jagatī gatatamam chandaḥ = jagatī is the most moving metre | jala-cara-gatiḥ vā = or of the movement of water-dwellers | jalgalyamānaḥ asṛjat iti ca brāhmaṇam = “he created, rippling”, says a Brāhmaṇa | virāṭ virājanāt vā virādhanāt vā viprāpaṇāt vā = virāj from shining, from perfecting, or from reaching | virājanāt saṃpūrṇa-akṣarā = from shining, with full syllables | virādhanāt ūna-akṣarā = from perfecting, with fewer syllables | viprāpaṇāt adhika-akṣarā = from reaching, with more syllables | pipīlika-madhyā iti aupamikam = “ant-waisted”, by comparison | pipīlikā pelateḥ gati-karmaṇaḥ = pipīlikā from “to go” | iti imāḥ devatāḥ anukrāntāḥ = thus these deities have been gone through | sūkta-bhājaḥ havir-bhājaḥ ṛg-bhājaḥ ca bhūyiṣṭhāḥ = mostly sharers of hymns, offerings, verses | kāścit nipāta-bhājaḥ = some sharers of particles | atha uta abhidhānaiḥ saṃyujya haviḥ codayati = also joining with titles he urges the offering | indrāya vṛtra-ghne = to Indra, slayer of Vṛtra | indrāya vṛtra-ture = to Indra, conqueror of Vṛtra | indrāya aṃhomuce iti = to Indra, releaser from distress | tāni api eke samāmananti = some also list these | bhūyāṃsi tu samāmnānāt = but it is more by listing | yat tu saṃvijñāna-bhūtam syāt prādhānya-stuti tat samāmane = what is of known sense, a chief praise, is in the list | atha uta karmabhiḥ ṛṣiḥ devatāḥ stauti = also the seer praises deities by their deeds | vṛtra-hā purandaraḥ iti = Vṛtra-slayer, city-splitter | tāni api eke samāmananti = some list these too | bhūyāṃsi tu samāmnānāt = but more by listing | vyañjana-mātram tu tat tasya abhidhānasya bhavati = but that is merely a mark of that title | yathā brāhmaṇāya bubhukṣitāya odanam dehi = as “give rice to the hungry brahmin” | snātāya anulepanam = ointment to the bathed | pipāsate pānīyam iti = water to the thirsty

25 haviṣpāntam ajaram svarvidi divispṛśi āhutam juṣṭam agnau = the offering-drinker, ageless, finder of light, touching heaven, offered, welcomed in Agni | tasya bharmaṇe bhuvanāya devāḥ dharmaṇe kam svadhayā apaprathanta = for his bearing, for the world, for upholding, the gods spread by their own power | haviḥ yat pānīyam = the offering is what is to be drunk | ajaram sūryavidi divispṛśi abhihutam juṣṭam agnau = ageless, finder of the Sun, touching heaven, offered, welcomed in Agni | tasya bharaṇāya ca bhāvanāya ca dhāraṇāya ca = for his bearing, his coming to be, and his holding | etebhyaḥ sarvebhyaḥ karmabhyaḥ devāḥ imam agnim annena apaprathanta = for all these actions the gods spread this Agni with food | atha api āha = and he also says
26 apām upasthe mahiṣāḥ agṛbhṇata = in the lap of the waters the mighty took him | viśaḥ rājānam upa tasthuḥ ṛgmiyam = the clans stood by the king, worthy of praise | ā dūtaḥ agnim abharat vivasvataḥ = the messenger brought Agni from Vivasvat | vaiśvānaram mātariśvā parāvataḥ = Vaiśvānara, Mātariśvan, from afar | apām upasthe upasthāne mahati antarikṣa-loke āsīnāḥ mahāntaḥ iti vā agṛbhṇata mādhyamikāḥ deva-gaṇāḥ = in the lap of the waters, in the seat, in the great mid-air world, the mid-air hosts of gods, seated, or the mighty, took him | viśaḥ iva rājānam upatasthuḥ = like clans they stood by the king | ṛgmiyam ṛgmantam iti vā arcanīyam iti vā pūjanīyam iti vā = ṛgmiya: possessing ṛc, or worthy of praise, or worthy of honour | aharat yam dūtaḥ devānām vivasvataḥ ādityāt = the messenger of the gods brought him from Vivasvat, the Sun | vivasvān vivāsanavān = vivasvat: possessing radiance | preritavataḥ parāgatāt vā = or from one sent forth, gone far | api vā asya agneḥ vaiśvānarasya mātariśvānam āhartāram āha = or he calls Mātariśvan the bringer of this Agni Vaiśvānara | mātariśvā vāyuḥ = Mātariśvan is the wind | mātari antarikṣe śvasiti = he breathes in the mother, the mid-air | mātari āśvanati iti vā = or he sounds in the mother | atha enam etābhyām sarvāṇi sthānāni abhyāpādam stauti = then with these two he praises him at every place, foot by foot
27 mūrdhā bhuvaḥ bhavati naktam agniḥ = Agni by night is the head of the earth | tataḥ sūryaḥ jāyate prātaḥ udyan = then he is born as the Sun, rising in the morning | māyām u tu yajñiyānām etām apaḥ yat tūrṇiḥ carati prajānan = this is the wisdom of the worthy gods, that, knowing, he moves swiftly through the waters | mūrdhā mūrtam asmin dhīyate = mūrdhan: form is placed in it | mūrdhā yaḥ sarveṣām bhūtānām bhavati naktam agniḥ = the head of all beings is Agni by night | tataḥ sūryaḥ jāyate prātaḥ udyan saḥ eva = then the Sun is born rising in the morning: the same one | prajñām tu etām manyante yajñiyānām devānām yajña-saṃpādinām = but they take this as the wisdom of the worthy gods who accomplish sacrifice | apaḥ yat karma carati prajānan sarvāṇi sthānāni anusaṃcarati tvaramāṇaḥ = the waters, since knowing he does the action, he goes through all places hastening | tasya uttarā bhūyase nirvacanāya = the next verse is for fuller explanation
28 stomena hi divi devāsaḥ agnim ajījanan = by hymn the gods in heaven generated Agni | śaktibhiḥ rodasi-prām = by powers, filling the two worlds | tam u akṛṇvan tredhā bhuve kam = they made him threefold for being | saḥ oṣadhīḥ pacati viśva-rūpāḥ = he ripens plants of all forms | stomena hi yam divi devāḥ agnim ajanayan śaktibhiḥ karmabhiḥ dyāvāpṛthivyoḥ ā pūraṇam = for him whom the gods in heaven generated by hymn, by powers, by works, the filling of heaven and earth | tam akurvan tredhā-bhāvāya = they made him for threefold being | pṛthivyām antarikṣe divi iti śākapūṇiḥ = on earth, in the mid-air, in heaven, says Śākapūṇi | yat asya divi tṛtīyam tat asau ādityaḥ iti hi brāhmaṇam = “his third in heaven is that Āditya”, says a Brāhmaṇa | tat agnī-kṛtya stauti = making that Agni, he praises | atha enam etayā ādityī-kṛtya stauti = then with this he praises him making him the Sun
29 yat īm enam adadhuḥ yajñiyāsaḥ = when the worthy ones set him | divi devāḥ sūryam āditeyam = the gods in heaven, the Sun, son of Aditi | yadā cariṣṇū mithunau abhūtām = when the two moving ones became a pair | āt it prāpaśyan bhuvanāni viśvā = then all the worlds saw | yat enam adadhuḥ yajñiyāḥ sarve divi devāḥ sūryam āditeyam aditeḥ putram = when all the worthy gods in heaven set him, the Sun, Aditi’s son | yadā cariṣṇū mithunau prādurabhūtām sarvadā sahacāriṇau = when the two moving ones appeared as a pair, always going together | uṣāḥ ca ādityaḥ ca = Dawn and the Sun | mithunau kasmāt = why “mithuna” | minotiḥ śrayati-karmā = mi is a verb of resting | thu iti nāma-karaṇaḥ = thu makes the noun | thakāraḥ vā nayatiḥ paraḥ = or the th with nī after | vaniḥ vā = or van | samāśritau anyonyam nayataḥ = resting on each other they lead each other | vanutaḥ vā = or they desire | manuṣya-mithunau api etasmāt eva = a human pair too is from this | methantau anyonyam vanutaḥ iti vā = or they come together, desiring each other | atha enam etayā agnī-kṛtya stauti = then with this he praises him making him Agni
30 yatra vadete avaraḥ paraḥ ca = where the lower and the higher speak | yajña-nyoḥ kataraḥ nau vi veda = which of us two knows the sacrifice better | ā śekuḥ it sadhamādam sakhāyaḥ = friends, sharing the feast, were able | nakṣanta yajñam kaḥ idam vi vocat = they reach the sacrifice; who will declare this | yatra vivadete daivyau hotārau = where the two divine Hotṛs dispute | ayam ca agniḥ asau ca madhyamaḥ = this Agni and that middle one | kataraḥ nau yajñe bhūyaḥ veda iti āśaknuvanti = “which of us knows more of the sacrifice” — they are unable | tat saha-madanam samāna-ākhyānāḥ ṛtvijaḥ teṣām yajñam samaśnuvānām kaḥ naḥ idam vivakṣyati iti = at that joint feasting the priests of one account, attaining the sacrifice, ask who will declare this to us | tasya uttarā bhūyase nirvacanāya = the next verse is for fuller explanation
31 yāvat-mātram uṣasaḥ na pratīkam = as much as the face of dawn | suparṇyaḥ vasate mātariśvā = the fair-winged ones, Mātariśvan clothes himself | tāvat dadhāti upa yajñam āyan = so much he places, coming to the sacrifice | brāhmaṇaḥ hotuḥ avaraḥ niṣīdan = the brahmin sits below the Hotṛ | yāvanmātram uṣasaḥ pratyaktam bhavati pratidarśanam iti vā = as much as the dawn is turned toward, or seen back | asti upamānasya saṃpratyarthe prayogaḥ = there is a use of the comparison in the sense of “now” | iha iva nidhehi iti yathā = as “put it right here” | suparṇyaḥ su-patanāḥ = suparṇī: swift-flying | etāḥ rātrayaḥ vasate mātariśvan jyotir-varṇasya = these nights Mātariśvan clothes, of the light-coloured | tāvat upadadhāti yajñam āgacchan brāhmaṇaḥ hotā asya agneḥ hoturavaraḥ niṣīdan = so much he places, the brahmin Hotṛ coming to the sacrifice, sitting below Agni the Hotṛ | hotṛ-japaḥ tu anagniḥ vaiśvānarīyaḥ bhavati = but the Hotṛ’s muttering is not of Agni but of Vaiśvānara | deva savitaḥ etam tvā vṛṇate agnim hotrāya saha pitrā vaiśvānareṇa iti = O god Savitṛ, they choose you, this Agni, for the Hotṛ’s office, with the father Vaiśvānara | imam eva agnim savitāram āha = he calls this very Agni Savitṛ | sarvasya prasavitāram = the impeller of all | madhyamam vā uttamam vā pitaram = the middle or the highest father | yaḥ tu sūktam bhajate yasmai haviḥ nirupyate ayam eva saḥ agniḥ vaiśvānaraḥ = but whichever shares the hymn and for whom the offering is made, that is this very Agni Vaiśvānara | nipātam eva ete uttare jyotiṣī etena nāmadheyena bhajete = these two later lights share by this name merely as occasion
