# Bhartṛhari, Śṛṅgāraśatakam — word by word.
1 śambhu-svayambhu-harayaḥ = Śiva, Brahmā and Viṣṇu | hariṇa-īkṣaṇānām = to women with doe-like eyes | yena = by whom | akriyanta = were made | satatam = always | gṛha-kumbha-dāsāḥ = servants in the house, water-carriers | vācām a-gocara-carita-vicitritāya = whose wondrous deeds are beyond speech | tasmai = to him | namaḥ = homage | bhagavate = to the lord | makara-dhvajāya = Love, whose banner is the sea-monster
2 smitena = by a smile | bhāvena ca = and by feeling | lajjayā = by shyness | bhiyā = by fear | parāṅ-mukhaiḥ = by turned-away faces | ardha-kaṭākṣa-vīkṣaṇaiḥ = by half-sidelong glances | vacobhiḥ = by words | īrṣyā-kalahena = by jealous quarrel | līlayā = by play | samasta-bhāvaiḥ = by all their moods | khalu = indeed | bandhanam = a snare | striyaḥ = women are
3 bhrū-cāturyāt = by the cleverness of the brow | kuñcita-akṣāḥ = narrowed eyes | kaṭākṣāḥ = side-glances | snigdhāḥ vācaḥ = tender words | lajjita-antāḥ ca hāsāḥ = laughter that ends in shyness | līlā-mandam = slow in play | prasthitam ca = the going | sthitam ca = and the standing | strīṇām = of women | etat = this | bhūṣaṇam ca āyudham ca = is both ornament and weapon
4 kvacit = sometimes | sa-bhrū-bhaṅgaiḥ = with knitted brows | kvacit api ca lajjā-parigataiḥ = and sometimes seized by shyness | kvacit bhūri-trastaiḥ = sometimes very frightened | kvacit api ca līlā-vilalitaiḥ = sometimes languid with play | kumārīṇām = of young girls | etaiḥ = by these | madana-subhagaiḥ = charming with love | netra-valitaiḥ = turned eyes | sphurat-nīla-abjānām prakara-parikīrṇāḥ iva = as if scattered with heaps of quivering blue lotuses | diśaḥ = the quarters
5 vaktram = the face | candra-vikāsi = shines like the moon | paṅkaja-parīhāsa-kṣame locane = eyes that can mock lotuses | varṇaḥ = the complexion | svarṇam apākariṣṇuḥ = putting gold to shame | alinī-jiṣṇuḥ kacānām cayaḥ = a mass of hair that surpasses a row of bees | vakṣojau = the breasts | ibha-kumbha-vibhrama-harau = taking away the grace of an elephant’s temples | gurvī nitamba-sthalī = heavy hips | vācām hāri ca mārdavam = and a softness of speech that charms | yuvatīṣu = in young women | svābhāvikam maṇḍanam = a natural ornament
6 smita-kiñcit-mugdham = a smile slightly shy | sarala-taralaḥ dṛṣṭi-vibhavaḥ = a glance, straight yet restless | parispandaḥ vācām = the stir of speech | abhinava-vilāsa-ukti-sarasaḥ = sweet with fresh coquettish words | gatānām ārambhaḥ = the start of her walking | kisalayita-līlā-parikaraḥ = with a sportive retinue like budding shoots | spṛśantyāḥ tāruṇyam = of one touched by youth | kim iva na hi ramyam = what is not lovely | mṛga-dṛśaḥ = in the doe-eyed
7 draṣṭavyeṣu = among things to see | kim uttamam = what is best | mṛga-dṛśaḥ prema-prasannam mukham = the doe-eyed one’s face bright with love | ghrātavyeṣu api = among things to smell | kim = what | tat-āsya-pavanaḥ = the breath of her mouth | śravyeṣu = among things to hear | kim tat-vacaḥ = what but her speech | kim svādyeṣu = among things to taste | tat-oṣṭha-pallava-rasaḥ = the juice of her lip like a shoot | spṛśyeṣu = among things to touch | kim tat-vapuḥ = what but her body | dhyeyam = to meditate on | kim = what | nava-yauvane = in fresh youth | sa-hṛdayaiḥ = for the feeling | sarvatra tat-vibhramāḥ = everywhere her graces
8 etāḥ = these | calat-valaya-saṃhati-mekhalā-uttha-jhaṅkāra-nūpura-parājita-rāja-haṃsyaḥ = they who outdo the royal swans by the jingle of their moving bracelets, girdles and anklets | kurvanti = make | kasya na manaḥ vivaśam = whose mind not helpless | taruṇyaḥ = young women | vitrasta-mugdha-hariṇī-sadṛśaiḥ kaṭākṣaiḥ = with glances like a frightened, simple doe
9 kuṅkuma-paṅka-kalaṅkita-dehā = her body marked with saffron paste | gaura-payodhara-kampita-hārā = her necklace trembling on pale breasts | nūpura-haṃsa-raṇat-padmā = her lotus feet sounding with anklets like swans | kam na vaśīkurute = whom does she not enslave | bhuvi = on earth | rāmā = a beautiful woman
10 nūnam hi = surely | te kavi-varāḥ = those great poets | viparīta-vācaḥ = speak the opposite of the truth | ye nityam āhuḥ = who always call | abalāḥ iti = “weak” | kāminīḥ tāḥ = those women | yābhiḥ = by whom | vilolita-rata-tārakā-dṛṣṭi-pātaiḥ = with glances of rolling eyes | śakra-ādayaḥ api vijitāḥ = even Indra and others were conquered | tu = how then | abalāḥ katham tāḥ = can they be weak
11 nūnam = surely | ājñākaraḥ = obedient | tasyāḥ = of her | su-bhruvaḥ = the fair-browed | makara-dhvajaḥ = Love | yataḥ = since | tat-netra-sañcāra-sūciteṣu = in what is signalled by the movement of her eyes | pravartate = he acts
12 keśāḥ saṃyaminaḥ = her hair restrained | śruteḥ api param pāram gate locane = eyes that have gone beyond the Vedas’ far shore (reaching her ears) | antar-vaktram api = even inside the mouth | svabhāva-śuci-bhīḥ = naturally pure and fearful | kīrṇam dvijānām gaṇaiḥ = scattered with troops of teeth | muktānām satata-adhivāsa-rucirau = lovely with pearls always lying on them | vakṣoja-kumbhau imau = these two breast-jars | ittham = so | tanvi = O slender one | vapuḥ praśāntam api te = your body even though tranquil | rāgam karoti eva naḥ = does stir passion in us
13 mugdhe = O simple girl | dhānuṣkatā kā iyam apūrvā = what new archery is this | tvayi dṛśyate = seen in you | yayā vidhyasi cetāṃsi = by which you pierce hearts | guṇaiḥ eva = with strings (virtues) alone | na sāyakaiḥ = not with arrows
14 sati pradīpe = though there is a lamp | sati agnau = though there is fire | satsu tārā-ravi-induṣu = though stars, sun and moon exist | vinā me = without me | mṛga-śāva-akṣyā = the fawn-eyed one | tamaḥ-bhūtam = become darkness | idam jagat = this world
15 udvṛttaḥ stana-bhāraḥ eṣaḥ = this swelling weight of breasts | tarale netre = restless eyes | cale bhrū-latā = a trembling brow-creeper | rāga-adhiṣṭhitam oṣṭha-pallavam idam = this red lip like a shoot | kurvantu nāma vyathām = let them cause pain | saubhāgya-akṣara-mālikā iva = like a string of letters of good fortune | likhitā puṣpa-āyudhena svayam = written by the flower-armed god himself | madhya-sthā api = though in the middle | karoti tāpam adhikam = it gives greater pain | roma-āvaliḥ = the line of down | kena sā = by what is it
16 mukhena candra-kāntena = with a face like moonstone | mahā-nīlaiḥ śiroruhaiḥ = with hair like great sapphires | karābhyām padma-rāgābhyām = with hands like rubies | reje = she shone | ratna-mayī iva sā = as if made of jewels
17 guruṇā stana-bhāreṇa = with heavy breasts | mukha-candreṇa bhāsvatā = with a shining moon-face | śanaiścarābhyām pādābhyām = with slow-moving feet (like Saturn) | reje = shone | graha-mayī iva sā = as if made of planets
18 tasyāḥ stanau yadi ghanau = if her breasts are full | jaghanam ca hāri = and her hips charming | vaktram ca cāru = and her face lovely | tava citta = O my mind | kim ākulatvam = why be agitated | puṇyam kuruṣva = do good deeds | yadi teṣu tava asti vāñchā = if you have a wish for them | puṇyaiḥ vinā na hi = without merit indeed | bhavanti = come | samīhita-arthāḥ = desired ends
19 ime = these | tāruṇya-śrī-nava-parimalāḥ = fresh fragrance of the glory of youth | prauḍha-surata-pratāpa-prārambhāḥ = the opening of the fierce heat of mature love | smara-vijaya-dāna-pratibhuvaḥ = the pledges of the gift of Love’s victory | ciram cetaḥ-corāḥ = long thieves of the heart | abhinava-vikāra-eka-guravaḥ = sole teachers of fresh stirrings | vilāsa-vyāpārāḥ = the plays of coquetry | kim api vijayante = triumph in some wondrous way | mṛga-dṛśām = of the doe-eyed
20 praṇaya-madhurāḥ = sweet with love | prema-udgārāḥ = outbursts of affection | rasa-āśrayatām gatāḥ = become the home of sentiment | phaṇiti-madhurāḥ = sweet in their murmuring | mugdha-prāyāḥ = mostly artless | prakāśita-sammadāḥ = showing delight | prakṛti-subhagāḥ = lovely by nature | visrambha-ārdrāḥ = moist with trust | smara-udaya-dāyinī = raising love | rahasi = in secret | kim api = some | svaira-ālāpāḥ = free talks | haranti = steal the heart | mṛgī-dṛśām = of the doe-eyed
21 viśramya viśramya = resting again and again | vana-drumāṇām chāyāsu = in the shade of forest trees | tanvī = a slender woman | vicacāra = wandered | kācit = some | stana-uttarīyeṇa = with the cloth over her breasts | kara-uddhṛtena = held up by her hand | nivārayantī = warding off | śaśinaḥ mayūkhān = the moon’s rays
22 adarśane = when not seen | darśana-mātra-kāmā = longing only for a sight | dṛṣṭvā = on seeing | pariṣvaṅga-sukha-eka-lolā = eager only for the joy of embrace | āliṅgitāyām = when embraced | punaḥ = again | āyata-akṣyām = with long-eyed one | āśāsmahe = we hope | vigrahayoḥ abhedam = for the two bodies to be undivided (the text is partly corrupt; the rendering is tentative)
23 mālatī śirasi = a jasmine garland on the head | jṛmbhaṇam mukhe = a yawn in the mouth | candanam vapuṣi = sandal on the body | kuṅkuma-āvilam = stained with saffron | vakṣasi priyatamā = the beloved on the breast | mada-alasā = languid with wine | svargaḥ eṣaḥ = this is heaven | pariśiṣṭaḥ āgamaḥ = the one remaining scripture (the rest is teaching)
24 prāk = first | mām eti = comes to me | manāk = a little | anāgata-rasam = with sentiment not yet come | jāta-abhilāṣām = then desire is born | tataḥ = then | sa-vrīḍam = with shame | tad-anu = after that | ślatha-udyamam = with slackened effort | atha = then | pradhvasta-dhairyam = with composure shattered | punaḥ = again | prema-ārdram = moist with love | spṛhaṇīya-nirbhara-rahaḥ-krīḍā-pragalbham = bold in the longed-for full play in secret | tataḥ = then | niḥsaṅga-aṅga-vikarṣaṇa-adhika-sukha-ramyam = delightful with the greater joy of limbs drawn together without restraint | kula-strī-ratam = the love of a wife of good family
25 urasi nipatitānām = fallen on the breast | srasta-dhammillakānām = their braids loosened | mukulita-nayanānām = with eyes half-closed | kiñcit unmīlitānām = a little opened | upari = above | surata-kheda-svinna-gaṇḍa-sthalānām = their cheeks damp with the weariness of love | adhara-madhu = the honey of the lip | vadhūnām = of wives | bhāgyavantaḥ pibanti = the fortunate drink
26 āmīlita-nayanānām = of those with eyes closed | yaḥ surata-rasaḥ = the delight of love | anu saṃvidam = following awareness | bhāti = shines | mithuraiḥ mithaḥ avadhāritam = known to each pair, one by the other | avitatham = true | idam eva kāma-nirbarhaṇam = this alone is the fulfilment of love (the text is corrupt; tentative)
27 idam anucitam akramaḥ ca = this is unfitting and out of order | puṃsām = for men | yat iha = that here | jarāsu api = even in old age | manmatha-vikārāḥ = the stirrings of love | tat api = yet | ca na kṛtam = is not done | nitambinīnām = by the broad-hipped | stana-patana-avadhi = until the breasts droop | jīvitam = life | ratam vā = or love-making
28 rājaḥ-tṛṣṇā-ambu-rāśeḥ = of the ocean of the thirst of desire | na hi jagati gataḥ = no one in the world has gone | kaścit eva avasānam = to its end | kaḥ vā arthaḥ arthaiḥ prabhūtaiḥ = what use is much wealth | sva-vapuṣi galite = when one’s body wastes | yauvane sa-anurāge = in loving youth | gacchāmaḥ sadma = let us go home | yāvat = while | vikasita-nayana-indīvara-ālokinīnām = to women who look with open lotus-eyes | ākramya ākramya = seizing again and again | rūpam = the beauty | jhaṭiti na jarayā lupyate = is not at once wiped out by age | preyasīnām = of our beloved (the verse is run together in the print; tentative)
29 rāgasya āgāram ekam = the one home of passion | naraka-śata-mahā-duḥkha-samprāpti-hetuḥ = the cause of reaching the great sorrow of a hundred hells | mohasya utpatti-bījam = the seed of the birth of delusion | jaladhara-paṭalam = a cloud-bank | jñāna-tārā-adhipasya = over the moon of knowledge | kandarpasya eka-mitram = Love’s only friend | prakaṭita-vividha-spaṣṭa-doṣa-prabandham = showing a clear series of many faults | loke asmin = in this world | na hi artha-vraja-kula-bhavana-yauvanāt anyat asti = there is nothing but wealth, herds, family, house and youth
30 śṛṅgāra-druma-nīrade = rain-cloud to the tree of love | prasṛmara-krīḍā-rasa-srotasi = a stream of spreading sport and sentiment | pradyumna-priya-bāndhave = the dear friend of Pradyumna (Love) | catura-vāk-muktā-phala-udanvati = an ocean of the pearls of clever speech | tanvī-netra-cakora-pāvana-vidhau = the means of purifying the cakora-eyes of slender women | saubhāgya-lakṣmī-nidhau = the treasury of good-fortune’s glory | dhanyaḥ kaḥ api = what fortunate man | na vikriyām kalayati = is not changed | prāpte nave yauvane = when fresh youth comes
31 saṃsāre asmin asāre = in this empty world | ku-nṛpa-bhavana-dvāra-sevā-kalaṅka-vyāsaṅga = the stain of attendance at the gates of bad kings | vyasta-dhairyam = with composure scattered | katham = how | amala-dhiyaḥ mānasam saṃvidadhyuḥ = could the pure-minded steady their minds | yadi etāḥ = if these | prodyat-indu-dyuti-nicaya-bhṛtaḥ = bearing the light of a rising moon | na syuḥ ambhoja-netrāḥ = were not lotus-eyed | preṅkhat-kāñcī-kalāpāḥ = with swaying girdles | stana-bhara-vinamat-madhya-bhājaḥ = with waists bent by the weight of their breasts | taruṇyaḥ = young women
32 siddha-adhyāsita-kandare = in caves dwelt in by perfected ones | hara-vṛṣa-skandha-avarugṇa-drume = where trees are broken by the shoulder of Śiva’s bull | gaṅgā-dhauta-śilā-tale = with rock-slabs washed by the Gaṅgā | himavataḥ sthāne sthite śreyasi = while the better place, the Himālaya, stands | kaḥ kurvīta = who would make | śiraḥ praṇāma-malinam = his head dirty with bowing | mlānam = withered | manasvī janaḥ = a proud man | yat = if | vitrasta-kuraṅga-śāva-nayanāḥ = frightened fawn-eyed | na syuḥ = were not | smara-astram striyaḥ = women, Love’s weapon
33 saṃsāra = O world | tava paryanta-padavī = the road to your end | na davīyasī = is not very far | antarā = in between | dustarā na syuḥ = would not be hard to cross | yadi te = if there were not | madira-īkṣaṇāḥ = women with wine-bright eyes
34 diśa = give | vana-hariṇībhyaḥ = to the forest deer | vaṃśa-kāṇḍa-chavīnām = the colour of bamboo stalks | kavalam = a mouthful | upala-koṭi-chinna-mūlam = with roots cut by the rock’s edge | kuśānām = of kuśa grass | śaka-yuvati-kapola-āpāṇḍu-tāmbūla-valli-dalam = a betel leaf pale as a young Śaka woman’s cheek | aruṇa-nakha-agraiḥ pāṭitam = torn with red nail-tips | vā vadhūbhyaḥ = or to the wives (the verse is obscure; tentative)
35 asārāḥ sarve te = all those are worthless | virati-virasāḥ = tasteless to the dispassionate | pāpa-viṣayāḥ = objects of sin | jugupsyantām = let them be scorned | yat vā nanu = or indeed | sakala-doṣa-āspadam iti = “the home of every fault” | tathā api = yet | etat bhūmau = on earth | na hi para-hitāt puṇyam adhikam = there is no merit greater than doing others good | na ca asmin saṃsāre = and in this world | kuvalaya-dṛśaḥ = than lotus-eyed women | ramyam aparam = nothing more lovely
36 etat kāma-phalaḥ loke = this is the fruit of love in the world | yat dvayoḥ eka-cittatā = that two have one mind | anya-citta-kṛte kāme = when love is made with another mind | śavayoḥ iva saṅgamaḥ = it is like the union of two corpses
37 mātsaryam utsārya = putting aside envy | vicārya kāryam = considering the matter | āryāḥ = O worthy ones | samaryādam idam vadantu = say honestly this | sevyāḥ = to be resorted to | nitambāḥ kim u bhū-dharāṇām = the slopes of mountains | ataḥ = or | smara-smera-vilāsinīnām = the hips of women smiling with love (the text is partly corrupt; tentative)
38 saṃsāre svapna-sāre = in this world whose essence is a dream | pariṇati-tarale = fickle in its end | dve gatī paṇḍitānām = two courses for the wise | tattva-jñāna-amṛta-ambhaḥ-plava-lalita-dhiyām = for those whose minds play in the nectar-waters of true knowledge | yātu kālaḥ kathañcit = let time pass somehow | no cet = if not | mugdha-aṅganānām = for artless women | stana-jaghana-ghana-ābhoga-sambhoginīnām = enjoying the close fullness of breasts and hips | sthūla-upastha-sthalīṣu = on their broad lap | sthagita-kara-tala-sparśa-līlā-udyamānām = playing with a hidden touch of the palm (a corrupt verse; tentative)
39 āvāsaḥ kriyatām = make your home | gaṅge pāpa-hāriṇi vāriṇi = on the Gaṅgā, whose water takes away sin | stana-dvaye taruṇyāḥ vā = or on a young woman’s two breasts | manohāriṇi hāriṇi = that charm the mind, and wear a necklace
