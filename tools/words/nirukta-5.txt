3 na yasya dyāvāpṛthivī na dhanva = for whom neither heaven and earth nor the desert | na antarikṣam na adrayaḥ somaḥ akṣāḥ = nor mid-air, nor mountains: the soma flows | aśnoteḥ iti evam eke = some it shining | svapnam etat mādhyamikam jyotiḥ anitya-darśanam = this mid-air light is a dream, seen not lastingly | tat iva avidat jvājvalyamānam = he found it as if blazing | dvitā ca sattā svadhayā 4 varāhaḥ meghaḥ bhavati = varāha is a cloud | varāhāraḥ = he takes the best | varam āhāram āhārṣīḥ iti ca brāhmaṇam = “you took the best food”, says a Brāhmaṇa | vidhyat varāham tiraḥ adrim astā = the archer pierced the boar through the mountain | iti api nigamaḥ bhavati = so too a text | ayam api itaraḥ varāhaḥ etasmāt eva = this other varāha (boar) too is for this reason | bṛhati mūlāni = it gathers roots | varam varam mūlam bṛhati iti vā = or it uproots the best roots | varāham indra emuṣam = O Indra, the boar Emuṣa | aṅgirasaḥ api varāhāḥ ucyante = the Aṅgirases too are called varāha | brahmaṇaspatiḥ vṛṣabhiḥ varāhaiḥ = Brahmaṇaspati with bulls and boars | atha api ete mādhyamikāḥ deva-gaṇāḥ varāhavaḥ ucyante = also these mid-air hosts of gods are called varāhu | paśyan hiraṇya-cakrān ayo-daṃṣṭrān vidhāvataḥ varāhūn = seeing the varāhus with golden wheels, iron tusks, running about | svasarāṇi ahāni bhavanti = svasara are days | svayam sārīṇi api vā = or “going by themselves” | svar ādityaḥ bhavati = svar is the Sun | saḥ enāni sārayati = he sends them forth | usrāḥ iva svasarāṇi = like cows, the days | iti api nigamaḥ bhavati = so too a text | śaryāḥ aṅgulayaḥ bhavanti = śaryā are fingers | sṛjanti karmāṇi = they release works | śaryāḥ iṣavaḥ śara-mayyaḥ = śaryā are arrows made of reed | śaraḥ śṛṇāteḥ = śara from “to crush” | śaryābhiḥ na bharamāṇaḥ gabhastyoḥ = like carrying with the reeds in his hands | iti api nigamaḥ bhavati = so too a text | arkaḥ devaḥ bhavati yat enam arcanti = arka is a god, because they praise him | arkaḥ mantraḥ bhavati yat anena arcanti = arka is a mantra, because they praise by it | arkam annam bhavati = arka is food | arcati bhūtāni = it honours beings | arkaḥ vṛkṣaḥ bhavati = arka is a tree | saṃvṛttaḥ kaṭukimnā = grown with bitterness
5 gāyanti tvā gāyatriṇaḥ arcanti arkam arkiṇaḥ = the singers sing you, the praisers praise the song | brahmāṇaḥ tvā śatakrato ud vaṃśam iva yemire = the priests raised you, O hundred-willed, like a 6 pavitram punāteḥ = pavitra is from “to purify” | mantraḥ pavitram ucyate = a mantra is called pavitra | yena devāḥ pavitreṇa ātmānam punate sadā = by which the gods always purify themselves | iti api nigamaḥ bhavati = so too a text | raśmayaḥ pavitram ucyante = rays are called pavitra | gabhasti-pūtaḥ nṛbhiḥ adribhiḥ sutaḥ = purified by the hands, pressed by men and stones | iti api nigamaḥ bhavati = so too a text | āpaḥ pavitram ucyante = waters are called pavitra | śata-pavitrāḥ svadhayā madantīḥ = of a hundred purifiers, rejoicing in their own power | bahu-udakāḥ = of much water | agniḥ pavitram ucyate = Agni is called pavitra | vāyuḥ pavitram ucyate = Vāyu is called pavitra | somaḥ pavitram ucyate = Soma is called pavitra | sūryaḥ pavitram ucyate = Sūrya is called pavitra | indraḥ pavitram ucyate = Indra is called pavitra | agniḥ pavitram saḥ mā punātu vāyuḥ somaḥ sūryaḥ indraḥ = may Agni, the purifier, purify me, Vāyu, Soma, Sūrya, Indra | pavitram te mā punantu = may your purifiers purify me | iti api nigamaḥ bhavati = so too a text | todaḥ tudyateḥ = toda is from “to goad”
7 puru tvā dāśvān voce ariḥ agne tava svit ā = the giver, I have said much to you, O Agni, indeed yours | todasya iva śaraṇe ā mahasya = like a goad’s protection of the great | bahu dāśvān tvām eva abhihvayāmi = I, the giver, call on you alone often | ariḥ aramitraḥ ṛcchateḥ = ari is from “to go” | īśvaraḥ api ariḥ etasmāt eva = a lord too is ari from this | yat anya-devatyāḥ agnau āhutayaḥ hūyante iti etat dṛṣṭvā evam avakṣyat = seeing that offerings meant for other deities are offered in Agni, he would have spoken so | todasya iva śaraṇe ā mahasya = like a goad’s protection of the great | tudasya iva śaraṇe adhi mahataḥ = like the goad’s protection of the great | svañcāḥ su añcanaḥ = svañca is “well-bending” | akra juhvānaḥ ghṛta-pṛṣṭhaḥ svañcāḥ = he made the offerings, ghee-backed, well-bent | iti api nigamaḥ bhavati = so too a text | śipiviṣṭaḥ viṣṇuḥ iti viṣṇoḥ dve nāmanī bhavataḥ = śipiviṣṭa and Viṣṇu are two names of Viṣṇu | kutsita-arthīyam pūrvam bhavati iti aupamanyavaḥ = the first has a sense of contempt, says Aupamanyava

9 pra tat te adya śipiviṣṭa nāma = today I proclaim that name of yours, Śipiviṣṭa | aryaḥ śaṃsāmi vayunāni vidvān = I, the lord, knowing the ways | tam tvā gṛṇāmi tavasam atavyān = I praise you, the mighty, to the weak | kṣayantam asya rajasaḥ parāke = dwelling at the far side of this realm | tat te adya śipiviṣṭa nāma aryaḥ praśaṃsāmi = today I, the lord, praise that name of yours | aryaḥ aham asmi īśvaraḥ stomānām = I am a lord of hymns | aryaḥ tvam asi iti vā = or “you are the lord” | tam tvā staumi tavasam atavyān = I praise you, the mighty, to the weak | tavasaḥ iti mahataḥ nāmadheyam udita bhavati = tavas is declared a name of “great” | nivasantam asya rajasaḥ = dwelling in this realm | parāke parākrānte = far off: gone beyond | āghṛṇiḥ āgata-hṛṇiḥ = āghṛṇi: one whose anger has come | ā ghṛṇe saṃ sacāvahai = O blazing one, let us two keep company | āgata-hṛṇe saṃsevāvahai = O one whose anger has come, let us two serve together | pṛthujrayāḥ pṛthu-javaḥ = pṛthujrayas: of wide speed | pṛthujrayāḥ aminat āyuḥ dasyoḥ = the wide-speeding one cut down the life of the dasyu | prāmāpayat āyuḥ dasyoḥ = he destroyed the life of the dasyu

25 pṛthak prāyan prathamāḥ deva-hūtayaḥ = they went apart first, calling the gods | akṛṇvata śravasyāni duṣṭarā = they made hard-to-do deeds of fame | na ye śekuḥ yajñiyām nāvam āruham = those who could not climb the sacrificial ship | īrmā eva te nyaviśanta kepayaḥ = those wretches settled just on the arm | pṛthak prāyan pṛthak prathateḥ = pṛthak from “to spread” | prathamāḥ deva-hūtayaḥ ye devān āhvayanta = first the god-callers, who called the gods | akurvata śravaṇīyāni yaśāṃsi duranukarāṇi anyaiḥ = they made renowned fames hard for others to imitate | ye aśaknuvan yajñiyām nāvam āroḍhum = who were able to climb the sacrificial ship | atha ye na aśaknuvan yajñiyām nāvam āroḍhum = but those who were not able to climb the sacrificial ship | īrmā eva te nyaviśanta = they settled just on īrma | iha eva te nyaviśanta = they settled just here | ṛṇe ha eva te nyaviśanta = they settled in debt | asmin eva loke iti vā = or in this world itself | īrmā iti bāhu-nāma = īrma is a name of arm | samīritataraḥ bhavati = it is more set in motion | etā viśvā savanā tūtumā kṛṣe = you have swiftly made all these pressings | svayam sūno sahasaḥ yāni dadhiṣe = those, O son of strength, you yourself hold | etāni sarvāṇi sthānāni tūrṇam upākuruṣe = you quickly provide all these places | svayam balasya putra yāni dhatsva = O son of strength, those you yourself establish | aṃsatram aṃhasaḥ trāṇam = aṃsatra is protection from distress | dhanuḥ vā kavacam vā = a bow or an armour | kavacam ku añcitam bhavati = kavaca is ill-bent | kāñcitam bhavati = it is “well-bent” | kāye añcitam bhavati iti vā = or bent on the body
26 prīṇīta aśvān hitam jayatha = please the horses, win the good | svasti-vāham ratham it kṛṇudhvam = make the chariot bear well-being | droṇa-āhāvam avatam aśma-cakram = the trough-pouring well, the stone wheel | aṃsatra-kośam siñcata nṛpāṇam = pour the shoulder-guard store, the man-drinking | prīṇīta aśvān suhitam jayatha = please the horses, win well | jayanam vaḥ hitam astu = may your victory be good | svasti-vāhanam ratham kurudhvam = make a chariot carrying well-being | droṇa-āhāvam droṇam drumamayam bhavati = droṇa is made of wood | āhāvaḥ āhvānāt = āhāva from “calling” | āvahaḥ āvahanāt = āvaha from “bringing” | avataḥ avātitaḥ mahān bhavati = avata is great, not blown | aśma-cakram aśana-cakram asana-cakram iti vā = “stone-wheel”, “eating-wheel”, “throwing-wheel” | aṃsatra-kośam aṃsatrāṇi vaḥ kośa-sthānīyāni santu = may your shoulder-guards be like the store | kośaḥ kuṣṇāteḥ = kośa from “to tear out” | vikuṣitaḥ bhavati = it is torn out | ayam api itaraḥ kośaḥ etasmāt eva = this other kośa too is from this | sañcayaḥ ācita-mātraḥ mahān bhavati = a heap is great, merely piled | siñcata nṛpāṇam narapāṇam = pour the man’s drink | kūpa-karmaṇā saṃgrāmam upamimīte = he compares the battle to the action of a well | kākudam tātvī iti ācakṣate = they call it kākuda, tālu | jihvā kokuvā sā asmin dhīyate = the tongue is kokuvā, it is placed in it | jihvā kokuvā kokūyamānā varṇān nudati iti vā = the tongue, kokuvā, urges letters while uttering | kokūyateḥ vā syāt śabda-karmaṇaḥ = or from kokūy, a verb of sounding | jihvā johuvā = the tongue is johuvā | tālu tarateḥ = tālu from “crossing” | tīrṇatamam aṅgam = the most crossed limb | latateḥ vā syāt lambaka-karmaṇaḥ viparītāt = or from lat, a verb of hanging, reversed | yathā talam lata iti aviparyayaḥ = as tala, so lata, without reversal
27 sudevaḥ asi varuṇa yasya te sapta sindhavaḥ = you are of good gods, Varuṇa, whose seven rivers | anukṣaranti kākudam sūrmyam suṣirām iva = flow after through the palate, like a pipe through a hollow | sudevaḥ tvam kalyāṇa-dānaḥ = you are of good giving | yasya tava deva sapta sindhavaḥ prāṇāya anukṣaranti kākudam = whose seven rivers, O god, flow after the palate for breath | sūrmyam suṣirām iva iti = “like a pipe through a hollow” | api nigamaḥ bhavati = also there is a text | sudevaḥ tvam kalyāṇa-devaḥ kamanīya-devaḥ vā bhavasi varuṇa = you, Varuṇa, are of good gods or lovely gods | yasya te sapta sindhavaḥ = whose seven rivers | sindhuḥ sravaṇāt = sindhu from “flowing” | yasya te sapta srotāṃsi = whose seven streams | tāni te kākudam anukṣaranti = they flow after your palate | sūrmiḥ kalyāṇa-ūrmiḥ = sūrmi is a good wave | srotaḥ suṣiram anu yathā = a stream along a hollow | bīriṭam taiṭīkiḥ antarikṣam evam āha = Taiṭīki says bīriṭa is the mid-air | pūrvam vayateḥ uttaram irateḥ = the first part from “to weave”, the latter from “to move” | vayāṃsi īranti asmin = birds move in it | bhāṃsi vā = or lights | tat etasyām ṛci udāharanti = they quote this on this verse | api nigamaḥ bhavati = also there is a text
28 pra vāvṛje su-prayā barhiḥ eṣām = their well-spread grass is laid out | ā viśpatī iva bīriṭe iyāte = the two come like lords of the people to the mid-air | viśām aktoḥ uṣasaḥ pūrva-hūtau = at the first calling of the people, night and dawn | vāyuḥ pūṣā svastaye niyutvān = Vāyu and Pūṣan, with their teams, for well-being | pravṛjyate su-prāyaṇam barhiḥ eṣām = their well-going grass is spread | eyāte sarvasya pātārau vā pālayitārau vā = the two come, protectors or guardians of all | bīriṭam antarikṣam bhiyaḥ vā bhāsaḥ vā tatiḥ = bīriṭa is the mid-air, a spread of fear or of lights | api vā upamā-arthe syāt = or it may be in the sense of comparison | sarvapatī iva rājānau = like two kings, lords of all | bīriṭe gaṇe manuṣyāṇām = in the host of men | rātryāḥ vivāse pūrvasyām abhihūtau = at night’s departure, in the first calling | vāyuḥ ca niyutvān pūṣā ca svastyayanāya = Vāyu with his team and Pūṣan for well-being | niyutvān niyutaḥ asya aśvāḥ = niyutvān: his horses are niyuts | niyutaḥ niyamanāt vā niyojanāt vā = niyut from “restraining” or “yoking” | acchā ābheḥ āptum iti śākapūṇiḥ = acchā-ābheḥ: to reach, says Śākapūṇi | parī sīm iti vyākhyātam = “parī sīm” has been explained | enam enām asyāḥ asya iti etena vyākhyātam = enam, enām, asyāḥ, asya are explained by this | sṛṇiḥ aṅkuśaḥ bhavati saraṇāt = sṛṇi is a goad, from “going” | aṅkuśaḥ añcateḥ ākuñcitaḥ bhavati iti vā = aṅkuśa from “to bend”: it is hooked | nedīyaḥ it sṛṇyaḥ pakvam eyāt = may the ripe one come nearer the goad | iti api nigamaḥ bhavati = so too a text | antikatamam aṅkuśāt āyāt = may it come nearest the goad | pakvam auṣadham āgacchatu iti āgacchatu iti = may the ripe herb come, may it come
29 sasnim asaścantī na yasya varāhaḥ gāyanti tvā pavitram = “sasnim”; “asaścantī”; “na yasya”; “varāha”; “gāyanti tvā”; “pavitram” | puru tvā kim it te pra tat te agnim naraḥ = “puru tvā”; “kim it te”; “pra tat te”; “agnim naraḥ” | ekayā āpāntamanyuḥ urvaśī apsarāḥ utāsi = “ekayā”; “āpāntamanyuḥ”; “urvaśī”; “apsarā”; “utāsi” | saḥ it niyat kadā martam patnīvantaḥ sugurasat = “sa it”; “niyat”; “kadā martam”; “patnīvantaḥ”; “sugurasat” | vṛkaḥ candramāḥ aruṇaḥ mā yaḥ indrāgnī mā naḥ samasya = “vṛkaś candramā”; “aruṇo mā”; “ya indrāgnī”; “mā naḥ samasya” | haviṣā jāraḥ pṛthak-prāyan prīṇīta aśvān sudevaḥ pra vāvṛje = “haviṣā jāraḥ”; “pṛthakprāyan”; “prīṇītāśvān”; “sudevaḥ”; “pra vāvṛje” | aṣṭāviṃśatiḥ = twenty-eight (the number of sections)
