# Nirukta, adhyāya 9 — word by word. Etymologies are given as Yāska states them; examples quoted for form are glossed as examples.
1 atha yāni pṛthivī-āyatanāni sattvāni stutim labhante tāni ataḥ anukramiṣyāmaḥ = now those beings whose abodes are on earth and which receive praise we shall go through from here | teṣām aśvaḥ prathama-āgāmī bhavati = of them the horse comes first | aśvaḥ vyākhyātaḥ = aśva has been explained | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
2 aśvaḥ voḍhā sukham ratham = the horse is the carrier, the chariot pleasantly | hasanām upamantriṇaḥ = the laughing one, the counsellors | śepaḥ romaṇvantau bhedau = the organ, the two hairy cleavers | vāri nmaṇḍūkaḥ icchati = the frog desires water | indrāya indo pari srava = flow round for Indra, O drop | aśvaḥ voḍhā = the horse is the carrier | sukham voḍhā ratham voḍhā = the carrier pleasantly, the carrier of the chariot | sukham iti kalyāṇa-nāma = sukha is a name of “good” | kalyāṇam puṇyam suhitam bhavati = good is merit, well placed | suhitam gamyati iti vā = or “it is gone to well” | hasā etā vā pātā vā pālayitā vā = hasa: he goes, or protector, guardian | śepam ṛcchati iti = he goes to the organ | vāri vārayati = water: it restrains | mā naḥ vyākhyātaḥ = “mā naḥ” has been explained | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
3 mā naḥ mitraḥ varuṇaḥ aryamā āyuḥ = let not Mitra, Varuṇa, Aryaman, Āyu | indraḥ ṛbhukṣāḥ marutaḥ pari khyan = Indra, Ṛbhukṣan, the Maruts overlook us | yat vājinaḥ deva-jātasya sapteḥ = when of the strong steed born of gods | pravakṣyāmaḥ vidathe vīryāṇi = we shall proclaim the heroic deeds at the rite | yat vājinaḥ devaiḥ jātasya sapteḥ saraṇasya pravakṣyāmaḥ yajñe vidathe vīryāṇi = when we shall proclaim at the sacrifice the deeds of the swift horse born of the gods | mā naḥ tvam mitraḥ ca varuṇaḥ ca aryamā ca āyuḥ ca vāyuḥ ayanaḥ indraḥ ca urukṣayaṇaḥ ṛbhūṇām rājā iti vā marutaḥ ca parikhyan = let not Mitra, Varuṇa, Aryaman, Āyu (the wind, the goer), Indra of wide dwelling, the king of the Ṛbhus, or the Maruts overlook us | śakuniḥ śaknoti unnetum ātmānam = the bird is able to lift itself | śaknoti naditum iti vā = or it is able to sound | śaknoti takitum iti vā = or it is able to go | sarvataḥ śaṅkaraḥ astu iti vā = or “let it be auspicious everywhere” | śaknoteḥ vā = or from “to be able” | tasya eṣā bhavati = of it there is this verse
4 kanikradat januṣam prabruvāṇaḥ = crying, declaring its birth | iyarti vācam ariteva nāvam = it sends forth speech like an oarsman a boat | sumaṅgalaḥ ca śakune bhavāsi = and be of good omen, O bird | mā tvā kācit abhibhā viśvyā vidat = let no all-overcoming one find you | nyakrandīt janma prabruvāṇaḥ = it cried out declaring its birth | yathā asya śabdaḥ tathā nāma īrayati vācam = as its sound is, so its name sends forth speech | īrayitā iva nāvam = like one who moves a boat | sumaṅgalaḥ ca śakune bhava = and be of good omen, O bird | kalyāṇa-maṅgalaḥ = of auspicious fortune | maṅgalam girateḥ gṛṇāti-arthe = maṅgala from “to swallow” in the sense of “to praise” | giraty anarthān iti vā = or “it swallows what is useless” | aṅgalam aṅgavat = aṅgala: having limbs | majjayati pāpakam iti nairuktāḥ = it drowns evil, say the etymologists | mām gacchatu iti vā = or “may it come to me” | mā ca tvā kācit abhibhūtiḥ sarvataḥ vidat = let no overpowering one find you anywhere | gṛtsamadam artham abhyutthitam kapiñjalaḥ abhivavāśe = the partridge cried out toward Gṛtsamada, who had risen for the matter | tat abhivādinī eṣā ṛk bhavati = this verse speaks of that
5 bhadram vada dakṣiṇataḥ bhadram uttarataḥ vada = speak good from the right, speak good from the left | bhadram purastāt naḥ vada bhadram paścāt kapiñjala = speak good before us, good behind, O partridge | iti sā nigada-vyākhyātā = it is explained by its own recital | gṛtsamadaḥ gṛtsamadanaḥ = Gṛtsamada: Gṛtsa-delighting | gṛtsaḥ iti medhāvi-nāma = gṛtsa is a name of the wise | gṛṇāteḥ stuti-karmaṇaḥ = from “to praise” | maṇḍūkāḥ majjūkāḥ majjanāt = maṇḍūka from majj, “sinking” | madateḥ vā modati-karmaṇaḥ = or from mad, a verb of rejoicing | mandateḥ vā tṛpti-karmaṇaḥ = or from mand, a verb of satisfying | maṇḍayateḥ iti vaiyākaraṇāḥ = from “to adorn”, say the grammarians | maṇḍaḥ eṣām okaḥ iti vā = or “the scum is their home” | maṇḍaḥ madeḥ vā mudeḥ vā = maṇḍa from mad or mud | teṣām eṣā bhavati = of them there is this verse
6 saṃvatsaram śaśayānāḥ brāhmaṇāḥ vrata-cāriṇaḥ = lying for a year, brahmins keeping a vow | vācam parjanya-jinvitām pra maṇḍūkāḥ avādiṣuḥ = the frogs have uttered the speech urged by Parjanya | saṃvatsaram śiśyānāḥ brāhmaṇāḥ vrata-cāriṇaḥ abruvāṇāḥ = brahmins keeping a vow, lying a year, without speaking | api vā upamā-arthe syāt = or it may be in the sense of comparison | brāhmaṇāḥ iva vrata-cāriṇaḥ iti = like brahmins keeping a vow | vācam parjanya-prītām prāvādiṣuḥ maṇḍūkāḥ = the frogs uttered the speech pleasing to Parjanya | vasiṣṭhaḥ varṣa-kāmaḥ parjanyam tuṣṭāva = Vasiṣṭha, desiring rain, praised Parjanya | tam maṇḍūkāḥ anvamodanta = the frogs rejoiced after him | saḥ maṇḍūkān anumodamānān dṛṣṭvā tuṣṭāva = seeing the frogs rejoicing, he praised them | tat abhivādinī eṣā ṛk bhavati = this verse speaks of that
7 upa pra vada maṇḍūki varṣam ā vada tāduri = speak up, O frog, call down the rain, O she-splasher | madhye hradasya plavasva vigṛhya caturaḥ padaḥ = swim in the middle of the pool, spreading your four legs | iti sā nigada-vyākhyātā = it is explained by its own recital | akṣāḥ aśnuvate enān iti vā = akṣa (dice): they reach them | abhyaśnuvate ebhiḥ iti vā = or they reach by these | teṣām eṣā bhavati = of them there is this verse
8 prāvepāḥ mā bṛhataḥ mādayanti = the trembling ones of the great tree make me glad | pravāte-jāḥ iriṇe varvṛtānāḥ = wind-born, rolling on the barren ground | somasya iva maujavatasya bhakṣaḥ = like a draught of the Mūjavat soma | vibhīdakaḥ jāgṛviḥ mahyam acchān = the vibhīdaka, ever-wakeful, has shone for me | pravepiṇaḥ mā mahataḥ vibhīdakasya phalāni mādayanti = the trembling fruits of the great vibhīdaka make me glad | pravāte-jāḥ pravata-jāḥ = born in the wind: born on the slope | iriṇe vartamānāḥ = rolling on barren ground | iriṇam nirṛṇam ṛṇāteḥ = iriṇa is without debt, from “to go” | apārṇam bhavati = it is without a store | aparatā asmāt oṣadhayaḥ iti vā = or plants are unconnected with it | somasya iva maujavatasya bhakṣaḥ = like a draught of the Mūjavat soma | maujavataḥ mūjavati jātaḥ = Maujavata is born on Mūjavat | mūjavān parvataḥ muñjavān = Mūjavat is the mountain with muñja grass | muñjaḥ vimucyate iṣīkayā = muñja is freed by the reed-shoot | iṣīkā iṣeḥ gati-karmaṇaḥ = iṣīkā from iṣ, a verb of going | iyam api itarā iṣīkā etasmāt eva = this other reed too is from this | vibhīdakaḥ vibhedanāt = vibhīdaka from “splitting” | jāgṛviḥ jāgaraṇāt = jāgṛvi from “waking” | mahyam acacchadat = it has shone for me | praśaṃsati enān prathamayā nindati uttarābhiḥ = he praises them in the first verse, blames them in the later ones | ṛṣeḥ akṣa-paridyūnasya etat ārṣam vedayante = they report this as the seer’s, who was ruined by dice | grāvāṇaḥ hanteḥ vā gṛṇāteḥ vā gṛhṇāteḥ vā = grāvan (press-stones) from “to strike”, “to praise”, or “to seize” | teṣām eṣā bhavati = of them there is this verse
9 pra ete vadantu pra vayam vadāma = let them speak forth, we speak forth | grāvabhyaḥ vācam vadata vadadbhyaḥ = speak words to the sounding stones | yat adrayaḥ parvatāḥ sākam āśavaḥ = when the stones, the mountains, together swift | ślokam ghoṣam bharata indrāya sominaḥ = bring a verse and a cry for Indra, you soma-possessors | pravadantu ete = let these speak forth | pravadāma vayam = we speak forth | grāvabhyaḥ vācam vadata vadadbhyaḥ = speak words to the sounding stones | yat adrayaḥ parvatāḥ adaraṇīyāḥ saha somam āśavaḥ kṣipra-kāriṇaḥ = when the stones, the mountains, not to be split, with soma, swift, quick-acting | ślokaḥ śṛṇoteḥ = śloka from “to hear” | ghoṣaḥ ghuṣyateḥ = ghoṣa from “to cry out” | sominaḥ yūyam stha iti vā = or “you are soma-possessors” | sominaḥ gṛheṣu iti vā = or “in the houses of soma-possessors” | yena narāḥ praśasyante saḥ nārāśaṃsaḥ mantraḥ = a mantra by which men are praised is Nārāśaṃsa | tasya eṣā bhavati = of it there is this verse
10 amandān stomān pra bhare manīṣā = I bring unfaltering hymns with my thought | sindhau adhi kṣiyataḥ bhāvyasya = for the one dwelling on the river, who is to be | yaḥ me sahasram amimīta savān = who measured out a thousand pressings for me | atūrtaḥ rājā śravaḥ icchamānaḥ = the unhurried king, desiring fame | amandān stomān abāliśān analpān vā = unfaltering hymns: not foolish, not small | bālaḥ balavartī bhartavyaḥ bhavati = bāla is a youth, one to be supported | ambā asmai alam bhavati iti vā = or “the mother is enough for him” | ambā asmai balam bhavati iti vā = or “the mother is strength for him” | balaḥ vā pratiṣedha-vyavahitaḥ = or bala separated by a negation | prabhare manīṣayā manasaḥ īṣayā stutyā prajñayā vā = I bring with thought, with mind’s impulse, praise or wisdom | sindhau adhi nivasataḥ bhāvayavyasya rājñaḥ = of the king dwelling on the river, the Bhāvayavya | yaḥ me sahasram niramimīta savān = who measured out a thousand pressings for me | atūrtaḥ rājā atūrṇaḥ iti vā atvaramāṇaḥ iti vā = unhurried king: not hasty, not hurrying | praśaṃsām icchamānaḥ = wishing for praise
11 yajña-saṃyogāt rājā stutim labheta = through connection with sacrifice a king receives praise | rāja-saṃyogāt yuddha-upakaraṇāni = through connection with a king, the implements of war | teṣām rathaḥ prathama-āgāmī bhavati = of them the chariot comes first | rathaḥ raṃhateḥ gati-karmaṇaḥ = ratha from raṃh, a verb of going | sthirateḥ vā syāt viparītasya = or from “to be firm”, reversed | ramamāṇaḥ asmin tiṣṭhati iti vā = or “delighting, one stands in it” | rapateḥ vā rasateḥ vā = or from “to speak” or “to sound” | tasya eṣā bhavati = of it there is this verse
12 vanaspate vīḍu-aṅgaḥ hi bhūyāḥ = Vanaspati, be firm-limbed | asmat-sakhā prataraṇaḥ su-vīraḥ = our friend, a carrier across, with good heroes | gobhiḥ sannaddhaḥ asi vīḍayasva = you are bound with leather thongs, be firm | āsthātā te jayatu jetvāni = may your rider win the things to be won | vanaspate dṛḍha-aṅgaḥ hi bhava = Vanaspati, be firm-limbed | asmat-sakhā prataraṇaḥ su-vīraḥ kalyāṇa-vīraḥ = our friend, a carrier across, with good heroes: of auspicious heroes | gobhiḥ sannaddhaḥ asi = you are bound with leather | vīḍayasva iti saṃstambhasva = vīḍayasva: make yourself firm | āsthātā te jayatu jetavyāni = may your rider win what is to be won | dundubhiḥ iti śabda-anukaraṇam = dundubhi is an imitation of sound | drumaḥ bhinnaḥ iti vā = or a cleft tree | dundubhyateḥ vā syāt śabda-karmaṇaḥ = or from dundubh, a verb of sounding | tasya eṣā bhavati = of it there is this verse
13 upa śvāsaya pṛthivīm uta dyām = make earth and heaven breathe | purutrā te manutām viṣṭhitam jagat = may the world spread out think of you in many places | saḥ dundubhe sa-jūḥ indreṇa devaiḥ = O drum, together with Indra and the gods | dūrāt davīyaḥ apa sedha śatrūn = drive the enemies far, far away | upaśvāsaya pṛthivīm ca divam ca = make earth and heaven breathe | bahudhā te ghoṣam manyatām = may they think of your sound in many ways | viṣṭhitam sthāvaram jaṅgamam ca yat = what is spread out: the standing and the moving | saḥ dundubhe sahajoṣaṇaḥ indreṇa ca devaiḥ ca = O drum, together with Indra and the gods | dūrāt dūrataram apasedha śatrūn = drive the enemies far from the far | iṣudhiḥ iṣūṇām nidhānam = iṣudhi is the store of arrows | tasya eṣā bhavati = of it there is this verse
14 bahvīnām pitā bahuḥ asya putraḥ = father of many, many are his sons | ciścā kṛṇoti samanā avagatya = he makes a hiss, going toward the gathering | iṣudhiḥ saṅkāḥ pṛtanāḥ ca sarvāḥ = the quiver wins all armies and battles | pṛṣṭhe ninaddhaḥ jayati prasūtaḥ = bound on the back, it wins when sent forth | bahvīnām pitā bahuḥ asya putraḥ iti iṣūn abhipretya = “father of many, many his sons”, meaning the arrows | prasmayataḥ iva apāvriyamāṇaḥ = as if smiling when uncovered | śabda-anukaraṇam vā = or an imitation of sound | saṅkāḥ sacateḥ saṃpūrvāt vā kirateḥ = saṅkā from “to cling” or from kṛ with sam | pṛṣṭhe ninaddhaḥ jayati prasūtaḥ iti vyākhyātam = “bound on the back, it wins when sent forth” has been explained | hastaghnaḥ haste hanyate = hastaghna is struck on the hand | tasya eṣā bhavati = of it there is this verse
15 ahiḥ iva bhogaiḥ pari eti bāhum = like a snake with its coils it goes round the arm | jyāyāḥ hetim paribādhamānaḥ = warding off the weapon of the bowstring | hastaghnaḥ viśvā vayunāni vidvān = the hand-guard, knowing all ways | pumān pumāṃsam pari pātu viśvataḥ = may a man protect a man on every side | ahiḥ iva bhogaiḥ pariveṣṭayati bāhum = it winds round the arm like a snake with coils | jyāyāḥ vadhāt paritrāyamāṇaḥ = guarding from the bowstring’s blow | hastaghnaḥ sarvāṇi prajñānāni prajānan = the hand-guard, knowing all knowledge | pumān puru-manāḥ bhavati puṃsateḥ = puman is of much mind, from “to crush” | abhīśavaḥ vyākhyātāḥ = abhīśu have been explained | teṣām eṣā bhavati = of them there is this verse
16 rathe tiṣṭhan nayati vājinaḥ puraḥ = standing in the chariot he leads the horses in front | yatra yatra kāmayate su-ṣārathiḥ = wherever the good charioteer wishes | abhīśūnām mahimānam panāyata = praise the greatness of the reins | manaḥ paścāt anu yacchanti raśmayaḥ = the reins follow the mind behind | rathe tiṣṭhan nayati vājinaḥ purastāt sataḥ yatra yatra kāmayate = standing in the chariot he leads the horses placed in front wherever he wishes | suṣārathiḥ kalyāṇa-sārathiḥ = good charioteer: of auspicious driving | abhīśūnām mahimānam pūjayāmi = I honour the greatness of the reins | manaḥ paścāt santaḥ anuyacchanti raśmayaḥ = the reins, being behind, follow the mind | dhanuḥ dhanvateḥ gati-karmaṇaḥ vadha-karmaṇaḥ vā = dhanus from dhanv, a verb of going or killing | dhanvanti asmāt iṣavaḥ = arrows run from it | tasya eṣā bhavati = of it there is this verse
17 dhanvanā gāḥ dhanvanā ājim jayema = with the bow may we win cows, with the bow the contest | dhanvanā tīvrāḥ samadaḥ jayema = with the bow may we win the fierce battles | dhanuḥ śatroḥ apakāmam kṛṇoti = the bow makes the enemy’s wish go away | dhanvanā sarvāḥ pradiśaḥ jayema = with the bow may we win all directions | iti sā nigada-vyākhyātā = it is explained by its own recital | samadaḥ samadaḥ vā atteḥ = samada from “eating together” | sammadaḥ vā madateḥ = or sammada from “to rejoice” | jyā jayateḥ vā jināteḥ vā prajāvayati iṣūn iti vā = jyā from “to win”, “to overpower”, or “it urges the arrows” | tasyāḥ eṣā bhavati = of it there is this verse
18 vakṣyantī iva ā ganīganti karṇam = as if about to speak it comes again and again to the ear | priyam sakhāyam pariṣasvajānā = embracing its dear friend | yoṣā iva śiṅkte vitatā adhi dhanvan = like a woman it twangs, stretched on the bow | jyā iyam samane pārayantī = this bowstring carrying across in battle | vakṣyantī iva ā gacchati karṇam priyam iva sakhāyam iṣum pariṣvajamānā = as if about to speak it comes to the ear, embracing the arrow like a dear friend | yoṣā iva śiṅkte śabdam karoti = like a woman it twangs: it makes sound | vitatā adhi dhanuṣi jyā iyam samane saṃgrāme pārayantī pāram nayantī = stretched on the bow, this bowstring in battle carrying across, leading to the far shore | iṣuḥ iṣateḥ gati-karmaṇaḥ vadha-karmaṇaḥ vā = iṣu from iṣ, a verb of going or killing | tasya eṣā bhavati = of it there is this verse
19 suparṇam vaste mṛgaḥ asyāḥ dantaḥ = she is clothed in feathers, the deer her tooth | gobhiḥ sannaddhā patati prasūtā = bound with leather she flies when sent forth | yatra naraḥ sam ca vi ca dravanti = where men run together and apart | tatra asmabhyam iṣavaḥ śarma yaṃsan = there may the arrows give us shelter | suparṇam vaste iti vājān abhipretya = “clothed in feathers”, meaning the feathers of the arrow | mṛgamayaḥ asyāḥ dantaḥ = her tooth is made of antelope-horn | mṛgayateḥ vā = or from “to hunt” | gobhiḥ sannaddhā patati prasūtā iti vyākhyātam = “bound with leather she flies when sent forth” has been explained | yatra narāḥ saṃdravanti ca vidravanti ca tatra asmabhyam iṣavaḥ śarma yacchantu śaraṇam saṃgrāmeṣu = where men run together and apart, there may the arrows give us shelter in battles | aśvājanīm kaśā iti āhuḥ = they call the horse-whip kaśā | kaśā prakāśayati bhayam aśvāya = the whip shows fear to the horse | kṛṣyateḥ vā aṇūbhāvāt = or from “to draw” through becoming thin | vāk punaḥ prakāśayati arthān = and speech shows meanings | khaśayā krośateḥ vā = or from “to cry out” | aśva-kaśāyāḥ eṣā bhavati = this is of the horse-whip
20 ā jaṅghanti sānu eṣām jaghanān upa jighnate = they strike their ridges, they strike the hinder parts | aśvājani pracetasaḥ aśvān samatsu codaya = O whip, wise one, urge the horses in battles | āghnanti sānūni eṣām saraṇāni sakthīni = they strike their ridges, their going parts, their thighs | sakthi sacateḥ = sakthi from “to cling” | āsaktaḥ asmin kāyaḥ = the body clings to it | jaghanāni ca upaghnanti = and they strike the hinder parts | jaghanam jaṅghanyateḥ = jaghana from “to strike repeatedly” | aśvājani pracetasaḥ pravṛddha-cetasaḥ aśvān samatsu samaraṇeṣu saṃgrāmeṣu codaya = O whip of grown mind, urge the horses in the battles, clashes, wars | ulūkhalam uru-karam vā = ulūkhala (mortar) is “wide-making” | ūrdhva-kham vā urkaram vā = or “raised hollow”, or “making strength” | uru me kuru iti abravīt = “make wide for me”, he said | tat ulūkhalam abhavat = that became the mortar | uru-karam vā etat ulūkhalam iti ācakṣate parokṣeṇa = they call it ulūkhala, “wide-maker”, indirectly | iti ca brāhmaṇam = so says a Brāhmaṇa | tasya eṣā bhavati = of it there is this verse
21 yat cit hi tvam gṛhe-gṛhe ulūkhalaka yujyase = since you, little mortar, are yoked in house after house | iha dyumattamam vada jayatām iva dundubhiḥ = here sound most brilliantly, like the drum of conquerors | iti sā nigada-vyākhyātā = it is explained by its own recital
