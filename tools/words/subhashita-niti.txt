# Bhartṛhari, Nītiśatakam — word by word.
1 dik-kāla-ādi-anavacchinna-ananta-cit-mātra-mūrttaye = to the form of pure, endless consciousness, unbounded by place, time and the like | sva-anubhūti-eka-mānāya = whose one proof is one’s own experience | namaḥ = homage | śāntāya = to the peaceful | tejase = to the radiant
2 yām = whom | cintayāmi = I think of | satatam = ever | mayi = toward me | sā = she | viraktā = is indifferent | sā api = and she | anyam = another | icchati = desires | janam = man | saḥ janaḥ = that man | anya-saktaḥ = attached to another | asmat-kṛte = for my sake | tu = but | pariśuṣyati = pines | kācit anyā = some other woman | dhik tām ca tam ca madanam ca imām ca mām ca = shame on her, him, Love, this woman and me
3 boddhāraḥ = those who understand | matsara-grastāḥ = are seized by envy | prabhavaḥ = the powerful | smaya-dūṣitāḥ = spoiled by pride | abodha-upahatāḥ = struck by ignorance | ca anye = and the others | jīrṇam aṅge = it withers in the body | subhāṣitam = a well-said verse
4 ajñaḥ = the ignorant | sukham ārādhyaḥ = is easily pleased | sukha-taram ārādhyate = more easily pleased | viśeṣa-jñaḥ = the man of special knowledge | jñāna-lava-durvidagdham = one half-baked with a scrap of knowledge | brahmā api = even Brahmā | tam naram = that man | na rañjayati = cannot please
5 prasahya = by force | maṇim = the jewel | uddharet = one might take | makara-vaktra-daṃṣṭra-antarāt = from between the teeth in a crocodile’s mouth | samudram api = even the ocean | santaret = one might cross | pracalat-ūrmi-mālā-ākulam = turbulent with garlands of heaving waves | bhujaṅgam api kopitam = even an angry snake | śirasi = on the head | puṣpa-vat = like a flower | dhārayet = one might wear | na tu = but not | pratiniviṣṭa-mūrkha-jana-cittam = the mind of an obstinate fool | ārādhayet = could one win over
6 labheta = one might get | sikatā-su-tailam api = oil even from sand | yatnataḥ = with effort | pīḍayan = by pressing | pibet ca = one might drink | mṛga-tṛṣṇikāsu = in mirages | salilam = water | pipāsā-arditaḥ = tormented by thirst | kadācit api = sometime | paryaṭan = wandering | śaśa-viṣāṇam = a hare’s horn | āsādayet = one might find | na tu = but not | pratiniviṣṭa-mūrkha-jana-cittam = the mind of an obstinate fool | ārādhayet = could one win over
7 vyālam = a rogue elephant | bāla-mṛṇāla-tantubhiḥ = with the fibres of a young lotus-stalk | asau = this man | roddhum = to hold back | samujjṛmbhate = sets about | chettum = to cut | vajra-maṇim = a diamond | śirīṣa-kusuma-prāntena = with the tip of a śirīṣa flower | saṃnahyate = he girds himself | mādhuryam = sweetness | madhu-bindunā = with a drop of honey | racayitum = to make | kṣāra-ambudheḥ = of the salt ocean | īhate = he tries | mūrkhān = fools | yaḥ = who | pratinetum icchati = wishes to lead right | balāt = by force | sūktaiḥ = with fine sayings | sudhā-syandibhiḥ = dripping nectar
8 sva-āyattam = in one’s own power | ekānta-guṇam = wholly good | vidhātrā = by the Creator | vinirmitam = made | chādanam = a covering | ajñatāyāḥ = for ignorance | viśeṣataḥ = especially | sarva-vidām samāje = in an assembly of the all-knowing | vibhūṣaṇam = an ornament | maunam = silence | apaṇḍitānām = for the unlearned
9 yadā = when | kiñcit-jñaḥ aham = I, knowing little | dvipaḥ iva = like an elephant | mada-andhaḥ = blind with rut | samabhavam = became | tadā = then | sarva-jñaḥ asmi iti = “I know everything” | abhavat = was | avaliptam = proud | mama manaḥ = my mind | yadā = when | kiñcit kiñcit = a little by a little | budha-jana-sakāśāt = from the learned | avagatam = was learned | tadā = then | mūrkhaḥ asmi iti = “I am a fool” | jvaraḥ iva = like a fever | madaḥ me vyapagataḥ = my pride left me
10 kṛmi-kula-citam = swarming with worms | lālā-klinnam = wet with saliva | vigandhi = foul | jugupsitam = loathsome | nirupama-rasam = to him of peerless taste | prītyā = gladly | khādan = chewing | nara-asthi = a human bone | nirāmiṣam = without meat | sura-patim api = even Indra | śvā = a dog | pārśva-stham = standing beside | vilokya = seeing | na śaṅkate = is not afraid | na hi gaṇayati = does not reckon | kṣudraḥ jantuḥ = a mean creature | parigraha-phalgutām = the pettiness of what it holds
11 śiraḥ = the head | śārvam = Śiva’s | svargāt = from heaven | paśupati-śirastaḥ = from Śiva’s head | kṣiti-dharam = to the mountain | mahīdhrāt uttuṅgāt = from the lofty mountain | avanim = to the earth | avaneḥ ca api = and from the earth | jaladhim = to the ocean | adhaḥ adhaḥ = lower and lower | gāṅgeyam = the Ganges’ | padam upagatā = has come down | stokam athavā = how little | viveka-bhraṣṭānām = for those fallen from discrimination | bhavati = there is | vinipātaḥ = a fall | śata-mukhaḥ = a hundredfold
12 śakyaḥ = it can be | vārayitum = warded off | jalena = with water | huta-bhuk = fire | chatreṇa = with an umbrella | sūrya-ātapaḥ = the sun’s heat | nāga-indraḥ = a great elephant | niśita-aṅkuśena = with a sharp goad | sa-madaḥ = in rut | daṇḍena = with a stick | go-gardabhau = cow and ass | vyādhiḥ = disease | bheṣaja-saṅgrahaiḥ ca = with collections of medicines | vividhaiḥ mantra-prayogaiḥ = with various mantras | viṣam = poison | sarvasya = for everything | auṣadham asti = there is a remedy | śāstra-vihitam = laid down in the texts | mūrkhasya = for the fool | na asti auṣadham = there is no remedy
13 sāhitya-saṅgīta-kalā-vihīnaḥ = without literature, music and the arts | sākṣāt paśuḥ = plainly an animal | puccha-viṣāṇa-hīnaḥ = without tail or horns | tṛṇam na khādan api = though he eats no grass | jīvamānaḥ = living | tat bhāgadheyam = that is the good fortune | paramam = great | paśūnām = of animals
14 yeṣām = those for whom | na vidyā = no learning | na tapaḥ = no austerity | na dānam = no giving | jñānam na = no knowledge | śīlam na = no conduct | na guṇaḥ = no virtue | na dharmaḥ = no duty | te = they | martya-loke = in the world of mortals | bhuvi = on earth | bhāra-bhūtāḥ = burdens | manuṣya-rūpeṇa = in human form | mṛgāḥ = beasts | caranti = wander
15 varam = better | parvata-durgeṣu = in mountain fastnesses | bhrāntam = to have roamed | vana-caraiḥ saha = with forest-dwellers | na = not | mūrkha-jana-samparkaḥ = the company of fools | sura-indra-bhavaneṣu api = even in the palaces of Indra
16 śāstra-upaskṛta-śabda-sundara-giraḥ = with speech made beautiful by words polished by learning | śiṣya-pradeya-āgamāḥ = with lore to hand on to pupils | vikhyātāḥ = renowned | kavayaḥ = poets | vasanti = live | viṣaye = in the realm | yasya prabhoḥ = of whatever lord | nirdhanāḥ = poor | tat jāḍyam = that is the dullness | vasudhā-adhipasya = of the king | kavayaḥ api = the poets themselves | artham vinā api = though without wealth | īśvarāḥ = lords | kutsyāḥ syuḥ = are to be despised | ku-parīkṣakāḥ = bad assessors | hi = indeed | maṇayaḥ = the gems | yaiḥ = by whom | arghataḥ pātitāḥ = their value was lowered
17 hartuḥ = to a thief | yāti na gocaram = does not come within reach | kim api = some | śam = happiness | puṣṇāti = it gives | yat = which | sarvadā = ever | hi arthibhyaḥ pratipādyamānam = being given to those who ask | aniśam = unceasingly | prāpnoti vṛddhim parām = it grows greatly | kalpa-anteṣu api = even at the world’s end | na prayāti nidhanam = does not perish | vidyā-ākhyam = called learning | antar-dhanam = the inner wealth | yeṣām = those who have | tān prati = toward them | mānam ujjhata = put away pride | nṛpāḥ = O kings | kaḥ taiḥ saha spardhate = who contends with them
18 adhigata-paramārthān = who have gained the highest truth | paṇḍitān = the learned | mā avamaṃsthāḥ = do not despise | tṛṇam iva = like a straw | laghu = light | lakṣmīḥ = wealth | na eva tān saṃruṇaddhi = does not hold them back | abhinava-mada-lekhā-śyāma-gaṇḍa-sthalānām = of those whose temples are dark with fresh lines of rut | bhavati na = is not | visa-tantuḥ = a lotus-fibre | vāraṇam = a restraint | vāraṇānām = for elephants
19 ambhojinī-vana-nivāsa-vilāsam eva = only the pleasure of dwelling in the lotus-beds | haṃsasya = of the swan | hanti nitarām = destroys utterly | kupitaḥ = angered | vidhātā = the Creator | na tu = but not | asya = his | dugdha-jala-bheda-vidhau = in separating milk from water | prasiddhām = renowned | vaidagdhya-kīrtim = fame for skill | apahartum = to take away | asau = he | samarthaḥ = is able
20 keyūrāṇi = armlets | na bhūṣayanti = do not adorn | puruṣam = a man | hārāḥ = necklaces | na candra-ujjvalāḥ = not bright as the moon | na snānam = not bathing | na vilepanam = not unguents | na kusumam = not flowers | na alaṅkṛtāḥ mūrdhajāḥ = nor adorned hair | vāṇī ekā = speech alone | samalaṅkaroti = adorns | puruṣam = a man | yā saṃskṛtā dhāryate = which is cultured and held | kṣīyante khalu = they wear away | bhūṣaṇāni = ornaments | satatam = always | vāk-bhūṣaṇam = the ornament of speech | bhūṣaṇam = is the ornament
