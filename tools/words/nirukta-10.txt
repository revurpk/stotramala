# Nirukta, adhyāya 10 — word by word. Etymologies are given as Yāska states them; examples quoted for form are glossed as examples.
1 atha ataḥ madhya-sthānāḥ devatāḥ = now therefore the deities of the middle place | tāsām vāyuḥ prathama-āgāmī bhavati = of them Vāyu comes first | vāyuḥ vāteḥ = vāyu from “to blow” | veteḥ vā syāt gati-karmaṇaḥ = or from vī, a verb of going | eteḥ iti sthaulāṣṭhīviḥ = from i “to go”, says Sthaulāṣṭhīvi | anarthakaḥ vakāraḥ = the v is meaningless | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
2 vāyo ā yāhi darśata ime somāḥ araṃkṛtāḥ = come, O Vāyu, lovely one; these somas are prepared | teṣām pāhi śrudhī havam = drink of them, hear our call | vāyo ā yāhi darśanīya ime somāḥ araṃkṛtāḥ alaṃkṛtāḥ = come, O Vāyu, lovely: these somas are prepared, adorned | teṣām piba śṛṇu naḥ hvānam iti = drink of them, hear our call | kam anyam madhyamāt devam evam avakṣyat = what other deity of the middle would he have addressed so | tasya eṣā aparā bhavati = of him there is this other verse
3 āsasrāṇāsaḥ śavasānam accha indram = coming swiftly toward Indra, the mighty | sucakre rathyāsaḥ aśvāḥ = the chariot-horses to the well-wheeled | abhi śravaḥ ṛjyantaḥ vaheyuḥ = they, straight-going, might carry him to food | nū cit nu vāyoḥ amṛtam vi dasyet = may the immortal drink of Vāyu not be wasted | āsasravāṃsaḥ = those who have come running | abhibalāyamānam indram = Indra growing mighty | kalyāṇa-cakre rathe yogāya = for yoking to the well-wheeled chariot | rathyāḥ aśvāḥ rathasya voḍhāraḥ = rathya horses are the bearers of the chariot | ṛjyantaḥ ṛju-gāminaḥ annam abhivaheyuḥ = ṛjyanta: straight-going, they might carry him to food | navam ca purāṇam ca = both new and old | śravaḥ iti anna-nāma = śravas is a name of food | śrūyate iti sataḥ = from “it is heard” | vāyoḥ ca asya bhakṣaḥ yathā na vidasyet iti = and Vāyu’s share, that it may not be wasted | indra-pradhānā iti eke = some say it is chiefly about Indra | naighaṇṭukam vāyu-karma = Vāyu’s act is Naighaṇṭuka | ubhaya-pradhānā iti aparam = another says it is chiefly about both | varuṇaḥ vṛṇoti iti sataḥ = Varuṇa: from “he covers” | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
4 nīcīna-bāram varuṇaḥ kabandham = Varuṇa the cloud with its opening downward | pra sasarja rodasī antarikṣam = sent forth heaven, earth and mid-air | tena viśvasya bhuvanasya rājā = by it he is king of the whole world | yavam na vṛṣṭiḥ vi unatti bhūma = as rain wets barley, he wets the earth | nīcīna-dvāram varuṇaḥ = Varuṇa with a downward door | kabandham megham = kabandha is a cloud | kavanam udakam bhavati = kavana is water | tat asmin dhīyate = it is placed in it | udakam api kabandham ucyate = water too is called kabandha | bandhiḥ anibhṛtatve kam anibhṛtam ca = bandhi in the sense of not being still, and ka is unstill | prasṛjati dyāvāpṛthivyau ca antarikṣam ca mahattvena = he sends forth heaven, earth and mid-air by greatness | tena sarvasya bhuvanasya rājā yavam iva vṛṣṭiḥ vyunatti bhūmim = by it he is king of all the world, and rain wets the earth like barley | tasya eṣā aparā bhavati = of him there is this other verse
5 tam u su samanā girā = him then with a united song | pitṝṇām ca manmabhiḥ = and with the thoughts of the fathers | nābhākasya praśastibhiḥ = with the praises of Nābhāka | yaḥ sindhūnām upodaye saptasvasā saḥ madhyamaḥ = who at the rising of the rivers, seven-sistered, that middle one | nabhantām anyake same = may all the others be destroyed | tam su abhiṣṭaumi samānayā girā gītyā stutyā = I praise him well with a united song, a chant, a praise | pitṝṇām ca mananīyaiḥ stomaiḥ = and with the thoughtful hymns of the fathers | nābhākasya praśastibhiḥ ṛṣiḥ nābhākaḥ babhūva = by the praises of Nābhāka, the seer | yaḥ syandamānānām āsām upodaye saptasvāsāram enam āha vāgbhiḥ saḥ madhyamaḥ iti nirucyate = he who at the rising of these flowing ones, whom he calls seven-sistered with his words, is explained as the middle one | atha eṣaḥ eva bhavati nabhantām anyake same = then there is this very one: “may all the others be destroyed” | mā bhūvan anyake sarve ye naḥ dviṣanti durdhiyaḥ pāpadhiyaḥ pāpa-saṅkalpāḥ = let all others be not, who hate us, evil-minded, of evil thought | rudraḥ rauti iti sataḥ = Rudra: from “he roars” | rorūyamāṇaḥ dravati iti vā = or “roaring he runs” | rodayateḥ vā = or from “to make weep” | yat arudat tat rudrasya rudratvam iti kāṭhakam = “because he wept, that is Rudra’s Rudra-ness”, says the Kāṭhaka | yat arodīt tat rudrasya rudratvam iti hāridravikam = “because he wailed”, says the Hāridravika | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
6 imāḥ rudrāya sthira-dhanvane giraḥ = these songs for Rudra of firm bow | kṣipra-iṣave devāya svadhā-vne = for the swift-arrowed god, rich in his own power | aṣāḍhāya sahamānāya vedhase = for the unconquered, the overcomer, the ordainer | tigma-āyudhāya bharata śṛṇotu naḥ = for the sharp-weaponed: bring them; may he hear us | imāḥ rudrāya dṛḍha-dhanvane giraḥ kṣipra-iṣave devāya annavate = these songs for Rudra of firm bow, the swift-arrowed god, rich in food | aṣāḍhāya anyaiḥ sahamānāya vidhātre tigma-āyudhāya = for the unconquered by others, the overcomer, the ordainer, the sharp-weaponed | bharata śṛṇotu naḥ = bring them; may he hear us | tigmam tejateḥ utsāha-karmaṇaḥ = tigma from tij, a verb of exertion | āyudham āyodhanāt = āyudha from “fighting” | tasya eṣā aparā bhavati = of him there is this other verse
7 yā te didyut avasṛṣṭā divaḥ pari = your lightning that is sent down from heaven | kṣmayā carati pari sā vṛṇaktu naḥ = that moves over the earth, may it pass us by | sahasram te sv-apivāta bheṣajā = a thousand are your remedies, O good protector | mā naḥ tokeṣu tanayeṣu rīriṣaḥ = do not harm our children and grandchildren | yā te didyut avasṛṣṭā divaḥ pari divaḥ adhi = your lightning sent down from heaven: from above heaven | didyut dyuteḥ vā dyotateḥ vā = didyut from “to shine” | kṣmayā carati kṣmā pṛthivī tasyām carati tayā carati = it moves over the earth: kṣmā is the earth, it moves on her | vikṣmāpayantī carati iti vā = or it moves making the earth tremble | parivṛṇaktu naḥ sā = may she pass us by | sahasram te svāpi-vacana bhaiṣajyāni = a thousand are your remedies, O good-speaker | mā naḥ tvam putreṣu ca pautreṣu ca rīriṣaḥ = do not harm our sons and grandsons | tokam tudyateḥ = toka from “to prick” | tanayam tanoteḥ = tanaya from “to extend” | agniḥ api rudraḥ ucyate = Agni too is called Rudra | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
8 jarā-bodha tat viviḍḍhi = O Jarābodha, attend to that | viśe-viśe yajñiyāya = for each clan, for the worshipful | stomam rudrāya dṛśīkam = a hymn for Rudra, lovely to see | jarā stutiḥ jarateḥ stuti-karmaṇaḥ = jarā is praise, from jṛ, a verb of praising | tām bodha tayā bodhayitar iti vā = awaken to it, or O awakener by it | tat viviḍḍhi tat kuru = attend to that: do that | manuṣyasya manuṣyasya yajanāya = for each man’s sacrifice | stomam rudrāya darśanīyam = a hymn to Rudra, lovely to see | indraḥ irām dṛṇāti iti vā = Indra: he splits the food | irām dadāti iti vā irām dadhāti iti vā = or he gives food, or he places food | irām dārayate iti vā irām dhārayate iti vā = or he divides food, or holds food | indave dravati iti vā = or he runs to the drop | indau ramate iti vā = or he delights in the drop | indhe bhūtāni iti vā = or he kindles beings | tat yat enam prāṇaiḥ samaindhan tat indrasya indratvam iti vijñāyate = “because they kindled him with breaths, that is Indra’s Indra-ness”, so it is known | idam karaṇāt iti āgrāyaṇaḥ = from “making this”, says Āgrāyaṇa | idam darśanāt iti aupamanyavaḥ = from “seeing this”, says Aupamanyava | indateḥ aiśvarya-karmaṇaḥ = from ind, a verb of lordship | indan śatrūṇām dārayitā vā drāvayitā vā ādarayitā ca yajvanām = one who splits or drives off enemies and respects the sacrificers | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
9 adardaruḥ utsam asṛjaḥ vi khāni = you split the spring, you opened the openings | tvam arṇavān badbadhānān aramṇāḥ = you released the flooding, pressed waters | mahāntam indra parvatam vi yat vaḥ = O Indra, the great mountain, when you | sṛjaḥ vi dhārāḥ ava dānavam han = let loose the streams, you struck down the Dānava | adṛṇāḥ utsam = you split the spring | utsaḥ utsaraṇāt vā utsadanāt vā utsyandanāt vā unatteḥ vā = utsa from “running out”, “sitting out”, “flowing out”, or “wetting” | vyasṛjaḥ asya khāni = you opened its openings | tvam arṇavān arṇasvataḥ = you released those with floods | etān mādhyamikān saṃstyāyān bābadhyamānān aramṇāḥ = these mid-air ones gathered and pressed, you released | ramṇātiḥ saṃyamana-karmā visarjana-karmā vā = ramṇā is a verb of restraining or letting go | mahāntam indra parvatam megham yat vyavṛṇoḥ = O Indra, the great mountain, the cloud, which you uncovered | vyasṛjaḥ asya dhārāḥ = you let loose its streams | avāhan enam dānavam dāna-karmāṇam = you struck down this Dānava, doer of giving | tasya eṣā aparā bhavati = of him there is this other verse
10 yaḥ jātaḥ eva prathamaḥ manasvān = who, as soon as born, was first, thoughtful | devaḥ devān kratunā pari abhūṣat = the god surpassed the gods by his will | yasya śuṣmāt rodasī abhyasetām = from whose force the two worlds feared | nṛmṇasya mahnā saḥ janāsaḥ indraḥ = by the greatness of his manliness: he, O people, is Indra | yaḥ jātaḥ eva prathamaḥ manasvī devaḥ devān = who, as soon as born, was first, thoughtful, a god among gods | kratunā karmaṇā paryabhavat paryagṛhṇāt paryarakṣat atyakrāmat iti vā = by will, by act, he surpassed, seized round, protected round, or overstepped | yasya balāt dyāvāpṛthivyau api abibhītām = from whose strength heaven and earth too were afraid | nṛmṇasya mahnā balasya mahattvena = by the greatness of manliness, by greatness of strength | saḥ janāsaḥ indraḥ iti = he, O people, is Indra | ṛṣeḥ dṛṣṭa-arthasya prītiḥ bhavati ākhyāna-saṃyuktā = the seer who has seen the meaning has pleasure joined with a story | parjanyaḥ tṛpeḥ ādi-anta-viparītasya = Parjanya from tṛp with beginning and end reversed | tarpayitā janyaḥ paraḥ jetā vā paraḥ janayitā vā prārjayitā vā rasānām = the satisfier, begotten, the far conqueror, the far begetter, the producer of juices | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
11 vi vṛkṣān hanti uta hanti rakṣasaḥ = he strikes the trees and strikes the demons | viśvam bibhāya bhuvanam mahā-vadhāt = the whole world fears the great weapon | uta anāgāḥ īṣate vṛṣṇya-vataḥ = even the guiltless flee from the one with virile power | yat parjanyaḥ stanayan hanti duṣkṛtaḥ = when Parjanya thundering strikes the evildoers | vihanti vṛkṣān vihanti ca rakṣāṃsi = he strikes the trees and strikes the demons | sarvāṇi ca asmāt bhūtāni bibhyati mahā-vadhāt = and all beings fear him because of the great weapon | mahān hi asya vadhaḥ = for his weapon is great | api anaparādhaḥ bhītaḥ palāyate = even the blameless, afraid, flees | varṣa-karmavataḥ yat parjanyaḥ stanayan hanti duṣkṛtaḥ pāpakṛtaḥ = of the one acting by rain, when Parjanya thundering strikes the evildoers, the sinners | bṛhaspatiḥ bṛhataḥ pātā vā pālayitā vā = Bṛhaspati: protector or guardian of the great | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
12 aśnā apinaddham madhu pari apaśyan = they saw the honey bound by the stone | matsyam na dīne udani kṣiyantam = like a fish dwelling in shallow water | niḥ taṭ jabhāra camasam na vṛkṣāt = Bṛhaspati took it out like a cup from a tree | bṛhaspatiḥ viraveṇa vikṛtya = by cleaving it with a cry | aśanavatā meghena apinaddham madhu paryapaśyat = he saw the honey covered by the stone-bearing cloud | matsyam iva dīne udake nivasantam = like a fish dwelling in shallow water | nirjahāra tat camasam iva vṛkṣāt = he took it out like a cup from a tree | camasaḥ kasmāt = why “camasa” | camanti asmin iti = they sip from it | bṛhaspatiḥ viraveṇa śabdena vikṛtya = Bṛhaspati, by cleaving with a cry, a sound | brahmaṇaspatiḥ brahmaṇaḥ pātā vā pālayitā vā = Brahmaṇaspati: protector of prayer | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
13 aśmāsyam avatam brahmaṇaspatiḥ = Brahmaṇaspati the stone-mouthed well | madhu-dhāram abhi yam ojasā atṛṇat = which with force he split, flowing with honey | tam eva viśve papire svardṛśaḥ = all the sun-seers drank it | bahu sākam sisicuḥ utsam udriṇam = together they poured much from the spring with its waters | aśanavantam āsyandanavantam avātitam brahmaṇaspatiḥ madhu-dhāram = the stone-having, flowing, unblown, Brahmaṇaspati, honey-streaming | abhi yam ojasā balena abhyatṛṇat tam eva sarve pibanti raśmayaḥ sūrya-dṛśaḥ = which he split by force, strength, that all rays drink, the sun-seers | bahu enam saha siñcanti utsam udriṇam udakavantam = together they pour much of it, the spring with water
14 kṣetrasya patiḥ = Kṣetrapati | kṣetram kṣiyateḥ nivāsa-karmaṇaḥ = kṣetra from kṣi, a verb of dwelling | tasya pātā vā pālayitā vā = its protector or guardian | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
15 kṣetrasya patinā vayam hitena iva jayāmasi = with the lord of the field, as with a friend, we win | gām aśvam poṣayitnu ā saḥ naḥ mṛḍāti īdṛśe = cow, horse, a nourisher: may he be gracious to us in such a case | kṣetrasya patinā vayam suhitena iva jayāmaḥ = with the lord of the field, as with a good friend, we win | gām aśvam puṣṭam poṣayitṛ ca āhara iti = bring the cow, the horse, nourished and nourishing | saḥ naḥ mṛḍāti īdṛśe balena vā dhanena vā = may he be gracious to us in such a case, by strength or by wealth | mṛḍatiḥ dāna-karmā = mṛḍ is a verb of giving | tasya eṣā aparā bhavati = of him there is this other verse
16 kṣetrasya pate madhumantam ūrmim = O lord of the field, a honeyed wave | dhenuḥ iva payaḥ asmāsu dhukṣva = like a cow, milk it for us | madhu-ścutam ghṛtam iva supūtam = dripping honey, like well-purified ghee | ṛtasya naḥ patayaḥ mṛḍayantu = may the lords of order be gracious to us | kṣetrasya pate madhumantam ūrmim dhenuḥ iva payaḥ asmāsu dhukṣva iti = O lord of the field, milk a honeyed wave for us as a cow its milk | madhu-ścutam ghṛtam iva udakam supūtam = water dripping honey, like well-purified ghee | ṛtasya naḥ pātāraḥ vā pālayitāraḥ vā mṛḍayantu = may our protectors or guardians of order be gracious | mṛḍayatiḥ upadayā-karmā pūjā-karmā vā = mṛḍay is a verb of sharing or honouring | tat yat samānyām ṛci samāna-abhivyāhāram bhavati tat jāmi bhavati iti ekam = where in the same verse there is repeated utterance, that is jāmi, says one | madhumantam madhu-ścutam iti yathā = as “honeyed, dripping honey” | yat eva samāne pāde samāna-abhivyāhāram bhavati tat jāmi bhavati iti aparam = another: where in the same foot there is repeated utterance, that is jāmi | hiraṇya-rūpaḥ saḥ hiraṇya-saṃdṛk iti yathā = as “of golden form, of golden look” | yathā kathā ca viśeṣaḥ ajāmi bhavati iti aparam = another: wherever there is a difference, it is ajāmi | maṇḍūkāḥ iva udakāt maṇḍūkāḥ udakāt iva iti yathā = as “like frogs from water” | vāstoṣpatiḥ = Vāstoṣpati | vāstuḥ vasateḥ nivāsa-karmaṇaḥ = vāstu from vas, a verb of dwelling | tasya pātā vā pālayitā vā = its protector or guardian | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
17 amīva-hā vāstoṣpate viśvā rūpāṇi āviśan = O Vāstoṣpati, destroyer of disease, entering all forms | sakhā su-śevaḥ edhi naḥ = be our friend, kind to us | abhyamana-hā vāstoṣpate sarvāṇi rūpāṇi āviśan sakhā naḥ susukhaḥ bhava = O Vāstoṣpati, destroyer of ills, entering all forms, be our friend, most pleasant | śevaḥ iti sukha-nāma = śeva is a name of happiness | śiṣyateḥ = from “to remain” | vakāraḥ nāma-karaṇaḥ = the v makes the noun | antasthā-antara-upaliṅgī vibhāṣita-guṇaḥ = it has a semivowel inserted, with a variable quality | śivam iti api asya bhavati = “śiva” also belongs to it | yat yat rūpam kāmayate tat tat devatā bhavati = whatever form one wishes, that becomes the deity | rūpam rūpam maghavā bobhavīti = the bounteous one becomes form after form | iti api nigamaḥ bhavati = so too a text | vācaspatiḥ vācaḥ pātā vā pālayitā vā = Vācaspati: protector or guardian of speech | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
18 punaḥ ehi vācaspate devena manasā saha = come again, Vācaspati, with the divine mind | vasoṣpate ni ramaya mayi eva tanvam mama = O lord of wealth, make my body rest in me alone | iti sā nigada-vyākhyātā = it is explained by its own recital | apām napāt tanūnaptrā vyākhyātaḥ = Apāṃ Napāt is explained by Tanūnapāt | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
19 yaḥ anidhmaḥ dīdayat apsu antaḥ = who without fuel shines within the waters | yam viprāsaḥ īḷate adhvareṣu = whom the wise praise at the rites | apām napāt madhumatīḥ apaḥ dāḥ = O son of waters, give honeyed waters | yābhiḥ indraḥ vāvṛdhe vīryāya = by which Indra grew for heroism | yaḥ anidhmaḥ dīdayat dīpyate abhyantaram apsu yam medhāvinaḥ stuvanti yajñeṣu = who, fuel-less, shines within in the waters, whom the wise praise in sacrifices | saḥ apām napāt madhumatīḥ apaḥ dehi abhiṣavāya = that Apāṃ Napāt, give honeyed waters for pressing | yābhiḥ indraḥ vardhate vīryāya vīra-karmaṇe = by which Indra grows for heroism, heroic action | yamaḥ yacchati iti sataḥ = Yama: from “he restrains” | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse

42 pra tat voceyam bhavyāya indave = I would proclaim that to the worthy drop | havyaḥ na yaḥ iṣavān manma rejati = like one to be invoked, who, rich in food, shakes our thought | rakṣaḥ-hā manma rejati = the demon-slayer shakes our thought | svayam saḥ asmat ā nidaḥ vadhaiḥ ajeta durmatim = may he himself drive from us the reviler and the ill-minded with blows | ava srave agha-śaṃsaḥ avataram ava kṣudram iva srave = may the proclaimer of evil flow down, lower down, like a small stream | prabravīmi tat bhavyāya indave = I proclaim that to the worthy drop | havanārhaḥ iva = like one worth calling | yaḥ iṣavān annavān kāmavān vā = who is rich in food, or desire | mananāni ca naḥ rejayati = and makes our thoughts tremble | rakṣaḥ-hā ca balena rejayati = and, a demon-slayer, makes tremble by strength | svayam saḥ asmat abhininditāram = may he himself drive the reviler of us | vadhaiḥ ajeta durmatim = with blows the ill-minded | avasravet agha-śaṃsaḥ = may the proclaimer of evil flow down | tataḥ ca avataram kṣudram iva avasravet = and further down, like a small one flow down | abhyāse bhūyāṃsam artham manyante yathā aho darśanīya aho darśanīya iti = in repetition they take a greater sense, as “oh, lovely to see, oh, lovely to see” | tat parucchepasya śīlam = that is Parucchepa’s habit | parucchepaḥ ṛṣiḥ parvavat śepaḥ = Parucchepa is the seer: one whose organ has joints | paruṣi-paruṣi śepaḥ asya iti vā = or one who has an organ in every joint | iti imāni saptaviṃśatiḥ devatā-nāmadheyāni anukrāntāni = thus these twenty-seven names of deities have been gone through | sūkta-bhāñji havir-bhāñji = sharers of hymns, sharers of offerings | teṣām etāni a-havir-bhāñji = of them these are non-sharers of offerings | venaḥ asunītiḥ ṛtaḥ induḥ = Vena, Asunīti, Ṛta, Indu | prajāpatiḥ prajānām pātā vā pālayitā vā = Prajāpati: protector or guardian of creatures | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
43 prajāpate na tvat etāni anyaḥ = O Prajāpati, none other than you | viśvā jātāni pari tā babhūva = has encompassed all these born things | yat-kāmāḥ te juhumaḥ tat naḥ astu = may what we wish when we offer to you be ours | vayam syāma patayaḥ rayīṇām = may we be lords of riches | prajāpate na hi tvat etāni anyaḥ sarvāṇi jātāni tāni paribabhūva = O Prajāpati, none other than you has encompassed all these born things | yat-kāmāḥ te juhumaḥ tat naḥ astu vayam syāma patayaḥ rayīṇām iti āśīḥ = what we wish when offering to you, may it be ours; may we be lords of riches: so a blessing | ahiḥ vyākhyātaḥ = ahi has been explained | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
44 abjām ukthaiḥ ahim gṛṇīṣe = you praise the water-born snake with hymns | budhne nadīnām rajaḥsu ṣīdan = sitting at the base of the rivers in the realms | apsu-jām ukthaiḥ ahim gṛṇīṣe budhne nadīnām rajassu udakeṣu sīdan = you praise the snake born in waters with hymns, sitting at the base of the rivers, in the realms, in the waters | budhnam antarikṣam = budhna is the mid-air | baddhāḥ asmin dhṛtāḥ āpaḥ iti vā = or the waters are bound and held in it | idam api itarat budhnam etasmāt eva = this other budhna is from this | baddhāḥ asmin dhṛtāḥ prāṇāḥ iti = breaths are bound and held in it | yaḥ ahiḥ saḥ budhnyaḥ = the snake is the Budhnya | budhnam antarikṣam tan-nivāsāt = budhna is the mid-air, from his dwelling there | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
45 mā naḥ ahiḥ budhnyaḥ riṣe dhāt = let not Ahi Budhnya give us to harm | mā yajñaḥ asya sridhat ṛta-āyoḥ = let not the sacrifice of this one lacking order fail | mā ca naḥ ahiḥ budhnyaḥ reṣaṇāya dhāt = and let not Ahi Budhnya give us to injury | mā asya yajñaḥ akhā ca sridhat yajña-kāmasya = let not the sacrifice of the sacrifice-desiring fail, nor the digging | suparṇaḥ vyākhyātaḥ = suparṇa has been explained | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
46 ekaḥ suparṇaḥ saḥ samudram ā viveśa = one fair-winged one entered the sea | saḥ idam viśvam bhuvanam vi caṣṭe = he looks over all this world | tam pākena manasā apaśyam antitaḥ = I saw him with ripe mind from near | tam mātā reḷhi saḥ u reḷhi mātaram = the mother licks him and he licks the mother | ekaḥ suparṇaḥ saḥ samudram āviśati = one fair-winged one enters the sea | saḥ imāni sarvāṇi bhūtāni abhivipaśyati = he looks over all these beings | tam pākena manasā apaśyam antitaḥ iti ṛṣeḥ dṛṣṭa-arthasya prītiḥ bhavati ākhyāna-saṃyuktā = “I saw him with ripe mind from near”: the seer who has seen the meaning has joy joined with a story | tam mātā reḷhi vāk eṣā mādhyamikā = the mother licks him: this is the mid-air speech | saḥ u mātaram reḷhi = and he licks the mother | purūravāḥ bahudhā rorūyate = Purūravas is much roaring | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
47 sam asmin jāyamāne āsata gnāḥ = the women together stayed near him as he was born | uta īm avardhan nadyaḥ svagūrtāḥ = and the self-moving rivers made him grow | mahe yat tvā purūravaḥ raṇāya = O Purūravas, that for great delight | avardhayan dasyu-hatyāya devāḥ = the gods made you grow for the slaying of the dasyu | samāsata asmin jāyamāne = they sat together near him as he was born | gnāḥ gamanāt āpaḥ devapatnyaḥ vā = gnā: waters from going, or the gods’ wives | api ca enam avardhayan nadyaḥ svagūrtāḥ svayaṃ-gāminyaḥ = and the self-moving rivers made him grow | mahate ca yat tvā purūravaḥ raṇāya ramaṇīyāya saṃgrāmāya avardhayan dasyu-hatyāya ca devāḥ devāḥ = and, O Purūravas, for great delight, for the delightful battle, the gods made you grow, and for the slaying of the dasyu
