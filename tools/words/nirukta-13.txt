# Nirukta, adhyāya 13 — word by word. Etymologies are given as Yāska states them; examples quoted for form are glossed as examples.
1 atha imāḥ atistutayaḥ iti ācakṣate = now they call these the over-praises | api vā saṃpratyayaḥ eva syāt māhābhāgyāt devatāyāḥ = or it may be mere assurance, from the greatness of the deity | saḥ agnim eva prathamam āha = he addresses Agni first | tvam agne dyubhiḥ tvam āśuśukṣaṇiḥ = you, Agni, by the days, you the quick-kindler | iti yathā etasmin sūkte = as in this hymn | na hi tvat āre nimiṣaḥ ca neśe = for none can rule a wink beyond you | iti varuṇasya = this is of Varuṇa | atha eṣā indrasya = now this one is of Indra
2 yat dyāvaḥ indra te śatam śatam bhūmīḥ uta syuḥ = if, O Indra, you had a hundred heavens and a hundred earths | na tvā vajrin sahasram sūryāḥ anu = not a thousand suns would follow you, O thunderbolt-bearer | na jātam aṣṭa rodasī = not the born, nor the two worlds | yadi te indra śatam divaḥ śatam bhūmayaḥ pratimānāni syuḥ = if, Indra, a hundred heavens and a hundred earths were your counterparts | na tvā vajrin sahasram api sūryāḥ na dyāvāpṛthivyau api abhyaśnuvītām iti = not even a thousand suns, nor heaven and earth, would reach you, O thunderbolt-bearer | atha eṣā ādityasya = now this one is of the Sun
3 yat udañcaḥ vṛṣākape gṛham indra ajagantana = when, Vṛṣākapi, you came northward, to Indra’s house | kva sya pulvaghaḥ mṛgaḥ = where is that much-slaying beast | kam agan janayopanaḥ = to what place did the begetter of men go | viśvasmāt indraḥ uttaraḥ = Indra is higher than all | yat udañcaḥ vṛṣākape gṛham indra ajagamata = when, Vṛṣākapi, you came northward to Indra’s house | kva sya pulvaghaḥ mṛgaḥ kva saḥ bahvādī mṛgaḥ = where is that much-slaying beast, where that much-eating beast | mṛgaḥ mārṣṭeḥ gati-karmaṇaḥ = mṛga from mṛj, a verb of seeking | kam agamat deśam janayopanaḥ = to what place did the begetter go | sarvasmāt yaḥ indraḥ uttaraḥ tam etat brūmaḥ ādityam = we say this of that Indra who is higher than all: the Sun | atha eṣā āditya-raśmīnām = now this one is of the Sun’s rays
4 vi hi sotoḥ asṛkṣata na indram devam amaṃsata = they set forth apart for pressing and did not think of the god Indra | yatra amadat vṛṣākapiḥ aryaḥ puṣṭeṣu mat-sakhā = where Vṛṣākapi rejoiced, the lord, among the fed, my friend | viśvasmāt indraḥ uttaraḥ = Indra is higher than all | vyasṛkṣata hi prasavāya na ca indram devam amaṃsata = they went apart for impelling and did not think of the god Indra | yatra amādyat vṛṣākapiḥ aryaḥ īśvaraḥ puṣṭeṣu poṣeṣu = where Vṛṣākapi rejoiced, the lord, among the fed, the nourishments | matsakhā mama sakhā madana-sakhā = my friend: friend of exhilaration | ye naḥ sakhāyaḥ taiḥ saha iti vā = or “with those who are our friends” | sarvasmāt yaḥ indraḥ uttaraḥ tam etat brūmaḥ ādityam = we say this of that Indra who is higher than all: the Sun | atha eṣā aśvinoḥ = now this one is of the Aśvins
5 sṛṇyā iva jarbharī turpharītū = like two goads, bearing, striking | naitośā iva turpharī parpharīkā = like Naitośa’s two, striking, bearing | udanyajā iva jemanā maderū = like two water-born, victorious, gladdening | tā me jarāyu ajaram marāyu = those to me the afterbirth, unaging, mortal | sṛṇyā iva iti dvi-vidhā sṛṇiḥ bhavati bhartā ca hantā ca = “like goads”: a goad is of two kinds, bearer and striker | tathā aśvinau ca api bhartārau = so the Aśvins too are bearers | jarbharī bhartārau iti arthaḥ = jarbharī means the two bearers | turpharītū hantārau = turpharītū the two strikers | naitośā iva turpharī parpharīkā = like Naitośa’s two, striking, bearing | nitośasya apatyam naitośam = Naitośa is the offspring of Nitośa | naitośā iva turpharī kṣipra-hantārau = like Naitośa’s two, swift strikers | udanyajā iva jemanā maderū = like two water-born, victorious, gladdening | udanyajā iva iti udaka-je iva ratne sāmudre cāndramase vā = “like two water-born”: like two jewels, of the sea or of the moon | jemane jayamāne = jemanā: the two conquering | jemanā maderū = the two victorious, gladdening | tā me jarāyu ajaram marāyu = those to me the afterbirth, unaging, mortal | etat jarāyu-jam śarīram śaradam ajīrṇam = this body born of the afterbirth, a year (autumn) unwasted | atha eṣā somasya = now this one is of Soma
6 taratsaḥ mandī dhāvati dhārā sutasya andhasaḥ = the gladdening Taratsa runs, a stream of pressed food | taratsaḥ mandī dhāvati = Taratsa the gladdening runs | tarati saḥ pāpam sarvam mandī yaḥ stauti dhāvati gacchati ūrdhvām gatim = he crosses all evil, the gladdened one who praises, runs, goes the upward way | dhārā sutasya andhasaḥ dhārayā abhiṣutasya somasya mantra-pūtasya vācā stutasya = by the stream of the pressed food: of the soma pressed with a stream, purified by mantra, praised by speech | atha eṣā yajñasya = now this one is of the sacrifice
7 catvāri śṛṅgā trayaḥ asya pādāḥ = four horns, three feet has he | dve śīrṣe sapta hastāsaḥ asya = two heads, seven hands | tridhā baddhaḥ vṛṣabhaḥ roravīti = bound threefold, the bull roars | mahaḥ devaḥ martyān ā viveśa = the great god has entered mortals | catvāri śṛṅgā iti vedāḥ vai ete uktāḥ = “four horns”: these are said to be the Vedas | trayaḥ asya pādāḥ iti savanāni trīṇi = “three feet”: the three pressings | dve śīrṣe prāyaṇīya-udayanīye = “two heads”: the Prāyaṇīya and Udayanīya | sapta hastāsaḥ sapta chandāṃsi = “seven hands”: the seven metres | tridhā baddhaḥ tredhā baddhaḥ mantra-brāhmaṇa-kalpaiḥ = bound threefold: bound in three by mantra, Brāhmaṇa and Kalpa | vṛṣabhaḥ roravīti roravaṇam asya savana-krameṇa ṛgbhiḥ yajurbhiḥ sāmabhiḥ yat enam ṛgbhiḥ śaṃsanti yajurbhiḥ yajanti sāmabhiḥ stuvanti = the bull roars: his roaring is in order of the pressings, with ṛcs, yajuses and sāmans, since they recite him with ṛcs, sacrifice with yajuses, praise with sāmans | mahaḥ devaḥ iti eṣaḥ hi mahān devaḥ yat yajñaḥ martyān āviveśa iti = “the great god”: for this sacrifice is the great god who has entered mortals | eṣaḥ hi manuṣyān āviśati yajanāya = for he enters men for sacrificing | tasya uttarā bhūyase nirvacanāya = the next is for fuller explanation
8 svaḥ yantaḥ na apekṣanta ā dyām rohanti rodasī = those going to heaven do not look back; they mount the sky and the two worlds | yajñam ye viśvatodhāram suvidvāṃsaḥ vitenire = who, knowing well, spread the sacrifice that upholds on all sides | svar gacchantaḥ ījānāḥ vā na īkṣante = those going to heaven, the sacrificers, do not look back | te amum eva lokam gatavantam īkṣantam iti = they look only at that world they have reached | ā dyām rohanti rodasī = they mount the sky and the two worlds | yajñam ye viśvatodhāram sarvatodhāram suvidvāṃsaḥ vitenire iti = who, knowing well, have spread the sacrifice that upholds on all sides | atha eṣā vācaḥ pravalhitā iva = now this one is as if of speech, enigmatic
9 catvāri vāk parimitā padāni = speech is measured in four steps | tāni viduḥ brāhmaṇāḥ ye manīṣiṇaḥ = those brahmins who are wise know them | guhā trīṇi nihitā na iṅgayanti = three are laid in a hidden place and do not stir | turīyam vācaḥ manuṣyāḥ vadanti = the fourth of speech men speak | catvāri vācaḥ parimitāni padāni = four steps of speech are measured | tāni viduḥ brāhmaṇāḥ ye medhāvinaḥ = those wise brahmins know them | guhāyām trīṇi nihitāni na artham vedayante = three laid in a hidden place do not make meaning known | guhā gūhateḥ = guhā from “to hide” | turīyam tvarateḥ = turīya from “to hasten” | katamāni tāni catvāri padāni = which are those four steps | oṃkāraḥ mahāvyāhṛtayaḥ ca iti ārṣam = the syllable Om and the great utterances: so says the seer’s tradition | nāma-ākhyāte ca upasarga-nipātāḥ ca iti vaiyākaraṇāḥ = noun and verb, prefixes and particles, say the grammarians | mantraḥ kalpaḥ brāhmaṇam caturthī vyāvahārikī iti yājñikāḥ = mantra, Kalpa, Brāhmaṇa, and the fourth, the worldly speech, say the ritualists | ṛcaḥ yajūṃṣi sāmāni caturthī vyāvahārikī iti nairuktāḥ = ṛcs, yajuses, sāmans and the fourth, the worldly, say the etymologists | sarpāṇām vāk vayasām kṣudrasya sarīsṛpasya caturthī vyāvahārikī iti eke = some say the fourth, the worldly, is the speech of snakes, birds, small creeping things | paśuṣu tūṇaveṣu mṛgeṣu ātmani ca iti ātma-pravādāḥ = in beasts, in drums, in wild animals and in the self: so the doctrines of the self | atha api brāhmaṇam bhavati = also there is a Brāhmaṇa | sā vai vāk sṛṣṭā caturdhā vyabhavat = that speech, created, became fourfold | eṣu eva lokeṣu trīṇi paśuṣu turīyam = three in these worlds, the fourth in beasts | yā pṛthivyām sā agnau sā rathantare = what is on earth is in Agni, in the Rathantara | yā antarikṣe sā vāyau sā vāmadevye = what is in the mid-air is in Vāyu, in the Vāmadevya | yā divi sā āditye sā bṛhati sā stanayitnau = what is in heaven is in the Sun, in the Bṛhat, in thunder | atha paśuṣu tataḥ yā vāk atyaricyata tām brāhmaṇeṣu adadhuḥ = then in beasts: what speech was left over they placed in brahmins | tasmāt brāhmaṇāḥ ubhayīm vācam vadanti yā ca devānām yā ca manuṣyāṇām iti = therefore brahmins speak both speeches, that of the gods and that of men | atha eṣā akṣarasya = now this one is of the imperishable
10 ṛcaḥ akṣare parame vyoman = the verses are in the imperishable, in the highest heaven | yasmin devāḥ adhi viśve niṣeduḥ = in which all the gods have sat | yaḥ tat na veda kim ṛcā kariṣyati = what will he who does not know that do with a verse | ye it tat viduḥ te ime samāsate = those who know it sit together here | ṛcaḥ akṣare parame vyavane yasmin devāḥ adhiniṣaṇṇāḥ sarve = the verses in the imperishable, in the highest enclosure, in which all the gods are seated | yaḥ tat na veda kim saḥ ṛcā kariṣyati = what will he who does not know that do with a verse | ye it tat viduḥ te ime samāsate iti viduṣaḥ upadiśati = “those who know it sit together here”: he points to the knowers | katamat tat etat akṣaram = which is that imperishable | om iti eṣā vāk iti śākapūṇiḥ = Om, this is speech, says Śākapūṇi | ṛcaḥ ca hi akṣare parame vyavane dhīyante nānā-devateṣu ca mantreṣu = for the verses are placed in the imperishable, in the highest enclosure, and in mantras of various deities | etat ha vai etat akṣaram yat sarvām trayīm vidyām prati-prati iti ca brāhmaṇam = “this indeed is that imperishable, which is in each of all the threefold knowledge”, says a Brāhmaṇa
11 āditya iti putraḥ śākapūṇeḥ = Āditya, says the son of Śākapūṇi | eṣā ṛk bhavati yat enam arcanti pratyṛcaḥ sarvāṇi bhūtāni = this is the verse, because all beings praise him verse by verse | tasya yat anyat mantrebhyaḥ tat akṣaram bhavati = what in him is other than the mantras is the imperishable | raśmayaḥ atra devāḥ ucyante ye etasmin adhiniṣaṇṇāḥ iti adhidaivatam = the rays are here called gods, who are seated in him: so on the divine plane | atha adhyātmam = now on the plane of the self | śarīram atra ṛk ucyate yat enena arcanti pratyṛcaḥ sarvāṇi indriyāṇi = here the body is called the verse, because by it all the senses praise verse by verse | tasya yat avināśi-dharma tat akṣaram bhavati = what in it has the nature of non-destruction is the imperishable | indriyāṇi atra devāḥ ucyante yāni asmin ātmani ekam bhavanti iti ātma-pravādāḥ = the senses are here called gods, which become one in this self: so the doctrines of the self
12 akṣaram na kṣarati na kṣīyate vā kṣayam bhavati = akṣara: it does not flow, or is not diminished, or comes to decay | vācaḥ akṣaḥ iti vā akṣaḥ yānasya añjanāt = or the axle of speech; an axle from anointing a vehicle | tat-prakṛti itarat vartana-sāmānyāt iti = the other (akṣara, syllable) is its nature, by likeness of turning | ayam mantra-artha-cintā-abhyūhaḥ abhyūḍhaḥ = this is reasoning that reflects on the meaning of mantras, carried forward | api śrutitaḥ api tarkataḥ = both from tradition and from reasoning | na tu pṛthaktvena mantrāḥ nirvaktavyāḥ = but mantras are not to be explained separately | prakaraṇaśaḥ eva tu nirvaktavyāḥ = they are to be explained by context | na hi eṣu pratyakṣam asti anṛṣeḥ atapasaḥ vā = for there is no direct perception in them for one who is not a seer or without austerity | pārovarya-vitsu tu khalu veditṛṣu bhūyo-vidyaḥ praśasyaḥ bhavati iti uktam purastāt = among knowers who know the higher and lower, the one of more knowledge is praised: this was said before | manuṣyāḥ vai ṛṣiṣu utkrāmatsu devān abruvan = men, when the seers departed, said to the gods | kaḥ naḥ ṛṣiḥ bhaviṣyati iti = “who will be our seer” | tebhyaḥ etam tarkam ṛṣim prāyacchan mantra-artha-cintā-abhyūham abhyūḍham = they gave them this reasoning as a seer, reflection on the meaning of mantras, carried forward | tasmāt yat eva kiṃ ca anūcānaḥ abhyūhati ārṣam tat bhavati = therefore whatever a learned man reasons out becomes seer’s
13 hṛdā taṣṭeṣu manasaḥ javeṣu = in what is fashioned by the heart, in the speeds of the mind | yat brāhmaṇāḥ saṃyajante sakhāyaḥ = when brahmins, friends, sacrifice together | atra aha tvam vi jahuḥ vedyābhiḥ = here indeed they leave you aside by the things to be known | ūha-brahmāṇaḥ vi caranti u tve = the Brahmans of reasoning move about in you | hṛdā taṣṭeṣu manasām prajaveṣu = in what is fashioned by the heart, in the speeds of minds | yat brāhmaṇāḥ saṃyajante samāna-ākhyānāḥ ṛtvijaḥ = when brahmins, priests of one account, sacrifice together | atra aha tvam vijahuḥ vedyābhiḥ veditavyābhiḥ pravṛttibhiḥ = here indeed they leave you by the things to be known, by courses | ohabrahmāṇaḥ ūhabrahmāṇaḥ = ohabrahmāṇa: ūha-brahmans | ūhaḥ eṣām brahma iti vā = or reasoning is their brahman | sā iyam vidyā śruti-mati-buddhiḥ = this knowledge is hearing, thought, understanding | tasyāḥ tapasā pāram īpsitavyam = its far shore is to be sought by austerity | tat idam āyuḥ icchatā na nirvaktavyam = this is not to be explained by one wishing for life | tasmāt chandassu śeṣāḥ upekṣitavyāḥ = therefore the rest in the metres is to be passed over | atha āgamaḥ yām yām devatām nirāha tasyāḥ tasyāḥ tādbhāvyam anubhavati = then the tradition: whichever deity he speaks of, he experiences that deity’s state
14 vyākhyātam daivatam yajña-aṅgam ca = the deity-section and the part of sacrifice have been explained | atha ataḥ ūrdhva-mārga-gatim vyākhyāsyāmaḥ = now therefore we shall explain the going by the upward path | sūryaḥ ātmā = the Sun is the self | iti uditasya hi karma-draṣṭā = for he is the witness of the work of the risen one | atha etat anupravadanti = then they speak of this further | atha etam mahāntam ātmānam eṣā ṛk-gaṇaḥ pravadati = then this group of verses speaks of this great Self | indram mitram varuṇam agnim āhuḥ iti = “they call him Indra, Mitra, Varuṇa, Agni” | atha eṣaḥ mahān ātmā ātma-jijñāsayā ātmānam provāca = then this great Self, from the wish to know the Self, spoke of himself | agniḥ asmi janmanā jātavedāḥ = I am Agni, by birth Jātavedas | aham asmi prathama-jā = I am the first-born | ityetābhyām = by these two
15 agniḥ asmi janmanā jātavedāḥ = I am Agni, by birth Jātavedas | ghṛtam me cakṣuḥ amṛtam me āsan = ghee is my eye, immortality was in my mouth | arkaḥ tri-dhātuḥ rajasaḥ vimānaḥ = the hymn, threefold, the measuring of the realm | ajasraḥ gharmaḥ haviḥ asmi nāma = I am the unceasing heat, the offering, by name | aham asmi prathama-jā ṛtasya = I am the first-born of order | pūrvam devebhyaḥ amṛtasya nāma = before the gods, the name of immortality | yaḥ mā dadāti saḥ it eva āvat = whoever gives me, he preserves me indeed | aham annam annam adantam admi = I eat the eater of food | iti saḥ ha jñātvā prādurbabhūva = thus, having known, he appeared | evam tam vyājahāra ayantam ātmānam adhyātma-jam antikam anyasmai ācakṣva iti = so he spoke to him, the self that came, born of the inner self, near: tell another
16 apaśyam gopām anipadyamānam = I saw the herdsman, never settling | ā ca parā ca pathibhiḥ carantam = going hither and thither by paths | saḥ sadhrīcīḥ saḥ viṣūcīḥ vasānaḥ = he, clothed in the converging and diverging ones | ā varīvarti bhuvaneṣu antaḥ = turns again and again within the worlds | āvarīvarti bhuvaneṣu antaḥ iti = he turns again within the worlds | atha eṣaḥ mahān ātmā sattva-lakṣaṇaḥ = now this great Self is marked by sattva | tat param tat brahma tat satyam tat salilam tat avyaktam = that is the highest, that Brahman, that truth, that water, that unmanifest | tat asparśam tat arūpam tat arasam tat agandham = that without touch, without form, without taste, without smell | tat amṛtam tat śuklam tat niṣṭhaḥ bhūtātmā = that immortal, that bright, that foundation, the self of beings | sā eṣā bhūta-prakṛtiḥ iti eke = some say this is the nature of beings | tat kṣetram tat jñānāt kṣetrajñam anuprāpya nirātmakam = that is the field; by that knowledge, having reached the knower of the field, it becomes selfless | atha eṣaḥ mahān ātmā tri-vidhaḥ bhavati sattvam rajaḥ tamaḥ iti = then this great Self is threefold: sattva, rajas, tamas | sattvam tu madhye viśuddham tiṣṭhati = but sattva stands pure in the middle | abhitaḥ rajaḥ-tamasī iti = rajas and tamas on both sides | kāma-dveṣa-tamaḥ iti avijñātasya viśudhyataḥ vibhūtim kurvataḥ kṣetrajña-pṛthaktvāya kalpate = desire, hatred, darkness: for one not understanding, becoming purified, working his power, it is fit for distinction from the knower of the field | pratibhāti-liṅgaḥ mahān ātmā = the great Self is marked by shining forth | tamaḥ-liṅgaḥ vidyā prakāśa-liṅgaḥ tamaḥ = knowledge is marked by darkness, darkness by light (as the text has it) | api niścaya-liṅgaḥ ākāśaḥ = and space is marked by certainty
17 ākāśa-guṇaḥ śabdaḥ = the quality of space is sound | ākāśāt vāyuḥ dviguṇaḥ sparśena = from space, air, twofold by touch | vāyoḥ jyotiḥ triguṇam rūpeṇa = from air, fire, threefold by form | jyotiṣaḥ āpaḥ catur-guṇāḥ rasena = from fire, water, fourfold by taste | adbhyaḥ pṛthivī pañca-guṇā gandhena = from water, earth, fivefold by smell | pṛthivyāḥ bhūta-grāma-sthāvara-jaṅgamāḥ = from earth, the host of beings, standing and moving | tat etat ahar yuga-sahasram jāgarti = this wakes for a day of a thousand ages | tasya ante suṣupsyan aṅgāni pratyāharati = at its end, about to sleep, it draws in its limbs | bhūta-grāmāḥ pṛthivīm api yanti = the host of beings go into earth | pṛthivī apaḥ = earth into water | āpaḥ jyotiṣam = water into fire | jyotiḥ vāyum = fire into air | vāyuḥ ākāśam = air into space | ākāśaḥ manaḥ = space into mind | manaḥ vidyām = mind into knowledge | vidyā mahāntam ātmānam = knowledge into the great Self | mahān ātmā pratibhām = the great Self into radiance | pratibhā prakṛtim = radiance into nature | sā svapiti yuga-sahasram rātriḥ = she sleeps a night of a thousand ages | tau etau ahorātrau ajasram parivartete = those two, day and night, turn ceaselessly | saḥ kālaḥ tat etat ahaḥ bhavati = that time becomes that day | yuga-sahasra-paryantam ahar yat brahmaṇaḥ viduḥ = a day of Brahmā lasting a thousand ages, which they know | rātrim yuga-sahasra-antām te ahorātra-vidaḥ janāḥ = and a night ending after a thousand ages: those are the people who know day and night | iti = thus
18 tam parivartamānam anyaḥ anupravartate = another follows him as he turns | sraṣṭā draṣṭā vibhaktā atimātraḥ aham iti gamyate = the creator, the seer, the separate one, beyond measure, is understood as “I” | saḥ mithyā-darśana idam pāvakam mahā-bhūteṣu ciroṇ ākāśāt vāyoḥ prāṇaḥ cakṣuḥ ca vaktāram ca tejasaḥ adbhyaḥ sneham pṛthivyāḥ mūrtiḥ = (corrupt text) he, from false seeing, this fire among the great elements; breath from space and air, the eye, and the speaker from fire, moisture from waters, bodily form from earth | pārthivān tu aṣṭau guṇān vidyāt = one should know the eight qualities of earth | trīn mātṛtaḥ trīn pitṛtaḥ = three from the mother, three from the father | asthi-snāyu-majjānaḥ pitṛtaḥ = bone, sinew, marrow from the father | tvak-māṃsa-śoṇitāni mātṛtaḥ = skin, flesh, blood from the mother | anna-pānam iti aṣṭau = food and drink: so eight | saḥ ayam puruṣaḥ sarva-mayaḥ sarva-jñānaḥ api klṛptaḥ = this person is made of all, though fashioned as knowing all
19 saḥ yadi anurudhyate tat bhavati = whatever he clings to, that he becomes | yadi dharmam anurudhyate tat devaḥ bhavati = if he clings to duty, he becomes a god | yadi jñānam anurudhyate tat amṛtaḥ
