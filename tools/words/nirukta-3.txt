# Nirukta, adhyāya 3 — word by word. Etymologies are given as Yāska states them; examples quoted for form are glossed as examples.
1 karma-nāmāni uttarāṇi ṣaḍ-viṃśatiḥ = twenty-six names of action follow | karma kasmāt = why “karma” | kriyate iti sataḥ = from “it is done” | apatya-nāmāni uttarāṇi pañcadaśa = fifteen names of offspring follow | apatyam kasmāt = why “apatya” | apatatam bhavati = it is “not fallen” | na anena patati iti vā = or “by it one does not fall” | tat yathā janayituḥ prajā = so, as the progeny of the begetter | evam artha-īye ṛcā udāhariṣyāmaḥ = we shall give examples with a verse of this sense
2 pariṣadyam hi araṇasya rekṇaḥ = the stranger’s wealth is to be shunned | nityasya rāyaḥ patayaḥ syāma = may we be lords of the abiding wealth | na śeṣaḥ agne anya-jātam asti = O Agni, no offspring born of another is a remainder | acetānasya mā pathaḥ vi dukṣaḥ = do not milk the ways of the unthinking | parihartavyam hi na upasartavyam = for it is to be avoided, not approached | araṇasya rekṇaḥ = of the stranger’s wealth | araṇaḥ apārṇaḥ bhavati = araṇa is a stranger | rekṇaḥ iti dhana-nāma = rekṇa is a name of wealth | ricyate prayataḥ = it is left, given | nityasya rāyaḥ patayaḥ syāma = may we be lords of the abiding wealth | pitryasya iva dhanasya = as of ancestral wealth | na śeṣaḥ agne anya-jātam asti = no remainder born of another | śeṣaḥ iti apatya-nāma = śeṣa is a name of offspring | śiṣyate prayataḥ = it is left, given | acetayamānasya tat pramattasya bhavati = it is of one not thinking, of the careless | mā naḥ pathaḥ vidūduṣaḥ iti = “not of our ways, O knower” | tasya uttarā bhūyase nirvacanāya = the next verse is for fuller explanation
3 na hi grabhāya araṇaḥ su-śevaḥ = do not seize the stranger, though most pleasant | anyodaryaḥ manasā mantavai = one born of another’s womb is not to be thought of in mind | adha cit okaḥ punar it saḥ etya = then, he returns again to his home | naḥ vājī abhiṣāḷ etu navyaḥ = may the new strong victorious one come to us | na hi grahītavyaḥ araṇaḥ su-sukhatamaḥ api = the stranger is not to be taken, though most pleasant | anyodaryaḥ manasā api na mantavyaḥ = one of another’s womb is not to be thought of even in mind | mama ayam putraḥ iti = “this is my son” | atha saḥ okaḥ punar eva tadā = then he returns home again | iti yataḥ āgataḥ bhavati = from where he has come | okaḥ iti nivāsa-nāma ucyate = oka is called a name of dwelling | etu naḥ vājī vejanavān = may the swift, speedy one come to us | abhiṣahamāṇaḥ sapatnān = overcoming rivals | nava-jātaḥ saḥ eva putraḥ iti = the newborn is that very son | atha etām duhitṛ-dāyādyā udāharanti = then some cite this as concerning the daughter’s inheritance | putra-dāyādyā iti eke = others say it concerns the son’s inheritance
4 śāsat vahniḥ duhituḥ naptyam gāt = the bearer, instructing, went to the daughter’s grandson | vidvān ṛtasya dīdhitim saparyan = knowing, honouring the light of order | pitā yatra duhituḥ sekam ṛñjan = where the father provides the daughter’s seed | sam śagmyena manasā dadhanve = he has set himself with a willing mind | praśāsti voḍhā = the bearer instructs | santāna-karmaṇe duhituḥ putra-bhāvam = the daughter’s sonship, for the work of continuing the line | duhitā durhitā dūre hitā = duhitṛ: “ill-placed”, “placed far” | dogdheḥ vā = or from “to milk” | naptāram upāgamat = he approached the grandson | dauhitram pautram iti = the daughter’s son, the son’s son | vidvān prajanana-yajñasya retasaḥ vā = knowing the sacrifice of procreation, or the seed | aṅgāt aṅgāt saṃbhūtasya hṛdayāt adhijātasya = born of limb after limb, begotten from the heart | mātari pratyṛtasya = given back in the mother | vidhānam pūjayan = honouring the arrangement | aviśeṣeṇa mithunāḥ putrāḥ dāyādāḥ iti = sons and daughters alike are heirs | tat etat ṛk-ślokābhyām abhyuktam = this has been said in a verse and a śloka | aṅgāt aṅgāt saṃbhavasi hṛdayāt adhi jāyase = you arise from limb after limb, you are born from the heart | ātmā vai putra-nāma asi saḥ jīva śaradaḥ śatam = you are the self, named son; live a hundred autumns | aviśeṣeṇa putrāṇām dāyaḥ bhavati dharmataḥ = by law, sons share the inheritance alike | mithunānām visargādau manuḥ svāyaṃbhuvaḥ abravīt = at the beginning of creation Manu Svāyambhuva spoke of pairs | na duhitaraḥ iti eke = “not daughters”, say some | tasmāt pumān dāyādaḥ adāyādā strī = so the male is heir, the woman not | iti vijñāyate = so it is known | tasmāt striyam jātām parāsyanti na pumāṃsam = so they cast away a girl born, not a boy | iti ca = and so | strīṇām dāna-vikraya-atisargāḥ vidyante na puṃsaḥ = giving, selling, and abandonment are found for women, not men | puṃsaḥ api iti eke = “for men too”, say some | śaunaḥśepe darśanāt = from what is seen in the Śunaḥśepa story | abhrātṛmatī-vādaḥ iti aparam = another is the doctrine of the brotherless woman | amūryāḥ yanti jāmayaḥ sarvāḥ lohita-vāsasaḥ = those yonder go, all sisters in red garments | abhrātaraḥ iva yoṣāḥ tiṣṭhanti hata-vartmanaḥ = like women without brothers they stand, their path blocked | abhrātṛkāḥ iva yoṣāḥ tiṣṭhanti = they stand like brotherless women | santāna-karmaṇe piṇḍa-dānāya = for continuing the line, for giving the rice-balls | hata-vartmānaḥ = of blocked path | iti abhrātṛkāyāḥ anirvāhaḥ aupamikaḥ = so the brotherless girl’s not being given in marriage is used as a comparison | tasya uttarā bhūyase nirvacanāya = the next verse is for fuller explanation
5 abhrātā iva puṃsaḥ eti pratīcī = like a brotherless girl she goes toward the man, facing him | gartārug iva sanaye dhanānām = like one mounting the chariot for the gain of wealth | jāyā iva patye uśatī su-vāsāḥ = like a wife, longing, well-dressed, for her husband | uṣāḥ hasrā iva ni riṇīte apsaḥ = Dawn, as if laughing, uncovers her form | abhrātṛkā iva puṃsaḥ pitṝn eti abhimukhī = like a brotherless girl she goes toward her father’s men, facing them | santāna-karmaṇe piṇḍa-dānāya na patim = for continuing the line and giving rice-balls, not for a husband | gartā-rohiṇī iva dhana-lābhāya dākṣiṇājī = like one mounting the gartā for wealth, a lover of gifts | gartaḥ sabhā-sthāṇuḥ = garta is the post of the assembly | gṛṇāteḥ satya-saṅgaraḥ bhavati = from “to speak”: it becomes a truth-bound pact | tam tatra yā aputrā yā apatikā sā ārohati = the woman without son or husband mounts it there | tām tatra akṣaiḥ āghnanti = there they strike her with dice | sā riktham labhate = she receives the inheritance | śmaśāna-saṃcayaḥ api gartaḥ ucyate = a burial-ground heap too is called garta | gurateḥ apagūrṇaḥ bhavati = from “to raise”: it is lifted up | śmaśānam śma-śayanam = śmaśāna is the bed of the body | śma śarīram = śma is body | śarīram śṛṇāteḥ śamnāteḥ vā = śarīra from “to crush” or “to kill” | śmaśru loma = beard is hair | śmani śritam bhavati = it rests on the chin | loma lunāteḥ vā līyateḥ vā = loma from “to cut” or “to cling” | na uparasya āviṣkuryāt = let him not reveal it to another | yat uparasya āviṣkuryāt garteṣṭhāḥ syāt pramāyukaḥ yajamānaḥ = if he reveals it, the sacrificer would be at the pit, short-lived | iti api nigamaḥ bhavati = so too a Vedic text | rathaḥ api gartaḥ ucyate = a chariot too is called garta | gṛṇāteḥ stuti-karmaṇaḥ = from “to praise” | stutatamam yānam = the most praised vehicle | ā rohathaḥ varuṇa mitra gartam = mount the chariot, Varuṇa and Mitra | iti api nigamaḥ bhavati = so too a text | jāyā iva patye kāmayamānā su-vāsāḥ = like a wife desiring her husband, well clad | ṛtu-kāleṣu uṣāḥ hasana iva dantān vivṛṇute rūpāṇi iti = in the fertile seasons, Dawn uncovers her forms as if laughing and baring her teeth | catasraḥ upamāḥ = four comparisons | na abhrātṛm upayaccheta = one should not marry a brotherless girl | tokam hi asya tat bhavati = for that would be offspring of hers | iti abhrātṛkāyāḥ upayamana-pratiṣedhaḥ pratyakṣaḥ = so the prohibition of marrying a brotherless girl is plain | pituḥ ca putra-bhāvaḥ = and the father’s relation as son | pitā yatra duhitar aprattāyāḥ retaḥ-sekam prārjayati = where the father produces the seed-pouring of an ungiven daughter | saṃdadhāti ātmānam saṃgamena manasā iti = he joins himself in union with the mind | atha etām jāmyāḥ riktha-pratiṣedham udāharanti = then some cite this as a bar on the sister’s inheritance | jyeṣṭham putrikāyāḥ iti eke = others, the eldest, of the appointed daughter
6 na jāmaye tanvaḥ rikthām āraik = he did not give a sister her body’s inheritance | cakāra garbham saniṭuḥ nidhānam = he made the child, the store of the giver | yadi mātaraḥ janayanta vahnim = if the mothers bear the bearer | anyaḥ kartā su-kṛtoḥ anyaḥ ṛndhan = one is the doer, the other prospers | na jāmaye bhaginyai = not to the sister | jāmiḥ anye asyām janayanti jāmapatyam = others beget in her the offspring of a sister | jamateḥ vā syāt gati-karmaṇaḥ = or from jam, a verb of going | nirgamana-prāyā bhavati = she is mostly one who goes away | tānvaḥ ātmajaḥ putraḥ = “of the body” is the son born of oneself | riktham prāricat prādāt = he left, gave, the inheritance | cakāra enām garbha-nidhānīm = he made her the receptacle of the child | saniṭuḥ hasta-grāhasya = of the giver, of the one taking the hand | yadi mātaraḥ janayanta = if the mothers bear | vahnim putram = the bearer, the son | avahnim ca striyam = and the non-bearer, the daughter | anyataraḥ santāna-kartā bhavati pumān dāyādaḥ = one is the maker of the line, the male heir | anyataraḥ ardhayitvā jāmiḥ pradīyate parasmai = the other, sharing half, the sister is given to another
7 manuṣya-nāmāni uttarāṇi pañca-viṃśatiḥ = twenty-five names of man follow | manuṣyāḥ kasmāt = why “manuṣya” | matvā karmāṇi sīvyanti = having thought, they stitch works | manasyamānena sṛṣṭāḥ = created by one who thinks | manasyatiḥ punar manasvī-bhāve = manasyati again in the sense of being thoughtful | manoḥ apatyam = Manu’s offspring | manuṣaḥ vā = or from Manuṣ | tatra pañcajanāḥ iti etasya nigamāḥ bhavanti = of “pañcajanāḥ” there are Vedic texts
