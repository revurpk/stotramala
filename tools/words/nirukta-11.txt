# Nirukta, adhyāya 11 — word by word. Etymologies are given as Yāska states them; examples quoted for form are glossed as examples.
1 śyenaḥ vyākhyātaḥ = śyena has been explained | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
2 ādāya śyenaḥ abharat somam = the hawk took the soma and brought it | sahasram savān ayutam ca sākam = a thousand pressings and ten thousand together | atra purandhiḥ ajahāt arātīḥ = here Purandhi abandoned the foes | made somasya mūrā amūraḥ = in the exhilaration of soma, the foolish, the wise | ādāya śyenaḥ aharat somam = the hawk took and carried off the soma | sahasram savān ayutam ca saha = a thousand pressings and ten thousand together | sahasram sahasra-sāvyam abhipretya = meaning the thousand-pressing | tatra ayutam soma-bhakṣāḥ = there the ten thousand are soma-drinkings | tat-saṃbandhena ayutam dakṣiṇāḥ iti vā = or in connection with it, ten thousand fees | tatra purandhiḥ ajahāt amitrān adānān iti vā = or there Purandhi abandoned the foes, the non-givers | made somasya mūrā amūraḥ iti = in the exhilaration of soma, the foolish, the wise | aindre ca sūkte soma-pānena ca stutaḥ = he is praised in the hymn to Indra and by soma-drinking | tasmāt indram manyante = therefore they take it as Indra | oṣadhiḥ somaḥ sunoteḥ = soma is a plant, from “to press” | yat enam abhiṣuṇvanti = because they press it | bahulam asya naighaṇṭukam vṛttam = his Naighaṇṭuka sense is much used | āścaryam iva prādhānyena = as if chiefly marvellous | tasya pāvamānīṣu nidarśanāya udāhariṣyāmaḥ = for an illustration of him we shall quote from the Pavamānī hymns
3 svādiṣṭhayā madiṣṭhayā pavasva soma dhārayā = flow, O Soma, in your sweetest, most exhilarating stream | indrāya pātave sutaḥ = pressed for Indra to drink | iti sā nigada-vyākhyātā = it is explained by its own recital | atha eṣā aparā bhavati = then there is this other verse | candramasaḥ vā etasya vā = of the moon, or of this (soma)
4 somam manyate papivān yat saṃpiṃṣanti oṣadhim = he thinks he has drunk soma when they grind the plant | somam yam brahmāṇaḥ viduḥ na tasya aśnāti kaścana = but the soma which the brahmins know, none eats of it | somam manyate papivān yat saṃpiṃṣanti oṣadhim iti vṛthā-sutam asomam āha = “he thinks he has drunk soma when they grind the plant”: he calls the vainly pressed one not soma | somam yam brahmāṇaḥ viduḥ iti na tasya aśnāti kaścana ayajvā iti adhiyajñam = “the soma which brahmins know, none eats of it”: the non-sacrificer — this on the sacrificial plane | atha adhidaivatam = now on the divine plane | somam manyate papivān yat saṃpiṃṣanti oṣadhim iti yajuḥ-sutam asomam āha = “he thinks he has drunk soma when they grind the plant”: he calls the soma pressed by yajus not soma | somam yam brahmāṇaḥ viduḥ candramasam = the soma which brahmins know is the moon | na tasya aśnāti kaścana adevaḥ iti = none eats of it, who is not a god | atha eṣā aparā bhavati = then there is this other verse | candramasaḥ vā etasya vā = of the moon, or of this
5 yat tvā deva pra pibanti tataḥ ā pyāyase punaḥ = when, O god, they drink you forth, you swell again | vāyuḥ somasya rakṣitā samānām māsaḥ ākṛtiḥ = Vāyu is the guardian of soma; the months are the form of the years | yat tvā deva prapibanti tataḥ āpyāyase punaḥ iti nārāśaṃsān abhipretya = “when they drink you forth you swell again” — meaning the Nārāśaṃsa cups | pūrvapakṣa-aparapakṣau iti vā = or the bright and dark fortnights | vāyuḥ somasya rakṣitā vāyum asya rakṣitāram āha sāhacaryāt rasa-haraṇāt vā = Vāyu is the guardian of soma: he calls Vāyu its guardian, by companionship or by taking juices | samānām saṃvatsarāṇām māsaḥ ākṛtiḥ somaḥ rūpa-viśeṣaiḥ oṣadhiḥ candramāḥ vā = of the years, the months are the form: soma by differences of form, the plant or the moon | candramāḥ ca ayam candramati = the moon: he glitters | candraḥ mātā = candra is the measurer | cāndram mānam asya iti vā = or his measure is lunar | candraḥ candateḥ kānti-karmaṇaḥ = candra from cand, a verb of shining | candanam iti api asya bhavati = “candana” also belongs to it | cāru dramati ciram dramati = he runs beautifully, he runs long | camervā pūrvam cāru rucerviparītasya = from cam before, or cāru from ruc reversed | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
6 navaḥ-navaḥ bhavati jāyamānaḥ = ever new he becomes as he is born | ahnām ketuḥ uṣasām eti agram = the banner of days, he goes at the head of the dawns | bhāgam devebhyaḥ vi dadhāti āyan = coming, he apportions the share to the gods | pra candramāḥ tirate dīrgham āyuḥ = the moon prolongs long life | navaḥ-navaḥ bhavati jāyamānaḥ iti pūrvapakṣa-ādim abhipretya = “ever new he becomes as he is born”, meaning the start of the bright fortnight | ahnām ketuḥ uṣasām eti agram iti aparapakṣa-antam abhipretya = “the banner of days he goes at the head of dawns”, meaning the end of the dark fortnight | āditya-daivataḥ dvitīyaḥ pādaḥ iti eke = some say the second foot is of the Sun | bhāgam devebhyaḥ vidadhāti āyan iti ardha-māsa-ijyām abhipretya = “coming, he apportions the share to the gods”, meaning the half-month sacrifices | pravardhayate candramāḥ dīrgham āyuḥ = the moon prolongs long life | mṛtyuḥ mārayati iti sataḥ = mṛtyu: from “it kills” | mṛtam cyāvayati iti vā śatabalākṣaḥ maudgalyaḥ = or “it makes fall the dead”, says Śatabalākṣa Maudgalya | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
7 param mṛtyo anu parehi panthām = go on, Death, along the other path | yaḥ te svaḥ itaraḥ devayānāt = which is your own, other than the way of the gods | cakṣuṣmate śṛṇvate te bravīmi = to you who have eyes and hear I say | mā naḥ prajām rīriṣaḥ mā uta vīrān = do not harm our offspring or our heroes | param mṛtyo dhruvam mṛtyo dhruvam parehi mṛtyo kathitam tena mṛtyo = O Death, go to the far, to the fixed; go to the fixed, O Death; so is it said of Death | mṛtam cyāvayate bhavati mṛtyo madeḥ vā mudeḥ vā = Mṛtyu makes the dead fall; from mad or mud | teṣām eṣā bhavati = of them there is this verse
8 tveṣam ittha samaraṇam śimīvatoḥ = so fierce is the meeting of the two active ones | indrāviṣṇū sutapāḥ vām uruṣyati = O Indra and Viṣṇu, the soma-drinker protects you | yā martyāya pratidhīyamānam it = which, set against the mortal | kṛśānoḥ astuḥ asanām uruṣyathaḥ = you two protect from the arrow-shot of the archer Kṛśānu | iti sā nigada-vyākhyātā = it is explained by its own recital | viśvānaraḥ vyākhyātaḥ = Viśvānara has been explained | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
9 pra vaḥ mahe mandamānāya andhasaḥ = to the great, rejoicing, of food | arcā viśvānarāya viśva-bhuve = praise Viśvānara, the all-being | indrasya yasya sumakham saḥ mahi = whose, Indra’s, goodly bounty and great might | śravaḥ nṛmṇam ca rodasī saparyataḥ = heaven and earth honour his fame and manliness | prārcata yūyam stutim mahate andhasaḥ annasya dātre = praise, you all, with praise the great giver of food | mandamānāya modamānāya stūyamānāya śabdāyamānāya iti vā = rejoicing: delighting, being praised, sounding | viśvānarāya sarvam vibhūtāya = to Viśvānara, the all-pervading | indrasya yasya prītau sumahat balam = in whose, Indra’s, delight is very great strength | mahat ca śravaṇīyam yaśaḥ = and great, celebrated fame | nṛmṇam ca balam nṛnatam = manliness is strength bent to men | dyāvāpṛthivyau vaḥ paricarataḥ iti = heaven and earth serve you | kam anyam madhyamāt devam evam avakṣyat = what other deity of the middle would he have addressed so | tasya eṣā aparā bhavati = of him there is this other verse
10 ud u jyotiḥ amṛtam viśva-janyam = up has risen the immortal, all-peoples’ light | viśvānaraḥ savitā devaḥ aśret = the god Viśvānara, Savitṛ, has risen | udaśiśriyat jyotiḥ amṛtam sarvajanyam viśvānaraḥ savitā devaḥ iti = he has raised the light, the immortal, of all peoples: Viśvānara, Savitṛ, the god | dhātā sarvasya vidhātā = Dhātṛ is the establisher of all, the ordainer | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
11 dhātā dadātu dāśuṣe prācīm jīvātum akṣitām = may Dhātṛ give to the giver an eastward, undiminished life | vayam devasya dhīmahi sumatim satya-dharmaṇaḥ = may we of true law hold the god’s good favour | dhātā dadātu dattavate pravṛddhām jīvikām anupakṣīṇām = may Dhātṛ give to the giver a grown, undiminished livelihood | vayam devasya dhīmahi sumatim kalyāṇīm matim satya-dharmaṇaḥ = may we of true law hold the god’s good thought | vidhātā dhātrā vyākhyātaḥ = Vidhātṛ is explained by Dhātṛ | tasya eṣaḥ nipātaḥ bhavati bahu-devatāyām ṛci = this is its occurrence in a verse of many deities
12 somasya rājñaḥ varuṇasya dharmaṇi = in the law of King Soma and of Varuṇa | bṛhaspateḥ anumatyā u śarmaṇi = by the assent of Bṛhaspati, in shelter | tava aham adya maghavan upastutau = today, O bounteous, in praising you | dhātaḥ vidhātaḥ kalaśān abhakṣayam = O Dhātṛ, O Vidhātṛ, I drank the jars | iti etābhiḥ devatābhiḥ abhiprasūtaḥ soma-kalaśān abhakṣayam iti = impelled by these deities, I drank the soma jars | kalaśaḥ kasmāt = why “kalaśa” | kalāḥ asmin śerate mātrāḥ = fractions lie in it, measures | kaliḥ ca kalāḥ ca kirateḥ vikīrṇa-mātrāḥ = kali and kalā from “to scatter”: scattered measures
13 atha ataḥ madhya-sthānāḥ deva-gaṇāḥ = now therefore the hosts of gods of the middle place | teṣām marutaḥ prathama-āgāminaḥ bhavanti = of them the Maruts come first | marutaḥ mita-rāviṇaḥ vā mita-rociṇaḥ vā mahat dravanti iti vā = Maruts: measured-sounding, measured-shining, or they run greatly | teṣām eṣā bhavati = of them there is this verse
14 ā vidyunmadbhiḥ marutaḥ svarkaiḥ = come, Maruts, with lightning-bearing, well-singing | rathebhiḥ yāta ṛṣṭi-madbhiḥ aśva-parṇaiḥ = go in chariots with spears, with horse-feathers | ā varṣiṣṭhayā naḥ iṣā vayaḥ na paptata sumāyāḥ = come with our best food like birds, O good-wise ones | vidyunmadbhiḥ marutaḥ = with lightning, O Maruts | svarkaiḥ svañcanaiḥ iti vā svarcanaiḥ iti vā svarcibhiḥ iti vā = well-singing: well-bending, well-praising, or well-flaming | rathaiḥ āyāta ṛṣṭimadbhiḥ = come in chariots with spears | aśvaparṇaiḥ aśva-patanaiḥ = with horse-feathers: with horse-flights | varṣiṣṭhena ca naḥ annena vayaḥ iva āpatata sumāyāḥ = come to us with the best food like birds, O good-wise ones | kalyāṇa-karmāṇaḥ vā kalyāṇa-prajñāḥ vā = of good deeds or good wisdom | rudrāḥ vyākhyātāḥ = the Rudras have been explained | teṣām eṣā bhavati = of them there is this verse
15 ā rudrāsaḥ indravantaḥ sajoṣasaḥ = come, Rudras, with Indra, in unity | hiraṇya-rathāḥ suvitāya gantana = golden-chariot ones, go for well-going | iyam vaḥ asmat prati haryate matiḥ = this thought of ours longs for you | tṛṣṇaje na divaḥ utsāḥ udanyave = like springs of heaven for the thirsty, for the water-seeker | āgacchata rudrāḥ indreṇa sahajoṣaṇāḥ suvitāya karmaṇe = come, Rudras, with Indra in unity, for good action | iyam vaḥ asmat api pratikāmayate matiḥ = this thought longs for you from us | tṛṣṇajaḥ iva divaḥ utsāḥ udanyave = like springs of heaven for the thirsty, for the water-seeker | tṛṣṇak tṛṣyateḥ = tṛṣṇaj from “to thirst” | udanyuḥ udanyateḥ = udanyu from “to wish for water” | ṛbhavaḥ uru bhānti iti vā ṛtena bhānti iti vā ṛtena bhavanti iti vā = Ṛbhus: they shine widely, shine by order, or exist by order | teṣām eṣā bhavati = of them there is this verse
16 viṣṭvī śamī taraṇitvena vāghataḥ = the all-pervading, active, with swiftness, the priests | martāsaḥ santaḥ amṛtatvam ānaśuḥ = being mortal they attained immortality | saudhanvanāḥ ṛbhavaḥ sūra-cakṣasaḥ = the sons of Sudhanvan, the Ṛbhus, sun-eyed | saṃvatsare sam apṛcyanta dhītibhiḥ = in a year they were joined by their works | kṛtvā karmāṇi kṣipratvena = having done works by swiftness | voḍhāraḥ medhāvinaḥ vā = carriers or wise | martāsaḥ santaḥ amṛtatvam ānaśire = being mortal they attained immortality | saudhanvanāḥ ṛbhavaḥ sūra-khyānāḥ vā sūra-prajñāḥ vā = Ṛbhus, sons of Sudhanvan: sun-famed or sun-wise | saṃvatsare samapṛcyanta dhītibhiḥ karmabhiḥ = in a year they were joined by their deeds | ṛbhuḥ vibhvā vājaḥ iti sudhanvanaḥ āṅgirasasya trayaḥ putrāḥ babhūvuḥ = Ṛbhu, Vibhvan, Vāja: Sudhanvan Āṅgirasa had three sons | teṣām prathama-uttamābhyām bahuvat nigamāḥ bhavanti na madhyamena = of them the texts in plural are with the first and last, not the middle | tat etat ṛbhoḥ ca bahu-vacanena ca camasasya ca saṃstavena bahūni daśatayīṣu sūktāni bhavanti = so in the ten books there are many hymns of the Ṛbhus in the plural and with praise of the cup | āditya-raśmayaḥ api ṛbhavaḥ ucyante = the Sun’s rays too are called Ṛbhus | agohyasya yat asastana gṛhe tat adya idam ṛbhavaḥ na anu gacchatha = since you lay in the house of the hidden one, now, O Ṛbhus, you do not follow this | agohyaḥ ādityaḥ agūhanīyaḥ = agohya is the Sun, not to be hidden | tasya yat asvapatha gṛhe yāvat tatra bhavatha na tāvat iha bhavatha iti = since you slept in his house, as long as you are there you are not here | aṅgirasaḥ vyākhyātāḥ = the Aṅgirases have been explained | teṣām eṣā bhavati = of them there is this verse
17 virūpāsaḥ it ṛṣayaḥ te it gambhīra-vepasaḥ = of varied forms are the seers, of deep tremor | te aṅgirasaḥ sūnavaḥ te agneḥ pari jajñire = those Aṅgirases are sons, they were born from Agni | bahu-rūpāḥ ṛṣayaḥ = the seers of many forms | te gambhīra-karmāṇaḥ vā gambhīra-prajñāḥ vā = they of deep deeds or deep wisdom | te aṅgirasaḥ putrāḥ te agneḥ adhi jajñire iti agni-janma = those sons, Aṅgirases, were born of Agni: so the birth from Agni | pitaraḥ vyākhyātāḥ = the fathers have been explained | teṣām eṣā bhavati = of them there is this verse
18 ud īratām avare ut parāsaḥ ut madhyamāḥ pitaraḥ somyāsaḥ = let the lower, the higher, the middle fathers, drinkers of soma, rise | asum ye īyuḥ avṛkāḥ ṛta-jñāḥ = who went to breath, harmless, knowers of order | te naḥ avantu pitaraḥ haveṣu = may those fathers help us at the calls | udīratām avare udīratām pare udīratām madhyamāḥ pitaraḥ = let the lower rise, let the higher rise, let the middle fathers rise | somyāḥ soma-saṃpādinaḥ te asum ye prāṇam anvīyuḥ = the soma-accomplishers, who went to breath | avṛkāḥ anamitrāḥ satya-jñāḥ vā yajña-jñāḥ vā = harmless: without enemies; knowers of truth or of sacrifice | te naḥ āgacchantu pitaraḥ hvāneṣu = may those fathers come to us at our calls | mādhyamikaḥ yamaḥ iti āhuḥ tasmāt mādhyamikān pitṝn manyante = they say Yama is of the middle; therefore they take the fathers as of the middle | aṅgirasaḥ vyākhyātāḥ pitaraḥ vyākhyātāḥ bhṛgavaḥ vyākhyātāḥ = the Aṅgirases, fathers, Bhṛgus have been explained | atharvāṇaḥ athanavantaḥ = the Atharvans are the unwavering | tharvatiḥ carati-karmā tat-pratiṣedhaḥ = tharv is a verb of moving; its negation | teṣām eṣā sādhāraṇā bhavati = of them this verse is common
19 aṅgirasaḥ naḥ pitaraḥ navagvāḥ = the Aṅgirases are our fathers, the nine-goers | atharvāṇaḥ bhṛgavaḥ somyāsaḥ = the Atharvans, the Bhṛgus, soma-drinkers | teṣām vayam sumatau yajñiyānām = we are in the good favour of those worthy of sacrifice | api bhadre saumanase syāma = may we be also in their auspicious good-mindedness | aṅgirasaḥ naḥ pitaraḥ navagatayaḥ navanīta-gatayaḥ vā = the Aṅgirases are our fathers: of new goings or of butter-goings | atharvāṇaḥ bhṛgavaḥ somyāḥ soma-sampādinaḥ = the Atharvans, Bhṛgus, soma-accomplishers | teṣām vayam sumatau kalyāṇyām matau = we in their good, kindly thought | yajñiyānām api ca eṣām bhadre bhandanīye bhājanavati vā kalyāṇe manasi syāma iti = also of these worthy of sacrifice, in the auspicious, praise-worthy, or shareable, kindly mind may we be | mādhyamikaḥ deva-gaṇaḥ iti nairuktāḥ pitaraḥ iti ākhyānam = a host of gods of the middle, say the etymologists; “fathers” is the tradition | atha api ṛṣayaḥ stūyante = also the seers are praised
20 sūryasya iva vakṣathaḥ jyotiḥ eṣām = their light is like the Sun’s | samudrasya iva mahimā gabhīraḥ = their greatness is deep like the sea’s | vātasya iva prajavaḥ na anyena stomaḥ = their speed is like the wind’s; no other hymn | vasiṣṭhāḥ anvetave vaḥ = O Vasiṣṭhas, to follow you | iti yathā = so, as | āptyāḥ āpnoteḥ = āptya from “to obtain” | teṣām eṣaḥ nipātaḥ bhavati aindryām ṛci = this is their occurrence in a verse of Indra
21 stuṣeyyam puru-varpasam ṛbhvam = I would praise the many-formed, the skilful | inatamam āptyam āptyānām = most powerful, the Āptya of the Āptyas | ā darṣate śavasā sapta dānūn = he shatters by might the seven givers (or Dānavas) | pra sākṣate pratimānāni bhūri = he overcomes many rivals | stotavyam bahu-rūpam uru-bhūtam īśvaratamam = to be praised, many-formed, become wide, most lordly | āptavyam āptavyānām = to be obtained among the obtainable | ādṛṇāti yaḥ = who splits | śavasā balena = by might: by strength | sapta-dātṝn iti vā sapta-dānavān iti vā = the seven givers or the seven Dānavas | prasākṣate pratimānāni bahūni = he overcomes many rivals | sākṣatiḥ āpnoti-karmā = sākṣ is a verb of attaining
22 atha ataḥ madhya-sthānāḥ striyaḥ = now therefore the goddesses of the middle place | tāsām aditiḥ prathama-āgāminī bhavati = of them Aditi comes first | aditiḥ vyākhyātā = Aditi has been explained | tasyāḥ eṣā bhavati = of her there is this verse
23 dakṣasya vā adite janmani vrate = O Aditi, in the birth of Dakṣa or in his law | rājānā mitrāvaruṇā vivāsasi = you serve the two kings, Mitra and Varuṇa | atūrta-panthāḥ puru-rathaḥ aryamā = Aryaman of unhurried path, of many chariots | sapta-hotā viṣu-rūpeṣu janmasu = seven-priested in births of varied forms | dakṣasya vā adite janmani vrate karmaṇi = O Aditi, in the birth of Dakṣa or in his law, in his work | rājānau mitrāvaruṇau paricarasi = you serve the two kings Mitra and Varuṇa | vivāsatiḥ paricaryāyām = vivās is in the sense of serving | haviṣmān āvivāsati = the offerer serves | iti āśāsteḥ vā = or from “to wish” | atūrta-panthāḥ atvaramāṇa-panthāḥ = of unhurried path: of path not hasty | bahu-rathaḥ = of many chariots | aryamā ādityaḥ = Aryaman is the Sun | arīn niyacchati = he restrains enemies | sapta-hotā = seven-priested | saptā asmai raśmayaḥ rasān abhisannāmayanti = seven rays bend the juices toward him | saptā enam ṛṣayaḥ stuvanti iti vā = or seven seers praise him | viṣama-rūpeṣu janmasu karma-sūdayeṣu = in births of uneven forms, in the ripenings of deeds | ādityaḥ dakṣaḥ ity āhuḥ = they say the Sun is Dakṣa | āditya-madhye ca stutaḥ = and he is praised among the Ādityas | aditiḥ dākṣāyaṇī = Aditi is Dākṣāyaṇī | aditeḥ dakṣaḥ ajāyata dakṣāt vā aditiḥ pari iti ca = Dakṣa was born from Aditi, and Aditi from Dakṣa | tat katham upapadyate = how is that tenable | samāna-janmānau syātām iti = they would be of the same birth | api vā deva-dharmeṇa itaretara-janmānau syātām itaretara-prakṛtī = or by the nature of the gods they may be born of each other, each the origin of the other | agniḥ api aditiḥ ucyate = Agni too is called Aditi | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
