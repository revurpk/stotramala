# Nirukta, adhyāya 4 — word by word. Etymologies are given as Yāska states them; examples quoted for form are glossed as examples.
1 eka-artham aneka-śabdam iti etat uktam = “one meaning, many words” has been said | atha yāni aneka-arthāni eka-śabdāni = now those that have many meanings in one word | tāni ataḥ anukramiṣyāmaḥ = these we shall go through from here | anavagata-saṃskārān ca nigamān = and Vedic words whose formation is not understood | tadā aikapadikam iti ācakṣate = they call this the one-word (section) | jahā jaghāna iti arthaḥ = jahā means “he struck”
2 kaḥ nu maryāḥ amithitaḥ sakhā sakhāyam abravīt = what man, unharmed, a friend, has spoken to a friend | jahā kaḥ asmat īṣate = strike; who flees from us | maryāḥ iti manuṣya-nāma = marya is a name of man | maryādā-abhidhānam vā syāt = or it may be a designation of a limit | maryādā maryaiḥ ādīyate = a limit is taken up by men | maryādā maryādinoḥ vibhāgaḥ = a limit is the division of two bounded things | methatiḥ ākrośa-karmā = meth is a verb of reviling | apāpakam jaghāna kam aham jātu = he struck one who is not evil — whom could I ever | kaḥ asmat bhītaḥ palāyate = who flees afraid of us | nidhā pāśyā bhavati = nidhā is a net | yat nidhīyate = what is laid | pāśyā pāśa-samūhaḥ = pāśyā is a mass of nooses | pāśaḥ pāśayateḥ = pāśa is from “to bind” | vipāśanāt = or from “releasing”
3 vayaḥ suparṇāḥ upa seduḥ indram = the birds, the fair-winged, sat by Indra | priyamedhāḥ ṛṣayaḥ nādhamānāḥ = the Priyamedha seers, begging | apa dhvāntam ūrṇuhi pūrdhi cakṣuḥ = uncover the darkness, fill the eye | mumugdhi asmān nidhayā iva baddhān = free us, bound as by a snare | vayaḥ veḥ bahu-vacanam = vayaḥ is the plural of “bird” | suparṇāḥ su-patanāḥ ādityaraśmayaḥ upaseduḥ indram yācamānāḥ = fair-winged, swift-flying rays of the Sun sat by Indra begging | apa ūrṇuhi adhvastam cakṣuḥ = uncover the eye that was darkened | cakṣuḥ khyāteḥ vā caṣṭe vā = cakṣus is from “to see” or “to tell” | pūrdhi pūraya dehi iti vā = pūrdhi: fill, give | muñca asmān pāśaiḥ iva baddhān = free us as if bound by nooses | pārśvataḥ śroṇitaḥ śitāmataḥ = from the side, from the hip, from the white | pārśvam parśu-mayam aṅgam bhavati = the side is a part made of ribs | parśuḥ spṛśateḥ = parśu from “to touch” | saṃspṛṣṭā pṛṣṭha-deśam = touching the region of the back | pṛṣṭham spṛśateḥ saṃspṛṣṭam aṅgaiḥ = pṛṣṭha from “to touch”: touched by the limbs | aṅgam aṅganāt añcanāt vā = aṅga from “marking” or “bending” | śroṇiḥ śroṇateḥ gati-cala-karmaṇaḥ = śroṇi from a verb of going and moving | śroṇiḥ calati iva gacchataḥ = the hip seems to move as one goes | doḥ śitāma bhavati = doḥ is “śitāma” | dordravateḥ = from “to run” | yoniḥ śitāmā iti śākapūṇiḥ = the womb is śitāma, says Śākapūṇi | viṣitaḥ bhavati = it is released | śyāmataḥ yakṛtaḥ iti taiṭīkiḥ = from śyāma, the liver, says Taiṭīki | śyāmam śyāyateḥ = śyāma is from “to go dark” | yakṛt yathā kathā ca kṛtyate = the liver is made however it is | śiti-māṃsataḥ medastaḥ iti gālavaḥ = from white flesh, from fat, says Gālava | śitiḥ śyateḥ = śiti from “to thin” | māṃsam mānanam vā mānasam vā = māṃsa from “measuring” or “mind” | manaḥ asmin sīdati iti vā = or “the mind rests in it” | medaḥ medyateḥ = medas from “to be greasy”
4 yat indra citra mā ihā na asti = what, O Indra, wondrous, is not here for me | tvā dātam adrivaḥ = to be given by you, O stone-holder | rādhaḥ naḥ vidat-vaso = give us wealth, O finder of goods | ubhayāhastyā bhara = bring with both hands | yat indra citram cāyanīyam maṃhanīyam dhanam asti = the wondrous wealth that is to be noticed and bestowed | yat me iha na asti iti vā = or what I do not have here | trīṇi madhyamāni padāni = the three middle words | tvayā naḥ tat dātavyam = that is to be given to us by you | adrivan adriḥ ādṛṇāti anena = adrivan: the stone is split with it | api vā atteḥ syāt = or from “to eat” | te somādaḥ iti ha vijñāyate = “they are Soma-eaters”, so it is known | rādhaḥ iti dhana-nāma = rādhas is a name of wealth | rādhnuvanti anena = by it they succeed | tat naḥ tvam vitta-dhana ubhābhyām hastābhyām āhara = so, O wealth-finder, bring that to us with both hands | ubhau samubdhau bhavataḥ = both are filled | damūnāḥ damamanāḥ vā dānamanāḥ vā dāntamanāḥ vā = damūnas: of home-mind, giving-mind, or tamed-mind | api vā damaḥ iti gṛha-nāma = or dama is a name of house | tat-manāḥ syāt = he would be house-minded | manaḥ manoteḥ = manas is from “to think”
5 juṣṭaḥ damūnāḥ atithiḥ duroṇe = welcomed, the house-friend, the guest in the dwelling | imam naḥ yajñam upa yāhi vidvān = come, knowing, to this our sacrifice | viśvāḥ agne abhiyujaḥ vihatya = O Agni, having struck down all assailants | śatrūyatām ābhara bhojanāni = bring the foods of the hostile | atithiḥ abhyatitaḥ gṛhān bhavati = a guest is one who goes to houses | abhyeti tithiṣu para-kulāni iti vā = or he approaches others’ families on days | para-gṛhāṇi iti vā = or others’ houses | duroṇaḥ iti gṛha-nāma = duroṇa is a name of house | duravāḥ bhavanti dustarpāḥ = they are hard to satisfy | imam naḥ yajñam upayāhi vidvān = come, knowing, to this our sacrifice | sarvāḥ agne abhiyujaḥ vihatya = O Agni, having struck down all assailants | śatrūyatām ābhara bhojanāni = bring the foods of the hostile | vihatya anyeṣām balāni śatrūṇām bhavanāt āhara bhojanāni iti vā = having crushed the forces of other enemies, bring foods | dhanāni iti vā = or wealth | mūṣaḥ mūṣikā iti arthaḥ = mūṣa means mouse | mūṣikāḥ punaḥ muṣṇāteḥ = mūṣikā again from “to steal” | mūṣaḥ api etasmāt eva = mūṣa too is from this
6 sam mā tapanti abhitaḥ sapatnīḥ iva parśavaḥ = the ribs burn me all round like co-wives | mūṣaḥ na śiśnā vyadanti mādhyaḥ = like a mouse, my desires bite with the organ | stotāram te śatakrato = your praiser, O hundred-willed | vittam me asya rodasī = Heaven and Earth, know this of me | santapanti mām abhitaḥ sapatnyaḥ iva imāḥ parśavaḥ kūpa-parśavaḥ = these ribs, pit-ribs, burn me all round like co-wives | mūṣikāḥ iva asnātāni sūtrāṇi vyadanti = like mice they bite the unwashed threads | svāṅga-abhidhānam vā syāt = or it may be a naming of one’s own limbs | śiśnāni vyadanti iti vā = or they bite the organs | santapanti mādhyaḥ kāmāḥ = my desires burn | stotāram te śatakrato = your praiser, O hundred-willed | jānītam me asya dyāvāpṛthivyau iti = “Heaven and Earth, know this of me” | tritam kūpe avahitam etat sūktam pratibabhau = this hymn flashed to Trita placed in a well | tatra brahma-itihāsa-miśram ṛṅ-miśram gāthā-miśram bhavati = there it is mixed with brahman, tale, verse and gāthā | tritaḥ tīrṇatamaḥ medhayā babhūva = Trita became the most crossed through insight | api vā saṃkhyānām eva abhipretam syāt = or the numbers alone may be meant | ekataḥ dvitaḥ tritaḥ iti trayaḥ babhūvuḥ = Ekata, Dvita, Trita — three there were
7 iṣireṇa te manasā sutasya bhakṣīmahi = with a ready mind may we partake of your pressed (soma) | pitryasya iva rāyaḥ = as of ancestral wealth | soma rājan pra naḥ āyūṃṣi tārīḥ = O King Soma, lengthen our lives | ahāni iva sūryaḥ vāsarāṇi = as the Sun the days | īṣaṇena vaiṣaṇena vārṣaṇena vā = with going, seeking or showering | te manasā sutasya bhakṣīmahi pitryasya iva dhanasya = with a mind may we partake of your pressed soma, as of ancestral wealth | pravardhaya ca naḥ āyūṃṣi somarājan = and prolong our lives, O King Soma | ahāni iva sūryaḥ vāsarāṇi = as the Sun the days | vāsarāṇi vesarāṇi vivāsanāni gamanāni iti vā = vāsara: vesara, “dwellings”, or “goings” | kurutana iti anarthakāḥ upajanāḥ bhavanti = in kurutana the added bits are meaningless | kartana hantana yātana iti = as in kartana, hantana, yātana | jaṭharam udaram bhavati = jaṭhara is the belly | jagdham asmin dhriyate dhīyate vā = what is eaten is held or placed in it
8 marutvān indra vṛṣabhaḥ raṇāya = O Indra with the Maruts, bull, for the fight | pibā somam anuṣvadham madāya = drink the soma as is your due, for exhilaration | ā siñcasva jaṭhare madhvaḥ ūrmim = pour into the belly a wave of sweetness | tvam rājā asi pradivaḥ sutānām = you are king of the pressed since old | marutvān indra marudbhiḥ tad-vān = Indra possessed of the Maruts | vṛṣabhaḥ varṣitā apām = the bull, rainer of waters | raṇāya ramaṇīyāya saṃgrāmāya = for the delightful fight | piba somam = drink the soma | anuṣvadham anu-annam = according to the food | madāya madanīyāya jaitrāya = for exhilaration, for victory | āsiñcasva jaṭhare madhunaḥ ūrmim = pour into the belly a wave of sweetness | madhu somam iti aupamikam mādyateḥ = “honey” for soma is by comparison, from “to be glad” | idam api itarat madhu etasmāt eva = this other honey too is for this reason | tvam rājā asi pūrveṣu api ahaḥsu sutānām = you are king of the pressed even in former days
9 titau paripavanam bhavati = titau is a sieve | tatavat vā tunnavat vā = or full of stretches, or of pierced places | tila-mātra-tunnam iti vā = or pierced to the size of a sesame seed
10 saktum iva titaunā punantaḥ = purifying like meal with a sieve | yatra dhīrāḥ manasā vācam akrata = where the wise made speech with the mind | atra sakhāyaḥ sakhyāni jānate = here friends know friendships | bhadrā eṣām lakṣmīḥ nihitā adhi vāci = their lucky fortune is placed on speech | saktum iva paripavanena punantaḥ = like meal purified with a sieve | saktuḥ sacateḥ = saktu is from “to cling” | durdhāvaḥ bhavati = it is hard to run through | kasateḥ vā syāt viparītasya = or from “to open”, reversed | vikasitaḥ bhavati = it is spread out | yatra dhīrāḥ manasā vācam akṛṣata prajñānam = where the wise ploughed speech by mind, by understanding | dhīrāḥ prajñānavantaḥ dhyānavantaḥ = the wise are those of understanding and meditation | tatra sakhāyaḥ sakhyāni saṃjānate = there friends recognize friendships | bhadrā eṣām lakṣmīḥ nihitā adhi vāci iti = their lucky fortune is placed on speech | bhadram bhagena vyākhyātam = bhadra has been explained with bhaga | bhajanīyam bhūtānām abhidravaṇīyam = it is to be shared, to be run to, by beings | bhavat ramayati iti vā = or it gladdens what is | bhājanavat vā = or like a vessel | lakṣmīḥ lābhāt vā lakṣaṇāt vā lapsyanāt vā lāñchanāt vā = lakṣmī from gain, mark, being obtained, or marking | laṣateḥ vā syāt prepsā-karmaṇaḥ = or from laṣ, a verb of wishing | lagyateḥ vā syāt āśleṣa-karmaṇaḥ = or from lag, a verb of clinging | lajjateḥ vā syāt aślāghā-karmaṇaḥ = or from laj, a verb of shame | śipre iti upariṣṭāt vyākhyāsyāmaḥ = śipre we shall explain later
11 tat sūryasya devatvam tat mahitvam = that is the Sun’s godhood, that his greatness | madhyā kartoḥ vitatam sam jabhāra = he gathered what was spread in the midst of works | yat īm ayukta haritaḥ sadhasthāt = when he yoked the tawny ones from their common seat | ā rātrī vāsaḥ tanute simasmai = then Night spreads her garment for all | tat sūryasya devatvam tat mahitvam madhye yat karmaṇām kriyamāṇānām vitatam saṃhriyate = that is the Sun’s godhood, his greatness, that what is spread amid works being done is gathered up | yadā asau ayuṅkta haraṇāt ādityaraśmīn = when he yoked the Sun’s rays, from carrying | harita aśvān iti vā = or the tawny horses | atha rātrī vāsaḥ tanute simasmai = then Night spreads her garment for all | vesaram aharavayuvatī sarvasmāt = vesara: a young woman for all (?) | api vā upamā-arthe syāt = or it may be in the sense of comparison | rātrī iva vāsaḥ tanute iti = Night spreads as a garment | tathā api nigamaḥ bhavati = so too a text | punaḥ sam avyat vitatam vayantī = again she wove together what was spread, weaving | samanāt sīta = from “sam” she drew in
12 indreṇa sam hi dṛkṣase = you are seen together with Indra | sañjagmānaḥ abibhyuṣā = going together with the fearless | mandū samāna-varcasā = two glad ones of equal splendour | indreṇa hi saṃdṛśyase saṃgacchamānaḥ abibhyuṣā gaṇena = you are seen with Indra, going with a fearless host | mandū madiṣṇū yuvām sthaḥ = glad ones, you two are | api vā mandunā tena iti syāt = or it may mean “with that glad one” | samāna-varcasā iti etena vyākhyātam = “of equal splendour” is explained by this
13 īrmāntāsaḥ silika-madhyamāsaḥ = with sides sped on, with middles joined | saṃśūraṇāsaḥ divyāsaḥ atyāḥ = rushing together, heavenly steeds | haṃsāḥ iva śreṇiśaḥ yatante = like geese they strive in rows | yat ākṣiṣuḥ divyam ajmam aśvāḥ = when the horses reached the heavenly course | īrmāntāḥ samīritāntāḥ susamīritāntāḥ = īrmānta: with sped-on ends, well sped-on | pṛthvantāḥ vā = or broad-ended | silika-madhyamāḥ saṃsṛta-madhyamāḥ śīrṣa-madhyamāḥ vā = with middles joined, or with the head in the middle | api vā śiraḥ ādityaḥ bhavati = or the head is the Sun | yat anuśete sarvāṇi bhūtāni = because all beings lie beneath it | madhye ca eṣām tiṣṭhati = and it stands in the midst of them | idam api itarat śiraḥ etasmāt eva = this other head too is for this reason | samāśritāni etat indriyāṇi bhavanti = the senses rest on it | saṃśūraṇāsaḥ divyāsaḥ atyāḥ = rushing together, heavenly, goers | śūraḥ śavateḥ gati-karmaṇaḥ = śūra is from śu, a verb of going | divyāḥ divi-jāḥ = divya: born in heaven | atyāḥ atanāḥ = atya: goers | haṃsāḥ iva śreṇiśaḥ yatante = like geese they strive in rows | haṃsāḥ hanteḥ ghnanti adhvānam = haṃsa from han: they strike the road | śreṇiśaḥ iti śreṇiḥ śrayateḥ = in rows: śreṇi from “to rest” | samāśritāḥ bhavanti = they are joined together | yat ākṣiṣuḥ yat āpan = when they reached, when they attained | divyam ajmam ajanim ājim aśvāḥ = the heavenly course, the contest, the horses | asti ādityastutiḥ aśvasya = there is a praise of the Sun in respect of the horse | ādityāt aśvaḥ nirataṣṭaḥ iti = from the Sun the horse was fashioned | sūrāt aśvam vasavaḥ nirataṣṭa = from the Sun the Vasus fashioned the horse | iti api nigamaḥ bhavati = so too a text
14 kāyamānaḥ vanā tvam yat mātṝḥ ajagan apaḥ = desiring the woods, when you went to the waters, the mothers | na tat te agne pramṛṣe nivartanam = that return of yours, O Agni, is not forgotten | yat dūre san iha abhavaḥ = that though far, you were here | kāyamānaḥ cāyamānaḥ kāmayamānaḥ iti vā = kāyamāna: noticing, or desiring | vanāni = woods | tvam yat mātṝḥ apaḥ agamaḥ upaśāmyan = when you went to the mothers, the waters, calming | na tat te agne pramṛṣyate nivartanam = that return of yours, Agni, is not forgotten | dūre yat san iha bhavasi jāyamānaḥ = that though far, you are here being born | lodham nayanti paśu manyamānāḥ = they lead the greedy one, thinking him an animal | lubdham ṛṣim nayanti paśum manyamānāḥ = they lead the greedy seer, thinking him an animal | śīram pāvaka-śociṣam = the one with purifying flame, lying | pāvaka-dīptim = of purifying radiance | anuśāyinam iti vā = or “lying along” | āśinam iti vā = or “eating”
15 kanīnakā iva vidradhe nave drupade arbhake = like two maidens, in a split, new, small post | babhrū yāmeṣu śobhete = the two brown ones shine on their journeys | kanīnake kanyake = kanīnaka: two girls | kanyā kamanīyā bhavati = a girl is lovely | kva iyam netavyā iti vā = or “where is she to be led” | kamanena anīyata iti vā = or “she is led by desire” | kanateḥ vā syāt kānti-karmaṇaḥ = or from kan, a verb of desiring | kanyayoḥ adhiṣṭhāna-pravacanāni = the two maidens’ place of standing | saptamyāḥ ekavacanāni iti śākapūṇiḥ = locative singulars, says Śākapūṇi | vidradhayoḥ dāru-pādayoḥ = of the two split (wooden) feet | dāru dṛṇāteḥ vā drūṇāteḥ vā = dāru from “to split” | tasmāt eva dru = from the same, dru | nave nava-jāte = new: newly born | arbhake avṛddhe = small: not grown | te yathā tat-adhiṣṭhāneṣu śobhete = as they shine in their places | evam babhrū yāmeṣu śobhete = so the brown ones shine on their journeys | babhrvoḥ aśvayoḥ saṃstavaḥ = it is a praise of the two brown horses | idam ca me adāt idam ca me adāt iti ṛṣiḥ prasaṃkhyāya āha = “he gave me this, and this”, says the seer, counting | suvāstvāḥ adhi tugvani = on the Suvāstu at the ford | suvāstuḥ nadī = Suvāstu is a river | tugva tīrtham bhavati = tugva is a ford | tūrṇam etat āyanti = they come to it quickly | kuvit naṃsante marutaḥ punaḥ naḥ = will the Maruts bow to us again | punaḥ naḥ namante marutaḥ = the Maruts bow to us again | nasante iti upariṣṭāt vyākhyāsyāmaḥ = nasante we shall explain later | ye te madāḥ āhanasaḥ vihāyasaḥ = those exhilarations of yours, striking, far-reaching | tebhiḥ indram codaya dātave magham = by them urge Indra to give wealth | ye te madāḥ āhanavantaḥ vaṃcanavantaḥ = those exhilarations of yours, having striking and swerving | taiḥ indram codaya dānāya magham = by them urge Indra to give wealth
16 upo adarśi śundhyuvaḥ na vakṣaḥ = the breast of the radiant one has appeared | nodhāḥ iva āviḥ akṛta priyāṇi = like Nodhas she made dear things plain | admasat na sasataḥ bodhayantī = waking the sleepers, like the one eating | śaśvattamā āgāt punaḥ eyuṣīṇām = she came, most constant, of those who return | upādarśi śundhyuvaḥ = the radiant one has appeared | śundhyuḥ ādityaḥ bhavati = śundhyu is the Sun | śodhanāt = from cleansing | tasya eva vakṣaḥ bhāsā adhyūḍham = his breast, covered over with light | idam api itarat vakṣaḥ etasmāt eva = this other breast too is for this reason | adhyūḍham kāye = laid on the body | śakuniḥ api śundhyuḥ ucyate = a bird too is called śundhyu | śodhanāt eva = from cleansing too | udaka-caraḥ bhavati = it goes in water | āpaḥ api śundhyuvaḥ ucyante = waters too are called śundhyu | nodhāḥ ṛṣiḥ bhavati = Nodhas is a seer | navanam dadhāti = he places newness | saḥ yathā stutyā kāmān āviṣkurute = as he makes desires plain by praise | evam uṣāḥ rūpāṇi āviṣkurute = so Dawn makes forms plain | admasat admānnam bhavati = admasat is food | admasādinī iti vā admasāninī iti vā = “eating food” or “giving food” | sasataḥ bodhayantī = waking the sleepers | śaśvattamā āgāt punaḥ eyuṣīṇām = the most constant came, of those returning | svapataḥ bodhayantī = waking the sleeping | śāśvatikatamā āgāt punaḥ eyuṣīṇām = the most lasting came, of those who return | te vāśīmantaḥ iṣmiṇaḥ = those with axes, with arrows | īṣaṇinaḥ iti vaiṣaṇinaḥ iti vārṣaṇinaḥ iti vā = “going, seeking, showering” | vāśī iti vāṅ-nāma = vāśī is a name of speech | vāśyate iti satyāḥ = from “it is cried out” | śaṃsā adhvaryo prati me gṛṇīhi = recite, O Adhvaryu, answer me | indrāya vāhaḥ kṛṇavāva juṣṭam = let us make a welcome offering for Indra | abhivahana-stutim
