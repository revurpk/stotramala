# Nirukta, adhyāya 2 — word by word. Etymologies are given as Yāska states them; examples quoted for form are glossed as examples.
1 atha = now | nirvacanam = the explanation (etymology) | tat = then | yeṣu padeṣu = in those words in which | svara-saṃskārau samarthau = accent and formation are fitting | prādeśikena vikāreṇa anvitau syātām = and accompanied by a local modification | tathā tāni nirbrūyāt = so one should explain them | atha ananvite arthe aprādeśike vikāre = then where the sense does not follow and there is no local modification | artha-nityaḥ parīkṣeta = one who holds to the sense should examine | kenacit vṛtti-sāmānyena = by some likeness of function | avidyamāne sāmānye api = even where there is no likeness | akṣara-varṇa-sāmānyāt nirbrūyāt = he may explain by likeness of syllable or letter | na tu eva na nirbrūyāt = but he should never refuse to explain | na saṃskāram ādriyeta = let him not be bound by the grammatical form | viṣayavatyaḥ hi vṛttayaḥ bhavanti = for the functions of words have their own fields | yathā-artham vibhaktīḥ sannamayet = he should bend the case-endings to the sense | pratta-vat tam iti = “given”, “like it” | dhātu-ādī eva śiṣyete = only the first letter of the root remains | atha api asteḥ nivṛtti-sthāneṣu ādi-lopaḥ bhavati = also with as the first letter drops where it ceases | staḥ = they two are | santi = they are | atha api anta-lopaḥ bhavati = also the last letter drops | gatvā = having gone | gatam = gone | atha api upadhā-lopaḥ bhavati = also the penultimate drops | jagmatuḥ jagmuḥ iti = they two went, they went | atha api upadhā-vikāraḥ bhavati = also the penultimate changes | rājā daṇḍī iti = king, staff-bearer | atha api varṇa-lopaḥ bhavati = also a letter drops | tattvā yāmi iti = “I come to you” | atha api dvi-varṇa-lopaḥ = also two letters drop | tṛcaḥ iti = of the three-verse (tṛca) | atha api ādi-viparyayaḥ bhavati = also the first letter changes place | jyotiḥ ghanaḥ binduḥ vāṭyaḥ iti = light, mass, drop, (vāṭya) | atha api ādi-anta-viparyayaḥ bhavati = also first and last interchange | stokāḥ rajjuḥ sikatāḥ tarkuḥ iti = drops, rope, sand, spindle | atha api anta-vyāpattiḥ bhavati = also the end is lost
2 oghaḥ meghaḥ nādhaḥ gādhaḥ vadhūḥ madhu iti = flood, cloud, (nādha), ford, bride, honey | atha api varṇa-upajanaḥ = also a letter is added | āsthat dvāraḥ bharūjā iti = (āsthat), door, (bharūjā) | tat yatra svarāt anantara-anta-sthā-antardhātuḥ bhavati = where the root hides in a semivowel after a vowel | tat dvi-prakṛtīnām sthānam iti pradiśanti = they point to this as the place of words of two bases | tatra siddhāyām anupapadyamānāyām itarayā upapipādayiṣet = if one derivation fails there, let him establish by the other | tatra api eke alpa-niṣpattayaḥ bhavanti = even there some words have little formation | tat yathā etat = as these | ūtiḥ mṛduḥ pṛthuḥ pṛṣataḥ kuṇārum iti = help, soft, broad, spotted deer, (kuṇāru) | atha api bhāṣikebhyaḥ dhātubhyaḥ naigamāḥ kṛtaḥ bhāṣyante = also the Vedic forms from common roots are used | damūnāḥ kṣetra-sādhāḥ iti = (damūnas), field-achievers | atha api naigamebhyaḥ bhāṣikāḥ = also common words from Vedic ones | uṣṇam ghṛtam iti = hot, ghee | atha api prakṛtayaḥ eva ekeṣu bhāṣyante vikṛtayaḥ ekeṣu = also in some regions the bases alone are used, in others the derived forms | śavatiḥ gati-karmā kaṃbojeṣu eva bhāṣyate = śav, a verb of going, is used only among the Kambojas | kaṃbojāḥ kaṃbala-bhojāḥ = the Kambojas, blanket-eaters | kamanīya-bhojāḥ vā = or those with lovely food | kaṃbalaḥ kamanīyaḥ bhavati = a blanket is something lovely | vikāram asya āryeṣu bhāṣante = among the Āryas they use a derived form of it | śavaḥ iti = “śava” | dātiḥ lavana-arthe prācyeṣu = dāti, “to cut”, is used among the easterners | dātram udīcyeṣu = dātra (sickle) among the northerners | evam eka-padāni nirbrūyāt = so he should explain single words | atha taddhita-samāseṣu eka-parvasu vā aneka-parvasu ca = now in secondary derivatives and compounds of one or several joints | pūrvam pūrvam aparam aparam pravibhajya nirbrūyāt = dividing the earlier and the later each in turn, he should explain | daṇḍyaḥ puruṣaḥ = a man deserving the staff | daṇḍa-puruṣaḥ = staff-man | daṇḍam arhati iti vā = or “he deserves the staff” | daṇḍena sampadyate iti vā = or “he is endowed with the staff” | daṇḍaḥ dadāteḥ dhārayati-karmaṇaḥ = daṇḍa is from dā, “to hold” | akrūraḥ dadate maṇim iti abhibhāṣante = they say “Akrūra gives the jewel” | damanāt iti aupamanyavaḥ = from “taming”, says Aupamanyava | daṇḍam asya ākarṣati iti garhāyām = “he drags his staff”, in reproach | kakṣyā rajjuḥ aśvasya = kakṣyā is a horse’s rope | kakṣam sevate = it serves the side | kakṣaḥ gāhateḥ = kakṣa is from gāh, “to plunge” | ksa iti nāma-karaṇaḥ = ks forms the noun | khyāteḥ vā anarthakaḥ abhyāsaḥ = or the reduplication from khyā is meaningless | kim asmin khyānam iti = “what is its fame?” | kaṣateḥ vā = or from kaṣ, “to rub” | tat-sāmānyāt manuṣya-kakṣaḥ = by likeness to that, the human armpit | bāhu-mūla-sāmānyāt aśvasya = of the horse by likeness to the arm-root
3 rājñaḥ puruṣaḥ rāja-puruṣaḥ = the king’s man, royal servant | rājā rājateḥ = king is from rāj, “to shine” | puruṣaḥ puri ṣādaḥ = puruṣa is “he who sits in the city” | puri śayaḥ = “lying in the city” | pūrayateḥ vā = or from pūr, “to fill” | pūrayati antaḥ iti = “he fills the inside” | antara-puruṣam abhipretya = meaning the inner person | yasmāt param na aparam asti kiñcit = than whom there is nothing higher or lower | yasmāt na aṇīyaḥ na jyāyaḥ asti kaścit = than whom none is subtler or greater | vṛkṣaḥ iva stabdhaḥ divi tiṣṭhati ekaḥ = he stands alone in heaven like a tree, firm | tena idam pūrṇam puruṣeṇa sarvam = by that person all this is filled | iti api nigamaḥ bhavati = so also there is a Vedic text | viśva-kadra-ākarṣaḥ = (a name) | vi iti cakadraḥ iti śva-gatau bhāṣyate = “vi” and “cakadra” are used in the sense of going | drāti iti gati-kutsanā = “drāti” is bad going | kadrāti iti drāti-kutsanā = “kadrāti” is bad running | cakadrāti kadrāti iti sataḥ anarthakaḥ abhyāsaḥ = the reduplication of cakadrāti, kadrāti is meaningless | tat asmin asti iti viśva-kadraḥ = in whom that is, “Viśvakadra” | kalyāṇa-varṇa-rūpaḥ = of fair colour and form | kalyāṇa-varṇasya iva asya rūpam = his form is as of fair colour | kalyāṇam kamanīyam bhavati = kalyāṇa is what is desirable | varṇaḥ vṛṇoteḥ = varṇa is from vṛ, “to choose, cover” | rūpam rocateḥ = rūpa is from ruc, “to please” | evam taddhita-samāsān nirbrūyāt = so he should explain derivatives and compounds | na eka-padāni nirbrūyāt = he should not explain single words | na avaiyākaraṇāya = to one without grammar | na anupasannāya = to one who has not come as a pupil | anidam-vide vā = or to one who does not know this | nityam hi avijñātuḥ vijñāne asūyā = for the ignorant always scorn knowledge | upasannāya tu nirbrūyāt = but to a pupil he should explain | yaḥ vā alam vijñātum syāt = or to one fit to understand | medhāvine = to the intelligent | tapasvine vā = or the austere
4 vidyā ha vai brāhmaṇam ā jagāma = knowledge came to the brahmin | gopāya mā = guard me | śevadhiḥ te aham asmi = I am your treasure | asūyakāya anṛjave ayatāya = to one who sneers, is crooked, uncontrolled | na mā brūyāḥ = do not tell me | vīryavatī tathā syām = so I shall be potent | yaḥ ātṛṇatti avitathena karṇau = who pierces the ears with truth | aduḥkham kurvan amṛtam saṃ prayacchan = without pain, giving immortality | tam manyeta pitaram mātaram ca = let one think him father and mother | tasmai na druhyet katamat cana āha = let one never wrong him | adhyāpitāḥ ye gurum na ādriyante = those taught who do not honour the teacher | viprāḥ vācā manasā karmaṇā vā = brahmins in word, mind or deed | yathā eva te na guroḥ bhojanīyāḥ = as they are not fit to feed the teacher | tathā eva tān na bhunakti śrutam tat = so that learning does not nourish them | yam eva vidyāḥ śucim apramattam = whom you know as pure, watchful | medhāvinam brahmacarya-upapannam = intelligent, practising celibate study | yaḥ te na druhyet katamat cana āha = who would never wrong you | tasmai mā brūyāḥ nidhi-pāya brahman = tell me to him, O brahmin, the guardian of the treasure | iti = thus | nidhiḥ śevadhiḥ iti = nidhi means śevadhi
5 atha ataḥ anukramiṣyāmaḥ = now we shall go through in order | gauḥ iti pṛthivyāḥ nāmadheyam = gauḥ is a name of earth | yat dūram gatā bhavati = because she has gone far | yat ca asyām bhūtāni gacchanti = and because beings go upon her | gāteḥ vā aukāraḥ nāma-karaṇaḥ = or the au-ending makes the noun from gā, “to go” | atha api paśu-nāma iha bhavati etasmāt eva = also the name of the animal arises here from this | atha api asyām tāddhitena kṛtsna-vat nigamāḥ bhavanti = also there are Vedic texts about her with a secondary formation | gobhiḥ śrīṇīta matsaram = mix the soma with cow-products | iti payasaḥ = of milk | matsaraḥ somaḥ = matsara is soma | mandateḥ tṛpti-karmaṇaḥ = from mad, a verb of satisfying | matsaraḥ iti lobha-nāma = matsara is also a name of greed | abhimattaḥ enena dhanam bhavati = by this wealth is intoxicated | payaḥ pibateḥ vā pyāyateḥ vā = payas is from pā “to drink” or pyāy “to swell” | kṣīram kṣarateḥ = kṣīra is from kṣar, “to flow” | ghaseḥ vā ero nāma-karaṇaḥ = or from ghas, “to eat”, with era making the noun | uśīram iti yathā = as uśīra | aṃśum duhantaḥ adhyāsate gavi = milking the soma-stalk, they sit on the hide | iti adhiṣavaṇa-carmaṇaḥ = of the pressing-hide | aṃśuḥ śam-aṣṭa-mātraḥ bhavati = the aṃśu is a span in measure | ananāya śam bhavati iti vā = or it gives well-being to the breath | carma caratēḥ vā = carma is from car, “to move” | uccṛtam bhavati iti vā = or because it is stripped off | atha api carma ca śleṣmā ca = also hide and phlegm | gobhiḥ sannaddhaḥ asi vīḷayasva = you are bound with cow-hide; be firm | iti ratha-stutau = in praise of the chariot | atha api snāva ca śleṣmā ca = also sinew and phlegm | gobhiḥ sannaddhā patati prasūtā = bound with sinews, it flies when released | iti iṣu-stutau = in praise of the arrow | jyā api gauḥ ucyate = the bowstring too is called gau | gavyā cet tāddhitam = if it is a derivative of gau, gavyā | atha cet na = if not | gavyā gamayati iṣūn = it makes the arrows go
6 vṛkṣe vṛkṣe niyatā mīmayat gauḥ = on tree after tree the fixed cow (the cloud/voice) lows | tataḥ vayaḥ pra patān pūruṣa-adaḥ = then the birds fly forth to the man-eater (?) | vṛkṣe vṛkṣe dhanuṣi dhanuṣi = tree after tree, bow after bow | vṛkṣaḥ vraścanāt = vṛkṣa is from “cutting” | vṛtvā kṣām tiṣṭhati iti vā = or “having covered the earth it stands” | kṣā-kṣiyateḥ nivāsa-karmaṇaḥ = from kṣā, kṣi, “to dwell” | niyatā mīmayat gauḥ śabdam karoti = the fixed cow makes a sound | mīmayatiḥ śabda-karmā = mīmay is a verb of sounding | tataḥ vayaḥ prapatanti = then the birds fly forth | puruṣān adanāya = to eat men | viḥ iti śakuni-nāma = vi is a name of a bird | veteḥ gati-karmaṇaḥ = from vī, a verb of going | atha api iṣu-nāma iha bhavati etasmāt eva = also the arrow’s name arises here from this | ādityaḥ api gauḥ ucyate = the Sun too is called gau | utādaḥ paruṣe gavi = and he eats in the jointed cow (?) | parvavati bhāsvati iti aupamanyavaḥ = “jointed, shining”, says Aupamanyava | atha api asya ekaḥ raśmiḥ candramasam prati dīpyate = also one ray of his shines toward the moon | tat etena upekṣitavyam = this is to be passed over | ādityataḥ asya dīptiḥ bhavati = from the Sun comes the moon’s light | suṣumṇaḥ sūrya-raśmiḥ candramā gandharvaḥ = the Suṣumṇa ray of the Sun is the moon, the gandharva | iti api nigamaḥ bhavati = so also there is a Vedic text | saḥ api gauḥ ucyate = it too is called gau | atra āha goḥ amanvata = here they thought of the cow | iti = thus | tat upariṣṭāt vyākhyāsyāmaḥ = we shall explain it later | sarve api raśmayaḥ gāvaḥ ucyante = all rays are called cows
7 tā vām vāstūni uśmasi gamadhyai = we long to go to those two dwellings of yours | yatra gāvaḥ bhūri-śṛṅgāḥ ayāsaḥ = where the many-horned cows go | atra aha tat urugāyasya vṛṣṇaḥ = there indeed that of the far-striding, mighty one | paramam padam ava bhāti bhūri = the highest step shines forth greatly | tāni vām vāstūni kāmayāmahe gamanāya yatra gāvaḥ = we desire those two dwellings of yours, to go, where the cows are | bhūri-śṛṅgāḥ bahu-śṛṅgāḥ = many-horned | bhūri iti bahunaḥ nāmadheyam = bhūri is a name of “much” | prabhavati iti sataḥ = from “it arises” | śṛṅgam śrayateḥ vā = śṛṅga is from śri, “to rest” | śṛṇāteḥ vā = or from śṛ, “to crush” | śamnāteḥ vā = or from śam, “to kill” | śaraṇāya udgatam iti vā = or “risen for shelter” | śirasaḥ nirgatam iti vā = or “come out of the head” | ayāsaḥ ayanāḥ = ayāsaḥ are goings | tatra tat urugāyasya viṣṇoḥ mahā-gateḥ = there that of the far-striding Viṣṇu of great going | paramam padam parārdhya-stham avabhāti bhūri = the highest step standing in the farthest place shines greatly | pādaḥ padyateḥ = pāda is from pad, “to go” | tat-nidhānāt padam = from its being the place of that, padam | paśu-pāda-prakṛtiḥ prabhāga-pādaḥ = the base is an animal’s foot, a part-foot | prabhāga-pāda-sāmānyāt itarāṇi padāni = other padas by likeness to the part-foot | evam anyeṣām api sattvānām saṃdehāḥ vidyante = so there are doubts about other beings too | tāni cet samāna-karmāṇi samāna-nirvacanāni = if they have the same action, the same explanations | nānā-karmāṇi cet nānā-nirvacanāni = if different actions, different explanations | yathā-artham nirvaktavyāni = they should be explained as the sense requires | itīmāni ekaviṃśatiḥ pṛthivī-nāmadheyāni anukrāntāni = thus these twenty-one names of the earth have been gone through | tatra nirṛtiḥ niramaṇāt = of them nirṛti is from “no pleasure” | ṛcchateḥ kṛcchra-āpattiḥ itarā = the other is trouble, from ṛch | sā pṛthivyā saṃdihyate = it is confused with the earth | tayoḥ vibhāgaḥ = their distinction | tasyāḥ eṣā bhavati = of it there is this verse
8 yaḥ īm cakāra na saḥ asya veda = he who made this does not know it | yaḥ īm dadarśa hirugit nu tasmāt = he who saw it is well hidden from him | saḥ mātuḥ yonā parivītaḥ antaḥ = he, wrapped in his mother’s womb | bahu-prajāḥ nirṛtim ā viveśa = rich in offspring, entered Nirṛti | bahu-prajāḥ kṛcchram āpadyate iti parivrājakāḥ = “rich in offspring, he falls into trouble”, say the wanderers | varṣa-karma iti nairuktāḥ = the etymologists say it concerns rain | yaḥ īm cakāra iti karoti-kirati saṃdigdhau varṣa-karmaṇā = in “yaḥ īm cakāra”, kṛ “to make” and kṝ “to scatter” are in doubt, by the act of raining | na saḥ asya veda madhyamaḥ = the middle one does not know it | saḥ eva asya veda madhyamaḥ yaḥ dadarśa āditya-upahitam = only the middle one knows it who saw it as set by the Sun | saḥ mātuḥ yonau = he in the mother’s womb | mātā antarikṣam = the mother is the mid-air | nirmīyante asmin bhūtāni = beings are built in it | yoniḥ antarikṣam = the womb is the mid-air | mahān avayavaḥ = a great part | parivītaḥ vāyunā = wrapped in the wind | ayam api itaraḥ yoniḥ etasmāt eva = this other womb too is for this reason | pariyutaḥ bhavati = it is joined round | bahu-prajā bhūmim āpadyate varṣa-karmaṇā = rich in offspring, it comes to the earth by the act of rain | śākapūṇiḥ saṃkalpayāṃ cakre = Śākapūṇi resolved | sarvāḥ devatāḥ jānāni iti = “I will know all the deities” | tasmai devatā ubhaya-liṅgā prādurbabhūva = a deity of both genders appeared to him | tām na jajñe = he did not know her | tām papraccha = he asked her | vividiṣāṇi tvā iti = “may I come to know you” | sā asmai etām ṛcam ādideśa = she showed him this verse | eṣā mad-devatā iti = “this is my deity”
9 ayam saḥ śiṃkte yena gauḥ abhīvṛtā = this is he by whom the cow, enveloped, sounds | mimāti māyum dhvasanau adhi śritā = she bellows, resting on the cloud | sā cittibhiḥ ni hi cakāra martyam = by her deeds she has laid low the mortal | vidyut bhavantī prati vavrim auhata = becoming lightning, she cast off her covering | ayam saḥ śabdāyate yena gauḥ abhipravṛttā mimāti = this is he by whom the cow, set in motion, bellows | māyum śabdam karoti = she makes the sound of bellowing | māyum iva ādityam iti vā = or she makes the Sun like the bellow | vāk eṣā mādhyamikā = this is the speech of the mid-air | dhvaṃsane meghe adhiśritā = resting on the cloud, the shattering one | sā cittibhiḥ karmabhiḥ nīcaiḥ nikaroti martyam = with her deeds she brings low the mortal | vidyut bhavantī pratyūhate vavrim = becoming lightning she puts back her covering | vavriḥ iti rūpa-nāma = vavri is a name of form | vṛṇoti iti sataḥ = from “it covers” | varṣeṇa pracchādya pṛthivīm tat punaḥ ādatte = covering the earth with rain, it takes it back again
10 hiraṇya-nāmāni uttarāṇi pañcadaśa = fifteen names of gold follow | hiraṇyam kasmāt = why “hiraṇya” | hriyate āyamyamānam iti vā = because it is carried off when held out | hriyate janāt janam iti vā = because it is carried from person to person | hita-ramaṇam bhavati iti vā = or it is a pleasing good | hṛdaya-ramaṇam bhavati iti vā = or it delights the heart | haryateḥ vā syāt prepsā-karmaṇaḥ = or from hary, “to desire” | antarikṣa-nāmāni uttarāṇi ṣoḍaśa = sixteen names of the mid-air follow | antarikṣam kasmāt = why “antarikṣa” | antarā kṣāntam bhavati = it lies in between | antarā īme iti vā = or “between these two” | śarīreṣu antara-kṣayam iti vā = or “dwelling inside bodies” | tatra samudraḥ iti etat pārthivena samudreṇa saṃdihyate = there samudra is confused with the earthly ocean | samudraḥ kasmāt = why “samudra” | samuddravanti asmāt āpaḥ = waters run together from it | sam abhidravanti enam āpaḥ = waters run to it | saṃmodante asmin bhūtāni = beings rejoice in it | samudakaḥ bhavati = it is rich in water | samunatti iti vā = or “it wets” | tayoḥ vibhāgaḥ = their distinction | tatra itihāsam ācakṣate = there they tell the old story | devāpiḥ ca ārṣṭiṣeṇaḥ śaṃtanuḥ ca kauravyau bhrātarau babhūvatuḥ = Devāpi Ārṣṭiṣeṇa and Śaṃtanu, Kauravyas, were brothers | saḥ śaṃtanuḥ kanīyān abhiṣecayāṃ cakre = the younger Śaṃtanu had himself consecrated | devāpiḥ tapaḥ pratipede = Devāpi took to austerity | tataḥ śaṃtanoḥ rājye dvādaśa varṣāṇi devaḥ na vavarṣa = then in Śaṃtanu’s kingdom the god did not rain for twelve years | tam ūcuḥ brāhmaṇāḥ = the brahmins said to him | adharmaḥ tvayā caritaḥ = you have done wrong | jyeṣṭham bhrātaram antaḥ iti abhiṣecitam = by consecrating yourself with the elder brother passed over | tasmāt te devaḥ na varṣati iti =
