# Nirukta, adhyāya 12 — word by word. Etymologies are given as Yāska states them; examples quoted for form are glossed as examples.
1 atha ataḥ dyu-sthānāḥ devatāḥ = now therefore the deities whose place is heaven | tāsām aśvinau prathama-āgāminau bhavataḥ = of them the Aśvins come first | aśvinau yat vyaśnuvāte sarvam = Aśvins: because the two pervade all | rasena anyaḥ jyotiṣā anyaḥ = one with sap, the other with light | aśvaiḥ aśvinau iti aurṇavābhaḥ = “by horses, the Aśvins”, says Aurṇavābha | tat kau aśvinau = who then are the Aśvins | dyāvāpṛthivyau iti eke = heaven and earth, say some | ahorātrau iti eke = day and night, say some | sūryā-candramasau iti eke = sun and moon, say some | rājānau puṇya-kṛtau iti aitihāsikāḥ = two kings, doers of merit, say the historians | tayoḥ kālaḥ ūrdhvam ardharātrāt prakāśī-bhāvasya anuviṣṭambham = their time is after midnight, with the supporting of coming to light | anutamaḥ-bhāgaḥ hi madhyamaḥ jyotir-bhāgaḥ ādityaḥ = for the middle is the part after darkness, the Sun is the part of light | tayoḥ eṣā bhavati = of the two there is this verse
2 vasātiṣu sma carathaḥ asitau petvau iva = in the dwellings you move, like two dark, tame ones | kadā idam aśvinā yuvam abhi devān agacchatam = when, O Aśvins, did you two go to the gods | iti sā nigada-vyākhyātā = it is explained by its own recital | tayoḥ samāna-kālayoḥ samāna-karmaṇoḥ saṃstuta-prāyayoḥ asaṃstavena eṣaḥ ardharcaḥ bhavati = of these two of the same time and action, mostly praised together, this half-verse is without joint praise | vāsātyaḥ anyaḥ ucyate = one is called Vāsātya | uṣaḥ putraḥ tu anyaḥ iti = the other the son of Dawn | tayoḥ eṣā aparā bhavati = of the two there is this other verse
3 iha iha jātā sam avāvaśītām = born here and here they cried together | arepasā tanvā nāmabhiḥ svaiḥ = with unstained body and their own names | jiṣṇuḥ vām anyaḥ sumakhasya sūriḥ = one of you is the victor, the lord of fair bounty | divaḥ anyaḥ subhagaḥ putraḥ ūhe = the other, the fortunate son of heaven, is carried | iha ca iha ca jātau saṃstūyete pāpena alipyamānayā tanvā nāmabhiḥ ca svaiḥ = born here and here the two are praised, with body unsmeared by evil and by their own names | jiṣṇuḥ vām anyaḥ sumahataḥ balasya erayitā madhyamaḥ = one of you is the victor, the mover of great strength, the middle one | divaḥ anyaḥ subhagaḥ putraḥ ūhyate ādityaḥ = the other, the fortunate son of heaven, the Sun, is carried | tayoḥ eṣā aparā bhavati = of the two there is this other verse
4 prātaḥ-yujā vi bodhaya aśvinau ā iha gacchatām = awaken the two morning-yoked Aśvins; may they come here | asya somasya pītaye = to drink this soma | prātaḥ-yoginau vibodhaya aśvinau iha āgacchatām asya somasya pānāya = awaken the two morning-yoked Aśvins; may they come here to drink this soma | tayoḥ eṣā aparā bhavati = of the two there is this other verse
5 prātaḥ yajadhvam aśvinā hinota na sāyam asti deva-yā ajuṣṭam = sacrifice in the morning, send to the Aśvins; there is no evening god-worship welcome | uta anyaḥ asmat yajate vi ca āvaḥ = another too sacrifices ahead of us and wins | pūrvaḥ-pūrvaḥ yajamānaḥ vanīyān = each earlier sacrificer is the better winner | prātaḥ yajadhvam aśvinau prahiṇuta na sāyam asti deva-ijyā ajuṣṭam etat = sacrifice in the morning, send to the Aśvins; there is no evening sacrifice to the gods welcome | api anyaḥ asmat yajate vi ca āvaḥ = another too sacrifices ahead of us and wins | pūrvaḥ-pūrvaḥ yajamānaḥ vanīyān vanayitṛtamaḥ = each earlier sacrificer is the better winner: the greatest winner | tayoḥ kālaḥ sūryodaya-paryantaḥ = their time lasts until sunrise | tasmin anyāḥ devatāḥ opyante = in it other deities are sown | uṣāḥ vaṣṭeḥ kānti-karmaṇaḥ = Uṣas from vaś, a verb of desiring | ucchateḥ itarā mādhyamikā = the other, of the middle, from “to shine out” | tasyāḥ eṣā bhavati = of her there is this verse
6 uṣaḥ tat citram ā bhara asmabhyam vājinīvati = O Dawn, bring that wondrous thing to us, O rich in strength | yena tokam ca tanayam ca dhāmahe = by which we may hold sons and grandsons | uṣaḥ tat citram cāyanīyam maṃhanīyam dhanam āhara = O Dawn, bring that wondrous, noticeable, bountiful wealth | asmabhyam annavati = to us, O rich in food | yena putrān ca pautrān ca dadhīmahi = by which we may hold sons and grandsons | tasyāḥ eṣā aparā bhavati = of her there is this other verse
7 etāḥ u tyāḥ uṣasaḥ ketum akrata = these very Dawns have made their banner | pūrve ardhe rajasaḥ bhānum añjate = in the eastern half of the realm they anoint the light | niṣkṛṇvānāḥ āyudhāni iva dhṛṣṇavaḥ = preparing like bold ones their weapons | prati gāvaḥ aruṣīḥ yanti mātaraḥ = the ruddy cows, the mothers, go forth | etāḥ tāḥ uṣasaḥ ketum akṛṣata prajñānam = these Dawns have made a banner: a sign of knowing | ekasyāḥ eva pūjana-arthe bahu-vacanam syāt = the plural may be for honouring a single one | pūrve ardhe antarikṣa-lokasya samañjate bhānunā = in the eastern half of the mid-air world they anoint with light | niṣkṛṇvānāḥ āyudhāni iva dhṛṣṇavaḥ = preparing like bold ones their weapons | niḥ iti eṣaḥ saṃ iti etasya sthāne = nir stands in the place of sam | emīt eṣām niṣkṛtam jāriṇī iva = she comes to their arranged place like a lover | iti api nigamaḥ bhavati = so too a text | prati yanti = they go forth | gāvaḥ gamanāt = gāvaḥ from “going” | aruṣīḥ ārocanāt = aruṣī from “shining” | mātaraḥ bhāsaḥ nirmātryaḥ = mothers: lights, makers | sūryā sūryasya patnī = Sūryā is the Sun’s wife | eṣā eva abhisṛṣṭa-kālatamā = she has the most fitting time | tasyāḥ eṣā bhavati = of her there is this verse
8 su-kiṃśukam śalmalim viśva-rūpam = the fine kiṃśuka, the śalmali, of all forms | hiraṇya-varṇam su-vṛtam su-cakram = of golden colour, well-rolling, well-wheeled | ā roha sūrye amṛtasya lokam = mount, O Sūryā, the world of immortality | syonam patye vahatum kṛṇuṣva = make a pleasant wedding-journey for your husband | su-kāśanam śam-nam alam sarva-rūpam = well-shining, soothing, enough, of all forms | api vā upamā-arthe syāt = or it may be in the sense of comparison | sukiṃśukam iva śalmalim iti = like a fine kiṃśuka, a śalmali | kiṃśukam kraṃśateḥ prakāśayati-karmaṇaḥ = kiṃśuka from kraṃś, a verb of shining | śalmaliḥ su-śaraḥ bhavati śaravān vā = śalmali is of good reeds or reedy | āroha sūrye amṛtasya lokam udakasya = mount, O Sūryā, the world of immortality, of water | sukham patye vahatum kuruṣva = make a pleasant wedding-journey for your husband | savitā sūryām prāyacchat somāya rājñe prajāpataye vā iti ca brāhmaṇam = “Savitṛ gave Sūryā to King Soma or Prajāpati”, says a Brāhmaṇa | vṛṣākapāyī vṛṣākapeḥ patnī = Vṛṣākapāyī is Vṛṣākapi’s wife | eṣā eva abhisṛṣṭa-kālatamā = she has the most fitting time | tasyāḥ eṣā bhavati = of her there is this verse
9 vṛṣākapāyi revati su-putre ādu su-snuṣe = O Vṛṣākapāyī, wealthy one, with good sons and good daughters-in-law | ghasat te indraḥ ukṣaṇaḥ priyam kācit karam haviḥ = may Indra eat your bulls, a dear offering made | viśvasmāt indraḥ uttaraḥ = Indra is higher than all | vṛṣākapāyi revati su-putre madhyamena su-snuṣe mādhyamikayā vācā = O Vṛṣākapāyī, wealthy one, with a good son by the middle one, a good daughter-in-law by the speech of the middle | snuṣā sādhu-sādinī iti vā sādhu-sāninī iti vā = snuṣā: she who sits well, or wins well | svapatyam tat sanoti iti vā = or she gains good offspring | prāśnātu te indraḥ ukṣaṇaḥ etān mādhyamikān saṃstyāyān = may Indra eat your bulls, these mid-air gathered ones | ukṣaṇaḥ ukṣateḥ vṛddhi-karmaṇaḥ = ukṣan from ukṣ, a verb of growing | ukṣanti udakena iti vā = or they sprinkle with water | priyam kuruṣva sukha-ācaya-karam haviḥ sukha-karam haviḥ = make a dear offering that brings heaped happiness: a happiness-making offering | sarvasmāt yaḥ indraḥ uttaraḥ tam etat brūmaḥ ādityam = we say this of that Indra who is higher than all: the Sun | saraṇyūḥ saraṇāt = Saraṇyū from “going” | tasyāḥ eṣā bhavati = of her there is this verse
10 apa agūhan amṛtām martyebhyaḥ = they hid the immortal woman from mortals | kṛtvī savarṇām adadur vivasvate = having made a woman of like colour they gave her to Vivasvat | uta aśvinau abharat yat tat āsīt = and she bore the Aśvins, that which was | ajahāt u dvā mithunā saraṇyūḥ = Saraṇyū abandoned the two pairs | apa agūhan amṛtām martyebhyaḥ kṛtvī savarṇām adaduḥ vivasvate = they hid the immortal woman from mortals; having made her like in colour they gave her to Vivasvat | api aśvinau abharat yat tat āsīt = she also bore the Aśvins, that which was | ajahāt dvau mithunau saraṇyūḥ = Saraṇyū abandoned the two pairs | madhyamam ca mādhyamikām ca vācam iti nairuktāḥ = the middle one and the speech of the middle, say the etymologists | yamam ca yamīm ca iti aitihāsikāḥ = Yama and Yamī, say the historians | tatra itihāsam ācakṣate = there they tell the old story | tvāṣṭrī saraṇyūḥ vivasvataḥ ādityāt yamau mithunau janayāñcakāra = Saraṇyū, daughter of Tvaṣṭṛ, bore twin Yama and Yamī to Vivasvat the Āditya | saḥ savarṇām anyām pratinidhāya aśvam rūpam kṛtvā pradudrāva = having set another of like colour in her place, she took a horse’s form and ran away | saḥ vivasvān ādityaḥ āśvam eva rūpam kṛtvā tām anusṛtya saṃbabhūva = Vivasvat the Āditya, taking a horse’s form too, followed her and joined her | tataḥ aśvinau jajñāte = then the two Aśvins were born | savarṇāyām manuḥ = by the like-coloured one, Manu | tat abhivādinī eṣā ṛk bhavati = this verse speaks of that
11 tvaṣṭā duhitre vahatum kṛṇoti iti idam viśvam bhuvanam sam eti = Tvaṣṭṛ makes a wedding for his daughter: so all this world assembles | yamasya mātā paryuhyamānā = Yama’s mother being carried round | mahaḥ jāyā vivasvataḥ nanāśa = the great wife of Vivasvat was lost | tvaṣṭā duhituḥ vahanam karoti iti idam viśvam bhuvanam sameti = Tvaṣṭṛ makes his daughter’s wedding-journey: so all this world assembles | imāni ca sarvāṇi bhūtāni abhisamāgacchanti = and all these beings come together | yamasya mātā paryuhyamānā mahataḥ jāyā vivasvataḥ nanāśa = Yama’s mother, being carried round, the wife of great Vivasvat, was lost | rātriḥ ādityasya ādityodaye antardhīyate = night is the Sun’s: at sunrise she is hidden
12 savitā vyākhyātaḥ = Savitṛ has been explained | tasya kālaḥ yadā dyauḥ apahata-tamaskā ākīrṇa-raśmiḥ bhavati = his time is when the sky has darkness driven off and is strewn with rays | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
13 viśvā rūpāṇi prati muñcate kaviḥ = the seer puts on all forms | prāsāvīt bhadram dvipade catuṣpade = he has sent forth good for the two-footed and four-footed | vi nākam akhyat savitā vareṇyaḥ = Savitṛ, the choice one, has looked over heaven | anu prayāṇam uṣasaḥ vi rājati = he shines after the going of the dawns | sarvāṇi prajñānāni pratimuñcate medhāvī = the wise one puts on all knowledges | kaviḥ krānta-darśanaḥ bhavati kavateḥ vā = kavi is one of far-reaching sight, or from “to sound” | prasuvati bhadram dvipadbhyaḥ ca catuṣpādbhyaḥ ca = he sends forth good to the two-footed and four-footed | vyacikhyapat nākam savitā varaṇīyaḥ = Savitṛ the choice has shown heaven | prayāṇam anu uṣasaḥ virājati = he shines after the going of the dawns | adhorāmaḥ sāvitraḥ iti paśu-samāmnāye vijñāyate = “a black-below animal belongs to Savitṛ”, so it is known in the list of animals | kasmāt sāmānyāt iti = from what likeness | adhastāt tat-velāyām tamaḥ bhavati etasmāt sāmānyāt = from this likeness, that below at that time there is darkness | adhastāt rāmaḥ adhastāt kṛṣṇaḥ = black below: dark below | kasmāt sāmānyāt iti = from what likeness | agnim citvā na rāmām upeyāt = having piled the fire one should not approach a dark woman | rāmā ramaṇāya upeyate na dharmāya = a rāmā is approached for pleasure, not for duty | kṛṣṇa-jātīyā etasmāt sāmānyāt = she is of dark kind from this likeness | kṛkavākuḥ sāvitraḥ iti paśu-samāmnāye vijñāyate = “the cock belongs to Savitṛ”, so it is known in the list of animals | kasmāt sāmānyāt iti = from what likeness | kāla-anuvādam parītya = having gone round a repeating of time | kṛkavākoḥ pūrvam śabda-anukaraṇam vacaḥ uttaram = kṛkavāku: first an imitation of sound, then a word | bhagaḥ vyākhyātaḥ = Bhaga has been explained | tasya kālaḥ prāk utsarpaṇāt = his time is before the rising | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
14 prātar-jitam bhagam ugram huvema = let us call Bhaga the fierce, the morning-conqueror | vayam putram aditeḥ yaḥ vidhartā = we, the son of Aditi, who is the upholder | ādhraḥ cit yam manyamānaḥ turaḥ cit = whom even the poor man, thinking, even the swift | rājā cit yam bhagam bhakṣi iti āha = whom even the king, says “let me partake Bhaga” | prātar-jitam bhagam ugram hvayema vayam putram aditeḥ yaḥ vidhārayitā sarvasya = we call Bhaga, the fierce morning-conqueror, the son of Aditi, who upholds all | ādhraḥ cit yam manyamānaḥ āḍhyaḥ ālur daridraḥ = even the poor man, thinking him, the rich or the indigent | turaḥ cit tura iti yama-nāma = turas is a name of Yama | taratervā tvaratervā tvarayā tūrṇa-gatiḥ yamaḥ = from “to cross” or “to hasten”: Yama of hastening quick going | rājā cit yam bhagam bhakṣi iti āha = even a king says “let me partake of Bhaga” | andhaḥ bhagaḥ iti āhuḥ anutsṛptaḥ na dṛśyate = they say Bhaga is blind, not seen when not risen | prāśitram asya akṣiṇī nirjaghāna iti ca brāhmaṇam = “he struck out the two eyes of his portion”, says a Brāhmaṇa | janam bhagaḥ gacchati iti vā vijñāyate = “Bhaga goes to the man”, so it is known | janam gacchati āditya udayena = he goes to the man with the rising of the Sun | sūryaḥ sarteḥ vā suvateḥ vā svīryateḥ vā = sūrya from “to go”, “to impel”, or “to be strong” | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
15 ud u tyam jātavedasam devam vahanti ketavaḥ = the rays bear aloft that god, the Jātavedas | dṛśe viśvāya sūryam = the Sun, for all to see | udvahanti tam jātavedasam raśmayaḥ ketavaḥ = the rays, the banners, bear aloft that Jātavedas | sarveṣām bhūtānām darśanāya sūryam iti = the Sun for the seeing of all beings | kam anyam ādityāt devam evam avakṣyat = what other god than the Sun would he have said so of | tasya eṣā aparā bhavati = of him there is this other verse
16 citram devānām ud agāt anīkam = the wondrous face of the gods has risen | cakṣuḥ mitrasya varuṇasya agneḥ = the eye of Mitra, Varuṇa, Agni | ā aprāḥ dyāvāpṛthivī antarikṣam = he has filled heaven, earth and mid-air | sūryaḥ ātmā jagataḥ tasthuṣaḥ ca = the Sun is the self of the moving and the standing | cāyanīyam devānām udagamat anīkam khyānam mitrasya varuṇasya agneḥ ca = the noticeable face of the gods has risen: the sight of Mitra, Varuṇa and Agni | āpūpuraḥ dyāvāpṛthivyau ca antarikṣam ca mahattvena = he has filled heaven, earth and mid-air by greatness | sūryaḥ ātmā jaṅgamasya ca sthāvarasya ca = the Sun is the self of the moving and the standing | atha yat raśmi-poṣam puṣyati tat pūṣā bhavati = now when he nourishes the nourishment of rays, that is Pūṣan | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
17 śukram te anyat yajatam te anyat = one of yours is bright, the other worshipful | viṣu-rūpe ahanī dyauḥ iva asi = your two days are of differing forms; you are like the sky | viśvā hi māyāḥ avasi svadhāvaḥ = for you protect all wisdoms, O self-powered | bhadrā te pūṣan iha rātiḥ astu = may your good gift be here, O Pūṣan | śukram te anyat lohitam te anyat yajatam te anyat yajñiyam te anyat = one of yours is bright, another red, another worshipful, another fit for sacrifice | viṣama-rūpe te ahanī karma dyauḥ iva ca asi = your two days are of differing forms, action, and you are like the sky | sarvāṇi prajñānāni avasi annavan = you protect all knowledges, O rich in food | bhājanavatī te pūṣan iha dattiḥ astu = may your giving, O Pūṣan, be a shareable one here | tasya eṣā aparā bhavati = of him there is this other verse
18 pathaḥ-pathaḥ pari-patim vacasyā = I praise the lord of every path | kāmena kṛtaḥ abhi ānaḷ arkam = made by desire, he reached the hymn | saḥ naḥ rāsat śurudhaḥ candra-agrāḥ = may he give us the delightful-topped śurudhs | dhiyam-dhiyam sīṣadhāti pra pūṣā = may Pūṣan accomplish each work | pathaḥ-pathaḥ adhipatim vacanena kāmena kṛtaḥ abhyānaḷarkam abhyāpannaḥ arkam iti vā = lord of each path, by speech, made by desire, he reached the hymn: or he came upon the song | saḥ naḥ dadātu cāyanīya-agrāṇi dhanāni karma-karma ca naḥ prasādhayatu pūṣā iti = may he give us wealth of noticeable top, and may Pūṣan accomplish each of our works | atha yat viṣitaḥ bhavati tat viṣṇuḥ bhavati = now when he is released, that is Viṣṇu | viṣṇuḥ viśateḥ vā vyaśnoteḥ vā = Viṣṇu from “to enter” or “to pervade” | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
19 idam viṣṇuḥ vi cakrame tredhā ni dadhe padam = Viṣṇu strode over this, he set his step in three ways | samūḍham asya pāṃsure = it is gathered in his dusty one | yat idam kim ca tat vikramate viṣṇuḥ = whatever this is, Viṣṇu strides over it | tridhā nidhatte padam tredhā-bhāvāya = he sets his step in three: for threefold being | pṛthivyām antarikṣe divi iti śākapūṇiḥ = on earth, in the mid-air, in heaven, says Śākapūṇi | samārohaṇe viṣṇu-pade gaya-śirasi iti aurṇavābhaḥ = at the mounting, at the Viṣṇu-step, at Gayaśiras, says Aurṇavābha | samūḍham asya pāṃsure pyāyane antarikṣe padam na dṛśyate = gathered in his dusty, swelling mid-air the step is not seen | api vā upamā-arthe syāt = or in the sense of comparison | samūḍham asya pāṃsula iva padam na dṛśyate iti = as if his step in a dusty place is not seen | pāṃsavaḥ pādaiḥ sūyante iti vā = or the dust is impelled by feet | pannāḥ śerate iti vā = or the fallen lie | paṃsanīyāḥ bhavanti iti vā = or they are to be crushed
20 viśvānaraḥ vyākhyātaḥ = Viśvānara has been explained | tasya eṣaḥ nipātaḥ bhavati aindryām ṛci = this is its occurrence in a verse of Indra
21 viśvānarasya vaḥ patim anānatasya śavasaḥ = the lord of Viśvānara, of unbowed strength | evaiḥ ca carṣaṇīnām ūtī huve rathānām = and by goings, by protection of the peoples and chariots I call | viśvānarasya ādityasya anānatasya śavasaḥ mahataḥ balasya = of Viśvānara, the Sun, of unbowed, great strength | evaiḥ ca kāmaiḥ ayanaiḥ avanaiḥ vā = and by desires, goings or protections | carṣaṇīnām manuṣyāṇām = of the peoples: of men | ūtyā ca pathā rathānām = and by the protection and path of chariots | indram asmin yajñe hvayāmi = I call Indra in this sacrifice | varuṇaḥ vyākhyātaḥ = Varuṇa has been explained | tasya eṣā bhavati = of him there is this verse
22 yena pāvaka cakṣasā bhuraṇyantam janān anu = by which eye, O purifier, you look on the swift-moving peoples | tvam varuṇa paśyasi = you, O Varuṇa, see | bhuraṇyuḥ iti kṣipra-nāma = bhuraṇyu is a name of “quick” | bhuraṇyuḥ śakuniḥ = bhuraṇyu is a bird | bhūri-madhvānam nayati svargasya lokasya api voḍhā = it leads over a long road, also the carrier to heaven’s world | tat-saṃpātī bhuraṇyuḥ = it flies to that, the swift | anena pāvaka-khyānena bhuraṇyantam janān anu tvam varuṇa paśyasi = by this seeing of the purifier, you, O Varuṇa, look on the swift-moving peoples | tat te vayam stumaḥ iti vākya-śeṣaḥ = “that of you we praise” is the rest of the sentence | api vā uttarasyām = or it is of the next verse

40 omāsaḥ carṣaṇī-dhṛtaḥ viśve devāsaḥ ā gata = O helpers, upholders of men, all gods, come | dāśvāṃsaḥ dāśuṣaḥ sutam = you givers, to the pressed of the giver | avitāraḥ vā avanīyāḥ vā manuṣya-dhṛtaḥ sarve ca devāḥ iha āgacchata = protectors or to be helped, upholders of men, and all gods, come here | dattavantaḥ dattavataḥ sutam iti = those who have given, to the pressed of the giver | tat etat ekam eva vaiśvadevam gāyatram tṛcam daśatayīṣu vidyate = this one Gāyatrī triplet to the All-gods is found in the ten books | yat tu kiṃcit bahu-daivatam tat vaiśvadevānām sthāne yujyate = whatever is of many deities is applied in the place of the Vaiśvadeva | yat eva viśva-liṅgam iti śākapūṇiḥ = whatever has the All as its mark, says Śākapūṇi | anatyanta-gataḥ tu eṣaḥ uddeśaḥ bhavati = but this indication is not of unlimited reach | babhruḥ ekaḥ iti daśa dvipadāḥ aliṅgāḥ = “babhrur eko”: ten two-foot ones, without mark | bhūtāṃśaḥ kāśyapaḥ āśvinam eka-liṅgam = Bhūtāṃśa Kāśyapa’s hymn to the Aśvins has one mark | abhitaṣṭīyam sūktam eka-liṅgam = the Abhitaṣṭīya hymn has one mark | sādhyāḥ devāḥ sādhanāt = the Sādhyas are gods, from “accomplishing” | teṣām eṣā bhavati = of them there is this verse
41 yajñena yajñam ayajanta devāḥ = the gods sacrificed to sacrifice with sacrifice | tāni dharmāṇi prathamāni āsan = those were the first laws | te ha nākam mahimānaḥ sacanta = those great ones reach the vault of heaven | yatra pūrve sādhyāḥ santi devāḥ = where the ancient Sādhya gods are | yajñena yajñam ayajanta devāḥ agninā agnim ayajanta devāḥ = the gods sacrificed to sacrifice with sacrifice, to Agni with Agni | agniḥ paśuḥ āsīt tam ālabhanta tena ayajanta iti ca brāhmaṇam = “Agni was the victim; they seized him and sacrificed with him”, says a Brāhmaṇa | tāni dharmāṇi prathamāni āsan = those were the first laws | te ha nākam mahimānaḥ saṃsevyanta = those great ones serve the vault of heaven | yatra pūrve sādhyāḥ santi devāḥ sādhanāḥ = where the ancient Sādhya gods, accomplishers, are | dyu-sthānaḥ deva-gaṇaḥ iti nairuktāḥ = a host of gods whose place is heaven, say the etymologists | pūrvam deva-yugam iti ākhyānam = “the former age of gods”, says the tradition | vasavaḥ yat vivasate sarvam = Vasus: because they dwell in, or shine on, all | agniḥ vasubhiḥ vāsavaḥ iti samākhyā = Agni with the Vasus: “Vāsava” is the title | tasmāt pṛthivī-sthānāḥ = therefore they have earth as place | indraḥ vasubhiḥ vāsavaḥ iti samākhyā tasmāt madhya-sthānāḥ = Indra with the Vasus: “Vāsava” is the title; therefore they have the middle as place | vasavaḥ āditya-raśmayaḥ vivāsanāt tasmāt dyu-sthānāḥ = the Vasus are the Sun’s rays, from shining; therefore they have heaven as place | teṣām eṣā bhavati = of them there is this verse
42 sugāḥ vaḥ devāḥ sadanam akarma = O gods, we have made your seat easy to reach | ye ājagmuḥ savanam idam juṣāṇāḥ = who have come, enjoying this pressing | jakṣivāṃsaḥ papivāṃsaḥ ca viśve = all having eaten and drunk | asme dhatta vasavaḥ vasūni = place wealths in us, O Vasus | svāgamanāni vaḥ devāḥ supathāni akarma = O gods, we have made your arrivals easy, the paths good | ye āgacchata savanāni imāni = who come to these pressings | juṣāṇāḥ khāditavantaḥ pītavantaḥ ca = enjoying, having eaten and drunk | sarve asmāsu dhatta vasavaḥ vasūni = all, place wealths in us, O Vasus | teṣām eṣā aparā bhavati = of them there is this other verse
43 jmayā atra vasavaḥ ranta devāḥ = here on earth the Vasu gods delighted | urau antarikṣe marjayanta śubhrāḥ = in the wide mid-air the bright ones cleansed | arvākpathaḥ uru-jrayaḥ kṛṇudhvam = make the paths turn toward us, O wide-speeding | śrotā dūtasya jagmuṣaḥ naḥ asya = hear us, the messenger who has come | jmayā atra vasavaḥ aramanta devāḥ = here on earth the Vasu gods delighted | jmā pṛthivī tasyām bhavāḥ urau ca antarikṣe marjayanta gamayanta ramayanta = jmā is the earth; those on it, and in the wide mid-air, they cleansed, made go, made delight | śubhrāḥ śobhamānāḥ = bright: shining | arvācaḥ enān pathaḥ bahu-javāḥ kurudhvam = make these paths of great speed turn toward us | śṛṇuta dūtasya jagmuṣaḥ naḥ asya agneḥ = hear the messenger who has come, of us, of this Agni | vājinaḥ vyākhyātāḥ = the Vājins have been explained | teṣām eṣā bhavati = of them there is this verse
44 śam naḥ bhavantu vājinaḥ haveṣu = may the Vājins be well for us at our calls | devatātā mita-dravaḥ svarkāḥ = at the sacrifice, measured-running, well-singing | jambhayantaḥ ahim vṛkam rakṣāṃsi = crushing the snake, wolf, demons | sanemi asmat yuyavan amīvāḥ = may they drive from us, quickly, the diseases | sukhāḥ naḥ bhavantu vājinaḥ hvāneṣu devatātau yajñe = may the Vājins be pleasant to us at our calls at the sacrifice | mita-dravaḥ sumita-dravaḥ = measured-running: well measured in running | svarkāḥ svañcanāḥ iti vā svarcanāḥ iti vā svarciṣaḥ iti vā = well-singing: well-bending, well-praising, well-flaming | jambhayantaḥ ahim ca vṛkam ca rakṣāṃsi ca = crushing the snake, wolf and demons | kṣipram asmat yāvayantu = may they quickly drive off from us | amīvāḥ deva-aśvāḥ iti vā = or “diseases”; or the gods’ horses | deva-patnyaḥ devānām patnyaḥ = the gods’ wives: the wives of gods | tāsām eṣā bhavati = of them there is this verse
45 devānām patnīḥ uśatīḥ avantu naḥ = may the longing wives of the gods help us | prāvantu naḥ tujaye vāja-sātaye = may they help us for offspring and the winning of strength | yāḥ pārthivāsaḥ yāḥ apām api vrate = those of earth, those even in the law of the waters | tāḥ naḥ devīḥ su-havāḥ śarma yacchata = those goddesses, easily called, give us shelter | devānām patnyaḥ uśatyaḥ avantu naḥ = may the longing wives of the gods help us | prāvantu naḥ tujaye apatya-jananāya ca anna-saṃsananāya ca = may they help us for offspring: for begetting children and sharing food | yāḥ pārthivāsaḥ yāḥ apām api vrate karmaṇi = those of earth, those even in the water’s rite, work | tāḥ naḥ devyaḥ suhavāḥ śarma yacchantu śaraṇam = may those goddesses, easily called, give us shelter | tāsām eṣā aparā bhavati = of them there is this other verse
46 uta gnāḥ vyantu deva-patnīḥ = may the gods’ wives come | indrāṇī agnāyī aśvinī rāṭ = Indrāṇī, Agnāyī, Aśvinī, Rāj | ā rodasī varuṇānī śṛṇotu = may Rodasī and Varuṇānī hear | vyantu devīḥ yaḥ ṛtuḥ janīnām = may the goddesses come, who is the season of wives | api ca gnāḥ vyantu deva-patnyaḥ = and may the gods’ wives come | indrāṇī indrasya patnī agnāyī agneḥ patnī aśvinī aśvinoḥ patnī = Indrāṇī is Indra’s wife, Agnāyī Agni’s, Aśvinī the Aśvins’ | rāṭ rājate = Rāj shines | rodasī rudrasya patnī varuṇānī ca varuṇasya patnī = Rodasī is Rudra’s wife and Varuṇānī Varuṇa’s | vyantu devyaḥ kāmayantām yaḥ ṛtu-kālaḥ jāyānām = may the goddesses come, may they desire, who is the season-time of wives | yaḥ ṛtu-kālaḥ jāyānām = who is the season-time of wives
47 atha ataḥ dyu-sthānāḥ vasātiṣu iha-iha jātā prātaḥ-yujā prātaḥ yajadhvam uṣaḥ tat citram etāḥ u tyāḥ su-kiṃśukam vṛṣākapāyī apa agūhan tvaṣṭā duhitre savitā viśvā rūpāṇi = “athāto dyusthānā”; “vasātiṣu”; “iheha jātā”; “prātaryujā”; “prātaryajadhvam”; “uṣastaccitram”; “etā u tyāḥ”; “sukiṃśukam”; “vṛṣākapāyi”; “apāgūhan”; “tvaṣṭā duhitre”; “savitā”; “viśvā rūpāṇi” | prātar-jitam ud u tyam citram śukram te pathaḥ-pathaḥ idam viṣṇuḥ viśvānaraḥ vyākhyātaḥ viśvānarasya yena pāvaka = “prātarjitam”; “udu tyam”; “citram”; “śukraṃ te”; “pathaspathaḥ”; “idaṃ viṣṇuḥ”; “viśvānaro vyākhyātaḥ”; “viśvānarasya”; “yenā pāvaka” | catuṣkam keśī agnim trayaḥ keśinaḥ punar ehi yasmin vṛkṣe pāvīravī yat indrāgnī pavitravantaḥ uta naḥ ahiḥ yām atharvā = the group of four; “keśyagniṃ”; “trayaḥ keśinaḥ”; “punarehi”; “yasmin vṛkṣe”; “pāvīravī”; “yadindrāgnī”; “pavitravanta”; “uta no’hiḥ”; “yām atharvā” | atha ataḥ dyu-sthānāḥ deva-gaṇāḥ imāḥ giraḥ sapta ṛṣayaḥ tiryag-bilaḥ devānām bhadrā omāsaḥ yajñena sugāḥ vaḥ devāḥ jmayā atra śam naḥ bhavantu = “athāto dyusthānā devagaṇāḥ”; “imā giraḥ”; “sapta ṛṣayaḥ”; “tiryagbilaḥ”; “devānāṃ bhadrā”; “omāsaḥ”; “yajñena”; “sugā vo devāḥ”; “jmayā atra”; “śaṃ no bhavantu” | devānām patnīḥ uta gnāḥ vyantu = “devānāṃ patnīḥ”; “uta gnā vyantu” | ṣaṭ-catvāriṃśat = forty-six (the number of sections)
