हिमाचलमहावंशपावनायै | who sanctified the great line of the Himālaya

लसन्मरकतस्वच्छविग्रहायै | whose form is clear as a shining emerald
महातिशयसौन्दर्यलावण्यायै | of surpassing beauty and grace
शशाङ्कशेखरप्राणवल्लभायै | beloved of the moon-crested one’s life
सदापञ्चदशात्मैक्यस्वरूपायै | whose nature is ever one with the fifteen-syllable mantra
वज्रमाणिक्यकटककिरीटायै | wearing a crown and bracelets of diamond and ruby
कस्तूरीतिलकोल्लासिनिटिलायै | whose brow shines with a musk mark
भस्मरेखाङ्कितलसन्मस्तकायै | whose radiant head is marked with lines of ash
विकचाम्भोरुहदळलोचनायै | with eyes like petals of an open lotus
शरच्चाम्पेयपुष्पाभनासिकायै | with a nose like an autumn campaka flower
लसत्काञ्चनताटङ्कयुगळायै | with a pair of shining golden ear-ornaments
मणिदर्पणसङ्काशकपोलायै | with cheeks like jewelled mirrors
ताम्बूलपूरितस्मेरवदनायै | with a smiling mouth filled with betel
सुपक्वदाडिमीबीजरदनायै | with teeth like ripe pomegranate seeds
कम्बुपूगसमच्छायकन्धरायै | whose neck has the sheen of conch and areca palm
स्थूलमुक्ताफलोदारसुहारायै | wearing a fine necklace of large pearls
गिरीशबद्धमाङ्गल्यमङ्गळायै | the auspicious one, on whom Śiva tied the wedding-thread
पद्मपाशाङ्कुशलसत्कराब्जायै | whose lotus hands hold lotus, noose and goad
पद्मकैरवमन्दारसुमालिन्यै | wearing a garland of lotus, water-lily and mandāra flowers
सुवर्णकुम्भयुग्माभसुकुचायै | with breasts like a pair of golden pots
रमणीयचतुर्बाहुसंयुक्तायै | endowed with four lovely arms
कनकाङ्गदकेयूरभूषितायै | adorned with golden armlets and bracelets
बृहत्सौवर्णसौन्दर्यवसनायै | in beautiful golden garments
बृहन्नितम्बविलसज्जघनायै | whose broad hips and loins shine
सौभाग्यजातशृङ्गारमध्यमायै | whose waist is the very adornment born of good fortune
दिव्यभूषणसन्दोहरञ्जितायै | resplendent with a host of divine ornaments
पारिजातगुणाधिक्यपदाब्जायै | whose lotus feet surpass the wishing-tree in giving
सुपद्मरागसङ्काशचरणायै | whose feet shine like fine rubies
कामकोटिमहापद्मपीठस्थायै | seated on the great lotus seat of Kāmakoṭi
श्रीकण्ठनेत्रकुमुदचन्द्रिकायै | moonlight to the night-lily eyes of Śrīkaṇṭha (Śiva)
सचामररमावाणिविराजितायै | attended by Ramā and Vāṇī with chowries
भक्तरक्षणदाक्षिण्यकटाक्षायै | whose kind sidelong glance protects devotees
भूतालिङ्गनोद्भूतपुलकाङ्ग्यै | whose limbs bristle with joy at the embrace of the Lord of beings
अनङ्गजनकापाङ्गवीक्षणायै | whose glance is that of the father of Ananga (Kāma)
ब्रह्मोपेन्द्रशिरोरत्नरञ्जितायै | whose feet are reddened by the crest-jewels of Brahmā and Upendra
शचीमुख्यामरवधूसेवितायै | served by Śacī and the other wives of the gods
लीलाकल्पितब्रह्माण्डमण्डलायै | who in play fashioned the sphere of the universe
अमृतादिमहाशक्तिसंवृतायै | surrounded by the great Śaktis, Amṛtā and the rest
एकातपत्रसाम्राज्यदायिकायै | giver of sovereignty under a single umbrella
सनकादिसमाराध्यपादुकायै | whose sandals are worshipped by Sanaka and the sages
देवर्षिभिः स्तूयमानवैभवायै | whose glory is praised by gods and seers
कलशोद्भवदुर्वासपूजितायै | worshipped by Agastya and Durvāsas
मत्तेभवक्त्रषड्वक्त्रवत्सलायै | tender to the elephant-faced and the six-faced
चक्रराजमहायन्त्रमध्यवर्तिन्यै | dwelling at the centre of the great yantra, Cakrarāja
चिदग्निकुण्डसम्भूतसुदेहायै | whose lovely body arose from the fire-pit of consciousness
शशाङ्कखण्डसंयुक्तमकुटायै | whose crown bears a crescent moon
मत्तहंसवधूमन्दगमनायै | who walks slowly like a swan in rut
वन्दारुजनसन्दोहवन्दितायै | praised by throngs of devotees
अन्तर्मुखजनानन्दफलदायै | giver of the fruit of bliss to those who turn within
पतिव्रताङ्गनाभीष्टफलदायै | giver of their wishes to devoted wives
अव्याजकरुणापूरपूरितायै | filled with a flood of causeless mercy
नितान्तसच्चिदानन्दसंयुक्तायै | wholly united with being, consciousness and bliss
सहस्रसूर्यसंयुक्तप्रकाशायै | whose radiance is that of a thousand suns
रत्नचिन्तामणिगृहमध्यस्थायै | dwelling in the middle of the house of wishing-jewels
हानिवृद्धिगुणाधिक्यरहितायै | free from loss, growth and excess of qualities
महापद्माटवीमध्यनिवासायै | dwelling in the midst of the great lotus forest
जाग्रत्स्वप्नसुषुप्तीनां साक्षिभूत्यै | the witness of waking, dream and deep sleep
महापापौघपापानां विनाशिन्यै | destroyer of floods of the gravest sins
दुष्टभीतिमहाभीतिभञ्जनायै | breaker of the great fear of the wicked
समस्तदेवदनुजप्रेरकायै | the impeller of all gods and demons
समस्तहृदयाम्भोजनिलयायै | dwelling in the lotus of every heart
अनाहतमहापद्ममन्दिरायै | whose temple is the great Anāhata lotus
सहस्रारसरोजातवासितायै | who dwells in the thousand-petalled lotus
पुनरावृत्तिरहितपुरस्थायै | who abides in the city from which none return
वाणीगायत्रीसावित्रीसन्नुतायै | praised by Vāṇī, Gāyatrī and Sāvitrī
रमाभूमिसुताराध्यपदाब्जायै | whose lotus feet are worshipped by Ramā and Sītā
लोपामुद्रार्चितश्रीमच्चरणायै | whose holy feet are worshipped by Lopāmudrā
सहस्ररतिसौन्दर्यशरीरायै | whose body has the beauty of a thousand Ratis
भावनामात्रसन्तुष्टहृदयायै | whose heart is pleased by mere contemplation
सत्यसम्पूर्णविज्ञानसिद्धिदायै | giver of the accomplishment of true and full knowledge
श्रीलोचनकृतोल्लासफलदायै | giver of fruit that delights the blessed eye (tentative)
श्रीसुधाब्धिमणिद्वीपमध्यगायै | dwelling in the midst of the jewel-island in the ocean of nectar
दक्षाध्वरविनिर्भेदसाधिकायै | who brought about the breaking of Dakṣa’s sacrifice
श्रीनाथसोदरीभूतशोभितायै | resplendent as the sister of Viṣṇu
चन्द्रशेखरभक्तार्तिभञ्जनायै | breaker of the sorrows of Śiva’s devotees
सर्वोपाधिविनिर्मुक्तचैतन्यायै | pure consciousness freed from all limiting adjuncts
नामपारायणाभीष्टफलदायै | giver of desired fruit to those who recite her names
सृष्टिस्थितितिरोधानसङ्कल्पायै | whose will is creation, preservation and withdrawal
श्रीषोडशाक्षरीमन्त्रमध्यगायै | at the heart of the sixteen-syllable mantra
अनाद्यन्तस्वयम्भूतदिव्यमूर्त्यै | of divine form, without beginning or end, self-existent
भक्तहंसपरिमुख्यवियोगायै | from whom the swan-like devotees chiefly cannot bear to be parted (tentative)
मातृमण्डलसंयुक्तललितायै | Lalitā, joined with the circle of Mothers
भण्डदैत्यमहासत्त्वनाशनायै | destroyer of the great demon Bhaṇḍa
क्रूरभण्डशिरश्छेदनिपुणायै | skilled at cutting off cruel Bhaṇḍa’s head
धात्र्यच्युतसुराधीशसुखदायै | giver of joy to Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Indra
चण्डमुण्डनिशुम्भादिखण्डनायै | who cut down Caṇḍa, Muṇḍa, Niśumbha and others
रक्ताक्षरक्तजिह्वादिशिक्षणायै | chastiser of Raktākṣa, Raktajihva and others

अभ्रकेशमहोत्साहकारणायै | cause of the great joy of Śiva, the sky-haired

वृषभध्वजविज्ञानभावनायै | contemplating the knowledge of the bull-bannered Lord

विधेयमुक्तविज्ञानसिद्धिदायै | giver of the perfection of knowledge that frees one from duty

सर्ववेदान्तसंसिद्धसुतत्त्वायै | the true principle established by all the Vedānta
श्रीवीरभक्तविज्ञाननिधानायै | treasury of knowledge for her heroic devotees
अशेषदुष्टदनुजसूदनायै | slayer of all the wicked demons
