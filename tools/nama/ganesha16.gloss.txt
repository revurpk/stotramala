1 | Sumukha (fair-faced), Ekadanta (one-tusked), Kapila (tawny), Gajakarṇaka (elephant-eared), Lambodara (pot-bellied), Vikaṭa (huge, or misshapen), Vighnanāśa (destroyer of obstacles), Vināyaka (the leader). |
2 | Dhūmraketu (smoke-bannered), Gaṇādhyakṣa (overseer of the hosts), Bhālacandra (moon on his brow), Gajānana (elephant-faced), Vakratuṇḍa (curved-trunked), Śūrpakarṇa (winnow-eared), Heramba (the protector of the helpless), Skandapūrvaja (elder brother of Skanda). |
3 | Whoever recites or even hears these sixteen names — at the beginning of study, at a wedding, on entering or on setting out — |
4 | in battle or in danger, no obstacle will arise for him. |
